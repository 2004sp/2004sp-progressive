8. World & areas

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

37 area_* folders cover essentially the full 2004 map. 10 were sampled across regions and file counts scale sensibly with real-world importance: Varrock is the largest at 49 files, Lumbridge has 14 (10 individually-scripted named NPCs), down to minor waypoint areas like Paterdomus and White Wolf Mountain at 2–4 files each — but even those aren't empty; each has at minimum NPC placement plus a working script (e.g. a members-area gate check). No sampled area was a bare placeholder.

Implemented, depth proportional to real significance
