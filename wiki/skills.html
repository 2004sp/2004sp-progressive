2. Skills

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

All 19 skills that existed by Sept 2004 are present (7 combat + 4 gathering + 6 artisan + 2 misc) — confirmed both by content folder coverage and by the engine's PlayerStat enum, which has exactly 21 slots with the two post-2004 slots (reserved for what would become Construction/Farming) explicitly flagged disabled. No anachronistic skill exists.

Headline: all 19 period-correct skills are implemented and functional. 16 are confirmed complete, Prayer is a known partial (3 quest-unlocked prayers missing), and Agility/Fletching look complete by file structure but weren't diffed line-by-line the way Smithing, Prayer, and Magic were.
