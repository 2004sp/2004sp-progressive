2. Skills

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

The 7 Sept 2004 target has 19 skills. Fifteen were available to free-to-play players and four were members-only: Agility, Fletching, Herblore, and Thieving. The newest skill was Runecraft, added with RuneScape 2 on 29 Mar 2004; period in-game interfaces used the name Runecraft, while Jagex web/update material also used Runecrafting. No later skill such as Slayer, Farming, or Construction belongs in the cutoff skill list.

Headline: all 19 period-correct skills are implemented and functional. All 19 are currently classed complete. Prayer has been reconciled to the September 2004 cutoff at 15/15 prayers, and Agility has now been audited obstacle-by-obstacle against the period target. Fletching has also been audited recipe-by-recipe against July-2004 evidence and build 254, with its known historical differences documented in the guide.
