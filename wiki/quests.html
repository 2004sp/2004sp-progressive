1. Quests

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

Every quest folder was opened and read — not just listed — to identify which real quest it implements (via in-game journal title strings) and whether its dialogue chain, item/level gates, and reward-granting code are actually wired up.

Headline: all 56 quests that existed in RuneScape by 7 Sept 2004 are implemented and reward-complete. Nothing is fully missing. Two bonus, non-canonical miniquests are also implemented (Pub Crawl, Witch's Potion). The one quest released just after the cutoff — Tai Bwo Wannai Trio (14 Sept 2004) — correctly does not appear.
