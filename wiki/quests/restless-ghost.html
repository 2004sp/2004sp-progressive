The Restless Ghost

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

The Restless Ghost
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to Father Aereck in the Lumbridge church.

A troubled spirit has been haunting the Lumbridge graveyard, unable to move on and unable to make himself understood by the living. The town priest hopes you can find a way to communicate with the ghost and discover what's keeping him tied to this world. The answer turns out to involve an old grudge, a missing body part, and a guilt-ridden former colleague.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Walkthrough

  1. Visit the church in Lumbridge and speak with Father Aereck about the strange happenings in the graveyard.
  2. Head into the graveyard behind the church and try speaking with the ghost haunting it — you won't understand a word he says.
  3. Report back to Father Aereck, who suggests the wizards at the Wizards' Tower might have something that can help.
  4. Travel south to the Wizards' Tower near Draynor Village and head down into the basement.
  5. Speak with the wizard there about your predicament; he provides you with a Ghostspeak amulet.
  6. Equip the amulet and return to the graveyard to talk to the ghost again — now you can understand him perfectly.
  7. Learn that the ghost is missing his skull, which was taken by a man named Father Urhney for an experiment.
  8. Locate Father Urhney's cottage in the swamp southwest of the Wizards' Tower and ask him about the skull.
  9. Search his home for the missing skull once he admits what he did.
  10. Bring the skull back to the graveyard and hand it over to the grateful ghost.
  11. Return once more to Father Aereck to let him know the matter has been resolved and claim your reward.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 1125 Prayer experience
