Start point
Speak to Jeremy, a prisoner held in a cage at General Khazard's arena compound west of Yanille.

General Khazard runs a brutal gladiator pit on the edge of the Kharazi jungle, forcing captured travellers to fight to the death for the entertainment of a paying crowd. One such prisoner, a terrified man named Jeremy, is due to be thrown into the arena and has no combat training to speak of. You are his only hope of getting out alive.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are strictly required to begin; everything you need is found within the arena compound during the quest.

Recommended

  • Food
  • A weapon and armour in case guards turn hostile

Walkthrough

  1. Head to the Khazard battlefield compound west of Yanille, across the log bridge, and find the row of prison cages.
  2. Talk to Jeremy in his cage. He explains he has been captured to fight in Khazard's arena and has no chance of surviving.
  3. Ask around the compound and speak to the arena's cook, Pikkupstix, in the kitchen area to learn about the daily routine.
  4. Work out that the only way to save Jeremy is to fake his death in front of the crowd so the guards believe he was killed.
  5. Obtain a cow's heart and some redberries, then use them together to prepare a burst sack of fake blood.
  6. Sneak into the kitchen and either distract or knock out Pikkupstix so you can take his chef's uniform without being noticed.
  7. Wearing the disguise, walk past the guards into the prisoner block and deliver the fake blood sack to Jeremy.
  8. Watch as Jeremy is dragged out to fight in the arena against General Khazard's champion.
  9. At the right moment Jeremy bursts the hidden sack against his chest, convincing the crowd and guards he has been slain.
  10. His 'body' is dumped in the graveyard pit behind the arena; head there and dig him out before he suffocates.
  11. Return with Jeremy to safety and speak to him once more to wrap up the escape and finish the quest.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • 12,175 coins
  • Access to General Khazard's arena compound for future visits
