Start point
Speak to Eluned, an elf leading a small camp of exiles hidden west of West Ardougne.

West of the plague-scarred city of Ardougne, a band of elven exiles struggles against the tyrant Iban and his hold over their homeland. Eluned asks you to brave the perilous Underground Pass, a gauntlet of traps and monstrous guardians, to reach the elven lands and strike a blow against Iban's grip.

Requirements

  • Completion of Biohazard

Items needed

  • Several logs, collected along the way, for building rafts across underground rivers
  • A reliable weapon for combat encounters

Recommended

  • Plenty of food
  • Antipoison potion
  • Prayer potions

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Eluned at the small elven camp hidden west of West Ardougne to learn about Iban's tyranny.
  2. Agree to venture through the Underground Pass to reach the elven lands beyond and weaken Iban's hold.
  3. Descend into the pass and begin navigating its long series of interconnected chambers and tunnels.
  4. Cross treacherous gaps and obstacles using Agility, taking damage if you fail a crossing.
  5. Gather logs where you find them and use them to construct rafts to cross underground rivers.
  6. Fight or avoid the dangerous creatures and traps scattered throughout the pass, including deadly guardians.
  7. Solve environmental puzzles blocking your progress, such as levers, doors, and hidden mechanisms.
  8. Push through a particularly grueling maze-like section requiring careful navigation to avoid dead ends.
  9. Reach a chamber guarded by one of Iban's powerful servants and prepare for a difficult confrontation.
  10. Defeat the guardian blocking the final stretch of the pass through skill and persistence.
  11. Continue onward until you reach the far end of the Underground Pass, close to the elven lands.
  12. Emerge from the pass and report your success back toward Eluned's camp to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points
  • 3,000 Agility experience
  • 2,000 Ranged experience
  • 3,000 Attack experience
  • Access to the borders of the elven lands, setting up later quests
