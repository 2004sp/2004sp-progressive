The Knight's Sword

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

The Knight's Sword
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to the Squire in Falador Castle's courtyard.

A clumsy squire has broken one of Sir Vyvin's prized ceremonial swords and is desperate to have it replaced before he's found out. The trouble is, no ordinary smith can work with the rare metal needed to make it right. You'll need to track down a reclusive dwarven craftsman and the unusual ore his forge demands.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Recommended

  • A pickaxe, for mining the blurite ore

Walkthrough

  1. Head into Falador Castle and find the Squire fretting in the courtyard.
  2. Learn that he's accidentally broken Sir Vyvin's prized ceremonial sword and is desperate to have it replaced before anyone notices.
  3. Agree to help him track down someone capable of forging a replacement.
  4. Travel to Ice Mountain and find Thurgo, a skilled dwarven smith living there alone.
  5. Ask Thurgo about forging a new sword; he explains he'd need a rare metal called blurite to do the job properly.
  6. Make your way to the mine where blurite can be found, dealing with whatever obstacles or hazards stand in your way underground.
  7. Mine a piece of blurite ore from the special rock deposit.
  8. Return to Thurgo with the ore in hand.
  9. Wait as he smiths the ore into a fine new sword using his furnace.
  10. Take the finished sword back to the Squire in Falador.
  11. Hand it over to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
