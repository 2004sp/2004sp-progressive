Tribal Totem

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Tribal Totem
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to a member of the jungle tribe near the Kharazi Jungle in southern Karamja.

A sacred totem belonging to Karamja's jungle tribe has gone missing, stolen by an opportunistic trader who saw more profit in it than the tribe's blessing. The tribe needs someone with a talent for sneaking around and a disregard for locked doors to track down the thief and reclaim what was taken.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • This quest is short and mostly requires exploration rather than items

Recommended

  • Nothing beyond a willingness to search thoroughly

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to a member of the jungle tribe and learn about the theft of their sacred totem.
  2. Ask around for information on who might have taken it.
  3. Track the trail to a trader's warehouse or storeroom where stolen goods are kept.
  4. Sneak inside, avoiding or dealing with anyone who might catch you.
  5. Search the crates and containers for the totem, since it's likely hidden among other loot.
  6. Retrieve the totem once you find it.
  7. Make your way back out without getting caught, if possible.
  8. Return the totem to the jungle tribe.
  9. Speak to the tribe member again to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Minor experience reward
  • Improved standing with the jungle tribe
