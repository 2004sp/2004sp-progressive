Start point
Speak to Gypsy Aris in her tent in the swamp south-east of Lumbridge.

Gypsy Aris claims to know of a hidden realm called Zanaris, home to the reclusive fairy folk, tucked away behind a strange hollow tree in the Lumbridge swamp. Few outsiders have ever set foot there, and fewer still have earned the fairies' trust. If you can prove yourself worthy and find a way through the tree, an entire lost city awaits.

Requirements

  • Level 42 Woodcutting

Items needed

  • A knife
  • Coins to pay Gypsy Aris for information

Recommended

  • An axe for cutting the branch
  • Combat gear in case of hostile creatures in Zanaris

Walkthrough

  1. Find Gypsy Aris in her tent in the swamp south-east of Lumbridge and pay her for information.
  2. Listen to her tale of Zanaris and the hollow tree, and let her use her crystal ball to give you a brief glimpse of the fairy realm.
  3. Travel to the hollow tree she describes, deep in the swamp.
  4. Attempt to enter the tree and learn from Aris that you'll need a Dramen staff of your own to come and go freely.
  5. Locate the Dramen tree and use a knife to cut a branch from it, which requires a reasonable Woodcutting level.
  6. Have the branch fashioned, or use it directly, to create a Dramen staff.
  7. Use the Dramen staff on the hollow tree to open the way into Zanaris.
  8. Explore Zanaris, speaking to the fairy folk you encounter about their history and their guarded relationship with the outside world.
  9. Visit the marketplace stalls and the altar area, taking note of the unique services only available in this hidden city.
  10. Deal with any hostile creatures that challenge you as you explore the more dangerous corners of Zanaris.
  11. Confirm with Aris or the fairies that you've proven yourself, completing the quest.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • Permanent access to Zanaris via the Dramen staff
  • Ability to craft Dragon daggers, Dragon longswords and Dragon medium helmets at the Zanaris furnace, with sufficient Smithing level
  • Woodcutting and Crafting experience
