The Imperial Guard's attack on Death Plateau has failed. Trolls from the stronghold to the north captured Dunstan's son, Godric, and Denulth asks you to cross Trollheim, enter the stronghold and bring him home.

Requirements

Required quest and level

  • Completion of Death Plateau.
  • 15 Agility — required to climb the rocks on the route to the troll arena in build 254. The surviving 31 August 2004 community guide omitted this from its requirement box despite using the same climbing-rock route; the project journal and obstacle scripts enforce it directly.

Required items

  • Climbing boots — they must be worn for the relevant rock obstacle while travelling toward Trollheim. After Death Plateau, Tenzing sells replacement climbing boots for 12 coins.
  • Combat equipment capable of defeating Dad and one Troll General. There is no fixed player Combat-level requirement.

Recommended

  • Food and strong melee-defence equipment.
  • 43 Prayer for Protect from Melee and Protect from Missiles. A contemporary 2004 guide strongly recommends both because Dad and the Troll General hit hard, while Thrower Trolls attack from range.
  • Prayer potion(s) if you rely on protection prayers.
  • 30 Thieving — optional. It lets you pickpocket the sleeping prison guards for their cell keys instead of killing them.

Before you start

This quest is a rescue mission, not an assault on the stronghold's leadership. Your required targets are Dad, the arena champion who blocks the route, and one Troll General, whose key opens the prison. The actual objective is to free Godric and report his safe return to Dunstan.

Bring your climbing boots before leaving Burthorpe. If you need another pair, take 12 coins to Tenzing. There is no poison mechanic in the verified route, so antipoison is not a quest requirement or a period-specific recommendation.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the rescue

  1. Speak to Denulth in Burthorpe. After Death Plateau, ask how the fight against the trolls is going.
  2. Learn what happened to the raid. Denulth explains that the Imperial Guard used the route you found, but trolls from the north ambushed them and captured Godric, Dunstan's son.
  3. Offer to rescue Godric. Accept the mission. Denulth warns that the way to the stronghold is dangerous and that many trolls defend it.

2. Reach the troll arena

  1. Get climbing boots if necessary. Travel to Tenzing's hut on the Death Plateau route. After completing Death Plateau he sells climbing boots for 12 coins.
  2. Use the secret path behind Tenzing's house. Leave by the back, cross the stile and follow the mountain route you scouted during Death Plateau.
  3. Take the climbing-rock branch. When the route forks, climb across the rocks toward the arena. Build 254 requires 15 Agility; one of the outward obstacles also requires the climbing boots to be worn.
  4. Expect failed climbs. The rock obstacles use an Agility success roll in build 254. A failure can knock off a small amount of health, so do not start the mountain route at dangerously low Hitpoints.
  5. Enter the troll arena. Dad blocks the far exit and will not let a human pass without defeating him.

3. Defeat Dad

  1. Speak to Dad and accept his challenge. You can also attack him directly; either way, the arena fight begins.
  2. Fight until he gives up. You do not need to kill Dad. In build 254 he yields when reduced below 20 Hitpoints, heals back up and admits defeat.
  3. Watch for his heavy blow. Build 254 gives Dad a special attack that can knock the player across the arena. Protect from Melee and good food make this section much safer.
  4. Leave through the far arena gate. Once Dad has yielded, the exit opens and the quest advances.

4. Cross Trollheim and enter the stronghold

  1. Pass through the cave beyond the arena. The contemporary route records three level-71 Mountain Trolls here. There is no reason to fight them for quest progress.
  2. Follow the outside of the mountain spiral. Stay on the outer route rather than climbing toward the summit.
  3. Run past the Thrower Trolls. They attack with rocks from range. Protect from Missiles is the safest period-appropriate way to cross this section if you have the Prayer level.
  4. Enter the Mountain Camp area. Continue through the troll settlement and head north into the Troll Stronghold.

5. Get the prison key

  1. Work through the stronghold to the Troll Generals. From the entrance, follow the interior route south until you reach the wooden door leading into their area.
  2. Fight one Troll General. You only need to kill one. Build 254 displays the generals as level 113 and gives them extremely high melee damage potential, so Protect from Melee is strongly recommended.
  3. Pick up the Prison key. A Troll General killed while you still need access to the prison drops the quest key. Do not leave it on the floor.
  4. Return through the wooden door. Head north through the stronghold and go down the stone staircase toward the prison.
  5. Unlock the prison door. The Prison key is consumed the first time you open it in build 254. Continue downstairs into the cell area.

6. Free Godric

  1. Find Twig and Berry. The two troll prison guards sleep near the cells. In build 254 both display as level 71.
  2. Get Cell key 1 from Twig. This is the key to Godric's cell. With 30 Thieving, pickpocket the sleeping Twig. If you are below 30 Thieving or prefer combat, kill him and take the key from his drop.
  3. Unlock Godric's cell. Use Cell key 1 on his door. Freeing Godric is the required rescue and advances the main quest to its final stage.
  4. Optional but period-authentic: free Mad Eadgar. Pickpocket or kill Berry for Cell key 2, then unlock Eadgar's cell. The surviving August 2004 guide tells players to free both prisoners, but build 254 only requires Godric for quest completion.
  5. Use the secret exit. Leave the prison through the southern passage. The route emerges through the stronghold's hidden back entrance; after opening it from inside, build 254 remembers that access for later use.

7. Finish with Dunstan

  1. Return to Burthorpe. You do not finish Troll Stronghold with Denulth.
  2. Talk to Dunstan at the smithy. Ask whether Godric has returned home. Dunstan confirms that his son is safe and rewards you with a family heirloom: a Law talisman.
  3. The quest completes immediately. There is no skill-XP reward and no additional boss fight or general-killing objective.

Combat encounters

  • Dad: mandatory arena champion. Build 254 displays level 101 with 120 Hitpoints and makes him surrender below 20 Hitpoints. His special attack can knock you backward. The contemporary August 2004 guide confirms the surrender/knockback behaviour but does not provide a dependable exact displayed level.
  • Mountain Trolls: avoidable route enemies; the contemporary guide records level 71, matching build 254's ordinary Mountain Troll value.
  • Thrower Trolls: avoidable ranged enemies. Build 254 displays level 68; run through with Protect from Missiles rather than spending supplies fighting them.
  • Troll General: one kill is mandatory for the Prison key. Build 254 displays level 113. Use Protect from Melee or a safe ranged/magic position where available.
  • Twig: Cell key 1 holder. Build 254 displays level 71. Pickpocketing at 30 Thieving avoids the fight; failure wakes him and can start combat.
  • Berry: Cell key 2 holder for Eadgar's optional rescue. Build 254 displays level 71 and uses the same pickpocket-or-kill choice.

The 24 August 2004 release also corrected misleading displayed combat levels for a number of NPCs. Because build 254's troll configs explicitly use later preserved/OSRS values for several enemies, this guide labels those exact numbers as build-254 values unless the period evidence independently supports them.

Recovery notes

  • Lost climbing boots: Tenzing sells another pair for 12 coins after Death Plateau.
  • Lost Prison key: while the prison is still locked and you do not already have the key, another Troll General can drop one in build 254.
  • Lost Cell key 1: before Godric is freed, Twig can supply another key through a successful pickpocket or death drop if no copy is already held.
  • Lost Cell key 2: before Eadgar is freed, Berry works the same way for his cell key.
  • Pickpocket failure: a failed attempt wakes the guard and makes him hostile. Wait for the guard to settle back to sleep or use the combat route.
  • Stronghold shortcut: once you have used the hidden prison exit, build 254 allows the secret external entrance to be reopened later.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1 Law talisman.
  • No skill XP reward.
  • Reusable knowledge/access to the Troll Stronghold's secret back entrance after opening it from the prison side in build 254.
  • If you also rescued Mad Eadgar, he can be found at his home near Trollheim and exchanges logs for his mountain-goat stew. This benefit is recorded by the surviving August 2004 guide and implemented in build 254.

Trollheim and the stronghold are reached during the quest rather than being a simple completion-only reward. Later quests and areas that use Troll Stronghold completion are not listed as September-2004 rewards when they were released after the 7 September cutoff.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Core objective: build 254 tracks Denulth start → Dad defeated → prison entered → Godric freed → Dunstan completion. It does not require defeating multiple generals or killing the troll leadership.
  • Agility gate: the project hard-checks 15 Agility at the climbing rocks and requires climbing boots to be worn on the relevant outward obstacle. This is why 15 Agility is treated as a player-facing requirement despite the surviving 31 August 2004 guide omitting it from its requirement box.
  • Dad: build 254 uses level 101 and progresses the quest when he falls below 20 Hitpoints. He heals and surrenders instead of needing to die.
  • Prison key: any of the build's Troll General variants can supply the Prison key while it is still needed. The visible level is 113.
  • Cell keys: Twig and Berry can be pickpocketed at 30 Thieving or killed. Twig's Cell key 1 frees Godric; Berry's Cell key 2 frees Eadgar.
  • Eadgar: freeing Eadgar is supported by the contemporary route and unlocks the log-for-stew interaction, but the build's main quest state advances to the finish as soon as Godric is freed.
  • Completion: Dunstan, not Denulth, queues completion. The script gives exactly one Law talisman and 1 Quest Point, with no stat advancement.
  • Replacement Law talisman: build 254 can give a free replacement from Dunstan when none is held, but the source itself marks that behaviour as guessed from later material. It is therefore not presented above as established September-2004 history.
  • Enemy levels: several troll NPC configs explicitly state that their stats/visible levels match later OSRS data. They are authoritative for build 254 but are not automatically proof of the exact 24 August/7 September 2004 display.
  • Post-cutoff content: Eadgar's Ruse was released after this wiki's cutoff, so it is not presented as a target-period progression reward.
