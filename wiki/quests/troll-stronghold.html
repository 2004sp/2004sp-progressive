Start point
Speak to Denulth in Burthorpe after completing Death Plateau.

With the route up Death Plateau scouted, Denulth is ready to move against the troll stronghold itself. You are sent ahead of the soldiers to infiltrate the fortress, work your way past its troll defenders, and confront the two brutish generals commanding it before Burthorpe can safely open the road into Trollheim.

Requirements

  • Completion of Death Plateau

Items needed

  • A weapon and armour capable of fighting trolls

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Denulth in Burthorpe once more and learn of the plan to assault the troll stronghold.
  2. Make your way back up Death Plateau and locate the entrance to the stronghold itself.
  3. Slip inside and begin working your way through the fortress's winding passages.
  4. Deal with the trolls guarding the stronghold's lower levels as you press deeper in.
  5. Solve whatever obstacles bar your way further into the fortress, from locked doors to guarded chokepoints.
  6. Continue upward through the stronghold toward the sound of its leaders.
  7. Reach the throne room and come face to face with the troll generals, Bentnoze and Wartface.
  8. Fight or outmanoeuvre the two generals to break the stronghold's command.
  9. Secure the stronghold's interior now that its leadership has been dealt with.
  10. Return to Denulth in Burthorpe to report that the way north is finally open.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Access to Trollheim
  • Combat experience from the stronghold's defenders
  • Continuation of the Burthorpe troll storyline
