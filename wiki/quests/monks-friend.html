Monk's Friend

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Monk's Friend
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to a monk near Port Sarim who is worried about a delayed ale delivery.

The monks at the Monastery rely on regular deliveries of ale from a Port Sarim brewer to keep their spirits up, but the latest shipment has gone missing somewhere along the road. A worried monk asks for your help sorting out the mess with the brewer before the whole monastery runs dry.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Coins in case payment is needed to settle the delivery dispute

Recommended

  • Nothing beyond some spare coins

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to the monk near Port Sarim about the delayed ale delivery.
  2. Head to the brewer responsible for supplying the Monastery.
  3. Ask the brewer about the hold-up and learn the real reason the delivery never arrived.
  4. Resolve the dispute or misunderstanding between the brewer and the Monastery, whether that means paying an outstanding debt or clearing up a mistake.
  5. Arrange for the ale to finally be delivered to the monks.
  6. Escort or confirm the delivery reaches the Monastery.
  7. Return to the original monk to let them know the matter is settled.
  8. Speak to him once more to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Free Prayer restoration from the monks at the Monastery
  • Coins
