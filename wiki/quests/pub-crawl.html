Start point
Speak to the bartender at the Blue Moon Inn in Varrock to pick up a Pub Crawl scroll.

Rather than saving the world, this bit of bonus fun is about seeing it one tankard at a time. Pick up a Pub Crawl scroll from a friendly bartender and set off on a good-natured tour of RuneScape's most famous watering holes, sampling a drink and a chat with each barkeep along the way. There is no grand villain here, just a checklist, a thirst, and a lot of walking between pubs.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Coins to buy a drink at each pub along the way

Recommended

  • A means of getting around quickly
  • Patience

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to a bartender at the Blue Moon Inn in Varrock and ask for a Pub Crawl scroll.
  2. Read the scroll to see the full list of pubs you are expected to visit.
  3. Order a drink from the Blue Moon Inn's bartender to mark the first stop off your list.
  4. Head to the Jolly Boar Inn north of Varrock and order a drink there as well.
  5. Make your way to the Rising Sun tavern in Falador and do the same.
  6. Continue on to the Rusty Anchor in Port Sarim to keep the crawl going.
  7. Cross into Karamja and visit the pub there for another stamp on your scroll.
  8. Travel to Ardougne and visit its local pub to keep working through the list.
  9. If your scroll calls for it, head further afield to the Forester's Arms in Seers' Village.
  10. Check your scroll periodically to see which stops remain and plan your route efficiently.
  11. Visit every remaining pub on the list, ordering a drink and chatting with each bartender in turn.
  12. Watch out for the odd bit of stumbling dialogue as the crawl wears on; it is all in good fun.
  13. Return to the scroll's starting bartender once every pub has been visited.
  14. Show the completed scroll to confirm you have finished the full crawl.

Rewards

  • No quest points
  • The satisfaction of a completed scroll and a tour of RuneScape's pubs
  • A bit of light-hearted bartender dialogue unlocked along the way
