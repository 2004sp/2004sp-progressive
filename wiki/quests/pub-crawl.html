The guards at the Barbarian Outpost will not simply take your word that you are a barbarian. To prove that you can drink like one, take their official Barcrawl card around RuneScape and have ten bartenders serve their strongest house drinks and sign the card. The pubs may be visited in any order; once all ten are signed, return to the outpost guard.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None. The Bar Crawl itself has no quest, skill or combat-level requirement.

Required items

  • 208 coins for the ten required Bar Crawl drinks in build 254. This is the drink total only; keep extra cash if your route uses paid transport.
  • Barcrawl card — the Barbarian guard gives this to you when the miniquest starts. Keep it in your inventory while visiting the pubs.

Recommended

  • Food. Several drinks damage your Hitpoints, and the Heart Stopper is the harshest in build 254.
  • A restore potion or time to recover. Many of the drinks temporarily drain combat or gathering skills.
  • Teleport runes or other September-2004 travel methods you already have. The ten stops span Misthalin, Asgarnia, Kandarin and Karamja.

Starting the Bar Crawl

  1. Travel to the Barbarian Outpost, north-west of the Tree Gnome Stronghold.
  2. Try to enter through the outpost gate and speak to the Barbarian guard.
  3. Say that you want to come through the gate, then claim that appearances are deceiving and that you really are a barbarian.
  4. The guard challenges you to complete Alfred Grimhand's Bar Crawl and gives you a Barcrawl card.
  5. Read the card whenever you need to check your progress. It lists all ten pubs and marks each one complete after its bartender signs.

The ten pubs

You can complete these in any order. Talk to the named bartender while carrying the Barcrawl card and choose the Alfred Grimhand's Barcrawl option. Using the card on the bartender also triggers the Bar Crawl dialogue at the supported stops in build 254.

  1. Blue Moon Inn — Varrock: buy Uncle Humphrey's Gutrot for 50 coins. Build 254 drains Attack, Defence, Strength and Smithing, then deals about 3% of your current Hitpoints.
  2. Blurberry's Bar — Grand Tree: speak to Blurberry, not the ordinary barman. Buy a Fire Toad Blast for 10 coins. Build 254 deals about 5% of your current Hitpoints.
  3. Dead Man's Chest — Brimhaven: buy Supergrog for 15 coins. Build 254 drains Attack, Defence, Prayer, Cooking and Herblore.
  4. Dragon Inn — Yanille: buy Fire Brandy for 12 coins. Build 254 drains Attack and Defence.
  5. Flying Horse Inn — East Ardougne: buy a Heart Stopper for 8 coins. Build 254 deals about 25% of your current Hitpoints.
  6. Forester's Arms — Seers' Village: buy Liverbane Ale for 18 coins. Build 254 drains Attack, Defence, Woodcutting, Fletching and Firemaking.
  7. Jolly Boar Inn — north-east of Varrock: buy Olde Suspiciouse for 10 coins. Build 254 drains Attack, Defence, Magic, Crafting and Mining.
  8. Karamja Spirits Bar — Musa Point: speak to Zambo and buy Ape Bite Liqueur for 7 coins. Build 254 applies no explicit stat drain or Hitpoints damage here.
  9. Rising Sun Inn — Falador: buy the Hand of Death cocktail for 70 coins. Build 254 drains Attack, Defence, Ranged and Fishing, shakes the camera, and deals about 5% of your current Hitpoints.
  10. Rusty Anchor Inn — Port Sarim: buy a Black Skull Ale for 8 coins. Build 254 blurs the sequence through dialogue/animation but applies no explicit stat drain or Hitpoints damage.

Total drink cost: 208 coins. Each successful stop sets one of the ten pub flags on the Barcrawl card. Ordinary beer purchases do not count: you must choose the Bar Crawl dialogue and drink the special house drink.

Finishing the miniquest

  1. Once all ten pubs have signed the card, return to the Barbarian guard at the Barbarian Outpost.
  2. Speak to the guard while carrying the completed card. Your character admits being too drunk to count the stops properly.
  3. The guard accepts the card, removes Barcrawl cards from your inventory and bank, and records the Bar Crawl as complete.
  4. In the intended September-2004 game state, this proves you are a barbarian and grants permanent access through the outpost gate.

Rewards

  • Permanent access to the Barbarian Outpost in the intended period game.
  • Access to the Barbarian Agility Course once you also have the required 35 Agility.
  • Progress needed for Scorpion Catcher, whose second scorpion is associated with the barbarian area in the period quest chain.
  • 0 Quest Points.
  • No direct XP, coin or item reward.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Keep the card with you. Build-254 bartenders only expose the Bar Crawl branch when the card is in your inventory and that pub is still unsigned.
  • A banked card is safe. If the card is in your bank, the guard tells you to retrieve it and your pub progress remains intact.
  • A genuinely lost card resets the crawl in build 254. If no Barcrawl card exists in either inventory or bank, the guard gives you another but resets the Bar Crawl state, so the ten pubs must be visited again.
  • You do not need to read the finished card. Build 254 detects completion from the ten pub flags. If you try to read it after all ten are marked, the script merely says you are too drunk to read it.
  • The 208-coin figure excludes travel. A contemporary RuneHQ guide entered on 7 May 2004 also describes the same ten pubs, while its later-edited total includes travel fares. The drink prices themselves sum to 208 in build 254.

2004scape implementation notes

  • State model: build 254 stores the miniquest in the permanent barcrawl varp. The ten pub-completion bits are indices 3 through 12; completion requires all ten.
  • Exact card list: the build's scroll interface names BlueMoon Inn, Blurberry's Bar, Dead Man's Chest, Dragon Inn, Flying Horse Inn, Foresters Arms, Jolly Boar Inn, Karamja Spirits Bar, Rising Sun Inn and Rusty Anchor Inn.
  • Start: the outpost guard, not a Varrock bartender, creates the Barcrawl card and starts the state.
  • Completion: returning to the guard after all ten pub bits are set changes the state to complete and deletes Barcrawl cards from inventory and bank. No XP, coins, items or quest points are awarded.
  • 35 Agility: the Barbarian course obstacle-pipe script explicitly checks for level 35 when entering the course side that requires it.
  • Gate divergence: build 254's outpost-gate handler checks Bar Crawl completion before calling the guard, but the gate-opening and movement code follows the dialogue without a second completion check or an explicit return. As written, physical gate enforcement therefore appears more permissive than the intended September-2004 unlock. Complete the Bar Crawl anyway to set the permanent state correctly.
  • Rusty Anchor edge case: the bartender says Black Skull Ale costs 8 coins and the script removes 8, but its affordability test checks for fewer than 7. Bring the full 8 coins rather than relying on that build-254 mismatch.
  • Cutoff note: later guides often mention Horror from the Deep as another Bar Crawl dependency, but that quest was released after the 7 September 2004 audit cutoff and is not treated as a period reward here.
