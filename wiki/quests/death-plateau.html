Trolls have occupied Death Plateau and are using it to raid Burthorpe. Denulth asks you to find an unguarded route that the Imperial Guard can use for a surprise attack, while also recovering the lost combination to Burthorpe Castle's equipment room.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None.
  • There is no skill-level, combat-level or quest-point requirement. Jagex's release announcement specifically described the quest as completable at any level, although the troll-held main road is dangerous for low-level players.

Required items

  • 10 loaves of bread — consumed by Tenzing.
  • 10 cooked trout — consumed by Tenzing.
  • 1 iron bar — consumed by Dunstan when he adds spikes to Tenzing's climbing boots.
  • 1 Asgarnian ale — consumed when you buy Harold a drink. Tostig's Toad and Chicken pub in Burthorpe sells Asgarnian ale, so it can be obtained during the quest.
  • Coins for Harold's gambling game — there is no fixed amount that must be consumed because you can win or lose individual bets. Bring enough money to keep gambling until Harold cannot pay your winnings; build 254 allows bets up to 1,000 coins.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Climbing boots, lent to you by Tenzing.
  • Certificate, issued by Denulth to prove that Dunstan's son has been accepted into the Imperial Guard.
  • Spiked boots, made by Dunstan from Tenzing's climbing boots and your iron bar.
  • IOU, given by Harold when he cannot cover his gambling debt.
  • Combination, discovered by reading the IOU.
  • Secret way map, given by Tenzing after you deliver his supplies.

Recommended

  • Blurberry Special — optional. Harold has a weakness for it, and giving him one makes the gambling section substantially easier. It is not a hard quest requirement.
  • Several free inventory spaces for the coloured stone balls and temporary quest items.
  • Food only if you intend to explore the troll-held main route. No enemy needs to be killed to complete Death Plateau.

Before you start

The quest has two objectives that can be progressed independently: unlock the Burthorpe Castle equipment room and find and scout a secret route up Death Plateau. Denulth only completes the quest after both are finished and you return with the combination and Tenzing's map.

Avoid confusing the secret route with the obvious road toward the troll camp. The main approach is watched by rock-throwing trolls; the path behind Tenzing's house is the route you are trying to prove safe.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the quest

  1. Speak to Denulth. Find him at the Imperial Guard training camp in Burthorpe and ask whether he has a quest for you.
  2. Agree to find another route. Denulth explains that trolls occupy Death Plateau and the normal path is heavily guarded. Offer to find a way the Imperial Guard can approach unseen.
  3. Take the second task. Denulth also explains that the guard who had the equipment-room combination lost it. You must recover the combination and unlock the equipment-room door in Burthorpe Castle.

2. Recover the equipment-room combination

  1. Talk to Eohric in Burthorpe Castle. Ask the head servant about the guard who was on duty the previous night. He identifies the guard as Harold and says that Harold is staying at the local inn, the Toad and Chicken.
  2. Find Harold upstairs at the Toad and Chicken. Talk to him about being the guard who was on duty. He refuses to discuss the lost combination.
  3. Return to Eohric. Tell Eohric that Harold will not talk. Eohric reveals Harold's weaknesses: drinking and gambling, and suggests buying him a beer.
  4. Give Harold an Asgarnian ale. Return to Harold with one Asgarnian ale. If you did not bring one, buy one from Tostig downstairs. After the drink, Harold becomes willing to gamble.
  5. Optional: give Harold a Blurberry Special. If you brought one, ask whether you can buy him another drink and give it to him. This is not required, but it makes him much easier to beat at dice.
  6. Gamble until Harold cannot pay. Challenge Harold to his dice game and place bets. The objective is not to reach a particular number of wins: keep playing until he loses enough that he cannot cover your winnings. He then gives you an IOU for the unpaid amount. Build 254 starts Harold with 100 coins and caps each wager at 1,000 coins, so the exact amount of cash you personally spend is variable.
  7. Read the IOU. Use its Read option. You discover that Harold wrote the IOU on the back of the missing equipment-room Combination, and the item becomes the combination sheet.
  8. Read the combination clues. The sheet gives five positional clues for the red, blue, yellow, purple and green stone balls. You do not need to solve them yourself; the correct layout is listed in the next step.
  9. Go to the equipment-room stone mechanism. At Burthorpe Castle, collect the five coloured stone balls around the mechanism and place them in this arrangement, with north at the top:
    • Green — top-right position.
    • Red — middle-left position.
    • Purple — middle-right position.
    • Blue — bottom-left position.
    • Yellow — bottom-right position.
    In other words, the left column is Blue below Red; the right column is Yellow below Purple below Green.
  10. Unlock the equipment room. When all five balls are in the correct positions, the equipment-room door unlocks. Keep the Combination; Denulth needs it at the end of the quest. The trapped archer inside is not required for progression, but speaking to him confirms that the door has been opened.

3. Find Tenzing and prepare the secret route

  1. Speak to Saba. Head toward the mountain west/north-west of Burthorpe and enter Saba's cave. Ask him whether he knows another way up Death Plateau. He complains about both humans and trolls, then mentions a nearby Sherpa who may know the mountains better.
  2. Find Tenzing's house. Continue through the rocky area toward the small house below Death Plateau. Do not take the obvious troll-held road farther up the mountain. Knock on Tenzing's front door and explain that Saba sent you.
  3. Agree to help Tenzing. Tenzing knows a hidden route, but asks for supplies before showing it to you: 10 bread, 10 cooked trout, and new spikes on his climbing boots. When you agree, he gives you the climbing boots.
  4. Take the boots to Dunstan. Return to Burthorpe and speak to Dunstan, the smith. Tell him Tenzing needs the climbing boots respiked. Dunstan agrees only if his 16-year-old son is accepted into the Imperial Guard and tells you that he will also need an iron bar for the spikes.
  5. Get Denulth's certificate. Speak to Denulth and explain Dunstan's condition. Denulth makes an exception and gives you a Certificate confirming that Dunstan's son has been accepted for Imperial Guard training.
  6. Have Dunstan make the spiked boots. Return to Dunstan with the certificate, Tenzing's climbing boots and one iron bar. Give him the certificate, then the boots and iron bar. He turns them into Spiked boots. No Smithing level is required from you.
  7. Deliver Tenzing's supplies. Return to Tenzing carrying the spiked boots, 10 loaves of bread and 10 cooked trout. He takes all three requirements and gives you a Secret way map.
  8. Scout the path yourself. Leave through the back door of Tenzing's house, climb over the stile and follow the path behind his property. Continue along the route until the quest reports that you can see Death Plateau, there are no trolls on the secret path, and you should return to Denulth. That message marks the route as successfully scouted.

4. Finish the quest

  1. Bring both pieces of proof to Denulth. Make sure the equipment-room door is unlocked and that you have the Combination. Also make sure you have the Secret way map and have reached the scouting point behind Tenzing's house.
  2. Talk to Denulth. Give him the map of the secret route and the equipment-room combination. If you have completed both branches, he congratulates you and finishes the quest.

Combat and recovery notes

  • No combat is required. The quest can be completed without killing a troll, guard or any other enemy.
  • Main-path danger: Death Plateau's obvious approach is guarded by hostile trolls, including rock-throwing trolls. Jagex's 9 August 2004 release notice explicitly warned lower-combat players to be cautious. The hidden path behind Tenzing's house is the route you need to scout.
  • Lost climbing boots: during the relevant quest stage, Tenzing can replace the pair he lent you if you have neither his climbing boots nor the spiked boots.
  • Lost certificate: Denulth can give you another certificate while you are on the Dunstan stage.
  • Lost IOU or combination: Harold can replace the paperwork after he has run out of money. If you still have the IOU, remember to read it so it becomes the combination.
  • Lost secret way map: after Tenzing has first drawn the map, he can draw another if you lose it.
  • Stone balls: the equipment-room door only unlocks after the combination has been discovered and all five balls are on the correct positions.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 3,000 Attack XP.
  • 1 pair of steel claws.
  • The ability to smith claw weapons at the normal Smithing levels. In build 254 the claw interface remains hidden until Death Plateau is complete; the claw levels are 13 bronze, 28 iron, 43 steel, 63 mithril, 83 adamant and 98 rune.
  • Honorary membership of the Imperial Guard, as stated by Denulth on completion.
  • Access to replacement climbing boots from Tenzing for 12 coins after the quest.
  • Progression to Troll Stronghold, released on 24 August 2004 and still inside the 7 September 2004 target cutoff.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Core quest route: build 254 closely matches the preserved 2004 quest: Denulth's two objectives, Eohric and Harold, the IOU/combination puzzle, Saba and Tenzing, Dunstan's certificate bargain, the secret-path scouting trigger, and the final hand-in are all implemented.
  • Harold's gambling: build 254 initializes Harold with 100 coins and allows wagers from 1 to 1,000 coins. Because results can vary, the guide does not present a modern guide's suggested cash amount as a hard historical requirement.
  • Stone-ball solution: build 254 checks for Blue at bottom-left, Yellow at bottom-right, Red at middle-left, Purple at middle-right and Green at top-right. This directly satisfies the five clues on the combination sheet.
  • Quest reward: the completion script grants exactly 3,000 Attack XP, adds steel claws, and awards 1 Quest Point.
  • Claw smithing: build 254 hides the claw-smithing column before quest completion and exposes it afterwards, making the claw recipe unlock an actual project requirement rather than merely flavour text.
  • Climbing boots: after completion, build 254 lets Tenzing sell climbing boots for 12 coins. The separate spiked boots used during the quest are handed back to Tenzing and are not the permanent reward.
