Death Plateau

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Death Plateau
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Denulth, the guard captain in Burthorpe.

Burthorpe's soldiers want to push north into troll territory, but the treacherous, troll-infested slopes of Death Plateau stand in their way. Captain Denulth needs a scout brave enough to find missing climbing equipment and fight through the mountain's guardians to prove the route can be taken at all.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A weapon and armour capable of fighting trolls

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Denulth in Burthorpe and hear his concerns about the trolls blocking the mountain pass.
  2. Agree to scout a path up Death Plateau on the soldiers' behalf.
  3. Track down a cowardly local who was supposed to deliver climbing equipment but never did.
  4. Persuade or search him to recover the missing climbing gear.
  5. Begin the ascent up the lower slopes of Death Plateau.
  6. Fight through the low-level trolls patrolling the mountainside.
  7. Use the recovered climbing equipment to scale the more difficult sections of the route.
  8. Push on toward the plateau despite the mountain's hostile inhabitants.
  9. Reach a vantage point overlooking the troll stronghold beyond.
  10. Return to Denulth with your report on the route and the stronghold's defences.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Sets up the Troll Stronghold questline
  • Combat experience from the trolls fought along the way
