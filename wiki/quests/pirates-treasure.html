Redbeard Frank knows where his late captain One-Eyed Hector hid some treasure, but he wants a bottle of Karamja rum before he will hand over the clue. The problem is that the customs officers will confiscate rum carried openly off Karamja, so the bottle has to leave the island inside one of Luthas's banana shipments.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • No skill-level requirement.
  • No fixed player Combat-level requirement. A gardener objects when you dig up the final flower patch, so be prepared to fight him or get clear of him and try again.

Required items

  • 60 coins available before the first Karamja trip. The outward fare costs 30 coins and Karamja rum costs 30 coins. Luthas pays 30 coins for the completed banana crate, which can cover the return fare.
  • 1 white apron. Wydin will hire you if you have one carried or worn, but in build 254 it must be worn to enter the employees-only back room. The contemporary March-2004 guide directs players to obtain one from the Varrock clothes shop if they do not already have one.
  • 1 spade. Bring one for the final dig. The contemporary guide notes that one can be found in a Falador house near the furnace.

Obtained during the quest

  • 1 Karamja rum from the Karamja wines and spirits shop for 30 coins.
  • 10 bananas from Luthas's plantation. The export crate holds exactly ten.
  • Chest key from Redbeard Frank after giving him the smuggled rum.
  • Pirate message from the locked chest upstairs in the Blue Moon Inn, Varrock.

Recommended

  • Have the white apron and spade before sailing. This avoids extra trips through Varrock/Falador during the middle of the quest.
  • Keep at least one inventory space free when retrieving the rum and opening the Blue Moon Inn chest.
  • Do not try to carry Karamja rum through customs. The customs officer confiscates it in build 254, and the period route exists specifically to smuggle it in the banana shipment.

Before you start

Prepare a white apron, spade and at least 60 coins. The period route is most efficient if you arrive in Port Sarim already wearing or carrying the apron. The quest is free-to-play and has no prerequisite quest or skill requirement.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Make a deal with Redbeard Frank

  1. Find Redbeard Frank in Port Sarim, just south of The Rusty Anchor.
  2. Speak to him and choose "I'm in search of treasure."
  3. Frank offers to tell you where One-Eyed Hector's treasure is hidden if you bring him Karamja rum.

2. Sail to Karamja and buy the rum

  1. Go south along the Port Sarim docks and speak to one of the sailors offering passage to Karamja.
  2. Pay the 30-coin fare and sail to Musa Point.
  3. Go to the nearby wines and spirits shop and buy 1 Karamja rum for 30 coins.
  4. Do not try to leave Karamja with the bottle in your inventory. Customs will search you and confiscate the rum.

3. Take Luthas's banana-plantation job

  1. Go to Luthas in the house by the banana plantation, north-east of the rum shop.
  2. Ask him for employment. He offers 30 coins if you fill his export crate with bananas.
  3. The crate outside is shipped to Wydin's grocery store in Port Sarim, and Luthas explains that customs no longer searches his banana exports.

4. Hide the rum and fill the crate

  1. Pick bananas from the plantation.
  2. Use the Karamja rum on Luthas's export crate. The bottle is hidden inside.
  3. Put bananas into the same crate until it contains 10 bananas. If you put some bananas in before the rum, that is fine; the important point is that the rum is inside and the crate reaches ten bananas.
  4. Return to Luthas. He pays you 30 coins and ships the crate to Wydin's store.

5. Return to Port Sarim

  1. Go back to the Karamja dock and speak to the customs officer.
  2. With the rum safely inside Luthas's shipment rather than in your inventory, pass the search and pay the 30-coin boarding charge.
  3. Sail back to Port Sarim.

6. Get hired by Wydin and recover the rum

  1. Go to Wydin's Food Store in Port Sarim.
  2. Make sure your white apron is worn in the torso slot, then ask Wydin for a job.
  3. Wydin hires you and permits you into the employees-only back room.
  4. Search the banana crate in the back room. You find the Karamja rum hidden among the bananas.

7. Trade the rum for Hector's key

  1. Return to Redbeard Frank with the recovered rum.
  2. Frank takes the bottle and gives you One-Eyed Hector's chest key.
  3. He tells you that the key opens Hector's old chest in an upstairs room of the Blue Moon Inn in Varrock.

8. Open the Blue Moon Inn chest

  1. Travel to Varrock and enter the Blue Moon Inn.
  2. Go upstairs and find the locked chest in the western room.
  3. Use the chest key on the chest. The key is consumed and you receive a pirate message.
  4. Read the message. It sends you to the city of the White Knights and tells you to look in the park where Saradomin points to the X.

9. Dig up the treasure in Falador Park

  1. Travel to Falador Park.
  2. Find the Saradomin statue and the flower patch west of it.
  3. Use your spade on the marked flowers.
  4. A nearby gardener becomes hostile. Deal with him, move clear if necessary, and dig at the flowers again.
  5. The successful dig immediately completes the quest and gives the treasure. You do not return to Redbeard Frank.

Combat and hazards

  • Customs confiscation: carrying Karamja rum directly through the Karamja customs check destroys the bottle in build 254. Smuggle it in the banana crate instead.
  • Falador Park gardener: digging in the final flower patch angers a nearby gardener. This is the only quest-specific hostile encounter; there is no skill-level combat requirement to start the quest.
  • No death-sensitive quest boss or dungeon route is involved.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the smuggled rum: build 254's quest journal and crate state allow the smuggling process to be repeated while the quest still needs rum.
  • Lost Hector's chest key: speak to Redbeard Frank while still on the chest-key stage and he gives you a replacement if you no longer have one.
  • Wydin will not hire you: have a white apron in your inventory or worn, then ask for the job. To enter the back room while Wydin can see you, the apron must actually be worn.
  • Cannot dig up the treasure: make sure you have read the pirate message. Build 254 only permits the completion dig after the note advances the quest state.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 450 coins.
  • 1 cut emerald.
  • 1 gold ring.

No skill XP reward is granted. The 30 coins paid by Luthas during the smuggling job are wages for filling the banana crate, separate from the final 450-coin treasure reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 tracks the rum request, smuggling/employment state, Hector's key, reading the pirate message, and the final Falador Park dig. The global completion state is 4.
  • Banana crate: Luthas's crate stores up to exactly 10 bananas. Karamja rum can be stashed separately; when the full crate is turned in, the rum shipment is moved to Wydin's store and Luthas pays 30 coins.
  • Customs: the Karamja customs officer explicitly confiscates Karamja rum found in the player's inventory. The return fare is exactly 30 coins.
  • Wydin: the hiring dialogue accepts a white apron either carried or worn, but the back-room door checks the torso equipment slot and requires the apron to be worn.
  • Redbeard recovery: Frank replaces a lost chest key during the appropriate quest stage. Giving him the rum consumes one bottle and advances to the key stage.
  • Blue Moon chest: using the key on the locked chest consumes the key and gives the pirate message. Reading the message advances the quest to the final dig state.
  • Completion reward: the Falador Park dig script adds exactly 450 coins, 1 gold ring and 1 emerald; the global quest constant awards 2 Quest Points. No stat-advance call is made.
