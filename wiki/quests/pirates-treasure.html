Pirate's Treasure

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Pirate's Treasure
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to Redbeard Frank in the Rusty Anchor pub, Port Sarim.

Old Redbeard Frank spins tales of treasure buried long ago on the shores of Karamja, treasure he's never quite managed to reclaim. All he needs is the map, currently lost somewhere in the hands of a fellow pirate down at the pub. It's a short, light-hearted hunt with a punchline waiting at the end.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Some coins, to buy a drink for a fellow pirate
  • A spade, for digging up the treasure

Recommended

  • A few extra coins for the boat trip to Karamja

Walkthrough

  1. Head into the Rusty Anchor pub in Port Sarim and speak with Redbeard Frank.
  2. Listen to his stories of a treasure he once buried and the map he lost track of long ago.
  3. Learn that a fellow pirate drinking in the same pub might know where the map ended up.
  4. Buy a drink for the other pirate to loosen his tongue.
  5. Obtain the old, weathered treasure map from him once he's had enough to drink.
  6. Bring the map back to Frank so he can help you decipher its directions.
  7. Head down to the docks and take a ship across to Karamja.
  8. Follow the map's clues across the island until you find the marked digging spot.
  9. Use your spade to dig up the buried chest.
  10. Open the chest to discover the underwhelming 'treasure' inside.
  11. Return to Port Sarim, if you like, to share the news with Frank and complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A bronze medium helmet
  • Any coins found in the buried chest
