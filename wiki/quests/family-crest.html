Family Crest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Family Crest
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Medium
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Dimintheis, an old man who can be found wandering near Al Kharid or Rimmington.

Dimintheis was once the head of a proud and wealthy family, but hard times scattered his four children across the land and shattered the ornate crest that once symbolised their status. He longs to see his family's honour restored, and he'll need you to track down each of his children, help them with their troubles, and reforge the broken crest from precious metals.

Requirements

  • Level 40 Mining
  • Level 40 Smithing

Items needed

  • Gold, silver and mithril bars for the crest pieces
  • Coins for travel and favours

Recommended

  • A pickaxe for mining additional ore
  • Combat gear for any trouble encountered while helping the children

Walkthrough

  1. Find Dimintheis and hear the sad story of his broken family and shattered crest.
  2. Agree to track down his four children, each living in a different corner of the kingdom.
  3. Visit the first child and help resolve whatever personal trouble is keeping them from their family.
  4. Visit the second child and do the same, learning more about why the family fell apart.
  5. Continue on to the third child, offering whatever assistance is needed to earn their cooperation.
  6. Track down the fourth and final child and help them in turn.
  7. With all four children willing to cooperate, learn what's needed to forge the replacement crest pieces.
  8. Mine or otherwise obtain the gold, silver and mithril required for the crest.
  9. Smith the metal into the crest pieces at an appropriate anvil and furnace.
  10. Have the pieces enchanted or fitted together as Dimintheis instructs.
  11. Return the completed family crest to Dimintheis.
  12. Speak to Dimintheis once more to complete the quest and unlock his services.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Mining and Smithing experience
  • Dimintheis will decorate items with gold, silver and mithril for a fee
  • Coins
