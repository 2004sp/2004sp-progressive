Doric's Quest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Doric's Quest
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to Doric at his anvils in the hills north of Falador.

Doric the dwarf keeps himself busy at his anvils in the hills north of Falador, but his stockpile of raw materials has run dry. It's a small, humble favour as far as quests go, requiring nothing more than a trip to gather some ore and clay. Doric isn't one for grand rewards, but he remembers those who help him out.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • 6 pieces of clay
  • 4 copper ore
  • 2 iron ore

Recommended

  • A pickaxe, to mine the ores yourself rather than buying them

Walkthrough

  1. Head north from Falador into the hills until you find Doric the dwarf working at his anvils.
  2. Speak with him and learn that he's running low on the raw materials he needs for his smithing projects.
  3. Agree to gather the ore he's after.
  4. Mine or otherwise acquire six pieces of clay.
  5. Mine or otherwise acquire four pieces of copper ore.
  6. Mine or otherwise acquire two pieces of iron ore.
  7. Make your way back to Doric once you have everything on his list.
  8. Hand over the clay, copper ore, and iron ore.
  9. Receive his thanks and your reward for the errand.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
  • Free use of Doric's anvils
