Start point
Speak to Achietties, the historian, in Burthorpe.

The Heroes' Guild only opens its doors to adventurers who have already proven themselves many times over. Achietties the historian is compiling evidence of your past deeds, but induction demands more than reputation: you must gather rare ingredients from across the realm for a legendary stew, calling on skills and connections earned through your earlier adventures.

Requirements

  • 55 Quest Points
  • Level 53 Fishing
  • Level 20 Cooking
  • Level 25 Herblore
  • Completion of Dragon Slayer
  • Completion of Druidic Ritual
  • Completion of Merlin's Crystal
  • Completion of Scorpion Catcher
  • Completion of Shield of Arrav or its Phoenix Gang equivalent

Items needed

  • Coins for travel and purchases
  • Access to a cooking range and Herblore ingredients

Recommended

  • Teleport runes or tablets to speed up the many fetch-quests
  • Food and combat gear

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Burthorpe and speak with Achietties about joining the Heroes' Guild.
  2. Learn that you must prove yourself worthy by preparing a legendary stew using rare ingredients from around the world.
  3. Track down each of the required ingredients in turn, travelling to the far corners of the map as directed.
  4. Use your Fishing skill to catch the specific rare fish needed for the recipe.
  5. Deal with any guardians, puzzles or dangerous creatures protecting the more obscure ingredients.
  6. Return each ingredient to the appropriate location or NPC as instructed along the way.
  7. Once all the components have been gathered, prepare the stew using your Cooking and Herblore knowledge.
  8. Present the finished stew as proof of your resourcefulness and dedication.
  9. Speak to the relevant authority figures to confirm your trials are complete.
  10. Return to Achietties in Burthorpe to be formally recognised as a Hero.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Access to the Heroes' Guild in Burthorpe, including its bank and resources
  • A substantial lump of experience spread across several skills
