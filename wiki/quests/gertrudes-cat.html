Gertrude's cat, Fluffs, has disappeared. Her sons Shilop and Wilough know where the cat wandered off to, but they will not give up their secret play area's location for free. Finding Fluffs is only the beginning: she is thirsty, hungry, and finally too worried about a missing kitten to return home.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • No hard skill requirement. Jagex's December-2003 quest manual lists 5 Fishing as recommended, which is enough to catch a raw sardine yourself. The sardine can instead be obtained another way, so Fishing is not a quest gate.
  • No mandatory combat. You do not need to kill anything during the quest.

Required items

  • 100 coins. Shilop or Wilough takes exactly 100 coins before revealing the Lumber Yard location.
  • 1 bucket of milk. Fluffs drinks the milk; build 254 returns an empty bucket.
  • 1 raw sardine. It must be seasoned before Fluffs will eat it.
  • 1 doogle leaf. Use it on the raw sardine to make a seasoned sardine. Doogle leaves grow among the trees behind Gertrude's house, so this item can be collected while starting the quest.

Obtained during the quest

  • Seasoned sardine — made by combining one raw sardine with one doogle leaf; both ingredients are consumed.
  • Fluffs' kitten — found by searching the correct crate in the Lumber Yard after Fluffs has been given milk and the seasoned sardine.

Recommended

  • Keep more than 3 Hitpoints remaining before trying to pick up or stroke Fluffs. The contemporary March-2004 guide and build 254 both record a 3-damage scratch.
  • Leave a few inventory spaces free for the quest kitten and the food rewards at completion.
  • Bring all four required items before going to the Lumber Yard to avoid repeated trips between Varrock and the yard.

Before you start

Pick a doogle leaf from the trees behind Gertrude's house before leaving the area. Have the 100 coins, bucket of milk and raw sardine ready as well. You can season the sardine in advance by using the doogle leaf on it.

The quest does not require you to carry Fluffs home. Once her missing kitten is returned, Fluffs runs home on her own; your final task is simply to report back to Gertrude.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Gertrude

  1. Talk to Gertrude at her house just west of Varrock.
  2. Ask what is wrong and agree to search for Fluffs.
  3. Gertrude explains that her sons Shilop and Wilough saw the cat last and should be around Varrock's marketplace.

2. Pay Shilop or Wilough for the clue

  1. Go to Varrock marketplace and speak to either Shilop or Wilough.
  2. Ask where their secret play area is. The boys refuse to tell you without payment.
  3. Pay 100 coins. They tell you that Fluffs followed them to an abandoned lumber mill north-east of Varrock, beyond the Jolly Boar Inn.
  4. They also warn that you must find a broken section of fence to get inside.

3. Find Fluffs in the Lumber Yard

  1. Travel north-east from Varrock past the Jolly Boar Inn to the Lumber Yard.
  2. Find the broken fence on the south side and climb over it. There is no Agility-level check in build 254.
  3. Climb the ladder inside the yard to reach the upper level and find Fluffs.
  4. If you try to pick her up or stroke her, she scratches you for 3 damage. The important clue is that she first appears thirsty.

4. Give Fluffs milk and a seasoned sardine

  1. Use the bucket of milk on Fluffs. She drinks it, but still refuses to leave.
  2. Fluffs now appears hungry. If you have not already done so, use a doogle leaf on a raw sardine to make a seasoned sardine.
  3. Use the seasoned sardine on Fluffs. Ordinary fish are not a substitute in build 254; she rejects them.
  4. After eating, Fluffs still looks afraid to leave. Interacting with her now reveals the important clue: kittens can be heard mewing nearby.

5. Search the crates for Fluffs' kitten

  1. Return to the ground level of the Lumber Yard and search the crates.
  2. The kitten's hiding place is not fixed. Contemporary March-2004 guidance says to search the pinkish crates, and build 254 randomly chooses one of six possible crate positions when you feed Fluffs the seasoned sardine.
  3. Empty crates and barrels can tell you that the mewing sounds close. Keep searching until you receive Fluffs' kitten.
  4. Do not drop the kitten. In build 254 it runs back to the selected crate, so you must search for it again.

6. Return the kitten to Fluffs

  1. Climb back to Fluffs and use Fluffs' kitten on her.
  2. Fluffs and the kitten reunite and run home together.
  3. You do not need to pick up or escort either cat after this point.

7. Return to Gertrude

  1. Go back to Gertrude's house and speak to her.
  2. Gertrude confirms that Fluffs has returned and explains that the cat had refused to leave because she could not find her kitten.
  3. Gertrude gives you your own pet kitten, followed by a chocolate cake and a stew.
  4. The quest completes, awarding 1 Quest Point and 1,525 Cooking XP.

Combat and hazards

  • No combat encounter is required.
  • Fluffs' scratch: attempting to pick up or stroke her before the rescue causes 3 damage in build 254, matching the March-2004 walkthrough evidence. Feeding her directly does not require a fight.
  • The Lumber Yard fence is not an Agility test. Build 254 simply lets members climb the broken section.

Useful notes and recovery

  • No sardine yet: 5 Fishing is only a period recommendation for catching one yourself. The quest does not check your Fishing level.
  • Wrong fish: build 254 rejects ordinary sardines and several other fish when offered to Fluffs. Combine a raw sardine + doogle leaf first.
  • Cannot find the kitten: the correct crate is random in build 254. Search the relevant crates after feeding the seasoned sardine; the selected crate can provide another kitten if the quest kitten is absent from both inventory and bank.
  • Dropped Fluffs' kitten: it runs back to its hiding place in build 254. Search the crates again.
  • Lost your pet after the quest: Gertrude can sell another kitten for 100 coins when you no longer have a qualifying kitten or cat available.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1,525 Cooking XP.
  • 1 pet kitten. The original 2003 release introduced the kitten as a small virtual pet: it grows if cared for and can run away if neglected.
  • 1 chocolate cake.
  • 1 stew.
  • Access to replacement kittens from Gertrude. Build 254 charges 100 coins when you no longer have a kitten/cat available.

Post-quest cat benefits

  • Rat catching: kittens and grown cats can be sent after nearby rats in build 254. A grown cat is much more reliable than a kitten; an overgrown cat can no longer chase vermin.
  • Cat training medal: after one of your cats has caught 100 rats in build 254, Gertrude can award the Cat training medal.
  • West Ardougne cat trade: a grown or overgrown cat can be given to certain civilians in West Ardougne for 100 Death runes. This is a repeatable cat-system benefit rather than part of Gertrude's Cat's immediate quest reward. Access to West Ardougne follows the Plague City/Biohazard storyline.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 tracks start → paid boy → milk → seasoned sardine → kitten returned → complete. The order of milk and seasoned sardine is enforced.
  • Boy payment: either Shilop or Wilough can advance the quest, and the payment is exactly 100 coins.
  • Fence: the Lumber Yard's broken fence has no skill check; the interaction is members-only from the outside.
  • Fluffs' scratch: the build applies exactly 3 damage when the player tries to handle Fluffs before the rescue.
  • Kitten location: feeding the seasoned sardine randomly selects one of six crate coordinates. Only the selected crate yields Fluffs' kitten.
  • Quest-kitten recovery: dropping Fluffs' kitten makes it run back to the chosen crate. The selected crate can issue another one when none is in inventory or bank.
  • Completion reward: the completion queue advances Cooking by 15,250 internal units, which is 1,525 XP under the project's tenths-of-XP convention, and the global quest constant awards 1 Quest Point. Gertrude separately gives the pet kitten, chocolate cake and stew before the completion queue.
  • Pet system: build 254 implements kitten hunger and attention, growth into a cat and then an overgrown cat, rat catching, replacement kittens, the 100-rat Cat training medal and the West Ardougne 100-Death-rune cat trade.
  • Dialogue provenance: the quest source explicitly notes that many line breaks were guessed for 2004-style presentation and some pet/post-quest text was reconstructed from RSC or later references. Those exact reconstructed lines are not treated here as direct September-2004 evidence.
