Gertrude's Cat

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Gertrude's Cat
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Gertrude in her house north of Varrock, near the chicken farm.

Gertrude's beloved cat has had a litter of kittens, but the boldest of the bunch has wandered off into Varrock and hasn't been seen since. Gertrude is beside herself with worry and asks you, a friendly passer-by, to track down her missing kitten and bring it home safely before something worse happens to it.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Coins to buy milk or a saucer for coaxing the kitten out of hiding

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Gertrude outside her house and learn that her kitten has gone missing somewhere in Varrock.
  2. Ask her for a description of the kitten so you know what you are looking for.
  3. Head into Varrock and ask around about recent kitten sightings.
  4. Follow the trail of clues from bystanders until you narrow down the kitten's likely hiding spot.
  5. Search the area carefully, since the kitten will be skittish and easily spooked.
  6. Coax the frightened kitten out of hiding using milk or a gentle approach.
  7. Once the kitten trusts you, pick it up carefully so it does not run off again.
  8. Return to Gertrude's house with the kitten safely in hand.
  9. Hand the kitten back to a relieved Gertrude.
  10. Accept her thanks and the small reward she offers for your trouble.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A pet kitten you can keep and raise
  • Some Cooking experience
  • A small amount of coins
