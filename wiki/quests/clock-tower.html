Start point
Speak to one of the two monk brothers standing outside the clock tower in Ardougne.

Ardougne's great clock tower has ground to a halt, and the two monk brothers who tend it are at a loss for how to fix it. Somewhere inside the tower's mechanism, vital cogs have gone missing, and someone brave enough to explore its strange, maze-like interior will need to track them down before the whole town loses track of time.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • This quest is largely a fetch-and-puzzle quest with no mandatory items

Recommended

  • Nothing significant; the tower's interior contains only weak monsters

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to the first monk brother outside the Ardougne clock tower about the broken mechanism.
  2. Speak to the second brother to get the full picture of what's gone wrong.
  3. Enter the clock tower and begin exploring its unusual, maze-like interior.
  4. Use the tower's teleport pads or levers to move between its different chambers.
  5. Search each chamber carefully for the missing cogs.
  6. Deal with any minor creatures that have taken up residence inside the tower.
  7. Collect all of the cogs needed to repair the mechanism.
  8. Return to the central mechanism room once you have everything you need.
  9. Fit the cogs into their correct places to restore the clock's movement.
  10. Speak to the monk brothers to confirm the repair is complete.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Crafting experience
  • Coins
  • The gratitude of Ardougne's monks
