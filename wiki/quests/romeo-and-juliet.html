Romeo and Juliet are in love, but Juliet's father opposes their marriage. You become their messenger, carry Juliet's note to Romeo, ask Father Lawrence for help, and obtain a Cadava potion from the Varrock Apothecary as part of a plan to make Juliet appear dead long enough to escape her father's control.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None. There are no skill checks and no combat encounter.

Required items

  • 1 bunch of Cadava berries. The Apothecary consumes one bunch to make the quest potion in build 254. The berries can be obtained during the quest, so nothing has to be brought to start.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Message from Juliet to Romeo.
  • Cadava potion from the Apothecary after supplying Cadava berries.

Recommended

  • Two free inventory spaces are enough for the message/berries and potion.
  • You may collect Cadava berries before starting if you want to shorten the trip.

Before you start

Every quest step is in or immediately around Varrock. Juliet is upstairs in the house just outside the western side of the city, Romeo is in or around Varrock Square, Father Lawrence is in the chapel north-east of the square, and the Apothecary lives behind the sword-shop area in south-west Varrock.

Jagex's December 2003 quest manual tells players to begin with Juliet at the house west of Varrock and calls the mission Short, with no minimum requirements. Target-proximate RuneScape 2 guide lineage and build 254 also allow Romeo to be approached first. Starting with Juliet is used below because it matches the official period manual and goes directly into the message-delivery objective.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Juliet and take her message

  1. Leave Varrock through the west side and enter the house immediately outside the city.
  2. Go upstairs and speak to Juliet.
  3. Agree to help her communicate with Romeo.
  4. Juliet gives you a Message and asks you to deliver it to Romeo.

Alternative start: in build 254 you can instead speak to Romeo in Varrock Square and agree to find Juliet. Juliet then gives you the same message and the routes merge.

2. Deliver the message to Romeo

  1. Return to Varrock Square and find Romeo.
  2. Speak to him while carrying Juliet's Message.
  3. You hand over the message. Romeo explains that Juliet's father opposes their marriage and that he is afraid to approach her family's land himself.
  4. Romeo asks you to find Father Lawrence, who may know a way to help.

3. Ask Father Lawrence for help

  1. Go to the Varrock chapel north-east of the central square.
  2. Speak to Father Lawrence and explain that Romeo sent you.
  3. He proposes a plan: Juliet can take a potion that makes her appear dead, allowing Romeo to collect her from the crypt later.
  4. Father Lawrence sends you to the Apothecary for a Cadava potion.

4. Visit the Apothecary and get Cadava berries

  1. Find the Apothecary in the small house behind the sword-shop area in south-west Varrock.
  2. Tell him that Father Lawrence sent you and that you need a Cadava potion for Romeo and Juliet.
  3. The Apothecary has the other ingredients, but asks you to bring him Cadava berries.
  4. If you do not already have them, go south-east of Varrock toward the mining area and the old Dark Wizards' stone-circle area and obtain one bunch of Cadava berries.

The exact scenery presentation of Cadava berries changed in later RuneScape. This guide therefore identifies the target-period area rather than projecting the later berry-bush graphics backward. Build 254 only requires the Cadava-berries item; no glove requirement is enforced by the quest hand-in.

5. Have the Cadava potion made

  1. Return to the Apothecary with at least one bunch of Cadava berries.
  2. Speak to him again.
  3. He takes one bunch of Cadava berries and gives you 1 Cadava potion.
  4. There is no coin fee for the quest potion in build 254.

6. Give the potion to Juliet

  1. Return to Juliet at her house west of Varrock.
  2. Speak to her while carrying the Cadava potion.
  3. You hand over the potion. Juliet asks you to make sure Romeo understands the plan and knows to collect her from the crypt.

7. Tell Romeo the plan

  1. Return to Romeo in Varrock Square.
  2. Tell him that Juliet has the potion and that the plan is ready.
  3. Romeo badly misunderstands what you tell him and concludes that Juliet is simply dead.
  4. The quest completes immediately after this conversation.

There is no successful reunion to perform and no further turn-in. The final Romeo conversation is the completion trigger.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost Juliet's Message? In build 254, Juliet checks both your inventory and bank. If you own no message, she gives you another one while the message-delivery stage is active.
  • Lost the Cadava potion? The Apothecary remains on the potion-making stage until Juliet receives it. Bring another bunch of Cadava berries and he can make another potion.
  • No combat preparation is needed. The route has no required enemy, dangerous dungeon or skill check.
  • Do not look for Juliet in a crypt to begin the quest. Her quest location is the upstairs part of her house west of Varrock. The crypt is only part of Father Lawrence's proposed story plan.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points.
  • No coins.
  • No skill XP.
  • No permanent item or access unlock is asserted for the September 2004 quest reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Two valid starts: the reconstructed quest journal points to Romeo, but Juliet's NPC script can also start the quest directly by giving the message. This is compatible with the period manual's Juliet start and target-proximate guides that allow either NPC.
  • State sequence: build 254 tracks Romeo/Juliet contact, Juliet's message, Father Lawrence, the Apothecary, Juliet receiving the potion, and the final Romeo conversation. Completion is state 100.
  • Message recovery: Juliet reissues the quest message if it is missing from both inventory and bank.
  • Potion ingredients: the Apothecary consumes exactly one bunch of Cadava berries and gives one Cadava potion. He charges no coins for this quest potion.
  • Glove dialogue: one reconstructed Father Lawrence branch says Cadava berries are poisonous to touch and that gloves are needed, but the quest scripts do not enforce a glove check and target-period walkthrough evidence does not establish gloves as a hard requirement. They are therefore not listed as required equipment.
  • Ending: after Juliet receives the potion, Romeo misunderstands the explanation and the completion queue fires during the next conversation with him. Build 254 does not require a crypt visit or successful reunion.
  • Reward: `^romeojuliet_questpoints` is 5. The completion script contains no coin grant and no skill-XP advancement.
  • Reconstruction caveat: the journal and several dialogue files cite later videos/reconstructed material, and some lines are plainly modern in tone. Exact build dialogue is therefore treated as implementation evidence rather than direct proof of September 2004 wording.
