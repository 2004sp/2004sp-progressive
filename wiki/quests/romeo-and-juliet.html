Romeo & Juliet

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Romeo & Juliet
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
5
Start point
Talk to Romeo, who stands near the fountain in Varrock's main square.

Varrock's own star-crossed lovers have been kept apart by a disapproving parent, and poor Romeo is too heartbroken to think straight. Juliet, meanwhile, is plotting a dramatic — if slightly deceptive — way out of her situation. You're roped in as the go-between, ferrying messages and a rather suspicious potion between the pair.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Walkthrough

  1. Find Romeo wandering near the fountain in Varrock's main square, lovesick and distraught.
  2. Ask him what's wrong; he explains he's fallen for a girl named Juliet but has lost track of her.
  3. Agree to help him and set off to search Varrock for her whereabouts.
  4. Locate Juliet inside a small crypt-like building southeast of the square and speak with her.
  5. Learn that her father disapproves of Romeo and is pressuring her into a marriage she doesn't want.
  6. Offer to help reunite the couple; Juliet asks for a potion that can simulate death so she can escape her situation.
  7. Head to the Apothecary's shop in western Varrock and ask him about a Cadava potion.
  8. Explain the situation to the Apothecary, who hands over a vial of the potion.
  9. Return to Juliet and give her the potion.
  10. Go back to Romeo and let him know Juliet is waiting for him.
  11. Watch as the two are reunited, and speak with Romeo once more to wrap up the quest.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
