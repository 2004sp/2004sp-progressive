Hazeel Cult

Start point
Speak to Safraz Rasmussen, a suspicious trader in East Ardougne's market square.

Beneath the streets of Ardougne, two secretive factions are locked in a hidden power struggle: the followers of the exiled god Hazeel, and their sworn enemies. When you get drawn into their conflict, you must decide who to trust and venture into forgotten tunnels beneath the city to settle the dispute once and for all.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start; you obtain what you need as you progress through the quest.

Recommended

  • A weapon and armour
  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Safraz Rasmussen in the East Ardougne market to learn about the rival factions operating in secret around the city.
  2. Follow his leads and speak to Jonathan Blackhood, who reveals he is opposed to the followers of Hazeel.
  3. Investigate further around Ardougne to find evidence of the cult's activity and locate their hidden entrance.
  4. Choose to side with the Hazeel cult and agree to help them retrieve an item they consider sacred.
  5. Descend into the tunnels beneath Ardougne, fighting your way past cultist guards stationed throughout the passages.
  6. Solve your way through the underground maze to reach an inner chamber holding a relic tied to Hazeel's old temple.
  7. Retrieve the relic and be prepared for a confrontation as rival cult members try to stop you from leaving with it.
  8. Escape the tunnels and deliver the relic back to the cult leader waiting above ground.
  9. Speak to the cult leader once more to receive your reward and complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A quantity of coins
  • The mark of Hazeel amulet, allowing access to a fence who buys stolen goods
  • Some Thieving and combat experience
