Start point
Speak to Wizard Cromperty in his house in Yanille.

Wizard Cromperty's oddly cramped house in Yanille hides a far bigger secret than its walls suggest. Beneath the floorboards lies a shrunken, elemental-powered workshop that Cromperty can no longer reach on his own, and he needs someone small enough and brave enough to venture inside and retrieve what he left behind.

Requirements

  • Level 20 Crafting

Items needed

  • A set of full metal armour to melt down for the shrinking mould

Recommended

  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Visit Wizard Cromperty in Yanille and ask him about the strange noises coming from beneath his house.
  2. Learn that he built a hidden workshop below and needs help reaching a device trapped inside.
  3. Hand over a set of full metal armour so Cromperty can use it to build a shrinking mould.
  4. Use Cromperty's device to shrink yourself down to size.
  5. Descend into the miniature workshop hidden beneath the house.
  6. Navigate through the earth-themed section of the workshop, avoiding its hazards.
  7. Make your way through the air-themed section, dealing with its particular obstacles.
  8. Push on through the fire-themed section, staying clear of open flames.
  9. Cross the water-themed section to reach the workshop's core.
  10. Retrieve the elemental metal and machinery parts Cromperty asked for.
  11. Make your way back out of the workshop and restore yourself to normal size.
  12. Return the retrieved parts to Wizard Cromperty and hear his plans for what comes next.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • The ability to make elemental shields at the workshop's furnace
  • Access to Elemental Workshop II in the future
