A battered book in Seers' Village describes a strange ore that can absorb, transform and focus elemental energy, and an old workshop built to work it. Hidden inside the book is the means to enter that sealed workshop. To recreate an Elemental shield, you must restore its water, air and furnace machinery, obtain elemental ore and work it into metal.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • 20 Mining — required to disturb the elemental rock and obtain its ore.
  • 20 Smithing — required to work the elemental metal into the shield.
  • 20 Crafting — required to repair the damaged bellows.
  • You must be able to defeat a level-35 Earth elemental. There is no separate numeric Combat requirement.

Required items

  • A pickaxe you can use — not consumed.
  • 1 thread — consumed when repairing the bellows.
  • 4 coal — consumed when smelting the elemental ore.
  • 1 hammer — needed at the workbench and not consumed.
  • A knife or other suitable sharp/slashing item — used to cut open the book. A knife is available at the starting house, so you do not have to bring one.

Obtained during the quest

  • Battered book — found in the starting bookcase. Keep it: the workshop instructions are needed again at the final workbench.
  • Battered key — hidden in the book's spine.
  • Needle and leather — found by searching boxes inside the workshop; the leather is consumed when repairing the bellows, while the needle is retained.
  • Stone bowl — found in the workshop and used to carry lava.
  • Elemental ore — dropped by the quest Earth elemental after you disturb its rock.
  • Elemental metal — made from the ore and four coal in the restored furnace.

Recommended

  • Food and ordinary combat equipment if a level-35 enemy is dangerous for you.
  • Bring the pickaxe, thread, four coal and hammer before entering so the whole quest can be completed in one workshop visit.
  • Leave enough inventory space for the book, key, workshop supplies, ore and metal.

Before you start

The quest begins from an object rather than an NPC. Go to the house immediately south-west of the Seers' Village bank and search the bookcase on its eastern wall. The target-period quest was simply called Elemental Workshop; the later Elemental Workshop I styling distinguishes it from the sequel released in 2006. This site keeps the existing elemental-workshop-i.html filename so old links continue to work.

Do not throw away the battered book after taking the key from it. The final workbench requires the instructions in the book before it will let you make the shield.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Find the book and hidden key

  1. Search the east-wall bookcase in the house south-west of Seers' Village bank. Take the Battered book, titled The elemental shield.
  2. Read the book. This starts the quest and explains that an unusual elemental ore was once worked in a sealed workshop in the village.
  3. Use a knife on the battered book. Cutting into the spine reveals a Battered key. Keep the book as well as the key.
  4. Go north to the Seers' Village smithy/anvil building. Look for the Odd Looking Wall.
  5. Use the battered key on the Odd Looking Wall. The hidden doorway opens. Go through and descend the spiral staircase into the workshop.

2. Gather the workshop supplies

  1. Search the boxes around the central workshop. You need to find a stone bowl, needle and piece of leather.
  2. The June-2004 walkthrough records that the useful box locations could vary between players. In build 254 the three useful boxes are fixed, so simply search the workshop boxes until you have all three items.
  3. Make sure you also have the thread you brought with you; the workshop does not supply the required thread in build 254.

3. Obtain elemental ore

  1. Go into the western earth section. Find the distinctive elemental rock.
  2. Try to mine the rock with your pickaxe. You need 20 Mining. Instead of yielding ore immediately, the rock becomes an aggressive level-35 Earth elemental.
  3. Defeat the Earth elemental. Pick up the Elemental ore it drops. This is the only mandatory combat encounter.

4. Restart the water wheel

  1. Go to the northern water section. There are two water controls/valves and a lever.
  2. Turn the eastern control, then the western control. Both sluice controls need to be open.
  3. Pull the water lever. With both controls set correctly, the water wheel starts running. If the controls are not both open, the mechanism resets instead.

5. Repair and run the bellows

  1. Go to the eastern air section and inspect the damaged bellows.
  2. Repair the bellows with the leather, thread and needle. This requires 20 Crafting and consumes one leather and one thread.
  3. Pull the air lever. The bellows only begin pumping when the water wheel is already running and the repair is complete.

6. Light the furnace

  1. Go to the southern fire section with the stone bowl.
  2. Use the stone bowl on the lava trough. The bowl fills with hot lava.
  3. Use the full stone bowl on the furnace. The lava brings the furnace to life; the bowl is returned empty.

7. Smelt the elemental metal

  1. Check that the water wheel and bellows are still running. The furnace needs the airflow before it is hot enough to refine the ore.
  2. Use the Elemental ore on the furnace while carrying four coal. The furnace consumes the ore and all four coal and gives you one bar of Elemental metal.

8. Make the Elemental shield

  1. Return to the central workbench. Have the Elemental metal, hammer and battered book with you.
  2. Use the Elemental metal on the workbench. You need 20 Smithing and the book's instructions.
  3. You smith an Elemental shield. Making the first shield completes the quest immediately; there is no NPC to return to.

Combat encounters

  • Earth elemental — level 35: mandatory. It appears when you try to mine the special rock and drops the elemental ore needed for the quest.
  • Other elementals: the workshop also contains air, fire, water and ordinary earth elementals, but killing them is not required for the quest.
  • If you leave without the ore, return to the western rock and disturb it again to obtain another quest Earth elemental.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost or forgotten knife: a knife can be obtained in the starting house. Build 254 also accepts other items with an appropriate slashing action when used on the book.
  • Workshop machinery stops: the water lever can stop the wheel, and stopping the water also stops the bellows. Re-open both water controls, restart the wheel and then pull the air lever again.
  • Keep the book: obtaining the key is not the end of the book's use. The final workbench checks that the battered book is in your inventory.
  • Need another shield later: return to the workshop, obtain another elemental ore, restore the machinery as necessary, smelt another elemental metal bar with four coal, and use the workbench with the book and hammer.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 5,000 Crafting experience.
  • 5,000 Smithing experience.
  • 1 Elemental shield.
  • Continued access to the Elemental Workshop and the ability to make additional Elemental shields.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest title: the build journal, the 2 June 2004 guide and Jagex's release announcement use Elemental Workshop. Elemental Workshop I is later retrospective styling after the sequel; the historical page title therefore uses the original name while retaining the old filename.
  • Requirements: build 254's quest journal explicitly lists 20 Mining, 20 Smithing and 20 Crafting. The mining interaction checks 20 Mining, repairing the bellows checks 20 Crafting, and the final workbench checks 20 Smithing.
  • Crate locations: the contemporary June-2004 walkthrough says the stone bowl, needle and leather can appear in different boxes for different players. Build 254 instead assigns them to fixed workshop boxes. The public route tells build players to search the boxes rather than relying on a historical random location.
  • The book after cutting: build 254 records a logical "slashed book" quest-state bit but leaves the inventory item as Battered book and requires that same book at the workbench. This agrees with the contemporary guide's instruction to keep the battered book and avoids projecting the later separate Slashed book item backward.
  • Elemental ore fight: build 254 spawns a dedicated level-35 Earth elemental from the rock. Its death drops one Elemental ore. The other workshop elementals are levels 34-35 but are not required kills.
  • Smelting and smithing: one Elemental ore plus four coal makes one Elemental metal bar once the furnace is lit and the bellows are running. Build 254 also awards 8 Smithing XP for smelting the bar and 20 Smithing XP for making the shield, in addition to the separate 5,000 Smithing XP completion reward.
  • Completion: making the first shield sets the completion bit and awards exactly 5,000 Crafting XP, 5,000 Smithing XP and 1 Quest Point.
  • Equipping the shield: build 254 applies only a level-1 Defence equipment check to the Elemental shield. Its source comment notes that a quest-completion equip requirement was added in a later rework, so no later quest gate has been projected into this September-2004 guide.
  • Post-cutoff sequel content: Elemental helmets, mind equipment and the lower workshop floors belong to Elemental Workshop II, released in October 2006, and are intentionally excluded.
