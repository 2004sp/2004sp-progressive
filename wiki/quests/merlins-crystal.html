Merlin's Crystal

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Merlin's Crystal
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Medium
Quest points
6
Start point
Speak to King Arthur inside Camelot castle.

The legendary wizard Merlin has been imprisoned inside a crystal by the vengeful sorceress Morgan Le Faye, leaving Camelot without its wisest advisor. King Arthur turns to you to infiltrate Morgan's manor, uncover what it will take to break the enchantment, and bring Merlin back before Camelot suffers for his absence.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • This quest provides most items as you progress; be prepared to search thoroughly

Recommended

  • A weapon and armour for the skeletons in Morgan's manor
  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Camelot castle and speak to King Arthur about Merlin's disappearance.
  2. Learn that Morgan Le Faye has trapped Merlin in a crystal somewhere in her manor to the south.
  3. Travel south to Morgan Le Faye's manor and let yourself inside.
  4. Search the manor's rooms for clues, ingredients and keys, fighting off the skeletons that patrol the halls.
  5. Locate the kitchen and gather what's needed to bake something to distract or placate the manor's occupants.
  6. Use what you find to get past any locked doors or guarded areas blocking your route deeper into the manor.
  7. Track down Morgan Le Faye herself and deal with her, whether through confrontation or trickery, to get her to reveal where Merlin is kept.
  8. Find the crystal holding Merlin, hidden away in one of the manor's more secure rooms.
  9. Free Merlin from the crystal.
  10. Escort or send word of Merlin's release back to King Arthur in Camelot.
  11. Speak to King Arthur again to complete the quest and claim Excalibur as your reward.

Rewards

  • 6 Quest Points
  • The sword Excalibur
  • Access to services in Camelot
  • Combat-related experience
