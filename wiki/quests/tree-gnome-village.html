General Khazard's army is pressing the tree gnomes toward extinction. Their village is normally protected by three magical orbs resting in the ancient Spirit Tree, but one orb was taken to the battlefield and captured. Help King Bolren recover it, breach the Khazard stronghold with the gnome ballista, and then reclaim the two remaining orbs when the war reaches the village itself.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • No skill or prerequisite-quest requirements.
  • Ability to defeat a level 112 Khazard warlord. This is the quest's mandatory combat encounter in build 254.

Items and preparation

Bring

  • 6 normal logs. Commander Montai takes all six to reinforce the gnome defences. Other log types do not substitute.
  • Combat equipment and food suitable for the level 112 Khazard warlord.

Obtained during the quest

  • Orb of protection. Recovered from the chest inside the breached Khazard stronghold.
  • Two orbs of protection. Recovered from the Khazard warlord after the village is raided.

Recommended

  • Extra food for crossing the battlefield and fighting the warlord.
  • Several free inventory spaces. You need room for the quest orbs, and carrying the six logs from the start avoids an extra trip.
September-2004 ballista behaviour: the target-period route uses three numerical coordinates. In build 254 the correct fixed solution is height 4, X 3, Y 5. Later versions changed how the tracker clues and ballista input are stored, so do not substitute a modern randomised-coordinate walkthrough.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Reach King Bolren in the maze

  1. Travel to the Tree Gnome Maze, west of Port Khazard and south of Ardougne.
  2. Navigate the hedge maze to its centre and squeeze through the loose railing into the village.
  3. Speak to King Bolren beside the ancient Spirit Tree.
  4. Ask if you can help. He explains that the Spirit Tree protects the gnomes while its three orbs are in place, but one orb was taken to the battlefield and captured by Khazard's troops.
  5. Agree to retrieve the stolen orb. Bolren sends you to Commander Montai on the battlefield north of the maze, and Elkoy guides you back out.

2. Give Commander Montai six logs

  1. Head north from the maze to the battlefield and find Commander Montai on the gnome side.
  2. Tell him King Bolren sent you to retrieve the orb.
  3. Montai says the orb is inside the Khazard stronghold, but the gnomes first need to reinforce their own battlements.
  4. Agree to help, then speak to him while carrying 6 normal logs. He removes all six.
  5. Speak to Montai again. With the defences strengthened, he explains that the gnome ballista can breach the enemy stronghold, but he needs its firing coordinates.
  6. Agree to find the three tracker gnomes scattered through the battlefield.

3. Learn the three firing coordinates

The 2004 route sends you to all three trackers. Their information resolves to the following fixed values:

  • Height coordinate: 4. One tracker is along the north-western side of the Khazard stronghold.
  • Y coordinate: 5. One tracker is being held inside a building on the eastern/north-eastern side of the Khazard position.
  • X coordinate: 3. The third tracker is south-west of the stronghold and has been driven half-mad by the fighting. His target-period riddle resolves to 3.

Build 254 does not store separate “tracker spoken to” flags, so a player who already knows the numbers can technically enter them at the ballista without visiting every tracker. Following the three trackers is still the intended 2004 quest route.

4. Fire the ballista: 4 — 3 — 5

  1. Return to the ballista on the gnome side of the battlefield.
  2. Fire it and enter the coordinates in the order requested:
  • Height: 0004
  • X: 0003
  • Y: 0005

A correct shot smashes the front of the Khazard stronghold and leaves a section of wall low enough to cross. A wrong combination simply misses; you can use the ballista again.

5. Recover the first orb

  1. Go north to the breached Khazard stronghold and climb over the crumbled wall.
  2. Khazard commanders guard the building. The period guide expects combat with the level-48 commanders while moving through the stronghold.
  3. Reach the chest, open it and search it to obtain the Orb of protection.
  4. In build 254, killing the commanders is not a quest-state requirement: opening/searching the chest can still produce the orb, although a nearby commander becomes hostile. Fight them if necessary or simply make the recovery and leave.
  5. Return south to the Tree Gnome Maze. Speak to Elkoy at the entrance and let him guide you back into the village.

6. Return the orb and learn of the raid

  1. Take the Orb of protection to King Bolren.
  2. Bolren tells you that while you were away, Khazard troops broke into the village, killed many gnomes and stole the other two orbs.
  3. He says a Khazard warlord carried the stolen orbs north of the battlefield.
  4. Agree to recover them. Bolren takes the first orb for safekeeping and Elkoy guides you out of the maze again.

7. Defeat the level 112 Khazard warlord

  1. Head north through/past the battlefield, then continue north-west toward the southern wall of West Ardougne.
  2. Find the Khazard warlord — level 112. He carries a warhammer and shield in build 254 and has 170 Hitpoints.
  3. Talk to him if you want the quest confrontation, then defeat him.
  4. On the quest kill, build 254 places the two remaining Orbs of protection directly into your inventory and advances the quest state.

8. Restore the orbs to the Spirit Tree

  1. Return to the Tree Gnome Maze and use Elkoy to reach the village quickly.
  2. Speak to King Bolren while carrying the two recovered orbs.
  3. The gnomes perform a ceremony and return the orbs to the ancient Spirit Tree.
  4. Bolren gives you a Gnome amulet and explains that, as a friend of the gnomes, you may now use the Spirit Tree's teleportation magic.
  5. The quest completes with 2 Quest Points and 11,450 Attack XP.

Combat and hazards

  • Khazard commanders — level 48. Contemporary 2004 guidance expects you to deal with commanders inside the breached stronghold. Build 254 does not require their deaths for quest progression; the chest itself advances the first-orb stage.
  • Khazard warlord — level 112. This fight is mandatory. Build 254 gives him 170 Hitpoints, 75 Attack, 78 Strength and 80 Defence, with crush-style melee.
  • Battlefield traffic: level-19 Khazard troopers roam the area, and the route to the warlord passes hostile territory. Bring food rather than treating the warlord as the only possible source of damage.
  • Wrong ballista coordinates are safe: the shot misses and you can immediately try again.

Rewards and permanent unlocks

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 11,450 Attack XP.
  • Gnome amulet. In build 254 it provides +13 stab defence, +13 slash defence and +13 crush defence.
  • Access to the Spirit Tree transport network. The Tree Gnome Village tree can send you to the Khazard battlefield tree, the tree north of Varrock and the Tree Gnome Stronghold.
  • Elkoy's maze escort remains available after the quest for quick travel between the maze entrance and village.

Stronghold caveat: Tree Gnome Village lets you travel to the Tree Gnome Stronghold spirit tree, but build 254 requires completion of The Grand Tree before that Stronghold tree itself will talk and teleport you away again.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: %treequest progresses through start, Montai, six logs, tracker/ballista, first orb, village raid, warlord and completion states.
  • Journal requirement: tree_journal.rs2 lists no skill requirement; it only warns that the player must be able to defeat a level 112 Warlord.
  • Logs: commander_montai.rs2 checks for exactly 6 normal logs and removes six before advancing the quest.
  • Tracker values: build dialogue gives height 4, Y 5, and the third tracker retains the period riddle whose answer is X 3.
  • Ballista: quest_tree.rs2 directly checks height = 4, x = 3, y = 5. The source comments note that later versions changed this area to tracker varbits/randomised handling.
  • First orb: searching the stronghold chest in the correct quest state advances %treequest and gives the orb. A nearby Khazard commander retaliates, but commander death is not checked.
  • Warlord: khazard_warlord is configured at visible combat level 112 with 170 Hitpoints. Its quest death handler gives the combined two-orb item and advances to tree_defeated_warlord.
  • Exact Attack XP: completion calls stat_advance(attack, 114500), which is 11,450 displayed XP in the build's tenths-based XP storage. A March/October 2004 LostHQ guide rounded this to 11,500, so the implementation value is used here.
  • Quest points: ^tree_questpoints = 2.
  • Gnome amulet: the build item config gives +13 stab, slash and crush defence, and King Bolren replaces a lost amulet after completion.
  • Spirit trees: the village and young-tree scripts require Tree Gnome Village completion. The Stronghold tree separately requires The Grand Tree, preserving the one-way Stronghold caveat until that quest is complete.
