Start point
Speak to King Bolren inside the Tree Gnome Village, west of Ardougne.

Deep in the maze-like forest west of Ardougne lies the secretive Tree Gnome Village, now under threat from the invading forces of General Khazard. King Bolren and his advisor Brimstail need outside help rallying the gnomes' scattered generals and preparing their unconventional defences before Khazard's army overruns the village entirely.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A weapon and armour for the battle against Khazard's forces

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison potions

Walkthrough

  1. Navigate the maze-like forest west of Ardougne to reach the Tree Gnome Village.
  2. Speak to King Bolren about the threat posed by General Khazard's invading troops.
  3. Meet with Brimstail, the wise gnome elder, to learn how the gnomes traditionally defend themselves.
  4. Track down each of the gnome generals scattered around the village and surrounding area.
  5. Convince or assist each general so they agree to rally their forces for the coming battle.
  6. Help prepare the gnomes' giant spiders to serve as mounts or war beasts against Khazard's army.
  7. Return to King Bolren once all the generals are on board.
  8. Take part in the defence of the village as the gnomes' forces clash with General Khazard's troops.
  9. Help turn the tide of the battle against the invaders.
  10. Speak to King Bolren after the battle to confirm the village is safe.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • Combat-related experience
  • Access to the Tree Gnome Village and its shops
  • Improved standing with the gnomes, opening the way for future dealings with them
