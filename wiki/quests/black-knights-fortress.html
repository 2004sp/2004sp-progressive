Black Knights' Fortress

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Black Knights' Fortress
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Medium
Quest points
3
Start point
Talk to Sir Amik Varze inside Falador Castle.

Tensions between the White Knights of Falador and the Black Knights to the north have been simmering for years, and Sir Amik Varze wants eyes inside enemy territory. Getting into the fortress means blending in with knights who would kill you on sight if your disguise ever slipped. It's a tense bit of espionage dressed up as a straightforward errand.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A black item of clothing, such as a black cape or black medium helmet, to use as a disguise

Recommended

  • Armour and a weapon in case of combat
  • Food for the fortress infiltration

Walkthrough

  1. Head to Falador Castle and speak with Sir Amik Varze, leader of the White Knights.
  2. Learn that the White Knights need intelligence on the Black Knights operating out of their fortress to the north.
  3. Agree to infiltrate the fortress on their behalf.
  4. Put together a black-coloured disguise so you can pass as one of the Black Knights' own.
  5. Travel north toward Ice Mountain and locate the Black Knights' Fortress.
  6. Wear your disguise and make your way past the outer guards without drawing suspicion.
  7. Explore the fortress's corridors and lower levels, staying alert for patrols.
  8. Witness or become caught up in a clash between infiltrating White Knights and the fortress's defenders.
  9. Push through to the fortress's lower chambers where important documents or secrets are kept.
  10. Retrieve whatever evidence or item the White Knights sent you to find.
  11. Make your way back out of the fortress and return to Falador.
  12. Report your findings to Sir Amik Varze to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
