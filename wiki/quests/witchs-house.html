Witch's House

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Witch's House
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Short
Quest points
4
Start point
Speak to the young boy crying outside the witch's house in Taverley.

A little boy's ball has bounced over the fence into the garden of the sinister house on the edge of Taverley, and he's too afraid to go and fetch it himself given the house's reputation. What starts as a simple favour quickly turns dangerous once you realise the witch inside has far worse in mind for curious visitors than scaring off children.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • This quest is largely self-contained; no items are strictly required to start

Recommended

  • Armour and a decent weapon for the spiders in the basement
  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to the crying boy outside the witch's house in Taverley and offer to retrieve his ball.
  2. Enter the garden and search for the ball among the hedges.
  3. Try the front door of the house and let yourself in.
  4. Explore the ground floor rooms, searching for clues about the witch and a way further into the house.
  5. Find the entrance to the basement and head downstairs.
  6. Fight your way past the giant spiders lurking in the cellar.
  7. Search the basement for a cage or cell and free whatever poor soul the witch has trapped down there.
  8. Locate the witch herself and confront her about her prisoner and the boy's ball.
  9. Escape or defeat the witch's opposition as she tries to stop you leaving.
  10. Return the ball to the boy waiting outside to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • Defence experience
  • Coins
