Farmer Fred's sheep are getting too woolly, so he asks you to shear them, spin the fleece and bring him 20 balls of wool. This was still a normal free-to-play quest in September 2004; the later black-wool rework and eventual miniquest reclassification are outside this guide's cutoff.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • No skill requirements.
  • No prerequisite quests and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No combat is required.

Required items

  • 20 balls of wool must ultimately be given to Farmer Fred. You may make them during the quest or bring ordinary balls of wool you already have.

Items used if making the wool yourself

  • Shears — used to collect wool from sheep. The April 2004 route directs players to the Lumbridge General Store for a pair; build 254 also stocks shears there.
  • 20 wool — shear ordinary sheep in Fred's nearby sheep pen, then spin each piece into a ball of wool.

Recommended

  • If you already have 20 balls of wool, bring them with you. Build 254 does not track where the wool came from, so the quest can be completed immediately after accepting it.
  • If starting from raw wool, leave enough inventory space to gather a useful batch before walking to the spinning wheel.

Before you start

Fred's farm is north-west of Lumbridge, beside the sheep pen and on the road toward the windmill. The spinning wheel used by the period route is in Lumbridge Castle, not in Fred's farmhouse.

You do not have to deliver all 20 balls at once in build 254. Fred remembers every ball already handed over, so partial deliveries are safe.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the quest with Farmer Fred

  1. Travel to Farmer Fred's farm north-west of Lumbridge.
  2. Talk to Farmer Fred and choose “I'm looking for a quest.”
  3. He explains that his sheep need shearing and asks you to bring him 20 balls of wool.
  4. Agree to help. The dialogue about “The Thing” is optional and does not change the task.

2. Shear 20 pieces of wool

  1. If you do not have shears, go south-east into Lumbridge and obtain a pair from the General Store.
  2. Return to the sheep pen beside Fred's farm.
  3. Use the shears on an ordinary woolly sheep to obtain wool.
  4. Continue until you have 20 wool. If a sheep has already been shorn, use another woolly sheep.

3. Spin the wool in Lumbridge Castle

  1. Go to Lumbridge Castle.
  2. Climb to the first floor above ground — called the second floor in some older US-numbered guides.
  3. Find the spinning wheel in the central room.
  4. Use each piece of wool on the wheel. One wool becomes one ball of wool.
  5. Repeat until you have made all 20 balls of wool.

4. Give the wool to Farmer Fred

  1. Return to Farmer Fred north-west of Lumbridge.
  2. Talk to him while carrying balls of wool. He takes them one at a time and remembers how many you have already delivered.
  3. If you have fewer than the remaining amount, he accepts what you have and tells you to bring more later.
  4. When you hand over the 20th ball of wool, the quest completes and Fred pays you.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Already have 20 balls of wool? Accept the quest and speak to Fred again. Build 254 accepts pre-collected ordinary balls of wool.
  • Only brought part of the total? That is fine. Fred's build-254 quest state records each ball as it is handed in, so you do not lose delivery progress by leaving and returning.
  • Need shears? The period route buys them from Lumbridge General Store. Fred himself does not sell them in build 254.
  • Cannot find the spinning wheel? It is in Lumbridge Castle on the floor immediately above the ground floor, not inside Fred's farmhouse.
  • Do not follow later black-sheep instructions. The black-wool version belongs to the 2009 rework, more than five years after this guide's target date.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 150 Crafting XP.
  • 60 coins.

If you shear and spin the wool yourself in build 254, spinning the 20 pieces awards a separate 2.5 Crafting XP per piece, for 50 Crafting XP during preparation. That action XP is separate from the 150 XP quest reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest location: the build-254 quest journal places Farmer Fred's farm a little way north-west of Lumbridge.
  • Twenty-ball progress: the quest starts at state 1. Fred deletes one ball of wool at a time and advances the state for each delivery; the final ball triggers the completion queue. This means partial hand-ins are persistent.
  • Exact reward: the completion script removes the final ball, gives 60 coins, awards 150 Crafting XP and completes a 1-Quest-Point quest.
  • Spinning: build 254 converts one wool into one ball of wool and awards 2.5 Crafting XP for each spin.
  • Shears: the build-254 Lumbridge General Store stock includes shears. Fred's dialogue has no shop or shears-sale branch.
  • Historical scope: Sheep Shearer remained a quest at the 7 September 2004 cutoff. The black-wool rework and the later removal from the Quest List are deliberately excluded from the historical walkthrough.
