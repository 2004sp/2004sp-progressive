Sheep Shearer

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Sheep Shearer
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to Fred the Farmer at his farm south of Lumbridge.

Fred the Farmer has more wool on his sheep than time on his hands, and he's willing to pay for the help. It's an unglamorous but honest bit of farm labour just south of Lumbridge. All it takes is a steady hand with a pair of shears and a willingness to work the spinning wheel.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A pair of shears, which can be bought from Fred or a general store

Walkthrough

  1. Head to the farm south of Lumbridge and speak with Fred the Farmer.
  2. Agree to help him shear his flock; buy a pair of shears from him if you don't already have some.
  3. Enter the sheep pen and use your shears on the sheep to collect wool, one fleece at a time.
  4. Continue shearing sheep until you're carrying a full load of wool.
  5. Head to the spinning wheel inside Fred's farmhouse.
  6. Use each piece of wool on the spinning wheel to turn it into balls of wool.
  7. Repeat the shearing and spinning process, making trips back to the pen as needed, until you have twenty balls of wool.
  8. Return to Fred with the finished balls of wool.
  9. Hand them over to complete the quest and receive your payment.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
