Sea Slug

Start point
Speak to a monk acting strangely inside the church at Witchaven, south of Ardougne.

The quiet fishing community around Witchaven has fallen under a peculiar influence, with monks and fishermen behaving as though they are no longer entirely themselves. You are drawn into investigating the cause, uncovering a parasitic creature that has been secretly taking hold of the townsfolk's minds.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start; you gather what you need as the quest progresses.

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison potion

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Witchaven, south of Ardougne, and enter the church to speak with the monk inside.
  2. Notice his odd, glassy-eyed behaviour and begin asking around the village about what has been happening.
  3. Speak to Holgart at the Fishing Platform offshore to learn more about the strange goings-on in the area.
  4. Investigate the platform's machinery and the workers there, who also seem to be acting under some influence.
  5. Follow the trail of clues to discover that sea slugs are attaching themselves to victims and controlling their minds.
  6. Head underground beneath the platform to find the source of the infestation and the slugs' true master.
  7. Work with Holgart to shut down or disrupt the mechanism the slugs are using to spread their influence.
  8. Confront the mastermind behind the sea slug scheme once the source has been exposed.
  9. Free the affected monks and workers from the parasites' control and report back on your success.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • Fishing experience
  • Crafting experience
  • Access to the Fishing Platform minigame area
