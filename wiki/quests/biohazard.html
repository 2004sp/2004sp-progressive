Start point
Speak to Edmond again in East Ardougne after completing Plague City.

With the plague in West Ardougne revealed as a sham, Edmond and Elena need your help to gather solid proof of King Lathas's deception and undo the damage his agents have caused. What follows is a tangle of poisoned wells, hidden documents, and dangerous errands deep within the quarantined city.

Requirements

  • Completion of Plague City

Items needed

  • No items are required to start; ingredients and props are collected during the quest.

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison potion

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Edmond in East Ardougne to learn what needs to be done following the events of Plague City.
  2. Head back into West Ardougne and speak to Elena about the next steps in exposing King Lathas.
  3. Investigate reports that the city's well has been deliberately poisoned to keep residents weak and compliant.
  4. Gather the materials needed to prepare an antidote or countermeasure for the poisoned water supply.
  5. Sneak into a restricted area to search for documents proving the plague story was fabricated.
  6. Deal with hostile ghasts and guards while exploring the more dangerous sections of the city.
  7. Deliver the antidote ingredients to the right person so the well can be treated.
  8. Collect the incriminating evidence you find and prepare to smuggle it out of West Ardougne.
  9. Escape the city with the evidence intact, avoiding King Lathas's agents along the way.
  10. Report back to Edmond and Elena with what you have uncovered to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • Herblore experience
  • Access to key areas of West Ardougne
  • Progression toward the Underground Pass storyline
