Witch's Potion

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Witch's Potion
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
0
Start point
Speak to Hetty, the witch who lives in a small house south of Rimmington.

Hetty the witch is brewing something in her cramped little cottage south of Rimmington, and she needs a helping hand gathering the decidedly unpleasant ingredients her recipe calls for. It is a simple errand on paper, though rounding up onions, rat tails, and burnt meat proves a stranger task than it sounds.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • An onion
  • A rat's tail
  • An eye of newt
  • A piece of burnt meat
  • A vial of vinegar

Walkthrough

  1. Visit Hetty's house south of Rimmington and speak with her about what she is brewing.
  2. Agree to help her gather the ingredients her potion requires.
  3. Pick up or buy an onion from a nearby farm or general store.
  4. Track down a rat and obtain a rat's tail from it.
  5. Find and obtain an eye of newt for the recipe.
  6. Cook a piece of meat until it burns to get the burnt meat Hetty needs.
  7. Obtain a vial of vinegar to round out the list of ingredients.
  8. Return to Hetty's cottage with all five ingredients in hand.
  9. Hand the ingredients over and watch as she brews her concoction.
  10. Agree to help her test whether the potion works as intended.

Rewards

  • No quest points
  • A small amount of coins
  • A bit of minor experience as thanks for your help
