Hetty offers to make a potion that will bring out your darker side and improve your magical power. She needs four simple ingredients: an onion, an eye of newt, a rat's tail and a piece of burnt meat. Gather the set, let Hetty brew it, then drink from her cauldron to finish the quest.

September 2004 date lock: Witch's Potion was still a normal free-to-play quest at the target date and awarded a Quest Point. It was not removed from the Quest List until 24 June 2010. The period Jagex quest manual describes the mission length as Short; later modern difficulty/length labels are not projected backward into this guide.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • No skill requirements.
  • No prerequisite quests and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No meaningful combat requirement beyond killing an ordinary rat for the quest-specific tail.

Required items

  • 1 onion — consumed by Hetty.
  • 1 eye of newt — consumed by Hetty.
  • 1 piece of burnt meat — consumed by Hetty.
  • 1 rat's tail — consumed by Hetty. This is a quest item obtained from a rat only after Witch's Potion has been started.

Recommended preparation

  • You may bring the onion, eye of newt and burnt meat before speaking to Hetty. Only the rat's tail has to be obtained after the quest starts.
  • A February-2004 RuneScape 2 route records an eye of newt for 3 coins at Betty's Magic Emporium in Port Sarim and raw beef for 1 coin at the nearby food shop. These are convenient period routes rather than mandatory purchases.
  • Leave enough inventory space to carry all four ingredients together when returning to Hetty.

Before you start

Hetty lives in Rimmington, west of Port Sarim. The four ingredients are all available nearby, so the quest needs no long journey.

If you want to prepare first, collect the onion, eye of newt and burnt meat before talking to Hetty. Do not spend time trying to obtain the rat's tail in advance: build 254 only allows the special tail to drop while Witch's Potion is active, matching the period route's instruction to start the quest first.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the quest with Hetty

  1. Go to Hetty's house in Rimmington and talk to her.
  2. Ask her for a quest and agree to let her help improve your magic or show you the darker arts.
  3. Hetty asks for four ingredients: an eye of newt, a rat's tail, an onion and a piece of burnt meat.

2. Obtain an eye of newt

  1. Travel east to Port Sarim.
  2. Buy an eye of newt from Betty's Magic Emporium. The surviving February-2004 route records a price of 3 coins.
  3. If you already brought an eye of newt, you can skip this step.

3. Obtain an onion

  1. Pick an onion from the field west of Rimmington, near the route toward Melzar's Maze.
  2. Only one onion is needed.

4. Make a piece of burnt meat

  1. Obtain a piece of raw meat. The period route suggests raw beef from the Port Sarim food shop or meat from a nearby animal.
  2. Cook the meat on a range or fire.
  3. If it cooks successfully instead of burning, use the cooked meat on the range again. The period route specifically uses this second cooking attempt to produce burnt meat.
  4. Keep one piece of burnt meat for Hetty.

5. Obtain the rat's tail after starting the quest

  1. Find an ordinary rat in or around Rimmington after Hetty has started the quest.
  2. Kill it and pick up the rat's tail.
  3. If a tail does not appear in build 254, check that you do not already have one in your inventory or bank; the implementation deliberately prevents extra quest tails from dropping while one is already owned.

Combat encounter

  • The only required fight is against an ordinary rat for the rat's tail.
  • No exact September-2004 rat combat level is asserted here because it is not needed to complete or prepare for the quest.
  • Build 254 attaches the rat's-tail drop to the player who receives the rat kill and only while Witch's Potion is active.

6. Give Hetty all four ingredients

  1. Return to Hetty with the onion, eye of newt, rat's tail and burnt meat in your inventory at the same time.
  2. Talk to her. She takes exactly one of each ingredient and puts them into the cauldron.
  3. Hetty chants over the mixture and tells you to drink it.

7. Drink from Hetty's cauldron

  1. Use the Drink-from option on the cauldron in Hetty's house.
  2. You drink the potion and feel yourself imbued with power.
  3. The quest completes immediately. There is no final return dialogue or additional hand-in.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Missing only the rat's tail? Kill another ordinary rat while the quest is active. In build 254 a tail can drop while the quest is at the ingredient-gathering stage as long as you do not already own one in your inventory or bank.
  • Some ingredients are in the bank? Withdraw them. Hetty's build-254 hand-in checks the carried inventory and requires all four together.
  • No partial hand-in: Hetty does not take individual ingredients early. If anything is missing, she leaves the items you do have untouched and repeats the full list.
  • Already handed the ingredients over? You do not need another set. Once Hetty has brewed the potion, simply drink from the cauldron.
  • Drinking too early? Build 254 refuses the cauldron interaction until Hetty has received all four ingredients.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 325 Magic XP.

There is no coin or item reward and no separate permanent unlock. The reward is the Magic experience and Quest Point.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 uses not started → ingredient gathering → ingredients given → complete.
  • Ingredient hand-in: Hetty checks for one burnt_meat, one rats_tail, one eye_of_newt and one onion in the carried inventory. If all four are present she deletes exactly one of each; otherwise nothing is partially consumed.
  • Rat's-tail rule: the rat death script only adds the quest tail while Witch's Potion is active. It also checks both inventory and bank and suppresses the drop if the player already owns a tail.
  • Cauldron: the Drink-from interaction only queues completion after the ingredient hand-in state. Before that, the player declines to drink it.
  • Reward: the completion script advances Magic by 3,250 internal tenths, which is 325 Magic XP, and ^hetty_questpoints is 1.
  • Historical agreement: the fixed 325-Magic-XP build reward agrees with the surviving February-2004 RuneScape 2 guide. RuneScape Classic's older level-dependent reward formula is therefore not substituted into this September-2004 RS2 guide.
  • Source-provenance caution: the reconstructed build journal cites later video/Old School material for wording. Exact journal phrasing is not treated as independent 2004 historical evidence; the quest mechanics and numerical reward above are cross-checked separately.
