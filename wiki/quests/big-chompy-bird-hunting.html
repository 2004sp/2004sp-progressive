Big Chompy Bird Hunting

Quest guide · September 2004 target · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Big Chompy Bird Hunting

Rantz wants to feed his family a Chompy bird, but he cannot make the special ogre arrows needed for the hunt. You must make his arrows, learn how the ogres use bloated swamp toads as bait, shoot a Chompy yourself, then cook it to the exact tastes of Rantz, Bugs and Fycie.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • Level 5 Fletching.
  • Level 30 Cooking.
  • Level 30 Ranged.

Required items

  • 1 axe — not consumed; used to cut Achey trees. Achey logs can be obtained beside Rantz during the quest.
  • 1 knife — not consumed; used to cut Achey logs into ogre arrow shafts.
  • 1 chisel — not consumed; used on wolf bones to make wolfbone arrowtips.
  • Feathers — four are used for each ogre arrow in build 254. Rantz takes six arrows and you need more for the hunt; 50–100 feathers is a sensible supply.
  • Wolf bones — each bone produces a variable number of arrowtips. Bring about 4–5, or obtain them from wolves in the area.
  • Three seasoning ingredients — the exact order is chosen during the quest. The possible ingredients are onion, potato, cabbage, tomato, equa leaves and doogle leaves; all can be collected in the surrounding ogre area.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Ogre bellows, from the chest in Rantz's cave.
  • Bloated toads, made by filling the bellows with swamp gas and using them on swamp toads.
  • Ogre bow, lent to you by Rantz after he repeatedly misses a Chompy.
  • Raw chompy, obtained by plucking the bird you kill.

Recommended

  • Food, armour and a weapon if you intend to obtain wolf bones from the nearby wolves.
  • Make at least 10–12 ogre arrows. Six are handed to Rantz, while the extras prevent a missed shot or lost arrow from forcing another supply run.
  • Leave several inventory spaces free for arrow components, the bellows, bloated toads, raw chompy and seasonings.

Before you start

From Yanille, travel south-east toward Gu'Tanoth and continue into the eastern Feldip Hills. Rantz is outside his cave, north of the nearby swamp pool. This route is appropriate to the September 2004 target; later conveniences such as Castle Wars and fairy-ring travel are not part of this guide.

If you already have wolf bones, feathers, a knife and a chisel, bring them. Otherwise, most of the quest's materials can be gathered locally. The nearby ogres and wolves are the main danger; the Chompy itself is a hunting target rather than a normal combat encounter.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start the quest with Rantz. Speak to Rantz and agree to make his “stabbers”, meaning ogre arrows. He explains that the shafts come from Achey trees and the tips are made from wolf bones.
  2. Make the ogre arrow shafts. Chop several Achey trees just south of Rantz. Use your knife on the Achey logs to cut them into ogre arrow shafts. Because the number of shafts produced is variable, keep cutting logs until you have enough for at least 10 arrows.
  3. Make the wolfbone arrowtips. Obtain wolf bones, either by bringing them with you or killing wolves in the surrounding area. Use your chisel on the wolf bones to make wolfbone arrowtips. The number of tips per bone is variable.
  4. Finish the arrows. Add feathers to the ogre arrow shafts, then combine the flighted shafts with wolfbone arrowtips. Keep at least 10–12 finished ogre arrows if possible.
  5. Give Rantz six arrows. Return to Rantz with the arrows you made yourself. He takes six. Continue through his questions about attracting Chompy birds; make sure you ask how the Chompies are made to come so the conversation reaches the “fatsy toadies” and his children playing with the bellows.
  6. Get the ogre bellows. Enter Rantz's cave just north of him. The chest is in the north-west part of the cave. Remove the heavy rock, open the chest and search it to take the ogre bellows. If the rock defeats your lifting attempt, try again; in build 254 a failed attempt can temporarily reduce Strength by 1, but there is no listed Strength level requirement for the quest.
  7. Fill the bellows with swamp gas. Leave the cave and head to the swamp west/south-west of Rantz. Use the empty ogre bellows on the swamp bubbles. A full set of bellows carries three loads of gas.
  8. Make bloated toads. Use the gas-filled bellows on swamp toads. Each successful use catches a bloated toad in your inventory and consumes one load. You can carry only three bloated toads at once, so three is a good batch.
  9. Show Rantz the bait. Return to Rantz with a bloated toad. He approves and points out the hunting clearing immediately south of him.
  10. Let Rantz try the first shot. Place a bloated toad in the clearing Rantz indicated. A Chompy may be attracted to it; if the bait fails or is lost, place another. Rantz eventually tries to shoot the bird and misses.
  11. Borrow the ogre bow. Speak to Rantz after his failed shot. Choose the option asking to have a go, then insist that you are strong enough to try. Rantz lends you an ogre bow.
  12. Hunt your Chompy. Equip the ogre bow and ogre arrows. If necessary, inflate more toads, then place another bloated toad in the hunting clearing. When a Chompy appears, attack it with the ogre bow. In build 254 the quest Chompy does not fight back, and ordinary melee weapons or other ranged weapons cannot replace the ogre bow for this kill.
  13. Pluck the carcass. Once the Chompy is dead, choose Pluck on its carcass. Raw chompy and bones are dropped on the ground, while feathers are also obtained. Pick up the raw chompy before leaving.
  14. Get the family's food orders. Show the raw chompy to Rantz. He asks you to cook it and gives his requested seasoning. Speak to Bugs and Fycie as well and note their individual requests. The three requests use the six possible ingredients: onion, potato, cabbage, tomato, equa leaves and doogle leaves.
  15. Collect the three seasonings. Onions and tomatoes can be found west of the swamp; doogle leaves are farther west; cabbages occur around the swamp/equa-leaf area; potatoes and equa leaves can be found south and east of Rantz near the shore. You need only the three ingredients requested in your dialogue.
  16. Cook the seasoned Chompy. Return to the spit-roast between Rantz and the cave. Keep the raw chompy and all three requested ingredients in your inventory, then use the raw chompy on the spit. If the cooking attempt burns, obtain another raw chompy and another set of the consumed seasonings, then try again.
  17. Finish the quest. Give the successfully seasoned chompy to Rantz. He and his family eat it and the quest completes.

Combat and failure notes

  • Wolves: only necessary if you need to obtain wolf bones locally. Their exact combat level is omitted here because period and later sources disagree after combat-system changes; bring suitable food and combat gear if fighting them.
  • Chompy bird: this is a ranged hunting encounter. Use the ogre bow with ogre arrows. The bird does not retaliate in build 254.
  • Lost ogre bow: Rantz can replace it after he has first lent it to you. See the build note below for the price difference in 2004scape.
  • Lost/failed bait: refill the ogre bellows at the swamp bubbles and inflate more toads. Nothing forces you to restart the quest.
  • Burnt Chompy: hunt and pluck another bird, recollect the three requested ingredients, and repeat the cooking attempt.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 262 Fletching XP.
  • 1,470 Cooking XP.
  • 735 Ranged XP.
  • An ogre bow.
  • The ability to continue making ogre arrows.
  • The ability to hunt and cook Chompy birds after the quest.

Post-quest Chompy hunting

Chompy hunting remains available after the quest. Your kill total is recorded and can be checked from the ogre bow. On 20 July 2004, before this wiki's 7 September cutoff, Jagex added Chompy Bird hats: Rantz gives hats for the Ogre Bowman rank and higher when you show him the notches on your bow. Build 254 implements the full rank ladder, beginning with the Ogre Bowman hat at 30 kills and ending with Expert Dragon Archer at 4,000 kills.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Extra Crafting gate: build 254 currently requires level 5 Crafting when chiselling wolf bones and also awards Crafting XP for that action. Contemporary 2004 quest guides list only 5 Fletching, 30 Cooking and 30 Ranged, and period-preserved descriptions of wolfbone arrowtips identify 5 Fletching rather than 5 Crafting. Treat 5 Crafting as a server-specific extra requirement.
  • Replacement bow price: a contemporary June 2004 guide records a replacement ogre bow from Rantz at 500 coins. Build 254 instead chooses a price from 500–550 coins.
  • 4,000-kill XP: build 254 awards 30,000 Ranged XP on the 4,000th post-quest Chompy kill. The July 2004 Jagex update verifies the hat system before the cutoff, but the audit did not find equally strong pre-7-September evidence for that 30,000-XP award, so it is not presented above as a historical 2004 reward.
