Big Chompy Bird Hunting

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Big Chompy Bird Hunting
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Medium
Quest points
1
Start point
Find Rantz, an ogre wandering near the Feldip Hills west of Castle Wars, and speak to him.

Rantz the ogre has taken a shine to you and offers to teach you the ogre way of hunting the region's prized Chompy birds. It is a task that demands patience as much as skill: crafting your own ogre-style arrows, disguising yourself to blend into the swamp, and lying in wait for a chompy to wander close enough to shoot.

Requirements

  • Level 30 Ranged is recommended, though not strictly enforced
  • Some Fletching ability is useful for making ogre arrows

Items needed

  • Logs and feathers to fletch ogre arrows
  • A knife for fletching
  • Materials to construct camouflage gear

Recommended

  • Food
  • Spare arrows in case some are lost

Walkthrough

  1. Track down Rantz near the Feldip Hills and ask him about hunting Chompy birds.
  2. Agree to prove yourself by learning the ogre method of the hunt.
  3. Obtain a comp ogre bow and fletch a supply of ogre arrows from logs and feathers using a knife.
  4. Gather the materials Rantz describes for a set of camouflage gear to keep you hidden from the birds.
  5. Wear the full camouflage outfit once it is assembled.
  6. Head to the designated hunting ground and dig yourself a hide to crouch in.
  7. Wait patiently inside the hide for a Chompy bird to wander within range.
  8. Fire your ogre arrows at passing Chompy birds using your comp ogre bow.
  9. Collect proof of a successful kill once you down a chompy.
  10. Return to Rantz and show him the results of your hunt.
  11. Listen to Rantz's assessment of your technique and hunt further chompies if he asks for more proof.
  12. Claim your reward once Rantz is satisfied you have mastered the hunt.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Ranged experience
  • The right to trade Rantz for ogre-related gear
  • Access to further optional Chompy bird hunts for cosmetic hats
