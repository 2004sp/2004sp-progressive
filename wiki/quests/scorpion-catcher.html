Thormac has accidentally let three rare lesser Kharid scorpions escape from their cage. They have scattered into three awkward places: deep inside Taverley Dungeon, the Barbarian Outpost, and the upper floor of the Monastery near the Dwarven Mine. Recover all three in Thormac's scorpion cage and he will reward you with Strength experience and the ability to have battlestaves improved into mystic staves.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • 31 Prayer. This is needed to join the Monastery's order and climb to the upper floor where the third Kharid Scorpion is found. Build 254 checks your base Prayer level at this gate.
  • Alfred Grimhand's Bar Crawl. The September-2004 route requires completion to enter the Barbarian Outpost normally and reach the second scorpion.
  • No quest prerequisite. In particular, Monk's Friend is unrelated to Scorpion Catcher.
  • Ability to survive deep Taverley Dungeon. The official 2003 quest manual lists going deep into the members' dungeon as a minimum requirement.

Required items

  • Scorpion cage — supplied by Thormac when you accept the quest. Use the cage on each Kharid Scorpion; do not simply choose Pick-up.
  • Dusty key for the period Taverley Dungeon route. You may bring one you already own or obtain one in the dungeon by freeing Velrak the explorer.
  • Jail key only if obtaining the Dusty key during this trip. The Jailer in the Black Knights' area drops it; use it to open Velrak's cell.

Recommended

  • Armour, a weapon and food for the Taverley Dungeon run.
  • Anti-dragon shield for passing the Blue Dragons.
  • Antipoison for the Poison Spider section.
  • Teleport runes or other September-2004 travel methods for the repeated trips between Seers' Village, Taverley, the Barbarian areas and the Monastery.
  • If you have not yet completed Alfred Grimhand's Bar Crawl, bring the coins and supplies listed in the Bar Crawl guide.

Before you start

The three quest scorpions are not combat targets. Thormac's cage records which individual scorpions you have caught and eventually becomes a full cage containing all three. Build 254 supports catching them in different orders, although its clue and access scripts make the route below the clearest intended progression.

The most dangerous part is the first scorpion. The 2 August 2004 LostHQ guide directs players through the Blue Dragons, Black Demons and Poison Spiders of Taverley Dungeon and recommends armour, food, an anti-dragon shield and antipoison. If you already have a Dusty key, you can skip the Jailer/Velrak detour.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start with Thormac and take the cage

  1. Go to the Sorcerer's Tower south of Seers' Village and climb to Thormac.
  2. Ask what assistance he needs. Thormac explains that he left his cage open and lost three lesser Kharid scorpions.
  3. Ask how to catch them. Thormac gives you a Scorpion cage.
  4. Agree to help. He tells you that the Seers in Seers' Village can locate the missing animals.

If you lose every copy of your scorpion cage and do not have one in the bank, Thormac can replace it while the quest is in progress.

2. Ask a Seer for the first scorpion

  1. Travel north to Seers' Village and speak to a Seer.
  2. Say that you need to locate some scorpions or mention that Thormac sent you.
  3. The Seer looks into a mirror and describes a scorpion near nasty spiders and two coffins, disappearing through a crack into a secret room.
  4. In build 254 this conversation is important: it advances the quest to the first-hint stage, and the hidden wall in Taverley Dungeon will otherwise behave as an ordinary wall.

3. First Kharid Scorpion — Taverley Dungeon

  1. Prepare for a deep dungeon run and enter the Taverley members' dungeon.
  2. If you already have a Dusty key, keep it with you and continue along the normal deep-dungeon route. Otherwise, head toward the Black Knights' jail.
  3. If you need the key: kill the Jailer and take his Jail key. Unlock Velrak the explorer's cell, talk to him about somewhere good to explore, and accept the Dusty key he offers.
  4. Return to the main route, pass the Lesser Demons and use the Dusty key on the locked deep-dungeon door.
  5. Continue past the Blue Dragons, then the Black Demons, and reach the Poison Spiders.
  6. Near the spiders and coffins, find the suspicious wall. After the Seer's first clue, opening it reveals the secret room.
  7. Inside, use the Scorpion cage on the Kharid Scorpion. The cage changes to record the captured scorpion.

4. Return to the Seer for the other clues

  1. Return to a Seer in Seers' Village after catching the Taverley scorpion.
  2. The Seer describes another Kharid Scorpion in an eastern village surrounded by uncivilised-looking warriors, where a merchant picked it up.
  3. The Seer also gives the final clue: a scorpion is in an upstairs room with brown clothing lying on a table.

A contemporary August 2004 guide notes that you do not need to keep returning to the Seer after every capture in RuneScape 2. Build 254 nevertheless contains the full clue chain, and this return cleanly unlocks the dialogue that points toward the second scorpion.

5. Follow the merchant clue to the Barbarian Outpost

  1. For the full build-254 clue route, go to Barbarian Village and speak to Peksa, the helmet merchant.
  2. Ask about the small scorpion. Peksa says he no longer has it: he gave it to his brother Ivor at the outpost north-west of Camelot.
  3. If you have not already done so, complete Alfred Grimhand's Bar Crawl so the Barbarian Outpost guard recognises you as a barbarian.
  4. Enter the Barbarian Outpost. The period guide places the scorpion in the building immediately inside/in front of the gate.
  5. Find the Kharid Scorpion and use your Scorpion cage on it.

6. Third Kharid Scorpion — Monastery upper floor

  1. Travel to the Monastery near the Dwarven Mine, north of Falador.
  2. Try to climb the ladder. If you have not joined the order, speak to Abbot Langley and ask to join.
  3. You need a base Prayer level of 31. With 31 Prayer, the Abbot accepts you and you may climb the ladder to the upper floor.
  4. Find the final Kharid Scorpion upstairs, near the area matching the Seer's clue, and use the cage on it.
  5. Your cage should now contain all three scorpions.

7. Return the full cage to Thormac

  1. Return to Thormac at the Sorcerer's Tower with the full Scorpion cage in your inventory.
  2. Speak to him. Your character says that all of his scorpions have been retrieved.
  3. Thormac takes the full cage, thanks you, and the quest completes.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Use the cage, do not bare-hand Pick-up. Build 254's ordinary Pick-up option makes the Kharid Scorpion sting you for a fixed 3 damage. Using the cage on it safely performs the capture.
  • Lost the cage? If no scorpion-cage variant exists in your inventory or bank, tell Thormac you have lost it. He gives a fresh empty cage if you have a free inventory slot.
  • Full cage banked? Thormac will not complete the quest from the bank. He tells you to fetch the scorpions, so withdraw the full cage first.
  • Taverley wall will not open? Speak to a Seer after starting the quest. Build 254 requires the first-hint quest state before the scorpion wall reveals its secret door.
  • Cannot climb the Monastery ladder? Speak to Abbot Langley and join the order. Build 254 checks base Prayer 31, so a temporary boost does not satisfy this script.
  • Barbarian Outpost: the intended September-2004 route requires the Bar Crawl. Build 254 has a known permissive gate-handler divergence documented in the Bar Crawl guide; do the Bar Crawl anyway so the permanent state is correct.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 6,625 Strength XP.
  • Thormac's battlestaff-enchanting service. After completion, Thormac can improve an eligible battlestaff into its mystic-staff form for 40,000 coins per staff.
  • No coin payout and no separate item reward. The full scorpion cage is removed when the quest completes.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 uses not started (0), started (1), first Seer hint (2), second-hint/clue stage (3), and complete (6). The three captured scorpions themselves are tracked by which cage object variant you carry rather than by three separate quest-state numbers.
  • Cage combinations: build 254 defines empty, every one-scorpion combination, every two-scorpion combination, and a full three-scorpion cage. This lets the capture script preserve progress even if the scorpions are caught in a nonstandard order.
  • Scorpion interaction: each quest Kharid Scorpion has a normal Pick-up option, but that path only stings the player for 3 damage. Using the appropriate cage object on the NPC deletes that scorpion and replaces the cage with the next progress variant.
  • First clue gate: the Taverley secret-wall handler checks that Scorpion Catcher has reached at least the first-hint stage. Before that it only reports that the wall looks like a wall.
  • Monastery gate: the Prayer Guild ladder uses a persistent membership flag. Joining through Abbot Langley requires stat_base(prayer) >= 31, matching the period quest requirement.
  • Bar Crawl start check: Thormac's script contains a commented-out attempt to require a prior Bar Crawl completion before even starting Scorpion Catcher. That check is disabled. Historically the Bar Crawl is needed for the second scorpion's outpost access, not as a gate on speaking to Thormac.
  • Outpost divergence: as documented on the Bar Crawl page, build 254's gate handler appears more permissive than the intended period access rule. The historical route and permanent Bar Crawl state remain the guide's canonical instructions.
  • Completion: Thormac requires the full cage in your inventory, deletes it, sets the quest complete, awards 1 Quest Point, and advances Strength by 6,625 XP.
  • Post-quest service: Thormac's build-254 dialogue charges 40,000 coins plus the battlestaff being enchanted and returns the corresponding mystic staff.
  • Journal evidence: the build journal itself lists 31 Prayer, Thormac at the Sorcerer's Tower, the Taverley secret-room clue, the warrior-village/merchant clue, and the upstairs-room/brown-clothing clue. Some journal comments cite later screenshots or videos, so exact reconstructed wording is not treated as primary September-2004 evidence.
