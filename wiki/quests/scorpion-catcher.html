Scorpion Catcher

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Scorpion Catcher
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Brother Kojo at the Monastery south of Ardougne.

The monks of the secluded Monastery are in distress: something has gone badly wrong with one of their sacred rituals involving the desert scorpions, and their holy symbol has been lost as a result. Brother Kojo needs someone brave enough to venture into scorpion-infested territory to set things right.

Requirements

  • Completion of Monk's Friend

Items needed

  • A weapon and armour suitable for fighting scorpions

Recommended

  • Antipoison potions
  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Visit the Monastery south of Ardougne and speak to Brother Kojo.
  2. Learn about the monastery's missing sacred item and its connection to the scorpions nearby.
  3. Head out to the area where the scorpions are known to gather.
  4. Search the surrounding terrain for clues to where the item ended up.
  5. Fight off the scorpions guarding the location, being wary of their poisonous stings.
  6. Retrieve the special charm or item needed to safely handle the situation.
  7. Use what you've found to deal with the largest, most dangerous scorpion guarding the sacred symbol.
  8. Recover the monastery's lost item once the scorpion threat is dealt with.
  9. Return the item to Brother Kojo at the Monastery.
  10. Speak to Brother Kojo once more to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Combat experience
  • Coins
  • Improved standing with the monks of the Monastery
