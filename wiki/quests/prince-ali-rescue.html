Prince Ali Rescue

Quest guide · September 2004 target · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Prince Ali Rescue
Access
Free-to-play
Released
28 February 2001
Period length
Long
Quest points
3
Start
Hassan, southern room of Al Kharid Palace
Requirements
None
Combat
No kill required; hostile jail guards are a danger

Prince Ali of Al Kharid has been kidnapped by Lady Keli. Chancellor Hassan hires you to help, while spymaster Osman and his daughter Leela devise a rescue: copy Keli's cell key, disguise the prince as his captor, distract the door guard and get Ali out before the gang realises what has happened.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None. The quest has no hard skill-level or combat-level requirement.

Required items

Everything can be obtained while the quest is underway, but collecting the materials in advance saves a large amount of walking.

  • 1 Soft clay — consumed when taking an imprint of Lady Keli's key.
  • 1 Bronze bar — given to Osman with the key imprint.
  • 1 Pink skirt — used as part of Prince Ali's disguise.
  • 3 Balls of wool — Ned turns these into the wig.
  • 1 Yellow dye — used on the wig. If you make it through Aggie, bring 2 onions and 5 coins.
  • 1 Ashes, 1 redberries, 1 pot of flour and 1 jug or bucket of water — Aggie consumes these to make the skin-coloured paste.
  • 1 Rope — consumed when Lady Keli is tied up. Ned can sell one for 15 coins or make one from 4 balls of wool.
  • 3 ordinary beers — Joe drinks all three during the distraction.

If you make both the wig and rope from wool, you need 7 balls of wool total. Hassan can also supply a jug of water near the beginning of the quest, so you do not necessarily need to bring water to Al Kharid.

Recommended

  • A little spare cash. Five coins are needed if Aggie makes your yellow dye and fifteen more can buy the rope from Ned. Before the rescue is complete, the Al Kharid toll gate normally costs 10 coins per crossing.
  • Food and light combat equipment if your combat level is low. You never need to kill a guard, but the jail area contains hostile guards.
  • Several free inventory spaces. The rescue requires the copied key, blonde wig, pink skirt and paste to be carried together when you give the disguise to Ali.

Before you start

This quest involves repeated travel between Al Kharid and Draynor Village. If you are preparing everything first, gather the ordinary materials before speaking to Hassan: soft clay, a bronze bar, a pink skirt, three beers, three balls of wool, and the ingredients for yellow dye and skin paste. Add either a rope, four more balls of wool, or 15 coins for Ned.

No fight is part of the quest objective. The danger comes from the jail guards around Lady Keli's compound. Do not linger there unnecessarily if they can threaten you.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Hassan and Osman

  1. Go to Al Kharid Palace and speak to Hassan in the southern room.
  2. Choose the option offering to help. Hassan says he needs someone's services and sends you to Osman, the spymaster.
  3. If you still need water for the paste, speak to Hassan again and complain about the heat. In build 254, if you have inventory space he hands you a jug of water.
  4. Find Osman just outside the palace and tell him Hassan sent you for instructions.
  5. Ask about both parts of the plan. Prince Ali is being held by Lady Keli near Draynor Village. Osman wants you to copy the cell key and assemble a disguise resembling Keli.
  6. The disguise needs a blonde wig, pink skirt and skin-coloured paste. You will also need rope to restrain Keli.
  7. For the key, Osman tells you to take an imprint of Keli's key in soft clay, then bring that imprint and a bronze bar back to him.

Build-254 progression note: finish Osman's instruction conversation with the response about going to find the items. That is the branch which advances the quest to the preparation stage.

2. Speak to Leela near Draynor

  1. Travel to Draynor Village and find Leela near the fields east of the village, close to Lady Keli's jail compound.
  2. Tell her you are helping free the prince. She confirms the plan and can repeat the requirements for the disguise, key and guard distraction.
  3. You can complete the disguise and key tasks in either order.

3. Make the blonde wig

  1. Speak to Ned in Draynor Village and ask whether he can make something else from wool.
  2. Ask for a wig and give him 3 balls of wool. He returns a plain grey woollen wig.
  3. If you do not already have yellow dye, speak to Aggie in Draynor Village. She makes one bottle from 2 onions and 5 coins.
  4. Use the yellow dye on the wig to turn it blonde.

4. Make the skin-coloured paste and obtain the rope

  1. Take 1 ashes, 1 redberries, 1 pot of flour and 1 jug or bucket of water to Aggie.
  2. Ask whether she can make skin paste. She consumes those four ingredients and gives you the paste needed for Ali's disguise.
  3. Make sure you also have a pink skirt.
  4. Obtain a rope. Ned can sell one for 15 coins, or he can make one from 4 balls of wool.

5. Copy Lady Keli's key

  1. Take soft clay to Lady Keli at the jail compound east of Draynor Village.
  2. Flatter her and steer the conversation toward her latest plan. Ask whether she can be sure nobody will rescue the prisoner.
  3. Keli boasts that the only cell key is on a chain around her neck. Ask to see the key.
  4. With soft clay in your inventory, ask to touch the key for a moment. You secretly press it into the clay, consuming the soft clay and receiving a key print.
  5. Return to Osman in Al Kharid with the key print and 1 bronze bar. He takes both and has a duplicate key made.
  6. Return to Leela near Draynor. She gives you the copied bronze key for Prince Ali's cell.

6. Prepare the final rescue

  1. Before the final sequence, make sure you are carrying the bronze key, blonde wig, pink skirt and paste. Also bring the rope and all 3 beers.
  2. Speak to Leela again with the copied key and the three disguise pieces. In build 254 this advances the quest to the stage where Joe can be dealt with.
  3. Enter the jail area carefully. The ordinary jail guards are hostile in build 254 and are not part of the quest objective.

7. Get Joe drunk

  1. Speak to Joe, Lady Keli's door guard, while carrying 3 beers.
  2. Offer him a beer. He drinks the first one.
  3. Continue the same conversation. You hand him the other 2 beers, and after all three he becomes too drunk to interfere with the rescue.

Important: carry all three beers before starting this exchange. In build 254, giving only one beer does not advance the quest; the two remaining beers need to be available immediately in the same sequence.

8. Tie up Lady Keli and free Prince Ali

  1. Use the rope on Lady Keli. The rope is consumed and Keli is restrained.
  2. Use the copied bronze key on Prince Ali's locked cell door.
  3. Speak to Prince Ali while carrying the bronze key, blonde wig, pink skirt and paste.
  4. Ali takes the key and all three disguise pieces, disguises himself as Lady Keli and escapes to meet Leela.
  5. You do not escort Ali across the map and you do not fight Lady Keli. Once he has accepted the disguise, the rescue itself is finished.

9. Return to Hassan

  1. Travel back to Al Kharid Palace.
  2. Speak to Hassan. He thanks you for rescuing the Emir's son and pays 700 coins.
  3. The quest completes with 3 Quest Points.

Combat and danger

  • No enemy must be defeated. The intended solution is stealth, disguise and distraction.
  • Jail guards are the main risk. Contemporary 2004 guide material warned players not to idle around the jail because the guards can kill an unprepared character.
  • Build-254 value: the generic Jail guard used around the compound displays combat level 26 and is configured as aggressive melee. The config comments say its exact stats/level were matched to later OSRS data, so level 26 is presented here as an implementation value rather than independent proof of the exact September-2004 display.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost copied key: build 254 can replace it before the rescue. Leela charges 15 coins if the claimed key is absent from both inventory and bank; Osman also has a route for arranging another key.
  • Keep the four final rescue items together: Prince Ali checks for the copied key, blonde wig, pink skirt and paste at the same time. Missing any one prevents the escape.
  • The rope is separate: it is consumed when you tie Keli and is therefore no longer present when Ali checks the disguise.
  • The beers are separate: Joe consumes all three before Keli can be tied.
  • Free toll passage begins as soon as Ali escapes in build 254. The final 700-coin payment and quest-complete screen still require returning to Hassan.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • 700 coins.
  • Permanent free passage through the Al Kharid toll gate. The normal gate toll is 10 coins in build 254.
  • No skill XP reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: Hassan starts the quest, Osman advances the instruction stage, Leela recognises the assembled equipment, Joe advances the drunk-guard stage, rope on Keli advances the restraint stage, and Prince Ali's escape advances the saved stage. Hassan then queues completion.
  • Osman dialogue: in build 254 the quest only reaches the post-Osman preparation state after the player finishes his instructions with the branch about going to find the items.
  • Paste recipe: Aggie requires exactly one redberries item, one pot of flour, one ashes and either a jug or bucket of water. No coins are charged for the paste itself.
  • Wig: Ned consumes exactly 3 balls of wool for the plain wig; yellow dye and the plain wig are then consumed to create the blonde wig.
  • Rope: Ned accepts either 15 coins or 4 balls of wool for one rope.
  • Key: soft clay becomes a key print through Lady Keli's dialogue; Osman consumes the print and one bronze bar; Leela supplies the copied bronze key.
  • Joe: the script consumes one beer first and then two more before setting the drunk-guard state, confirming the three-beer requirement.
  • Ali's escape: the prince consumes the copied key, blonde wig, pink skirt and paste. No escort or combat stage follows.
  • Reward: Hassan's completion queue adds exactly 700 coins; `^prince_questpoints` is 3; no stat-advance call awards skill XP.
  • Toll-gate unlock: the Al Kharid border-gate script waives its normal 10-coin toll whenever the Prince Ali quest state is at least the saved state, so build 254 grants free passage immediately after the prince gets away rather than waiting for Hassan's final dialogue.
  • Journal naming: build 254's journal header calls the quest The Rescue of Prince Ali, while the period public quest name used by Jagex's manual and contemporary guides is Prince Ali Rescue.
