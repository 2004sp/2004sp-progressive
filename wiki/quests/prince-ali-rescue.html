Prince Ali Rescue

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Prince Ali Rescue
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Medium
Quest points
3
Start point
Ask around about the palace in Al Kharid, or seek out Leela the thief.

Al Kharid's throne has been seized by an impostor, while the rightful Prince Ali languishes somewhere in the palace dungeons below. The city's people are too afraid to act, but a clever thief named Leela has a plan to get someone inside unnoticed. All that's needed is someone willing to take the risk.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Recommended

  • Some spare coins, in case you need to pay for items along the way

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Al Kharid and start asking around about the current state of the palace and its ruler.
  2. Learn that the man currently sitting on the throne is an impostor, and that the rightful heir, Prince Ali, is being held prisoner somewhere within the palace.
  3. Meet Leela, a resourceful thief who's sympathetic to the prince's plight and willing to help you sneak inside.
  4. Follow her advice on how to slip past the palace guards without raising an alarm, using a disguise or distraction she provides.
  5. Make your way carefully through the palace grounds, avoiding direct confrontation with the guards where possible.
  6. Find the entrance down into the dungeons beneath the palace.
  7. Search the cells until you locate Prince Ali, locked away and awaiting rescue.
  8. Free him from his cell, dealing with whatever lock or obstacle is keeping him confined.
  9. Escort him safely back out of the palace and away from danger.
  10. Confront the truth about the impostor Emir and help see Prince Ali restored to his rightful place.
  11. Speak to the relevant parties in Al Kharid once the dust settles to wrap up the quest and claim your reward.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
