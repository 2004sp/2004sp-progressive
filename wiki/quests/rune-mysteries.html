Rune Mysteries

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Rune Mysteries
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Duke Horacio inside Lumbridge Castle.

Duke Horacio has an urgent errand: a sealed research package must be delivered to the wizards at the Wizards' Tower without delay. What starts as a simple courier job quickly draws you into the wizards' investigation of runes and their true origin, leading you to the rune shop in Varrock and, eventually, to a hidden source of magical power few have ever seen.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be bought beforehand; all necessary items are provided during the quest

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Duke Horacio in Lumbridge Castle and agree to deliver his package.
  2. Travel to the Wizards' Tower south of Draynor Village and find the wizard Sedridor in the basement.
  3. Hand over the package and listen as Sedridor explains his research into the origin of runes.
  4. Agree to help him by visiting Aubury, the rune seller in Varrock, to ask about a special talisman.
  5. Speak to Aubury in his shop and learn about the strange air talisman he possesses.
  6. Return to Sedridor with what you learned and offer to investigate further.
  7. Take the talisman Sedridor provides and use it to reach the hidden essence mine.
  8. Collect a sample of the raw rune essence found within the mine.
  9. Bring the essence back to Sedridor at the Wizards' Tower for study.
  10. Listen to his conclusions about the essence and the future of rune creation.
  11. Speak to Sedridor once more to complete the quest and unlock access to the Rune Essence Mine.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Access to the Rune Essence Mine, unlocking the Runecrafting skill
  • An air talisman to keep
