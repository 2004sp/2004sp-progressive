Fishing Contest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Fishing Contest
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to the contest organiser in the pub at Hemenster, east of Ardougne.

The village of Hemenster is holding its annual fishing contest, and the local competitor has a well-earned reputation for winning through less-than-honest means. A suspicious old woman in the area seems to know a secret or two about the sort of bait that could turn the tide in your favour, if you're willing to bend the rules a little yourself.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A fishing rod
  • Normal fishing bait

Recommended

  • Coins in case you need to buy supplies locally

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Hemenster, east of Ardougne, and find the pub hosting the fishing contest.
  2. Speak to the organiser and sign up to compete.
  3. Learn that the reigning champion tends to win through underhanded means.
  4. Seek out the old woman rumoured to live nearby who might be able to even the odds.
  5. Speak to her and ask about any special bait she might have.
  6. Complete whatever small favour or request she asks of you in exchange for her help.
  7. Receive the enchanted or unusual bait from her.
  8. Return to the contest and use the special bait on your fishing rod.
  9. Take part in the contest and reel in your catch.
  10. Speak to the organiser to have your entry judged and the winner declared.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Fishing experience
  • Coins
