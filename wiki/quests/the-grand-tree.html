Start point
Speak to King Narnode Shareen inside the Grand Tree, west of Ardougne.

The Grand Tree, towering home of the gnome civilisation, is in crisis. King Narnode Shareen fears that something is deeply wrong within his kingdom and asks for your help proving your worth before revealing the full extent of the danger threatening his people from within their own ranks.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start; you gather what you need as you complete tasks for the gnomes.

Recommended

  • Food
  • A weapon and armour for the tunnels beneath the tree

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to the Grand Tree, west of Ardougne, and make your way up to speak with King Narnode Shareen.
  2. Agree to prove yourself useful to the gnomes before the king will share what is truly troubling him.
  3. Complete a series of favours for various gnomes around the tree, such as retrieving lost items and delivering messages.
  4. Help repair or assist with the tree's balloon transport system as one of the tasks assigned to you.
  5. Report back to King Narnode as you complete each task to earn his growing trust.
  6. Learn that the king suspects sabotage within his own ranks, pointing toward his advisor Glough.
  7. Investigate Glough's recent activities and notice inconsistencies in his account of events.
  8. Descend into the hidden tunnels beneath the Grand Tree to search for evidence of treachery.
  9. Battle through hostile creatures lurking in the tunnels as you press deeper underground.
  10. Uncover Glough's betrayal and confront him about his secret dealings against the gnome kingdom.
  11. Deal with the threat Glough has unleashed beneath the tree to protect the gnome civilisation.
  12. Return to King Narnode Shareen to report the full truth and conclude the quest.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points
  • Attack, Strength, Defence, or Agility experience (your choice)
  • Access to the gnome glider transport network
  • Access to the Grand Tree area and its shops
