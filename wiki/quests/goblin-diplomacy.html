Goblin Diplomacy

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Goblin Diplomacy
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
5
Start point
Talk to General Bentnoze or General Wartface in the goblin village north of Falador.

Deep in their village north of Falador, two goblin generals have been locked in a petty but heated argument over the 'correct' colour for their troops' armour. Neither side will budge, and the whole village is stuck in limbo until someone settles it. It falls to an outsider, you, to broker peace through paint pots and diplomacy.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A set of goblin mail armour
  • Pots of paint or dye in the colours needed to satisfy both generals

Walkthrough

  1. Venture north of Falador into the goblin village and find the two goblin generals, Bentnoze and Wartface.
  2. Listen to them arguing over what colour their soldiers' armour should be painted.
  3. Agree to help settle the dispute once and for all.
  4. Acquire a full set of goblin mail armour, piece by piece.
  5. Track down pots of paint or dye in a few different colours from shops or crafters.
  6. Bring the armour and dyes back to the goblin village.
  7. Apply the colours to the armour, working out through trial and conversation which combination will satisfy both generals.
  8. Present the finished, correctly-coloured armour to Bentnoze and Wartface.
  9. Watch as the two generals finally agree on a design and call off their feud.
  10. Speak to them once more to formally complete the quest and claim your reward.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
