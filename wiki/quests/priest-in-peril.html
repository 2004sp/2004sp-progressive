King Roald has lost contact with his friend Drezel at the temple guarding Misthalin's eastern border. The first voice you hear at the temple is not Drezel at all: Zamorakian monks have taken over, imprisoned the real priest and trick you into removing the guardian protecting the route toward Morytania. You must undo the damage, free Drezel and restore the blessing of the River Salve.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • Members' content. The quest and the Morytania access it unlocks are members content. Build 254 deliberately allows King Roald to offer the quest on a free world, matching the old start behaviour, but the guide should be completed on a members' world.
  • Rune Mysteries is listed as a required quest by the preserved period RuneHQ/LostHQ guide. Build 254 does not enforce it as a start or completion flag; mechanically it is needed if you intend to mine your own Rune essence rather than obtain it another way.
  • No skill-level requirement.
  • Combat ability to defeat two level-30 enemies. You must kill the Temple guardian and later a level-30 Monk of Zamorak.

Required items

  • 1 bucket. It is filled from the River Salve well, blessed by Drezel and emptied when used on the vampyre's coffin. Bring an ordinary empty bucket.
  • 50 unnoted Rune essence. Drezel consumes all fifty to purify the Salve. You may hand them in over multiple trips. The September-2004 route uses ordinary Rune essence; do not replace this with later Pure essence advice.

Obtained during the quest

  • Golden key from the level-30 Monk of Zamorak.
  • Iron key by exchanging the golden key at the correct monument beneath the temple.
  • Bucket of murky water from the well beneath Paterdomus, then a bucket of blessed water after Drezel blesses it.
  • Wolfbane as the completion reward.

Recommended

  • Weapon, armour and food for the two mandatory level-30 fights and the hostile Zamorakian monks inside the temple.
  • Varrock teleport runes or another period-appropriate return method if available. The quest sends you between Varrock and Paterdomus several times.
  • Bank the 50 Rune essence until Drezel asks for it. Carrying it through the combat and key stages only wastes inventory space.

Before you start

Bring an empty bucket, combat equipment and food. Keep 50 unnoted Rune essence ready in the bank for the final stage. The preserved period guide lists Rune Mysteries as a prerequisite; build 254 itself does not check that quest flag, although completing Rune Mysteries is required to use the normal essence-mine teleport network.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to King Roald

  1. Go to Varrock Palace and speak to King Roald.
  2. Tell him that you are looking for a quest. He explains that he has not heard from Drezel, the priest stationed at the temple on the River Salve east of Varrock.
  3. Agree to check on Drezel. The quest begins.

2. Knock on the temple doors

  1. Leave Varrock to the east, follow the route toward the River Salve and continue to the large temple at Paterdomus.
  2. The large doors are locked. Right-click a door and choose Knock-at.
  3. When asked why you are there, choose "Roald sent me to check on Drezel."
  4. The men inside clumsily pretend to be Drezel and ask you to do them a favour: kill the "horrible big dog" beneath the nearby mausoleum.
  5. Agree to help.

3. Kill the Temple guardian

  1. Use the dungeon entrance / trapdoor by the mausoleum just north of the temple.
  2. In the first chamber, attack the Temple guardian (level 30).
  3. Kill it. This is the first mandatory combat encounter.
  4. Return to the temple doors and knock again. The impostors are delighted, laugh about what you have done and suggest that you report your success to King Roald.

4. Report back to King Roald

  1. Return to King Roald in Varrock Palace.
  2. Tell him that you killed the dog for the people at the temple.
  3. Roald is furious: the guardian was protecting the route from Morytania, and the people who ordered you to kill it were clearly not Drezel.
  4. He orders you back to the temple to repair the damage immediately.

5. Enter the temple and get the golden key

  1. Return to Paterdomus. After King Roald's warning, the large temple doors can now be opened from outside.
  2. Inside are several Monks of Zamorak. Find and kill the level-30 Monk of Zamorak.
  3. The level-30 monk drops a Golden key. Pick it up; it is needed for the monument puzzle below the temple.
  4. Go upstairs to the top floor, where the real Drezel is imprisoned behind a makeshift cell door beside a vampyre's coffin.

6. Speak to the real Drezel

  1. Use Talk-through on Drezel's cell door.
  2. When Drezel says the story of the River Salve is long, choose "Tell me anyway." This leads to the explanation of the holy river and the seven warrior priests who died defending the pass.
  3. Agree to help him. Drezel explains that you need to find the key to his cell and also prevent the sleeping vampyre from attacking him when he leaves.
  4. The gate into the monument chamber beneath the temple is now available.

7. Exchange the golden key for the iron key

  1. Return to the mausoleum dungeon and pass through the first locked gate into the chamber containing seven monuments around the River Salve well.
  2. Study the monuments one by one. Their contents are not tied to a fixed position in build 254, so follow the inscription rather than a compass direction.
  3. Find the monument whose inscription reads "Saradomin is the key that unlocks the mysteries of life." Its display contains the iron key.
  4. Use the Golden key on that monument. The golden replica is exchanged for the Iron key.
  5. While you are here, use your empty bucket on the well in the centre of the monuments to obtain a bucket of murky water.
The other golden monument items are optional. A normal needle, feather, pot, tinderbox, hammer or unlit candle can be exchanged for its golden replica at the matching monument, but none of those exchanges is required to finish Priest in Peril.

8. Unlock Drezel's cell and bless the water

  1. Return to Drezel on the top floor of Paterdomus.
  2. Use the Iron key on the cell door. The key is consumed and the door is unlocked.
  3. Speak to Drezel. Although the cell is open, he cannot safely leave while the vampyre is able to wake and attack him.
  4. Give or use the bucket of murky Salve water on Drezel. He blesses it, turning it into a bucket of blessed water.

9. Seal the vampyre in its coffin

  1. Use the blessed water on the coffin beside Drezel.
  2. The blessed water is poured over the coffin, preventing the vampyre from leaving it long enough for Drezel to escape. Your bucket is returned empty.
  3. Speak to Drezel again. He tells you that he will meet you below by the monuments to assess the damage to the holy barrier.

10. Meet Drezel beneath the temple

  1. Return to the monument chamber beneath Paterdomus.
  2. Pass through the north-east gate beyond the monuments and speak to Drezel.
  3. He explains that the Zamorakians polluted the source of the River Salve. His own blessing is not strong enough to cleanse the whole river.
  4. Drezel asks for 50 Rune essence so the magic-absorbing ore can be used to remove the corruption.

11. Give Drezel 50 unnoted Rune essence

  1. Withdraw or mine 50 ordinary Rune essence. They must be unnoted.
  2. Return to Drezel beneath Paterdomus and give him the essence.
  3. You do not need all fifty in one inventory. Build 254 accepts partial hand-ins and remembers how many remain, so two or more trips are fine.
  4. When the fiftieth essence has been handed over, Drezel blesses the stones and uses them to purify the Salve.
  5. The quest completes and Drezel gives you Wolfbane.

12. Speak to Drezel once more before entering Morytania

  1. After the quest-complete screen, speak to Drezel again before trying to pass through the holy barrier.
  2. He warns you about Morytania and explains that Wolfbane prevents the werewolves around the nearby settlement from changing into their more dangerous wolf form while you fight with the dagger equipped.
  3. After this conversation, pass through the holy barrier to continue east into Morytania.

Combat and hazards

  • Temple guardian — level 30: mandatory. Build 254 only permits you to attack it after agreeing to the impostors' request; killing it advances the quest.
  • Monk of Zamorak — level 30: mandatory because this specific monk drops the Golden key required for Drezel's cell-key puzzle.
  • Other Zamorakian monks: they are hostile combatants but do not all need to be killed. Build 254's reconstructed monk attack behaviour contains guessed/later-sourced details, so this guide does not project exact special attacks backward into September 2004.
  • The vampyre is not a boss fight. Drezel explicitly advises against waking it. The solution is blessed River Salve water on the coffin.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Golden key does not fit the cell: this is intentional. Exchange it at the key monument beneath the temple for the Iron key.
  • Cannot find the correct monument: study all seven and look for the inscription about Saradomin being the key that unlocks the mysteries of life. Build 254 randomises the monument layout per player.
  • Trying to take a monument item directly: build 254's Take-from option triggers holy-power damage instead of giving the item. Use the matching item on the monument to exchange it.
  • Murky water will not seal the coffin: take it to Drezel first so he can bless it.
  • Drezel rejects your essence: use ordinary unnoted Rune essence. Build 254 rejects bank-certificate/noted essence for this step.
  • Only brought part of the 50 essence: hand in what you have. Drezel records the amount and tells you how many more are needed.
  • Quest complete but barrier still blocks you: speak to Drezel once more. In build 254 the quest finishes at the 50-essence state, but the barrier-access state is set by the post-quest warning conversation.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1,406 Prayer XP.
  • Wolfbane, a silver dagger that prevents werewolves from changing to wolf form in combat while it is equipped.
  • Access to Morytania through the holy barrier after the post-quest conversation with Drezel.
  • Quest progression: Priest in Peril is required for Nature Spirit.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start conditions: build 254's King Roald script has no members check and no Rune Mysteries check. A source comment deliberately preserves the historical ability for free players to start Priest in Peril; the quest remains members content for practical completion/access.
  • Period prerequisite conflict: the preserved RuneHQ/LostHQ guide lineage lists Rune Mysteries under Required Quests, while build 254 and the modern preserved quest state have no such hard prerequisite. The public guide keeps the period listing but states the build difference instead of silently choosing one.
  • Two level-30 fights: build 254 uses a level-30 Temple guardian and a level-30 Monk of Zamorak. The period guide independently says the player must be able to defeat two level-30 enemies.
  • Golden-key drop: only the level-30 quest Monk of Zamorak's death script adds the Golden key, and only before Drezel's cell has been unlocked.
  • Monument puzzle: seven monuments are assigned a shuffled layout per player. The key entry is identified by the inscription "Saradomin is the key that unlocks the mysteries of life"; using the Golden key on it returns the Iron key. Direct theft deals 1–5 damage in the current build.
  • Holy water: an empty bucket used on the central well becomes murky water; Drezel converts it to blessed water; using that on the coffin consumes the water and returns the empty bucket.
  • Rune essence hand-in: the quest state runs from 10 to 60 while Drezel receives essence, meaning exactly 50 individual Rune essence are consumed. Partial hand-ins are supported; essence certificates/notes are rejected.
  • Completion reward: the completion queue adds Wolfbane, advances Prayer by 14,060 internal XP units = 1,406 Prayer XP, and the global quest constant awards 1 Quest Point.
  • Barrier access: quest completion is state 60, while the holy barrier only permits passage at state 61. Speaking to Drezel after completion performs the warning dialogue and advances to the access state.
  • Lost Wolfbane: after completion, build 254 can replace Wolfbane if none exists in the player's inventory, bank or worn equipment. The source comments identify this recovery dialogue as later OSRS-derived, so it is presented as implementation behaviour rather than a September-2004 historical reward mechanic.
