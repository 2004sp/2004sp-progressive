Priest in Peril

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Priest in Peril
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak with Father Aereck at the church in Lumbridge about a missing priest named Drezel.

A priest named Drezel travelled to the temple of Paterdomus, on the very border of the cursed region of Morytania, and has not been heard from since. With the temple's holy wards weakening and something clearly wrong at the crossing, you set out to find Drezel and uncover why the barrier keeping Morytania's horrors at bay is starting to fail.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be bought beforehand; keys and tools needed are found during the quest

Recommended

  • Food
  • A weapon for dealing with hostile undead

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Father Aereck and learn about the missing priest, Drezel.
  2. Travel south-east to the temple of Paterdomus on the River Salve.
  3. Search the temple grounds and speak to the priests still stationed there about Drezel's disappearance.
  4. Discover the entrance to the crypts beneath the temple and head down.
  5. Explore the crypts carefully, avoiding or dealing with the undead and vampire guards within.
  6. Locate the cell where Drezel has been imprisoned.
  7. Find a way to unlock or force open his cell, freeing him.
  8. Listen to Drezel explain that Morytania's vampires sabotaged the temple to weaken the river's protective blessing.
  9. Help Drezel restore the temple's holy water systems so the barrier holds once more.
  10. Escort or assist Drezel back to safety within the temple.
  11. Speak to Drezel once the temple is secure to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Access to cross the River Salve into Morytania
  • Drezel's services for future quests set in Morytania
