Ernest the Chicken

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Ernest the Chicken
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Medium
Quest points
4
Start point
Talk to Veronica outside the gates of Draynor Manor.

Veronica's fiance went to interview for a position at the reclusive Draynor Manor and simply never returned. What she doesn't know is that the manor's owner has been dabbling in experiments well beyond ordinary science, with unfortunate results for anyone who wanders too close. You'll have to explore the manor's stranger corners to set things right.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Recommended

  • Some spare inventory space for notes and items found in the manor

Walkthrough

  1. Travel south of Draynor Village to the gates of Draynor Manor and speak with Veronica, who's waiting outside.
  2. Learn that her fiance Ernest went in to interview for a job with the manor's reclusive professor and never came back out.
  3. Make your way onto the manor grounds and find a way inside the building.
  4. Explore the ground floor rooms, watching out for the manor's short-tempered chef and other hazards.
  5. Search for a hidden mechanism, such as a piano or lever, that opens a passage down to the basement laboratory.
  6. Head down into the basement and take a look around the professor's strange machinery.
  7. Find the professor's research notes explaining how his transformation device works.
  8. Locate the pen holding several chickens and identify the one that used to be Ernest.
  9. Work out the correct sequence of dials or valves on the machine based on the notes you found.
  10. Place Ernest inside the device and activate it to reverse the transformation.
  11. Speak with the now-human Ernest and escort him back outside the manor.
  12. Return to Veronica with Ernest in tow to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
