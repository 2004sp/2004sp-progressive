Nature Spirit

Start point
Speak to Drezel at the Paterdomus temple after completing Priest in Peril and The Restless Ghost.

Deep within the rotting expanse of Mort Myre Swamp stands a forgotten chapel, home to a nature spirit bound and tormented by a corrupted spectre. With Drezel's blessing and the help of a reclusive swamp hermit, you venture into the mire to break the spirit's chains and set right whatever went wrong at that lonely chapel long ago.

Requirements

  • Completion of Priest in Peril
  • Completion of The Restless Ghost

Items needed

  • A sickle
  • A ghostspeak amulet

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison for the swamp's hazards

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Drezel at Paterdomus about the strange presence troubling Mort Myre Swamp.
  2. Head into the swamp and locate Filliman Tarlock, a hermit who has made his home among the trees.
  3. Ask Filliman about the swamp's ghostly troubles and the chapel at its heart.
  4. Make your way through Mort Myre Swamp to the ruined chapel.
  5. Investigate the chapel and encounter the corrupted spirit haunting it.
  6. Return to Drezel and have him bless your sickle so it can affect the malevolent spirit.
  7. Head back into the swamp to gather any additional items the ritual requires.
  8. Return to the chapel with your blessed sickle and the ritual components.
  9. Confront and put an end to the corrupted spirit tormenting the chapel.
  10. Free the nature spirit that had been trapped by the corruption.
  11. Speak with the newly freed nature spirit to hear its gratitude and guidance.
  12. Report back to Drezel to formally complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • The nature spirit's blessing, useful for future dealings in Mort Myre Swamp
  • Prayer experience
