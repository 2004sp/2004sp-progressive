Murder Mystery

Murder Mystery

Start point
Enter the manor house south-west of Port Sarim and speak to the household staff about the commotion inside.

A house party at a quiet manor south of Port Sarim has ended in tragedy: the household's gardener has been found dead among the vegetable beds. With the local guards stumped, it falls to you to play detective, sift through the manor's rooms for clues, and work out which of the guests or staff is responsible before the killer covers their tracks.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be bought beforehand; clue items are found inside the manor

Walkthrough

  1. Enter the manor house and learn that the gardener, Fygg Fyggingham, has been found murdered outside.
  2. Examine the body and the surrounding vegetable patch for anything the killer might have dropped.
  3. Search the manor's bedrooms, kitchen, and study for hidden clues, letters, and a possible murder weapon.
  4. Take note of anything unusual you find, such as a bloodstained item or a suspicious letter revealing a grudge.
  5. Speak to each of the residents and guests in turn and ask them about their whereabouts.
  6. Compare their alibis against what you have already discovered in your search of the house.
  7. Narrow the list of suspects down by ruling out anyone whose story matches the physical evidence.
  8. Identify which weapon was actually used, since several look-alike weapons are scattered around as red herrings.
  9. Confront the household with your conclusion and name the suspect you believe committed the murder.
  10. Present the evidence you gathered to justify your accusation and close the case.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • A small amount of coins
  • Insight into the manor's inhabitants that flavours later dialogue
