The Tourist Trap

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

The Tourist Trap
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Medium
Quest points
2
Start point
Speak to a nomad guide at the Bedabin Camp in the Kharidian Desert.

Out in the scorching Kharidian Desert, the nomads of the Bedabin Camp are eager to establish trade with the wider world, but their leader is missing and the desert route is riddled with danger. You are enlisted to track down the missing leader and prove the desert can be tamed for travellers and traders alike.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Waterskins or another means of surviving the desert heat

Recommended

  • Food

Walkthrough

  1. Travel south into the Kharidian Desert and find the Bedabin Camp among the dunes.
  2. Speak to the nomads there and learn that their leader has gone missing under strange circumstances.
  3. Agree to help search for him and investigate the surrounding desert for clues to his whereabouts.
  4. Fill your waterskins and prepare to endure the desert heat as you travel between locations.
  5. Follow the trail of clues toward Nardah, where more information about the missing nomad can be found.
  6. Speak to residents of Nardah who may know something about the nomad leader's disappearance.
  7. Track down the missing leader, who has been captured or is being held against his will.
  8. Find a way to free him from his captors, whether through combat, cunning, or negotiation.
  9. Escort the nomad leader safely back toward the Bedabin Camp once he has been rescued.
  10. Speak to him at the camp to confirm his safe return and finish up loose ends with the nomads.
  11. Report your success to the camp to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • Thieving experience
  • The desert amulet, allowing safer travel through the desert heat
  • Improved relations with the desert nomads
