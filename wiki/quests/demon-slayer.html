A demon named Delrith once came close to destroying Varrock. The Gypsy foresees his return and tells you that the legendary sword Silverlight and the same magical incantation used long ago are needed to banish him again. Sir Prysin still has Silverlight, but its case is locked by three separate keys.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • There is no hard skill-level requirement.
  • You must be able to defeat Delrith while Silverlight is equipped. The stone circle is also occupied by hostile Dark Wizards.

Required items

  • 1 coin — consumed when you cross the Gypsy's palm with silver and begin the quest.
  • 25 sets of regular bones — consumed by Wizard Traiborn's ritual. They can be collected before or during the quest, and Traiborn accepts them in more than one hand-in.
  • 1 bucket of water — the water is used to flush Sir Prysin's key through the palace drain; the empty bucket remains. A bucket and water can be obtained during the quest.
  • Silverlight — obtained during the quest after recovering all three keys. It must be wielded for the Delrith fight.

Items obtainable during the quest

  • Bucket: the period route takes one from upstairs near Varrock Palace's kitchen, then fills it from the kitchen sink.
  • Regular bones: any normal bones from suitable monster kills work for Traiborn. You need 25 in total.
  • Three keys: Captain Rovin gives one, Wizard Traiborn gives one after the bone ritual, and Sir Prysin's lost key is flushed into Varrock Sewers.
  • Silverlight: Sir Prysin gives you the sword after you return with all three keys.

Recommended

  • Armour and food for the final area. A contemporary 2004 guide recommends 41 Combat so the level-20 Dark Wizards around the stone circle will not aggressively target you under the period aggression rules; this is a recommendation, not a quest requirement.
  • If you collect all 25 bones in advance, visit Traiborn early in the key hunt. Turning 25 inventory slots into one key makes the remaining palace steps much easier to manage.
  • Write down the incantation before leaving the Gypsy: Carlem Aber Camerinthum Purchai Gabindo.

Before you start

Bring at least one coin. If you want the quickest route, also bring 25 regular bones, food, and your preferred armour. You do not need to bring a normal combat weapon for Delrith because Silverlight is obtained before the fight and must be equipped there.

The three keys can be collected in any order once Sir Prysin explains the problem. The walkthrough below handles Traiborn, Captain Rovin, and the drain separately so each recovery method is clear.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start with the Gypsy in Varrock Square. Enter the tent in Varrock Square and talk to the Gypsy. Agree to cross her palm with silver. She takes 1 coin, reads your future, and explains that Delrith is returning and that you are the person destined to stop him.
  2. Learn the old method of banishment. Continue the conversation and ask about the magical incantation. The target-period/build-254 incantation is fixed: Carlem Aber Camerinthum Purchai Gabindo. The Gypsy tells you that the hero who stopped Delrith before used the magical sword Silverlight and directs you to Sir Prysin in Varrock Palace.
  3. Speak to Sir Prysin. Find Sir Prysin in the south-west side of Varrock Palace. Ask about Silverlight. He still has the sword, but its case requires three keys: one held by Captain Rovin, one held by Wizard Traiborn, and one that Prysin accidentally dropped into the drain outside the palace kitchen.
  4. Traiborn's key — travel to the Wizards' Tower. Go to the Wizards' Tower on the island south of Draynor Village. Find Wizard Traiborn on the first floor above ground (the second floor in US numbering). Ask him about the key.
  5. Give Traiborn 25 regular bones. Traiborn explains that his key is sealed in a magical closet and that he needs 25 sets of regular bones to perform the ritual that opens it. Hand him the bones. You can bring all 25 at once or return with more until the total is complete. After the ritual he gives you his key.
  6. Captain Rovin's key — return to Varrock Palace. Go to the guard quarters in the north-west wing of the palace and climb to the upper floor. Talk to Captain Rovin. Insist that the matter is important and explain the danger to Varrock and your need for Silverlight. He eventually hands over his key; there is no chest fight or item payment involved.
  7. Sir Prysin's drain key — get a bucket. Go to the palace kitchen in the north-east part of the building. The contemporary route goes upstairs to take a bucket, then back down to fill it with water from the kitchen sink. If you brought a bucket of water, you can skip this preparation.
  8. Flush the key into Varrock Sewers. Go outside to the drain by the north-eastern corner of the palace kitchen and use the bucket of water on it. The water dislodges Prysin's key and carries it into the sewer below.
  9. Collect the sewer key promptly. Enter Varrock Sewers through the nearby manhole east of the palace. Head north a short distance through the sewer and pick up the key where the drain empties. If it is no longer there, return to the drain and pour down another bucket of water.
  10. Exchange all three keys for Silverlight. Return to Sir Prysin with the three keys in your inventory. He takes all three and opens the case, giving you Silverlight.
  11. Prepare for Delrith. Equip Silverlight and make sure you remember the incantation. Leave Varrock through the south gate and head to the Dark Wizards' stone circle south of the city. Lower-level players should be ready for the surrounding Dark Wizards.
  12. Fight Delrith with Silverlight equipped. Attack Delrith while wielding Silverlight. In build 254 Delrith is combat level 27; the period guide confirms the Silverlight fight but does not independently establish that exact September-2004 level, so treat 27 as the build value rather than a separately proven historical number.
  13. Choose the correct incantation. When Delrith has been weakened, the banishment dialogue appears. Choose Carlem Aber Camerinthum Purchai Gabindo. Choosing any other incantation restores Delrith and you must weaken him again before another attempt.
  14. Quest complete. The correct incantation banishes Delrith and completes the quest immediately. You do not need to return to Sir Prysin.

Combat and failure notes

  • Delrith: build 254 uses combat level 27. Silverlight must be equipped before the server will let you attack him. The exact level is presented as a build fact because the contemporary guide used for the September-2004 route does not state Delrith's level.
  • Dark Wizards: the stone circle contains level-20 and lower Dark Wizards. The contemporary 2004 guide recommends 41 Combat to avoid aggression from the level-20 wizards; otherwise bring food and armour.
  • Wrong incantation: Delrith returns to full health in build 254. Weaken him again and choose Carlem Aber Camerinthum Purchai Gabindo.
  • Drain key missing: the key does not remain indefinitely. Pour another bucket of water down the palace drain and return to the sewer.
  • Lost Traiborn key: build 254 resets that branch and requires another 25 regular bones before Traiborn will replace the key.
  • Lost Silverlight before finishing: build 254 lets Sir Prysin replace the sword for free while the quest is still incomplete.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • Silverlight.

There is no skill-XP reward. The contemporary 2004 guide also describes Silverlight as useful for weakening demons after the quest.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Period NPC naming: build 254's NPC config displays the starting character simply as Gypsy. The internal config/script name is aris; the public guide therefore uses the period-facing name rather than the later/common “Gypsy Aris” label.
  • Fixed incantation: the build hard-codes Carlem Aber Camerinthum Purchai Gabindo. Its source comment specifically notes that the incantation was not randomised in RuneScape Classic and may only have changed with the 2006 Demon Slayer rework. This matches the preserved March-2004 quest route.
  • Silverlight combat: the build blocks attacks on Delrith unless Silverlight is in the right-hand weapon slot. Against NPCs flagged as demon-vulnerable, Silverlight also raises melee maximum hit to 160% of normal. The Delrith script comments that magic can damage him as long as Silverlight remains equipped.
  • Shared sewer key bug: the flushed key is created as a normal world-visible ground item at a fixed coordinate rather than being instanced to the player. Another player can see or take it. This is also recorded on the Known bugs page.
  • Shared Delrith bug: Delrith is a single shared world NPC whose type and hitpoints are changed in place during the quest, so two players attempting the finale at the same time can interfere with one another. There is no per-player quest instance in the current engine.
  • Replacement Silverlight: after quest completion, build 254 offers a replacement Silverlight from Sir Prysin for 500 coins. That post-quest price is documented here as implementation behaviour rather than asserted as a September-2004 historical reward mechanic.
  • Completion: the correct incantation directly queues the completion screen and awards 3 Quest Points; there is no post-fight Sir Prysin hand-in and no skill XP.
