Demon Slayer

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Demon Slayer
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
3
Start point
Talk to Sir Prysin inside Varrock Castle.

A dusty prophecy in the halls of Varrock speaks of an ancient demon named Delrith, once bound long ago but destined to return. A power-hungry wizard is determined to bring that day forward by finishing the ritual himself, and only a legendary sword stands between Varrock and disaster. You're pulled into a race to recover that blade before the summoning is complete.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Recommended

  • Armour and a weapon for the final fight with Delrith
  • Food to heal during the battle

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Sir Prysin inside Varrock's palace; he mentions a dire prophecy and points you toward a fortune teller.
  2. Track down Gypsy Aris, who tells the tale of the demon Delrith and the enchanted blade Silverlight, the only weapon capable of ending him.
  3. Learn that a rogue wizard named Traiborn intends to summon Delrith using an ancient scroll and gem.
  4. Seek out Captain Rovin, who guards the locked chest containing Silverlight, and convince him to let you take the sword.
  5. Open the chest and equip Silverlight — no ordinary blade will do against the demon.
  6. Head to the ruined chapel south of Varrock where Traiborn is preparing his summoning ritual.
  7. Watch as Traiborn completes the ritual and Delrith bursts into the world in a flash of flame.
  8. Engage Delrith in combat while wielding Silverlight; its enchantment is what allows you to actually harm him.
  9. Whittle down the demon's health until he's banished back to wherever he came from.
  10. Return to Sir Prysin in Varrock to report that the threat has been dealt with and collect your reward.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • Permanent ownership of the Silverlight sword
