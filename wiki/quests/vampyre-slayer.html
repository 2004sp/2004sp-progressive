Vampyre Slayer

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Vampyre Slayer
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
3
Start point
Talk to Morgan in his house in Draynor Village.

Draynor Village has a vampire problem, and its citizens are running out of patience. Count Draynor has made the manor cellar his own personal hunting ground, and it's going to take more than a sharp sword to put him down for good. Morgan needs someone brave, or foolish, enough to finish the job.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A hammer
  • A wooden stake, obtainable from Morgan

Recommended

  • Garlic, to help ward off the vampire
  • Food for the fight

Walkthrough

  1. Head into Draynor Village and speak with Morgan inside his house.
  2. Listen to his account of Count Draynor, a vampire who has been terrorising the village from a manor cellar.
  3. Agree to help and ask Morgan what you'll need to bring the vampire down.
  4. Obtain a hammer and a proper wooden stake, the only weapon capable of finishing the vampire off.
  5. Consider picking up some garlic as well, since vampires despise the smell and it may help protect you.
  6. Make your way to Draynor Manor and find the entrance down into its cellar.
  7. Descend into the cellar and confront Count Draynor.
  8. Fight him in melee combat, wearing him down over the course of the battle.
  9. Once he's nearly beaten, finish him off using your stake and hammer together.
  10. Head back to Morgan in Draynor Village to report that the vampire has been dealt with.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points
  • 4825 Attack experience
