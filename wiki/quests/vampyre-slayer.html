Vampire Slayer

Vampire Slayer
Access

Medium

Start
Morgan, in his house in Draynor Village

Combat
Defeat Count Draynor (level 34 in the February-2004 RS2 guide and build 254)

The people of Draynor Village are being terrorised by a vampire living beneath Draynor Manor. Morgan asks you to seek advice from his old friend Dr Harlow, then enter the manor cellar and destroy Count Draynor for good.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None. There is no numerical Attack, Strength, Defence or Hitpoints gate, but you must be able to defeat the level-34 Count Draynor.

Required items

  • 1 beer — consumed by Dr Harlow before he gives his vampire-hunting advice and stake.
  • 1 stake — normally obtained from Dr Harlow after giving him the beer; consumed when Count Draynor is successfully staked.
  • 1 hammer — required with the stake to finish Count Draynor; it is not consumed.

Recommended

  • 1 garlic. Search the upstairs cupboard on the west side of Morgan's house. Garlic is not a hard completion requirement, but it weakens Count Draynor and was part of the period route.
  • Combat equipment and food suitable for a level-34 opponent.
  • Enough inventory space for the stake, hammer and garlic before entering the cellar.

Before you start

This is a short route geographically, but it includes a mandatory boss fight. You will travel from Draynor Village to the Jolly Boar Inn north-east of Varrock, then back to Draynor Manor.

The 2003 Jagex quest manual classed the mission length as Medium and required only that the player be able to defeat a vampire. A target-proximate RuneScape 2 guide entered on 28 February 2004 identifies Count Draynor as combat level 34 and records the same Morgan → Dr Harlow → manor route implemented by build 254.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Morgan in Draynor Village

  1. Go to Morgan's house in Draynor Village and speak to him.
  2. He explains that an evil vampire in the basement of the manor to the north has been attacking the village.
  3. Agree to help. Morgan tells you to find his old friend Dr Harlow, a retired vampire hunter who can normally be found in the Jolly Boar Inn in Varrock.

2. Take the garlic from Morgan's house

  1. Before leaving, go upstairs in Morgan's house.
  2. Search the cupboard on the west wall to obtain a clove of garlic.
  3. Keep it in your inventory for the Count Draynor fight. It is not consumed by the quest.

Garlic is a genuine combat aid rather than a hidden completion gate. Dr Harlow recommends carrying it, and build 254 has a dedicated weakening routine when Count Draynor detects garlic in your inventory.

3. Find Dr Harlow and buy him a beer

  1. Travel to the Jolly Boar Inn, just outside the north-east side of Varrock.
  2. Speak to Dr Harlow and tell him that Morgan needs his help.
  3. Harlow refuses to explain vampire hunting until you get him a drink. Bring him one beer and speak to him again.
  4. He consumes the beer, sobers up enough to explain the method and gives you a stake if you do not already have one in your carried inventory.
  5. He tells you that you also need an ordinary hammer to drive the stake in properly, and recommends Morgan's garlic because vampires are weakened by it.

4. Prepare for Count Draynor

Before entering the cellar, make sure you have:

  • Stake — mandatory for the finishing blow.
  • Hammer — mandatory for driving the stake in.
  • Garlic — strongly recommended and obtainable from Morgan's cupboard.
  • Weapon, armour and food appropriate for the fight.

If either the stake or hammer is missing when Count Draynor reaches the point where he should die, build 254 makes him regenerate instead of completing the quest.

5. Enter Draynor Manor's cellar

  1. Travel north from Draynor Village to Draynor Manor.
  2. Inside the manor, make your way to the stairs down on the east side and descend into the basement.
  3. Open the coffin. While the quest is active, Count Draynor emerges and attacks.

6. Defeat and stake Count Draynor

  1. Fight Count Draynor. The February-2004 RuneScape 2 guide and build 254 both display him as level 34.
  2. If you brought garlic, keep it in your inventory during the fight so that its weakening effect can apply.
  3. Reduce Count Draynor to defeat while carrying both the stake and hammer.
  4. The stake is driven into his chest, permanently finishing him. The stake is consumed; the hammer is retained.
  5. The quest completes immediately. You do not need to return to Morgan for the reward.

The surviving February-2004 guide describes using the stake once the Count's health is fully depleted. In build 254 the finishing sequence is automatic when the Count's death routine finds both the stake and hammer in your inventory.

Combat encounter

  • Enemy: Count Draynor.
  • Combat level: 34 in the target-proximate February-2004 RuneScape 2 guide and in build 254.
  • Required finishing items: stake + hammer.
  • Garlic: optional but strongly recommended. Build 254 lowers Count Draynor's current Hitpoints by 10 and also reduces Attack, Strength and Defence when the garlic weakening routine first triggers at full health. Those exact reductions are implementation values, not asserted as the exact September-2004 historical formula.
  • Failure state: without a stake, Count Draynor regenerates. With a stake but no hammer, the stake cannot be driven in far enough and he also regenerates.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the stake before the fight? In build 254, return to Dr Harlow with another beer. If no stake is in your carried inventory, he can give another one.
  • Banked stake quirk: Harlow's replacement check looks at the carried inventory rather than the bank, so build 254 can issue another stake if the original was banked.
  • Forgot the hammer? Leave, get an ordinary hammer and reopen the coffin while the quest is still active to try again.
  • Forgot garlic? The fight remains completable; garlic is a weakening aid rather than a hard quest-state requirement.
  • Returning to Morgan: after completion Morgan has thank-you dialogue, but talking to him is optional and awards nothing further.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • 4,825 Attack XP.

There is no coin or item reward after the kill. The February-2004 RuneScape 2 guide and build-254 completion script agree exactly on the 3 Quest Points and 4,825 Attack XP.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Period quest name: the build's quest journal/interface and pre-2020 historical material call the quest Vampire Slayer. The existing site filename remains `vampyre-slayer.html` only for link compatibility.
  • Quest states: build 254 uses not started → started with Morgan → spoke to Dr Harlow → complete. Beer, stake, hammer and the boss death routine handle the remainder rather than adding extra main quest states.
  • Dr Harlow: Harlow consumes one beer before giving the stake/advice. His stake check only examines the carried inventory.
  • Garlic weakening: when the routine first triggers while Count Draynor is at base Hitpoints, build 254 subtracts 10 Hitpoints, 10 Attack, 10 Strength and 40 Defence. The source comments use later reconstruction evidence, so these exact numbers are implementation facts rather than direct September-2004 proof.
  • Count Draynor: build config displays level 34 with 35 Hitpoints, 30 Attack, 25 Strength and 30 Defence. The visible level 34 is independently corroborated by the February-2004 RS2 guide; the full stat block is kept implementation-only because the config itself notes later OSRS-derived values.
  • Finishing check: reaching the death routine without a stake heals the Count to full. A stake without a hammer also fails and heals him to full. With both present, build 254 consumes the stake and automatically runs the successful staking animation.
  • Completion: Count Draynor's successful death queues the reward directly: 4,825 Attack XP and 3 Quest Points. Morgan is not a completion turn-in.
  • Coffin spawning: opening the basement coffin while the quest is active spawns Count Draynor and makes him attack the player. Opening it before the quest or after completion does not start another quest fight.
