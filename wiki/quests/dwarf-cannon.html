Dwarf Cannon

Start point
Speak to Captain Lawgof, who can be found near the entrance to the Dwarven Mine south of Falador.

The dwarves of the mine south of Falador have perfected a devastating new siege weapon, but its blueprints have gone missing under suspicious circumstances. Captain Lawgof suspects sabotage from within the ranks of the isolationist Black Guard, a faction of dwarves who believe the cannon breaks an old treaty with the surface-dwelling races. You are asked to get to the bottom of the theft before the weapon's secrets fall into the wrong hands.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A weapon and armour suitable for fighting armed dwarves

Recommended

  • Food
  • Armour offering good stab or slash protection
  • Extra coins for supplies

Walkthrough

  1. Head to the Dwarven Mine south of Falador and find Captain Lawgof patrolling near the entrance.
  2. Ask him about the missing cannon plans; he explains the weapon was designed by the dwarf inventor Nulodion and sends you to speak with him.
  3. Find Nulodion in his workshop and ask about the theft; he confirms pieces of his prototype and his notes have vanished.
  4. Lawgof suspects the Black Guard, a militant dwarven faction opposed to using cannons against outsiders, and asks you to investigate their barracks.
  5. Travel to the Black Guard's base and question the dwarves stationed there without immediately drawing a weapon.
  6. Piece together testimony that points to a specific guard acting alone to smuggle the plans out.
  7. Report your findings back to Lawgof, who authorises you to retrieve the stolen plans by force if necessary.
  8. Fight your way past the Black Guard soldiers standing between you and the culprit.
  9. Corner the guard responsible and recover the missing blueprints and cannon components.
  10. Return the plans to Nulodion so he can finish building a working prototype.
  11. Speak to Lawgof once more to smooth things over between the dwarves and confirm the cannon programme can continue.
  12. Collect your reward and the right to purchase your own Dwarf multicannon.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • The ability to buy and set up your own Dwarf multicannon
  • Access to purchase cannonballs from the dwarf trader
  • Improved standing with the dwarves of the Falador mine
