The Dwarven Black Guard is trying to hold a defensive position against goblin attacks, but its perimeter has been damaged and one of its guards has stopped reporting in. Captain Lawgof asks you to repair the defences, investigate the missing guard, rescue his son, repair the dwarves' new multicannon and finally obtain its ammunition instructions from Nulodion.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None. There is no quest, Quest Point or skill-level requirement to start Dwarf Cannon.
  • 20 Agility is not required. It only provides a convenient log shortcut from the Seers' Village side of the river; the outpost can be reached by walking around from the Fishing Guild/Ardougne side.
  • No monster must be killed to complete the quest.

Required items

You do not need to bring any quest item before starting. The important items are supplied or found during the quest:

  • 6 railings — supplied by Captain Lawgof for the damaged perimeter.
  • Dwarf remains — recovered from the Black Guard watchtower and handed to Lawgof.
  • Tool kit — supplied by Lawgof when he asks you to repair the multicannon.
  • Nulodion's notes and an ammo mould — supplied by Nulodion and handed to Lawgof to finish the quest.

A hammer is not a hard requirement in the April-2004 walkthrough or build 254 quest implementation.

Recommended

  • Food if you are low combat, because the Goblin Cave contains hostile creatures even though the quest itself requires no fight.
  • 20 Agility only if you want to use the log shortcut near the outpost.
  • Enough free inventory space to carry the supplied quest items. Lawgof and Nulodion can replace several lost items during the appropriate stages.

Before you start

Captain Lawgof is at the dwarven defensive camp north-west of the Fishing Guild, south of the Coal Trucks. The period Jagex quest manual describes the start as the Coal Trucks west of McGrubor's Wood. If you cannot use the nearby Agility log, approach from the Ardougne/Fishing Guild side instead; the shortcut is not a quest requirement.

This is the pre-13 June 2006 form of Dwarf Cannon. In the target-period quest the cannon-repair stage uses the older inspect-and-repair sequence, not the later three-tool repair interface found in modern guides.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Help Captain Lawgof repair the perimeter

  1. Speak to Captain Lawgof at the dwarven outpost. Agree to help the Black Guard defend the area from the goblin attacks.
  2. Take the six replacement railings. Lawgof gives you exactly six.
  3. Search the perimeter fence. Work around the fenced camp and search the railing sections until you find each of the six damaged sections.
  4. Repair every damaged railing. Use the replacement when prompted and continue until all six have been repaired. In build 254 a repair can fail and hurt you slightly; simply retry.
  5. Return to Lawgof. Once all six broken sections are fixed, he asks you to check on a guard who should be stationed in the Black Guard watchtower to the south.

2. Investigate the watchtower

  1. Go south to the watchtower. Climb to the upper level. Before this quest stage, build 254 keeps the relevant trapdoor closed.
  2. Search the tower and take the dwarf remains. The missing guard, Gilob, has been killed.
  3. Bring the remains back to Lawgof. He takes them and realises Gilob's young son, Lollk, is also missing.
  4. Agree to find the goblins' base and rescue the child.

3. Find the Goblin Cave and rescue Lollk

  1. Travel to the Goblin Cave south-east of the Fishing Guild. It lies on the Ardougne side of the river, near the windmill area.
  2. Enter the cave. In build 254, entering it at the correct stage records that you have found the goblins' base.
  3. Work through the cave toward the far north-western room. The period route takes the central passage and then continues north-west.
  4. Search the crates in the far room. One of the crates contains a tied-up dwarf youngster.
  5. Free Lollk. Speak to him after he comes out of the crate. He thanks you and runs home.
  6. Return to Captain Lawgof. He confirms that Lollk has made it back safely.

4. Repair the broken multicannon

  1. Accept Lawgof's next task. The Black Guard has sent the outpost a multicannon, but the dwarves have been unable to repair it.
  2. Take the tool kit. Lawgof gives it to you. If you lose it during this stage, he can supply another.
  3. Enter the nearby shed and inspect the broken multicannon. Keep the tool kit in your inventory.
  4. Repair the four damaged parts: the pipe, barrel, axle and shaft. This is the older pre-2006 repair sequence. Attempts can fail, so retry a part until it is fixed before moving on.
  5. Inspect the cannon once more after all four parts are repaired. The quest only records the cannon as working after the final inspection.
  6. Report back to Lawgof. He is pleased that the cannon works, but the shipment arrived without instructions explaining its ammunition.

5. Get the ammunition instructions from Nulodion

  1. Agree to visit the Black Guard's main base. Lawgof sends you to Nulodion, the cannon engineer, south of Ice Mountain.
  2. Find Nulodion near the Dwarven Mine. He is in the dwarven workshop/building by the entrance area south of Ice Mountain.
  3. Tell him Lawgof needs help with the cannon. Nulodion realises the shipment was missing its ammunition information.
  4. Take Nulodion's notes and the ammo mould. If you lose either before delivering them, build 254 lets Nulodion replace the missing item.
  5. Return both items to Captain Lawgof. He takes the notes and mould, understands that the cannonballs must be made, and completes the quest.

Combat encounters

  • No combat encounter is mandatory. You do not have to fight Black Guard dwarves, a saboteur or a quest boss.
  • Goblin Cave: hostile cave inhabitants may attack low-level players while travelling to Lollk's crate, so food is useful.
  • The cannon-repair failures in build 254 are skill-check failures rather than combat; they can cause a small amount of self-damage.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost railings: while the perimeter task is unfinished, Lawgof can give you another railing if you have run out.
  • Lost tool kit: Lawgof can replace it while you are still repairing the cannon.
  • Lost notes or mould before completion: return to Nulodion; build 254 replaces whichever quest item is missing.
  • Cannon repair keeps failing: there is no hard Smithing or Crafting requirement to start or complete the quest. Keep attempting the damaged part.
  • The quest mould is handed in: Lawgof consumes the mould and Nulodion's notes at completion in build 254. After the quest, Nulodion sells ammo moulds and cannon equipment.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 750 Crafting experience.
  • Permission to buy and use the Dwarf multicannon. The period 2004 guide records a complete cannon at 750,000 coins, or individual parts at about 200,000 coins each.
  • Access to cannonball manufacture. For the September-2004/build-254 setup, making cannonballs is members-only and requires 35 Smithing, an ammo mould and a steel bar. One steel bar produces 4 cannonballs for 37.5 Smithing XP.

Cannonballs themselves are not a quest reward and are not stocked by Nulodion's build-254 shop; his shop stocks cannon parts, the instruction manual and ammo moulds.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Captain Lawgof's name: RuneScape 2 changed the NPC from the older Dwarf commander identity to Captain Lawgof on 29 March 2004, so the historical September-2004 guide uses Captain Lawgof. Build 254 still displays Dwarf Commander, reflecting its RSC-like reconstruction.
  • Quest reconstruction: the build source explicitly notes that Dwarf Cannon was reworked on 13 June 2006 and that this implementation was reconstructed from the older maps/NPCs plus RSC replays and preserved pre-rework guides. Exact reconstructed dialogue should therefore not be mistaken for direct September-2004 transcript evidence.
  • Railings: build 254 tracks six individual damaged sections. A Crafting-based random check can fail and damage the player by up to 3 Hitpoints; this exact formula is implementation detail rather than a claimed period-authentic formula.
  • Old cannon-repair mechanic: build 254 uses the pre-rework pipe, barrel, axle and shaft sequence. Repair success uses reconstructed Smithing/Crafting random checks. Each successful part also awards 1.2 Crafting XP in the build, for 4.8 incidental XP if all four are repaired successfully; the main quest reward remains a separate 750 Crafting XP.
  • Completion: the completion script awards exactly 750 Crafting XP and the quest-point constant is 1. Lawgof removes Nulodion's notes and the quest ammo mould before completion.
  • Nulodion's trade: trading is blocked until the quest is complete. His direct full-set dialogue charges 750,000 coins. The same dialogue describes separate parts as 200,000 each, while the build's actual stock/pricing configuration can make a fully stocked part display slightly above that figure, so the public historical reward text follows the contemporary 2004 guide rather than treating the dynamic build shop price as a fixed historical value.
  • Cannonballs: build 254 requires members access, 35 Smithing, an ammo mould and one steel bar; it produces four cannonballs and awards 37.5 Smithing XP per bar. The source comments for this script cite June, July and August 2004 evidence, making it especially relevant to the target cutoff.
  • Cannon operation: build 254 requires Dwarf Cannon completion before setup, uses the four parts in base → stand → barrels → furnace order, and holds up to 30 cannonballs at once. Several placement restrictions in the cannon code are explicitly reconstructed from later evidence, so they are not presented here as September-2004 quest facts.
