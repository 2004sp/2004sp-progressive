Waterfall Quest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Waterfall Quest
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Medium
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Almera, whose house stands near the waterfall not far from McGrubor's Wood.

Almera's brother Godric set off to explore the hidden cave behind a great waterfall in search of ancient treasure, and he has not returned. Fearing the worst, she begs you to find him, leading you into a concealed grotto guarded by a fierce warrior sworn to protect its secrets.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A hatchet
  • A rope

Recommended

  • Food
  • Armour and a weapon for the fight with Glorial

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Almera at her house near the waterfall and agree to search for her missing brother Godric.
  2. Head toward the waterfall and use your hatchet to clear a path through the vines blocking the way.
  3. Search the area for a hidden entrance leading behind the falling water into a concealed cave system.
  4. Use your rope where needed to climb down through the cave and continue deeper underground.
  5. Navigate the winding passages, watching for a path that eventually opens into a larger cavern.
  6. Find Godric within the caves, weakened but alive, and learn what he discovered about the treasure.
  7. Press onward to confront Glorial, the powerful guardian warrior who protects the cave's inner chamber.
  8. Either defeat Glorial in combat or find another way to get past him, depending on your approach.
  9. Claim the treasure hidden within the inner chamber once Glorial has been dealt with.
  10. Escort Godric back out of the cave system and return with him to Almera's house.
  11. Speak to Almera to deliver the good news and complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 2,100 Attack experience
  • A treasure map leading to buried loot
