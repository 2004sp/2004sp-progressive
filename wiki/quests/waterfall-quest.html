Almera is worried about her son Hudon, who has gone treasure hunting around Baxtorian Falls. Checking on him leads into the history of King Baxtorian and Queen Glarial, a gnome prisoner named Golrie, Glarial's sealed tomb, and the dangerous caverns behind the waterfall.

September 2004 date lock: this route follows 2004scape build 254 together with surviving period material from 2003 and April-August 2004. Modern Old School RuneScape shortcuts, requirements and later guide conventions are intentionally excluded.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • No skill requirement.
  • No prerequisite quest and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No enemy must be killed to complete the quest. The Waterfall Dungeon is still dangerous, so low-level players should bring food and move carefully around the monsters inside.

Required items

  • 1 rope. The same rope can be reused for both crossings at Baxtorian Falls.
  • 6 Air runes.
  • 6 Earth runes.
  • 6 Water runes.

Recommended

  • Food, especially for low-level characters. The Waterfall Dungeon contains dangerous monsters even though no kill is required.
  • Combat gear for the Waterfall Dungeon if you need the protection. You must bank it before entering Glarial's Tomb and may retrieve it afterwards.
  • Leave room for quest items. Before the final use of Glarial's urn on the chalice, build 254 checks that you have at least five free inventory spaces.

Quest items obtained on the way

  • Book on Baxtorian — found while researching the falls.
  • Golrie's key and Glarial's pebble — used to reach Glarial's Tomb.
  • Glarial's amulet and Glarial's urn — recovered from the tomb and needed at the waterfall.
  • Baxtorian key — found inside the Waterfall Dungeon and used on the doors leading to the final chamber.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Help Almera and speak to Hudon

  1. Speak to Almera at her house near Baxtorian Falls and agree to help her son Hudon.
  2. Board the raft behind the house. It carries you down the river and crashes at the small island.
  3. Speak to Hudon. He is searching for treasure and refuses to return home.
  4. To leave the island safely, use your rope on the nearby rock, then use the same rope on the dead tree to reach the ledge by the waterfall.
  5. Leave the falls and continue the investigation. The nearby barrel provides a safe way to be carried downstream. Avoid trying to climb the dead tree directly; build 254 makes you fall and wash downstream.

2. Research King Baxtorian and Queen Glarial

  1. Visit the Tourist Centre near Baxtorian Falls and speak to Hadley.
  2. Use the upstairs archive and find the Book on Baxtorian.
  3. Read the book. It explains the history of King Baxtorian and Queen Glarial and points you toward the gnomes for the next part of the search.

3. Free Golrie and obtain Glarial's pebble

  1. Travel to the Tree Gnome Village and enter the dungeon beneath the maze.
  2. Search the crates until you find Golrie's key.
  3. Use the key to open the locked area containing Golrie.
  4. Speak to Golrie. He gives you Glarial's pebble, which opens Glarial's Tomb.

4. Enter Glarial's Tomb

  1. Bank your combat equipment, runes and unrelated tools or materials. Take Glarial's pebble and some food. The tomb has an inventory/equipment restriction; if the entrance rejects you, bank additional non-essential items and try again.
  2. Go to Glarial's tombstone and use Glarial's pebble on it to enter.
  3. Inside the tomb, search the chest to obtain Glarial's amulet.
  4. Search the coffin to obtain the full Glarial's urn.
  5. Leave the tomb and return to your bank to prepare for the Waterfall Dungeon.

5. Prepare for the waterfall

  1. Bring 1 rope, Glarial's amulet, the full Glarial's urn, 6 Air runes, 6 Earth runes and 6 Water runes.
  2. Bring food and any protective equipment you want for the dungeon.
  3. Wear Glarial's amulet. Build 254 also recognises it in your inventory at the waterfall door, but wearing it matches the contemporary route and avoids mistakes.

6. Enter the Waterfall Dungeon

  1. Return to Almera's raft and ride it to Hudon's island.
  2. Use the rope on the rock, then use the same rope on the dead tree to reach the waterfall ledge.
  3. Enter the door in the ledge while carrying or wearing Glarial's amulet.
  4. If you try the door without the amulet at the relevant stage, you are washed downstream and take damage.

7. Find Baxtorian's key

  1. Inside the Waterfall Dungeon, take the passage containing the searchable crates.
  2. Search the appropriate crate until you obtain the Baxtorian key.
  3. Return to the central area and take the other passage toward the locked rooms. Dangerous monsters occupy this route, but you do not need to kill them.
  4. Use the Baxtorian key on the locked doors to reach the pillar chamber.

8. Charge all six rune pillars

  1. The chamber contains six pillars.
  2. Use 1 Air rune, 1 Earth rune and 1 Water rune on each pillar.
  3. Across all six pillars, the puzzle consumes exactly 6 Air runes, 6 Earth runes and 6 Water runes.
  4. Do not use Glarial's amulet on the statue until every pillar has been completed. Build 254 punishes an early attempt with falling rock damage.
  5. Once all six pillars are charged, use Glarial's amulet on the statue. The amulet is accepted and the floor rises, giving access to the treasure chamber.

9. Use Glarial's urn on the chalice

  1. Before the final interaction, make sure you have at least five free inventory spaces.
  2. Use the full Glarial's urn on the chalice/trophy.
  3. Do not simply take the chalice. The intended solution is to honour Glarial by using her ashes from the urn.
  4. The quest completes immediately and the treasure is placed in your inventory.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Only one rope is needed. The rope used on the rock is reused on the dead tree.
  • Glarial's Tomb will not let you enter? Bank your worn equipment, runes and other unrelated items. Build 254's entrance filter is broader than the short armour/weapons/runes wording used by some period guides.
  • Washed away at the waterfall door? Retrieve or re-obtain Glarial's amulet and return. Wear it before entering for the clearest period-safe route.
  • The statue hurts you instead of opening the floor? One or more of the six pillars is incomplete. Each pillar needs one Air, Earth and Water rune before you use the amulet on the statue.
  • The chalice will not complete the quest? Keep the full Glarial's urn and use it on the chalice rather than taking the chalice directly. Also free at least five inventory spaces.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 13,750 Attack XP.
  • 13,750 Strength XP.
  • 2 diamonds.
  • 2 gold bars.
  • 40 mithril seeds.

Waterfall Quest is also part of the quest progression required for Legends' Quest in the period quest manual.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest progression: build 254 tracks Almera, Hudon, the Book on Baxtorian, Glarial's Tomb, entry to the waterfall, the final puzzle area, placement of Glarial's amulet and the urn/chalice completion.
  • No mandatory combat: the quest scripts do not require a monster kill. The Waterfall Dungeon is dangerous, so the absence of a combat gate should not be confused with a safe route.
  • Tomb inventory filter: build 254 checks a broader set of worn, combat, crafting and tool categories than the simplified restrictions often printed in contemporary guides. A minimal pebble-and-food inventory is the safest approach.
  • Waterfall amulet check: the build accepts Glarial's amulet either worn or in the inventory at the ledge door. The surviving August-2004 guide tells players to wear it, so this walkthrough recommends wearing it.
  • Door-damage discrepancy: build 254 and the surviving 2004 walkthrough disagree on the exact damage dealt when entering the waterfall without the amulet. The walkthrough therefore records the consequence without publishing a disputed number.
  • Six-pillar puzzle: each pillar consumes one Air, one Earth and one Water rune, for six of each rune in total. Using Glarial's amulet on the statue before the puzzle is complete does not consume it but does trigger falling-rock damage in build 254.
  • Final inventory check: the build requires five free inventory spaces before the full urn can be used on the chalice and the reward package issued.
  • Reward package: build 254 awards 1 Quest Point, 13,750 Attack XP, 13,750 Strength XP, 2 diamonds, 2 gold bars and 40 mithril seeds.
