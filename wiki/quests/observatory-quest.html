Observatory Quest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Observatory Quest
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to the professor at the Observatory, southwest of Yanille.

The astronomer at the Observatory is in a panic: his equipment has been sabotaged and his assistant has vanished, all while a band of goblins camps suspiciously nearby. He needs someone capable of dealing with the goblins and restoring his telescope before an important celestial event passes him by.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start the quest.

Recommended

  • A weapon for dealing with the goblins

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to the Observatory building southwest of Yanille and speak to the professor inside.
  2. Learn that his telescope equipment has been damaged and a vital lens is missing.
  3. Head outside and investigate the nearby goblin camp, which the professor suspects is responsible.
  4. Search the goblins' camp and defeat any that stand in your way to recover the missing components.
  5. Find the stolen or broken parts of the telescope hidden among the goblins' belongings.
  6. Return the recovered parts to the professor so he can begin repairing his equipment.
  7. Help him complete the repairs to get the telescope back in working order.
  8. Speak to the professor once more to confirm the Observatory is fully operational again.
  9. Complete the quest and receive the professor's thanks for saving his research.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 689 experience in a skill of your choice
  • Access to the Observatory's star-viewing feature
