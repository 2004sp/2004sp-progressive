Deep within Karamja's Kharazi jungle lies the secretive Shilo Village, isolated from the outside world and wary of strangers. When word reaches you of a missing traveller who went looking for it, you set out through dense jungle, hostile tribes, and hidden caves to earn the villagers' trust and uncover the settlement's history.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A machete
  • A light source, such as a candle or lit lantern, for the caves

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison potion
  • A weapon and armour for the jungle spiders

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Mosol Rei near Brimhaven about a relative who went missing while searching for Shilo Village.
  2. Set out into the Kharazi jungle, using your machete to cut through the thick vines blocking the paths south.
  3. Locate a jungle guide who can help point you toward the hidden route to the village.
  4. Deal with the giant jungle spiders that guard parts of the jungle as you make your way through.
  5. Discover a network of caves beneath the jungle and use your light source to explore them safely.
  6. Solve the puzzle involving the totem poles and smoke, driving off the spiders blocking the way forward.
  7. Continue through the caves until you emerge near the hidden village of Shilo itself.
  8. Speak to the suspicious villagers and their elders, who are reluctant to trust an outsider.
  9. Prove your good intentions by helping resolve a dispute or problem troubling the village.
  10. Learn the village's history and the reason for its deep isolation from the rest of Karamja.
  11. Track down the missing relative you were originally sent to find and confirm their fate.
  12. Return to Mosol Rei to report what you discovered and complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 8 Quest Points
  • 12,250 coins
  • Access to Shilo Village and its gem trading store
  • The ability to buy and use Cadava berries
