Shilo Village is under siege by the undead. Rashiliyia, the Queen of the Dead, has returned and a deadly green mist hangs over the settlement. Mosol Rei asks you to take word to Trufitus, whose knowledge of the lost temple of Ah Za Rhoon may reveal how to break Rashiliyia's curse and free the village.

Requirements

Required quest

  • Jungle Potion. Build 254 makes this an explicit start gate: Mosol Rei will not begin Shilo Village until Jungle Potion is complete.
  • Druidic Ritual is therefore an indirect prerequisite because it is required for Jungle Potion.

Required levels

  • 32 Agility. Required to squeeze through the fissure into Ah Za Rhoon and again for the narrow entrance into Bervirius' Tomb.
  • 20 Crafting. Required to chisel Zadimus' Bone Shard into the Bone Key after you have discovered Rashiliyia's bone lock.
  • Combat capable of defeating Nazastarool three times. Build 254 configures the three consecutive forms at combat levels 91 (zombie), 68 (skeleton) and 93 (ghost).

Required items

  • Spade. Used to excavate the mound hiding the entrance to Ah Za Rhoon.
  • Rope. Attached to the fissure and consumed.
  • A disposable valid light source: a lit candle, lit black candle or lit torch. Build 254 consumes it when you drop it into the fissure. Lanterns are not accepted by this quest interaction.
  • Chisel. Used for the Stone Plaque, Bervirius' ivory pommel and the Bone Shard.
  • Bronze wire. Combined with the ivory beads to make the Beads of the Dead. If you bring a bronze bar instead, you will also need the normal smithing tools to make wire.
  • 3 regular Bones. Used one at a time in the three recesses of the inner skeletal door in Rashiliyia's Tomb.

Recommended

  • Good armour, a weapon and plenty of food. The final encounter is three consecutive Nazastarool forms, and the ruins also contain hostile undead.
  • Antipoison. Southern Karamja contains poisonous enemies even though poison is not itself a quest requirement.
  • Prayer-restoring supplies if you plan to use the Locating Crystal repeatedly. Build 254 makes each activation draw a small amount of Prayer.
  • Several free inventory spaces. You collect multiple scrolls, bones, a crystal, a pommel, a key and finally Rashiliyia's remains.

Before you start

This quest is a chain of archaeological clues rather than a search for a missing traveller. You will investigate Ah Za Rhoon, release the spirit of Zadimus, enter the tomb of Rashiliyia's son Bervirius, craft a protective necklace, locate Rashiliyia's tomb and finally reunite her remains with her son.

Important build-254 item note: the fissure accepts only the explicitly scripted lit candle, lit black candle or lit torch. A generic lantern may be useful elsewhere in RuneScape, but it does not satisfy this interaction.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start with Mosol Rei

  1. Travel to southern Karamja and approach Shilo Village. Find Mosol Rei outside the village on its eastern side.
  2. Speak to him about the danger. He explains that Rashiliyia, Queen of the Dead, has returned with a plague of undead and that Shilo Village is trapped behind its gates in a deadly green mist.
  3. Offer to help and agree to visit Trufitus at Tai Bwo Wannai. Mosol gives you a Wampum belt to carry the warning.

2. Ask Trufitus about Rashiliyia and Ah Za Rhoon

  1. Go north-west to Tai Bwo Wannai village and speak to Trufitus Shakaya. Show him the Wampum belt or follow the dialogue about Mosol's message.
  2. Ask about Rashiliyia, the old legend and the temple of Ah Za Rhoon. Trufitus explains that the ruined temple may contain the clues needed to defeat her.
  3. Choose the option stating that you will seriously look for Ah Za Rhoon. This is the dialogue that advances build 254 into the active quest state; Trufitus keeps the Wampum belt while you investigate.

3. Excavate the entrance to Ah Za Rhoon

  1. Search the jungle east / south-east of Tai Bwo Wannai for the suspicious mound of earth associated with Ah Za Rhoon. The old clue describes the temple as “magnificence floating on water”, pointing you toward land between bodies of water.
  2. Use your Spade on the mound. You dig down to granite and expose a narrow fissure.
  3. Use a valid lit light source on the fissure. It is dropped into the crack and burns out, revealing a long fall and an anchor point. The item is consumed.
  4. Use your Rope on the fissure. The rope is attached and consumed.
  5. Enter the fissure. You need 32 Agility; on success you squeeze through the rock and lower yourself into the underground ruins.

4. Search Ah Za Rhoon for the four main clues

Search the ruins thoroughly. The following evidence is either required for the main route or gives Trufitus the intended identification and story clues:

  1. Stone Plaque: find the marked stone and cut the plaque free. A Chisel is the simplest tool. Show it to Trufitus later and he identifies the inscription as referring to Zadimus and Ah Za Rhoon.
  2. Tattered Scroll: search the loose rocks / hidden bookcase area. Read the scroll; it contains information about Bervirius, Rashiliyia's son. Reading it is important because build 254 uses that flag to let you discover the entrance to Bervirius' Tomb.
  3. Crumpled Scroll: search the old sacks and then read the scroll about Rashiliyia. Its information about a protective ward is also used by the necklace-crafting logic.
  4. Zadimus' remains: search the ancient gallows and remove the corpse from the noose.

You can leave the ruins by the dangerous waterfall path, and the ruined table also has a period raft interaction. Once you have the important clues, return to Trufitus.

5. Release Zadimus and obtain the Bone Shard

  1. Show Trufitus the items you found. In particular, show him Zadimus' remains and ask where sacred ground can be found.
  2. He tells you that the ground in the centre of Tai Bwo Wannai village is sacred.
  3. Take Zadimus' remains there and use their bury/drop interaction. At the correct sacred ground, you bury the corpse and Zadimus' spirit appears.
  4. Listen to his clue: “I am the key, but only kin may approach her.” A Bone Shard rises from the ground and is added to your inventory.
  5. Speak to Trufitus again if you want the clue explained. Rashiliyia's kin was her son Bervirius, whose tomb lies on a small island to the south-west.

6. Enter Bervirius' Tomb on Cairn Isle

  1. Travel south-west to Cairn Isle. Reach the island by the rock / bridge route. The crossing can fail and damage you, so keep your Hitpoints healthy.
  2. Make sure you have read the Tattered Scroll from Ah Za Rhoon.
  3. Search the stacked rocks hiding the tomb entrance. With the scroll clue active, you find a narrow crawl-way.
  4. Choose to enter. This squeeze requires 32 Agility and can fail for minor damage; retry if necessary.
  5. Inside, search Bervirius' dolmen. Take the ivory sword pommel, Locating Crystal and Bervirius' notes.

7. Make the Beads of the Dead

  1. Use your Chisel on the ivory sword pommel. The build checks for at least 15 Crafting and turns it into ivory / bone beads.
  2. Make sure you have read the Crumpled Scroll from Ah Za Rhoon; build 254 will not let the beads and wire combine until that ward information is known.
  3. Use the beads with Bronze wire. At 16 Crafting or higher they become the Beads of the Dead.
  4. You may show the necklace and Bervirius' notes to Trufitus. He confirms that the necklace should protect you from Rashiliyia and that the notes place her tomb north of Ah Za Rhoon.

8. Find Rashiliyia's hidden tomb and make the Bone Key

  1. Return to the jungle north of Ah Za Rhoon. Search the suspicious palm trees to reveal an ancient doorway in the hillside.
  2. Examine / search the closed door first. You discover that its strange lock is made of bone. This matters: build 254 does not allow the Bone Shard to become a key until the bone-lock discovery flag is set.
  3. Use your Chisel on the Bone Shard. With 20 Crafting and the lock discovered, you carve a Bone Key.
  4. Use the Bone Key on the hidden door and enter the cavern.
  5. Wear the Beads of the Dead before passing the inner gate. The gate is carved with a figure wearing something around its neck. Without the necklace worn, Rashiliyia can manifest and block / attack you; with it worn, the beads glow and you can proceed.

9. Open the skeletal door

  1. Continue through the tomb until you reach the ornate door depicting skeletal warriors.
  2. The door has three empty bone recesses. Use one regular Bone on the door, then a second, then a third.
  3. Each bone is consumed. After the third is fitted, the skeletal carvings come alive and the door opens.

10. Defeat Nazastarool's three forms

  1. Prepare for combat before touching / searching Rashiliyia's final dolmen.
  2. Search the dolmen to summon Nazastarool. In build 254 the first form is a level-91 zombie.
  3. Defeat it and continue the encounter. The second form is a level-68 skeleton.
  4. Defeat the skeleton. The third and final form is a level-93 ghost.
  5. Kill the ghost, then search the dolmen again and take Rashiliyia's mummified remains.

Period/build divergence: the preserved August-2004 LostHQ walkthrough recorded Nazastarool as levels 86, 65 and 88. Build 254 uses 91, 68 and 93 in its NPC configs, so this guide gives the build values for practical combat preparation.

11. Put Rashiliyia to rest

  1. Leave Rashiliyia's tomb. The Bone Key can be used on the inside of the outer door if needed.
  2. Return to Bervirius' Tomb on Cairn Isle.
  3. Use Rashiliyia's remains on Bervirius' dolmen.
  4. Rashiliyia appears, explains that her hatred was corrupted after Zamorak returned her son as an undead creature, and thanks you for releasing her spirit.
  5. The curse is broken and Shilo Village is freed. Quest complete.

Combat and hazard notes

  • Nazastarool is the mandatory fight. The build uses three separately configured forms at levels 91, 68 and 93, with 70, 70 and 80 Hitpoints respectively.
  • Undead in Ah Za Rhoon: the ruins contain hostile skeletons and zombies. They are not the final objective, but they can interfere while you search.
  • Agility failures: the Cairn Isle bridge, tomb squeeze and several rocky passages can fail and deal damage.
  • Rashiliyia's protection gate: merely carrying the Beads of the Dead is not enough for the inner tomb gate; wear them.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost a clue item? Several Shilo quest artefacts have special recovery / re-obtain behaviour in their original locations. Re-search the relevant scenery before assuming the quest is stuck.
  • Bone Key will not craft: first inspect Rashiliyia's hidden outer door and discover that its lock is made from bone. Only then use the Chisel on Zadimus' Bone Shard.
  • Beads will not craft: read the Crumpled Scroll, chisel Bervirius' ivory pommel into beads, and combine those beads with Bronze wire.
  • Cannot pass the inner gate: equip the Beads of the Dead in your necklace slot.
  • Locating Crystal: the crystal is optional navigation help. It glows more strongly as you approach Rashiliyia's tomb and drains a small amount of Prayer per activation in build 254.
  • Boss progress in build 254: the three Nazastarool defeats are tracked as separate quest bits. The source does not reset a defeated form merely because you leave the room, despite a contemporary walkthrough warning players not to retreat.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 3,875 Crafting XP.
  • Full access to Shilo Village. The normal gates become usable once the quest is complete.
  • Access to Shilo's services and gem rocks.
  • Cart travel between the Shilo area and Brimhaven. The build includes Vigroy's paid cart journey from Shilo to Brimhaven.
  • Up to 2,000 coins from leftover quest artefacts. Yanni Salika can buy the Bone Key, Stone Plaque, Tattered Scroll, Crumpled Scroll, Bervirius' notes, Locating Crystal and Beads of the Dead for a combined maximum of 2,000 coins if you still possess them.

There is no direct 12,250-coin quest reward, and Cadava berries are unrelated to Shilo Village.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start gate: mosol_rei.rs2 refuses the quest while Jungle Potion is incomplete. The quest journal independently lists Jungle Potion, 20 Crafting and 32 Agility as the prerequisites.
  • Quest-state chain: build 254 tracks the mound, excavation, illumination, rope, Ah Za Rhoon entry, Bervirius' Tomb, Rashiliyia's outer door, Beads gate, skeletal door and recovered corpse as distinct stages.
  • Specific fissure items: the Ah Za Rhoon interaction accepts lit_candle, lit_black_candle or torch_lit. It deletes the light source after illumination and deletes the Rope when attached.
  • Required crafting logic: the ivory pommel needs 15 Crafting with a Chisel; beads plus Bronze wire need 16 Crafting and the Crumpled Scroll flag; the Bone Shard needs 20 Crafting plus the discovered bone-lock flag to become the Bone Key.
  • Beads gate: the inner tomb gate explicitly checks the worn inventory for zqdeadbeads. Without them worn, Rashiliyia's summon logic runs.
  • Skeletal door: three ordinary bones are consumed one at a time. The third advances the quest to the unlocked-tomb-door state.
  • Nazastarool: build 254 configures the zombie, skeleton and ghost forms at levels 91, 68 and 93. Separate bits record each defeat before Rashiliyia's corpse becomes obtainable.
  • Completion: placing Rashiliyia's corpse on Bervirius' dolmen queues quest completion, advances Crafting by 38750 internal tenths (3,875 XP) and awards the quest-point constant ^zombiequeen_questpoints = 2.
  • Post-quest economy: Yanni's build script offers 100 coins each for five minor artefacts, 500 for the Locating Crystal and 1,000 for the Beads of the Dead, matching the period guide's “up to 2,000 coins” note.
  • Period evidence: LostHQ's Shilo Village guide was entered on 10 March 2004 and last updated on 2 August 2004, before this wiki's September-2004 target. Its main route, requirements and reward values agree with the reconstructed quest logic; where its old monster levels differ from build 254, the divergence is stated above rather than silently mixing eras.
