Plague City

Plague City
Access

Start point
Speak to Edmond, a worried man near the sealed gate to West Ardougne.

West Ardougne has been sealed off by order of King Lathas, its citizens quarantined behind a wall after reports of a deadly plague. A man named Edmond fears for his sister trapped inside and asks you to sneak past the guards to find her, setting you on the trail of a conspiracy far darker than any disease.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start the quest.

Recommended

  • Food, in case of combat with guards or ghasts

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Edmond near the barricaded gate on the eastern edge of West Ardougne.
  2. Agree to sneak into the walled-off city to search for his missing sister, Elena.
  3. Find a way past the guards blocking the main gate, such as using a disguise or an alternative route.
  4. Explore the decayed streets of West Ardougne, dealing with the plague-touched ghasts that lurk there.
  5. Track down Elena hiding within the city and speak to her about what has really been happening.
  6. Learn that the plague story is being used by King Lathas as a pretext to control the population of West Ardougne.
  7. Gather what evidence and information you can from Elena and the other residents about the true situation.
  8. Make your way back out of the city and report your findings to Edmond.
  9. Speak to Edmond to conclude the quest, setting the stage for further investigation.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
  • Access to explore West Ardougne
  • Progression into the Biohazard storyline
