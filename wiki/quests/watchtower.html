Watch Tower

Quest guide · September 2004 target · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Watch Tower
Access
Members
Released
7 May 2003
Period length
Long
Quest points
4
Start
Watchtower Wizard, on the top floor of the Watch Tower by Yanille
Required levels
14 Magic, 14 Herblore, 15 Thieving, 25 Agility, 40 Mining
Combat
Defeat Gorad and survive the Ogre Enclave

The magical shield protecting Yanille from the ogres has failed because its four powering crystals were stolen. Help the Watchtower Wizard trace the thieves, win the trust of the ogre tribes, learn enough Skavid speech to recover the crystals, destroy the protected ogre shamans and reactivate the Watch Tower.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • 14 Magic — required to use the enchanted potion on an ogre shaman.
  • 14 Herblore — required to make the ogre potion.
  • 15 Thieving — required to steal a rock cake in Gu'Tanoth.
  • 25 Agility — required at the Gu'Tanoth chasm. The initial climb into the Watch Tower itself only checks for level 18 in build 254.
  • 40 Mining — required to mine the final crystal from the Rock of Dalgroth.

Required or route-critical items

  • 20 coins — paid to an ogre guard before the 25-Agility chasm jump.
  • 1 gold bar — consumed by the Gu'Tanoth guard who demands gold.
  • 1 death rune — the answer to the city guard's riddle; consumed in exchange for a Skavid map.
  • 1 set of dragon bones — given to Toban.
  • Rope. The route uses ogre-island rope swings; bringing 2 ropes matches the period route and avoids having to source another while travelling.
  • A lit light source for the Skavid caves. Bring a tinderbox as backup if your light can go out.
  • Guam leaf + vial of water, jangerberries, and ground bat bones for the ogre potion. If bringing ordinary bat bones instead, also bring a pestle and mortar.
  • A pickaxe usable at your Mining level.
  • 2 cave nightshades for the two necessary entries past the Ogre Enclave guard. These can be collected during the Skavid-cave section.

Recommended

  • Weapon, armour and food for Gorad and the ogres on the route.
  • Anti-dragon shield and extra food for running through the Ogre Enclave, where blue dragons are present.
  • 43 Prayer if you want Protect from Melee available for the dangerous sections.
  • Collect jangerberries while visiting Grew's island so you do not need a later return trip.

Before you start

The September-2004 route is a long multi-stage quest. The target-proximate August-2004 guide lists 14 Magic, 14 Herblore, 15 Thieving, 25 Agility and 40 Mining, matching build 254's quest journal and hard checks. An older 2003 Jagex manual listed 30 Agility, so the page uses the later, target-appropriate 25-Agility requirement.

Do not confuse later Watchtower guides with this build at the ending: build 254 does not make you place the four crystals on coloured pillars. Once the wizard has recognised that you carry all four crystals, you only need to pull the lever.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the quest with the Watchtower Wizard

  1. Go to the Watch Tower by Yanille. Before the quest starts, the tower guard blocks the normal ladder.
  2. Use the climbing route on the outside wall to squeeze in through the upper window, then climb to the top floor. Build 254 checks for 18 Agility on this entry, but you need 25 Agility later anyway.
  3. Speak to the Watchtower Wizard. Ask what is wrong and offer to help when he explains that the four crystals powering Yanille's anti-ogre spell have been stolen.
  4. He asks you to search the area for evidence.

2. Find the Skavid fingernails

  1. Search the bushes around the Watch Tower.
  2. Several bushes contain red-herring objects such as an old robe, unusual armour, a damaged dagger or a tattered eye patch. These do not advance the quest.
  3. Find the bush containing finger nails and take them back to the Watchtower Wizard.
  4. Show him the fingernails. He identifies them as belonging to a Skavid, a servant race of the ogres, and tells you to investigate the ogre tribes around Gu'Tanoth.

3. Help Og, Grew and Toban

You need three pieces of an old ogre relic. The three tribal tasks can be progressed in whichever practical order you reach them.

  1. Og: ask about entering the ogre city. Og wants his stolen gold back from Toban and gives you Toban's key. Use the key on Toban's chest to recover the stolen gold, then return it to Og for relic part 1.
  2. Grew: offer to help him. He wants one of his enemy Gorad's teeth. Find and defeat Gorad, take the ogre tooth, and return it to Grew. He gives you relic part 2 and the first powering crystal.
  3. Toban: offer to prove your strength. Give Toban dragon bones and he gives you relic part 3.
  4. While travelling through this area, collect the jangerberries needed for the potion later.

4. Have the wizard assemble the ogre relic

  1. Return to the Watchtower Wizard with all three relic parts.
  2. Use each relic part on him. Trying to combine the pieces yourself only tells you that someone with experience is needed.
  3. After receiving all three parts, the wizard assembles them into a complete ogre relic.

5. Enter Gu'Tanoth and obtain the Skavid map

  1. Take the complete ogre relic to the relevant Gu'Tanoth gate guard. The guard initially demands proof that you are a friend of the ogres; show or use the complete relic to gain passage.
  2. At the market, steal a rock cake from the cake counter. This requires 15 Thieving. If the nearby ogre trader catches you, he attacks instead.
  3. Give the rock cake to the battlement guard who asks for a surprising market gift, then climb over the low wall.
  4. At the guarded chasm, pay 20 coins and make the jump. Build 254 requires 25 Agility here.
  5. Speak to the city guard who controls access to the Skavid caves. He gives you a riddle whose answer is a death rune.
  6. Use a death rune on the guard. He consumes it and gives you a Skavid map.

6. Learn the Skavid language

Carry the Skavid map and a lit light source. Build 254 will not let you navigate normally without the map. Entering without a lit light source sends you into a dark cave where you must find the escape rope.

  1. Find and speak to the scared Skavid. Choose the non-threatening response. It teaches you the common words ar, nod, gor, ig, cur.
  2. Visit the four ordinary Skavids and answer their phrases correctly:
  • Cur bidith → answer Ig.
  • Gor cur → answer Ar.
  • Bidith tanath → answer Cur.
  • Tanath gor → answer Nod.

Collect two cave nightshades while working through the caves. You need one for each trip into the Ogre Enclave.

7. Pass the gold guard and answer the mad Skavid

  1. Continue through Gu'Tanoth toward the cave containing the mad Skavid. A guard on this route demands a gold bar; give him one to gain passage.
  2. Speak to the mad Skavid. It chooses one of four phrases. Give the matching reply:
  • Ar cur → Gor.
  • Bidith ig → Cur.
  • Cur tanath → Bidith.
  • Gor nod → Tanath.

A correct answer earns the second powering crystal.

8. Enter the Ogre Enclave for the first time

  1. Go to the Ogre Enclave entrance and use cave nightshade on the enclave guard. The nightshade is consumed and the distracted guard lets you run past.
  2. Do not talk to or attack the ogre shamans. Their magical protection retaliates for heavy damage, and ordinary combat is not the intended solution.
  3. Run through the enclave, avoiding the blue dragons, and leave through the far exit.
  4. Return to the Watchtower Wizard and tell him about the shamans. He teaches you how to make a potion that can destroy them.

9. Make and enchant the ogre potion

Use the safe ingredient order below. Build 254 contains failure paths that consume the ingredients, leave an empty vial and deal damage when incompatible stages are mixed.

  1. Add a guam leaf to a vial of water to make the normal unfinished guam mixture.
  2. Add jangerberries.
  3. Grind bat bones with a pestle and mortar if necessary, then add the ground bat bones. This creates an ogre potion.
  4. Take the ogre potion to the Watchtower Wizard. He infuses it with magic and returns a magic ogre potion.

10. Destroy the six ogre shamans

  1. Return to the Ogre Enclave and use your second cave nightshade on the guard to enter again.
  2. Use the magic ogre potion on each of the six ogre shamans. Build 254 checks for at least 14 Magic when the potion is used.
  3. The potion destroys the shamans directly; do not fight them conventionally.
  4. The sixth shaman yields the third powering crystal. The potion is then consumed and an empty vial is returned.

11. Mine the fourth crystal

  1. After all six shamans are gone, find the Rock of Dalgroth inside the enclave.
  2. Use a pickaxe and 40 Mining to mine it.
  3. You prise out the fourth powering crystal.

12. Restore the Watch Tower

  1. Make sure all four powering crystals are in your inventory: the one from Grew, the mad Skavid's crystal, the shaman crystal and the Rock of Dalgroth crystal.
  2. Return to the Watchtower Wizard and speak to him or show him the crystals. Once build 254 detects all four, he tells you to activate the shield generator.
  3. Do not look for a crystal-placement puzzle in this build. The build-254 quest journal explicitly notes that the 2004 route has no crystal placing at this stage.
  4. Pull the lever. The magical forcefield activates and the quest completes.

Powering crystal checklist

  • Crystal 1: Grew gives it with relic part 2 after you bring him Gorad's tooth.
  • Crystal 2: the mad Skavid gives it after you learn the language and answer correctly.
  • Crystal 3: obtained when the sixth ogre shaman is destroyed with the magic ogre potion.
  • Crystal 4: mined from the Rock of Dalgroth after all six shamans are gone.

Danger and recovery notes

  • Dark Skavid cave: the map is mandatory for normal navigation. A lit light source is also needed; without one, build 254 sends you to a dark escape area rather than letting you proceed normally.
  • Lost Skavid map: after solving the riddle, the city guard can provide another copy if you no longer carry one.
  • Lost relic parts: Og, Grew and Toban each have replacement dialogue for the relic part they awarded if it is no longer in your carried inventory.
  • Lost first crystal: Grew can replace his crystal if you no longer carry it. The check is inventory-based.
  • Lost second crystal: the mad Skavid can replace it, with additional checks for an existing copy.
  • Lost third crystal: after all six shamans have been destroyed, searching the shaman robe can recover the shaman crystal if you do not already have it.
  • Ogre shamans: talking to a shaman triggers a 20-damage magical blast in build 254; attacking one can trigger a 30-damage retaliation. Use the enchanted potion instead.
  • Wrong potion order: volatile mixtures can explode, consume ingredients and deal 5 damage in build 254. Guam first, then jangerberries, then ground bat bones is the safe route.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 5,000 coins.
  • Build 254: 15,300 Magic XP.
  • Spell scroll teaching Watchtower Teleport after it is read.
  • Access to the quest's Gu'Tanoth and Ogre Enclave progression.

Historical reward discrepancy: the target-proximate LostHQ guide, last updated 2 August 2004, records 12,000 Magic XP, while build 254's completion script awards 15,300 Magic XP. This guide reports the build reward for playability and preserves the period value here rather than silently merging the two.

After completion, read the spell scroll. Build 254 does not allow Watchtower Teleport until the scroll has been read. The spell itself requires 58 Magic, 2 earth runes and 2 law runes.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Period name: the 2003 manual, August-2004 guide and build quest journal use Watch Tower. The quest was renamed Watchtower years later; this page keeps the existing `watchtower.html` slug for link compatibility.
  • Skill requirements: build 254's journal lists 14 Magic, 40 Mining, 14 Herblore, 15 Thieving and 25 Agility. The 2003 official manual preserved by the RuneScape Classic Wiki lists 30 Agility, while the August-2004 guide uses 25, so 25 is the September-2004 target value.
  • Quest states: the build tracks the fingernail clue, assembled relic, city-guard riddle, Skavid crystal, nightshade entry, potion knowledge, enchanted potion, all-crystals state and completion separately, with additional bitfields for the three ogre favours, Skavid vocabulary and six shaman kills.
  • Rock cake: the market counter directly checks for 15 Thieving and awards the cake only when the nearby trader does not catch you.
  • Skavid cave check: despite an internal procedure comment mentioning a tinderbox, the actual build check requires the Skavid map and an already-lit light-source category item.
  • Potion: the Wizard's intended recipe is guam, jangerberries and ground bat bones. The safe build route is the normal unfinished guam potion first, then jangerberries, then ground bat bones.
  • Shamans: six successful uses are tracked. The sixth gives the third crystal and consumes the magic ogre potion.
  • Final crystal: the Rock of Dalgroth requires 40 Mining and a usable pickaxe after all six shamans have been destroyed.
  • No pillar placement in build 254: once the Wizard recognises all four crystals, pulling the lever is sufficient. Later RuneScape/Old School guides that instruct you to place four coloured crystals on pillars do not describe this build's ending.
  • Completion reward: the build adds 4 Quest Points, 5,000 coins, a spell scroll and 15,300 Magic XP. The contemporary August-2004 guide instead records 12,000 Magic XP; the discrepancy is intentionally documented.
  • Teleport unlock: reading the reward scroll advances the post-completion state. Build 254 then allows Watchtower Teleport at level 58 for 2 earth runes and 2 law runes.
