Start point
Speak to Veronica at the ruined watchtower south of Yanille.

An ancient watchtower once used to spot orc invasions from across the plains has fallen silent, and its keeper Veronica fears the worst: an approaching orc army with no one left to sound the alarm. To restore the tower's power you must brew a shrinking potion and venture into a world scaled far smaller than your own.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • No items are required to start; ingredients for the shrinking potion are gathered during the quest.

Recommended

  • Food
  • A weapon and armour for the orc encounters

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to the ruined watchtower south of Yanille and speak to Veronica about the failing tower.
  2. Learn that the tower's scrying crystal has stopped working, leaving the region blind to an orc invasion.
  3. Agree to help repair the tower's mechanism, which requires you to become small enough to access its inner workings.
  4. Gather the ingredients needed to brew a shrinking potion, searching various locations for rare components.
  5. Return to Veronica or her associate to have the potion prepared once all ingredients are collected.
  6. Drink the shrinking potion and find yourself reduced to a tiny fraction of your normal size.
  7. Navigate the tower's interior from this new miniature perspective, avoiding creatures that are now enormous threats.
  8. Solve the mechanical puzzle inside the tower to restore power to the scrying crystal.
  9. Escape back out of the tower's inner workings and return to normal size once the repairs are complete.
  10. Use the restored crystal to scry out the orc army massing beyond the plains.
  11. Report the scale of the coming invasion to Veronica and help prepare a warning for the region.
  12. Speak to Veronica one final time to conclude the quest.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • 9,000 Herblore experience
  • 9,000 Crafting experience
  • Access to the Orb of Oculus item for remote map viewing
