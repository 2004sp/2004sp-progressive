Druidic Ritual

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Druidic Ritual
Access
Members
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Short
Quest points
4
Start point
Speak to Kaqemeex, a druid found at the stone circle just north of Taverley.

The druids of Taverley are trying to restore the old ways of Guthix, brewing potions and performing rites their order once knew by heart. Kaqemeex needs a capable outsider to prove trustworthy enough to be let in on the secrets of Herblore, but first he must be sure you're willing to help defend the circle and gather what's needed for the ritual.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • This quest supplies the ingredients you need as you go; nothing must be brought from home

Recommended

  • Armour and a weapon in case barbarians interfere

Walkthrough

  1. Head north of Taverley to the ancient stone circle and speak to the druid Kaqemeex.
  2. Agree to help him revive the old druidic Herblore traditions.
  3. Deal with the barbarians who keep harassing the druids at the circle, driving them off or fighting them as needed.
  4. Follow Kaqemeex's instructions to collect the herbs and ingredients he needs for a demonstration potion.
  5. Bring the ingredients back to him at the circle.
  6. Watch as Kaqemeex mixes the ingredients, then follow his lead to prove you can do the same.
  7. Help complete the ritual so the druids can safely resume their practices.
  8. Speak to Kaqemeex once more after the ritual is finished to be formally taught the basics of Herblore.
  9. Collect your reward and quest completion confirmation from Kaqemeex.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • The Herblore skill is unlocked
  • Some starting Herblore experience
  • Coins
