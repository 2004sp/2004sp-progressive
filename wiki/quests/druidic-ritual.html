The druids once used the stone circle south of Varrock, but dark wizards corrupted it and made it useless to their magic. Kaqemeex asks you to help prepare a purification ritual. Sanfew needs four different kinds of raw meat dipped individually into the Cauldron of Thunder; complete that task and Kaqemeex will teach you the fundamentals of Herblore.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None. The period Jagex quest manual lists no minimum requirements.
  • There is no skill-level or Quest Point gate to start Druidic Ritual.
  • You do, however, need to get through the Cauldron room entrance, where two level-19 Suits of armour animate one at a time.

Required items

Bring one of each of the following four meats. You may obtain them before starting the quest:

  • 1 raw beef — dipped in the Cauldron of Thunder and later consumed by Sanfew.
  • 1 raw chicken — dipped in the Cauldron of Thunder and later consumed by Sanfew.
  • 1 raw rat meat — dipped in the Cauldron of Thunder and later consumed by Sanfew.
  • 1 raw bear meat — dipped in the Cauldron of Thunder and later consumed by Sanfew.

The quest does not supply these meats. Build 254 accepts the ordinary raw-meat items and converts each one into its blue enchanted quest version at the cauldron.

Recommended

  • A weapon to deal with the two level-19 Suits of armour quickly.
  • Basic armour and food if your combat level is low. The dungeon contains other hostile creatures as well, although no separate monster kill is part of the ritual itself.
  • Carry all four raw meats before entering Taverley Dungeon so you only need one cauldron trip.

Before you start

The efficient route is to collect the four raw meats first, then start the quest with Kaqemeex. Sanfew only needs the four specific meats; there is no herb-gathering stage, demonstration potion, barbarian-defence task or coin payment in the September-2004 route.

The Cauldron of Thunder is in Taverley Dungeon, south-west of the town. The contemporary March-2004 walkthrough describes the entrance as being among a clump of dead trees.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Kaqemeex

  1. Go to the Druids' stone circle north of Taverley. Speak to Kaqemeex.
  2. Ask for a quest. Kaqemeex explains that the stone circle south of Varrock once belonged to the druids, but dark wizards cursed it and corrupted its power.
  3. Agree to help. Choose the option to help purify the circle. Kaqemeex sends you south into Taverley to speak to his fellow druid Sanfew, who is preparing the ritual.

2. Get Sanfew's instructions

  1. Find Sanfew in Taverley. The contemporary 2004 guide places him upstairs in the Herblore shop — the first floor above ground.
  2. Tell him Kaqemeex sent you. Sanfew says he needs the raw meat of four different animals for the potion used to honour Guthix.
  3. Note the exact ingredients. You need raw bear meat, raw chicken, raw rat meat and raw beef.
  4. Each meat must be dipped separately. Sanfew directs you to the Cauldron of Thunder in the underground halls south of Taverley. Speaking to him advances the quest so the cauldron can enchant the meats.

3. Reach the Cauldron of Thunder

  1. Enter Taverley Dungeon. From Taverley, head south and a little west to the dungeon entrance among the dead trees, then climb down.
  2. Head toward the nearby prison-style door. The Cauldron room is close to the dungeon entrance.
  3. Try to open the door. From outside the room, the first nearby Suit of armour comes to life and attacks.
  4. Defeat the first level-19 Suit of armour. Then try the door again; the second Suit of armour animates.
  5. Defeat the second Suit of armour. After both have been dealt with, open the door and enter the Cauldron room.

4. Enchant the four meats

  1. Use each raw meat on the Cauldron of Thunder. Do this once for raw beef, raw chicken, raw rat meat and raw bear meat.
  2. Check the result after each use. Each piece becomes a blue enchanted version of that meat. The four raw meats are replaced one-for-one; no extra ingredient is required.
  3. Leave with all four enchanted meats. Sanfew will not finish his hand-in until you have the full set.

5. Return to Sanfew and Kaqemeex

  1. Return to Sanfew in Taverley. With all four enchanted meats in your inventory, speak to him.
  2. Hand over the enchanted meats. Sanfew takes one enchanted beef, rat meat, bear meat and chicken. He tells you the ritual preparations are ready and sends you back to Kaqemeex.
  3. Return to Kaqemeex at the stone circle. He has heard from Sanfew that you helped with the purification preparations.
  4. Quest complete. Kaqemeex rewards you and begins explaining the fundamentals of Herblore: vials, herbs, ingredients, pestle and mortar use, and a basic Attack potion example.

Combat encounters

  • Suit of armour — level 19: two guard the prison door outside the Cauldron room. In build 254 they animate sequentially when the door is used from outside, so the intended route is to defeat one, try the door again, defeat the second, then enter.
  • No boss fight is required, and the quest does not ask you to kill barbarians or dark wizards.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Meats can be pre-collected: nothing in the quest tracks where or when the four raw meats were obtained.
  • Speak to Sanfew before using the cauldron: build 254 only performs the quest-meat transformation at the correct quest stage.
  • Missing a meat at Sanfew: he only removes the ingredients when the full enchanted set is present, so go back for the missing raw meat, enchant it at the cauldron and return.
  • No coin reward: the September-2004 reward is Quest Points, Herblore experience and the Herblore introduction/access; the old local guide's coin reward was unsupported.
  • Post-quest help: build 254 allows Kaqemeex to explain the Herblore fundamentals again after completion.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 250 Herblore experience.
  • Access/introduction to the Herblore skill.

There is no coin or item reward in the September-2004 reward set used by this guide.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 progresses from Kaqemeex start → Sanfew instructions → enchanted-meat hand-in → Kaqemeex completion. The quest journal explicitly names the four required meats and the Cauldron of Thunder.
  • Exact cauldron conversions: raw_beef, raw_chicken, raw_rat_meat and raw_bear_meat become their corresponding enchanted quest items only while the quest is at Sanfew's cauldron stage.
  • Armour guards: the dungeon door script has two separate Suit-of-armour positions. Each outside door attempt removes one scenery suit, spawns a level-19 suit_of_armour NPC and makes it retaliate; once both scenery suits are gone, the door opens normally.
  • Reward: completion advances Herblore by 2500 in the project's tenths-of-XP convention, giving 250 Herblore XP, and the quest-point constant is 4.
  • Herblore gating: the build's ordinary herb-identifying and potion-brewing scripts check membership and Herblore level but do not check Druidic Ritual completion. Period quest material describes the quest as the route to Herblore training/access, so the public reward keeps that historical wording while noting that build 254 does not enforce a universal quest-state gate on every Herblore action.
  • Dialogue provenance: some reconstructed post-quest Kaqemeex dialogue is explicitly marked in source as being taken from RuneScape Classic because a suitable pre-skillcape RS2 recording was not found. Exact post-quest wording is therefore not treated as direct September-2004 evidence.
