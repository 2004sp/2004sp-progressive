Shield of Arrav

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Shield of Arrav
Access

Medium

Start point
Talk to Katrine in Varrock or Straven near Lumbridge, depending on which gang you wish to join.

Beneath the streets of Varrock, two rival gangs have been feuding for years over territory, pride, and a legendary shield split cleanly in two. Joining one side means earning trust through dangerous errands and a fair bit of espionage against the other gang. Only by working with someone on the opposing side can the shield ever be made whole again.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest
  • A second player who has joined the opposing gang, needed to assemble the complete shield

Walkthrough

  1. Decide which of Varrock's two rival gangs you want to join: the Black Arm Gang or the Phoenix Gang.
  2. For the Black Arm Gang, find Katrine at their hideout in Varrock; for the Phoenix Gang, find Straven at their hideout near Lumbridge.
  3. Convince the gang leader to let you join and prove your loyalty by completing a small task they set for you.
  4. Once accepted, you'll be sent on an errand into enemy territory to steal information or an item from the rival gang.
  5. Sneak into the rival gang's base and retrieve what you were sent for, avoiding trouble where you can.
  6. Bring the stolen item or information back to your gang leader.
  7. Receive your gang's half of an ancient shield as a reward for your service.
  8. Find another player who has completed the same steps for the opposing gang and is holding the other half of the shield.
  9. Trade or combine the two halves together to form the complete Shield of Arrav.
  10. Take the assembled shield to the Varrock Museum curator to have it authenticated and claim your final reward.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
  • The completed Shield of Arrav, which can be kept or sold
