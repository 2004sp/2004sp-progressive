Shield of Arrav is a free-to-play quest about recovering Varrock's stolen royal shield. It requires two cooperating players: one must join the Phoenix Gang and the other must join the Black Arm Gang.

Requirements

Required levels

  • None.

Required players

  • 2 players, with one joining the Phoenix Gang and the other joining the Black Arm Gang.

Required items

  • The Phoenix Gang player needs 20 coins to pay Baraek for information.

Two-player requirement: the quest cannot be completed alone in this build. One player must take the Phoenix Gang route and the other must take the Black Arm Gang route.

Before you start

Decide which player will join each gang. The Phoenix player will obtain a key that the Black Arm player needs, and each gang member will recover a different half of the Shield of Arrav.

Keep enough inventory space for quest items. The players will need to trade the Phoenix key, a shield half, and later one of the certificates.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Investigate the Shield of Arrav

  1. Speak to Reldo in the library inside Varrock Palace and ask about a quest.
  2. Reldo tells you that a book in the library contains information about the Shield of Arrav. Search the bookcases until you find The Shield of Arrav, then read it.
  3. Speak to Reldo again and ask where the Phoenix Gang can be found. He directs you to Baraek, the fur trader in Varrock marketplace.

Build-254 note: a player taking the Black Arm route can also enter that branch directly by speaking to the tramp near the Blue Moon Inn. Reldo is the historical quest start and is the clearest way to begin the quest.

2A. Phoenix Gang player

  1. Speak to Baraek in Varrock marketplace about the Phoenix Gang. Pay him 20 coins for the location.
  2. Go to the Phoenix Gang hideout, operating as the VTAM Corporation in south-east Varrock, and speak to Straven.
  3. Ask to join. Straven gives you a test: obtain an Intelligence Report from Jonny the Beard.
  4. Go to the Blue Moon Inn, kill Jonny the Beard (level 2), and pick up the Intelligence Report he drops.
  5. Return the report to Straven. He admits you to the Phoenix Gang and gives you a Phoenix key for the gang's weapon store.
  6. Search the chest inside the Phoenix Gang headquarters to obtain the Phoenix half of the Shield of Arrav.
  7. Trade the Phoenix key to your Black Arm partner. They need it to enter the Phoenix weapon store and steal the crossbows required for their initiation.

2B. Black Arm Gang player

  1. Speak to the tramp near the Blue Moon Inn and ask about the nearby alley. He reveals the Black Arm Gang hideout and points you toward Katrine.
  2. Enter the Black Arm Gang headquarters and speak to Katrine. Ask to join the gang.
  3. Katrine requires two Phoenix crossbows as proof that you can be trusted.
  4. Receive the Phoenix key from your Phoenix Gang partner.
  5. Use the key to enter the Phoenix Gang weapon store in south-east Varrock and take two Phoenix crossbows.
  6. Return to Katrine with both crossbows. She takes them and admits you to the Black Arm Gang.
  7. Search the cupboard inside the Black Arm headquarters to obtain the Black Arm half of the Shield of Arrav.

Watch the Weaponsmaster: the Phoenix Gang Weaponsmaster can attack the Black Arm player while the crossbows are being stolen.

3. Authenticate the shield

  1. Meet up and trade one shield half so that one player is carrying both halves.
  2. That player takes both halves to the curator at Varrock Museum.
  3. The curator authenticates the shield, removes both halves from the player's inventory, and gives that player two Shield of Arrav certificates.
  4. Trade one certificate to the other player, so that each player has one.

Do not try to combine the two shield halves. Build 254 has no player-assembled completed-shield step. Give both separate halves to the museum curator; he exchanges them for two full certificates.

4. Claim the reward

  1. Each player takes their own certificate to King Roald in Varrock Palace.
  2. Give the certificate to the king. He explains that the 1,200-coin bounty is being split between the two people who recovered the shield.
  3. Each player receives 600 coins and 1 Quest Point, completing the quest for that player.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 600 coins per player.

The shield halves are taken by the museum curator. There is no completed Shield of Arrav item for the players to keep or sell.

2004scape implementation notes

  • The build-254 quest scripts support the two exclusive gang branches and require cooperation between a Phoenix Gang member and a Black Arm Gang member.
  • The Phoenix route uses Baraek's 20-coin information fee, Jonny the Beard's Intelligence Report, and Straven's Phoenix key.
  • The Black Arm route requires two Phoenix crossbows, after which Katrine grants gang membership and the second shield half becomes available.
  • When one player gives the curator both shield halves, the build gives that player two complete certificates. The players then split those certificates and each separately reports to King Roald.
  • King Roald awards 600 coins and 1 Quest Point to each completing player.
