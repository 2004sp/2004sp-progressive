Dragon Slayer

Start point
Speak to the Guildmaster inside the Champions' Guild, south of Varrock.

Rumours tell of a fearsome green dragon named Elvarg who lairs on the volcanic island of Crandor, having driven off or slain every previous challenger who dared face her. The Guildmaster of the Champions' Guild has heard enough tales of your exploits to think you might finally be the adventurer capable of ending her reign of terror. Prove your courage, find a way to the forbidden island, and slay the dragon once and for all.

Requirements

  • 32 Quest Points

Items needed

  • Coins for supplies and travel
  • A weapon and armour suitable for fighting a dragon

Recommended

  • Anti-dragon shield
  • Food such as lobsters or swordfish
  • Prayer potions

Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Champions' Guild south of Varrock (you need 32 Quest Points to get past the doorman) and speak to the Guildmaster.
  2. Listen to his account of Elvarg and Crandor, and accept the challenge to become a dragon slayer.
  3. Head to Port Sarim and ask around about ships bound for Crandor; you'll learn the island has been considered unreachable since the dragon's rampage.
  4. Track down Wormbrain, a would-be adventurer who has been asking the same questions, and press him for anything useful he's picked up about a seaworthy vessel.
  5. Visit the shipyards around Port Sarim and speak with the shipwrights to find out which of the old, damaged boats could realistically be repaired.
  6. Gather whatever information or materials the shipwright needs to make one of the vessels sail-worthy, then arrange passage to Crandor.
  7. Once you land on Crandor, make your way to the entrance of Melzar the Mad's maze.
  8. Inside the maze, examine the potions on the shelves carefully; drinking the wrong one will strip or curse your equipment, so match the potion to the worn items the maze expects of you.
  9. Navigate the maze's rooms, avoiding or dealing with the low-level monsters imprisoned inside, until you reach Melzar himself.
  10. Defeat Melzar, or otherwise get past him, and search the treasure area for the enchanted sword Silverlight.
  11. With Silverlight in hand, locate the passage down into Elvarg's lair beneath the island.
  12. Approach carefully: Elvarg breathes fire in bursts, so hold an anti-dragon shield or be ready to step out of the flame's path between attacks.
  13. Fight Elvarg with Silverlight, which is especially effective against dragons, until she is defeated.
  14. Return to the Guildmaster at the Champions' Guild to report your victory and claim your reward.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • Permanent ownership of the sword Silverlight
  • Ability to wear Rune platebody armour
  • Access to the island of Crandor and Melzar's Maze
  • Access to buy Anti-dragon shields and Rune platebodies from Oziach in Edgeville
  • Combat experience
