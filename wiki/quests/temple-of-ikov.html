Start point
Pick up and read a mysterious note, said to have been dropped by a bird, found near Ardougne.

A cryptic note hints at the location of a forgotten jungle temple dedicated to a long-vanished order, said to be devoted to the mysterious god Armadyl. Piecing together its riddles leads you south of Ardougne to a hidden temple full of ancient traps, puzzles of light and shadow, and a guardian determined to keep its treasures out of unworthy hands.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A crossbow and bolts for the temple's light-beam puzzle

Recommended

  • Ranging potions
  • Food
  • A weapon suitable for fighting the temple's guardian

Walkthrough

  1. Find and read the mysterious note that sets you on the trail of the hidden temple.
  2. Decipher its riddle to work out the temple's approximate location south of Ardougne.
  3. Search the area until you locate the hidden entrance.
  4. Make your way through the temple's outer chambers, watching for traps.
  5. Reach the room with the light-beam puzzle and work out how to redirect the beam using your crossbow and a mirror.
  6. Successfully align the beam to unlock the next section of the temple.
  7. Continue deeper, solving further obstacles as you go.
  8. Confront the guardian protecting the temple's inner sanctum.
  9. Defeat the guardian to gain access to the treasure chamber.
  10. Retrieve the valuable statuette and other treasures kept within.
  11. Make your way back out of the temple.
  12. Report your findings to confirm the quest is complete.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A golden or ivory statuette worth a substantial sum of coins
  • Ranged experience
  • Access to the temple's treasure room
