Lucien, a mysterious stranger in Ardougne, hires you to recover the Staff of Armadyl from the Temple of Ikov near Hemenster. Reaching it requires lightening your load, recovering ice arrows, disarming a trapped lever and defeating the Fire Warrior of Lesarkus. At the end you must choose whether to deliver the staff to Lucien or turn against him and help the Guardians of Armadyl.

Requirements

Required levels

  • 42 Thieving. Required to find and disable the trap on the dungeon lever before pulling it safely.
  • 40 Ranged. Required for the quest's ice arrows in build 254 and needed for the mandatory Fire Warrior fight.
  • Combat capable of defeating the level-84 Fire Warrior of Lesarkus with Ranged. He cannot be defeated with ordinary melee or Magic in build 254.

Required items

  • Knife. Used to cut through the web on the route to the Boots of Lightness.
  • A lit light source. The lower stairs leading toward the boots are dark; without a lit light source build 254 turns you back.
  • Yew bow or Magic bow. The target-period route uses one of these bows with the quest's ice arrows.
  • 20 Limpwurt roots. Winelda takes all twenty at once before teleporting you across the lava.

Obtained during the quest

  • Pendant of Lucien. Lucien gives this when you accept the quest. Wear it to pass the Chamber of Fear gate.
  • Boots of Lightness. Found below the temple. They reduce your carried weight enough to make the broken-bridge section possible when you strip down your inventory.
  • Lever. Found beyond the broken bridge, then fitted into the bracket near the temple entrance.
  • Ice arrows. Found in randomly selected chests in the ice cavern. Gather a generous supply before fighting Lesarkus.
  • Shiny key. Optional but strongly recommended; it opens the shortcut between the inner temple and McGrubor's Wood.
  • Armadyl pendant. Given by a Guardian if you choose to betray Lucien and help Armadyl. It must be worn to attack Lucien.

Recommended

  • Food and Magic-resistant armour. Lesarkus attacks with Magic, while other dungeon creatures can also chip away at your Hitpoints.
  • 43 Prayer and Prayer restoration if you intend to use Protect from Magic against Lesarkus.
  • Keep your first bridge trip extremely light. Your total carried weight must be below 0 kg in build 254. The Boots of Lightness alone do not help if the rest of your equipment pushes you back to zero or above.
  • Many free inventory spaces. The ice-arrow chests give small random stacks, so repeated searching is expected.

Before you start

The quest has one shared dungeon route and then two valid endings. Both endings award the same Quest Point and skill XP in build 254. The choice changes who you fight at the end and whether Lucien receives the Staff of Armadyl.

Do not confuse the requirements with the start dialogue. Build 254 allows Lucien to start the quest without checking 42 Thieving or 40 Ranged immediately, but those levels are enforced later by the trap and ice-arrow mechanics. They are genuine completion requirements.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Accept Lucien's mission

  1. Go to the Flying Horse Inn in north-west Ardougne and speak to Lucien.
  2. Tell him you are a hero and continue until you accept the mission to retrieve the Staff of Armadyl from the Temple of Ikov near Hemenster.
  3. Lucien gives you the Pendant of Lucien. Keep it: the northern gate inside the temple's Chamber of Fear will not let you enter unless the pendant is worn.
  4. Lucien says he will later move to a small house in the forest north of Varrock. That house is where either ending ultimately concludes.

2. Enter the temple and obtain the Boots of Lightness

  1. Travel to the Temple of Ikov entrance south-east of the Ranging Guild and climb down.
  2. Wear the Pendant of Lucien while moving through the temple. If you reach the Chamber of Fear gate without it worn, fear prevents you from entering.
  3. From the entrance area, go west and descend the dark stairs with a lit light source.
  4. Cut through the spider web with your Knife and collect the Boots of Lightness.
  5. Put the boots on. Return toward the main dungeon and discard or bank unnecessary weight before attempting the broken bridge.

3. Cross the broken bridge and recover the lever

  1. Go north through the skeleton room to the broken bridge.
  2. Check your carried weight. It must be strictly below 0 kg in build 254.
  3. Cross the bridge. If you are too heavy, the bridge gives way: build 254 deals 20 damage and drops you into the shallow lava area below.
  4. On the far side, pick up the loose Lever.
  5. Return to the main entrance section. Period guides commonly recommend banking here so you can re-equip for the next stages.

4. Fit the lever and search the ice cavern

  1. Find the empty lever bracket near the entrance section. Use the loose Lever on the bracket.
  2. Pull the repaired lever. You hear hidden machinery and the southern gate is unlocked.
  3. Follow the newly opened route into the ice cavern.
  4. Search the chests scattered through the side passages. Only one randomly selected chest contains ice arrows at a time; after a successful search the location changes again.
  5. Build 254 gives 1–5 ice arrows per successful chest. Keep searching until you have a comfortable supply. The August-2004 period guide suggested roughly 50 before fighting Lesarkus.
  6. Leave the ice area and prepare your bow, food and Magic defence for the Fire Warrior.

5. Disable the trapped lever

  1. Return to the dungeon, wear the Pendant of Lucien and head north toward the trapped lever.
  2. Do not pull it first. Use the lever's trap-search interaction.
  3. With 42 Thieving, you discover and disable the trap.
  4. Now pull the lever. This advances the quest and opens the way toward the Fire Warrior.
  5. If you pull the lever before disabling it, the trap sends you through the floor and build 254 deals 20 damage. Climb back up and try again properly.

6. Defeat the Fire Warrior of Lesarkus

  1. Follow the western route to the blocked doorway and try to open it. The Fire Warrior of Lesarkus appears and refuses to let you pass.
  2. Equip your Yew or Magic bow and the ice arrows.
  3. Defeat Lesarkus with Ranged. The August-2004 guide and build 254 both identify him as level 84.
  4. In build 254, melee and Magic are explicitly rejected against him. Your attack must be Ranged and the ice arrows must be equipped.
  5. Once he dies, the quest advances permanently past this stage and his door becomes usable.

7. Pay Winelda to cross the lava

  1. Continue through Lesarkus' door and speak to the witch Winelda beside the lava.
  2. Ask her to help you cross. She wants 20 Limpwurt roots for her potion.
  3. Bring all twenty roots in your inventory and speak to her again. Build 254 removes exactly twenty and teleports you across.
  4. After this payment stage, Winelda can teleport you across again without another twenty-root payment if you need to retrace the route.

8. Pick up the Shiny key

  1. Beyond Winelda, follow the passage past the Guardians' chamber toward the far end.
  2. Pick up the Shiny key.
  3. The key opens the shortcut door leading to a building just north of McGrubor's Wood. Taking it now makes both endings and later temple visits much easier.
  4. Return to the chamber containing the Guardians of Armadyl and the Staff. This is the point at which you must choose a side.

Ending A — Help the Guardians of Armadyl

  1. Remove the Pendant of Lucien before speaking to a Guardian. Keeping Lucien's pendant on marks you as his agent and can make the Guardians hostile.
  2. Speak to a Guardian and explain that Lucien sent you for the Staff. Follow the dialogue in which the Guardian warns you what Lucien is and offers to cleanse you.
  3. Agree to turn against Lucien and help the Guardians. You receive an Armadyl pendant.
  4. Use the Shiny key shortcut or leave the temple normally, then travel to Lucien's house north of Varrock.
  5. Wear the Armadyl pendant and attack Lucien. Without it, build 254 makes Lucien mind-trick you into deciding not to attack him.
  6. Defeat Lucien. The target-period guide and build 254 both use level 14 for this final fight. His defeat immediately completes the quest on the Armadyl route.

Ending B — Remain loyal to Lucien

  1. Keep working for Lucien and enter the staff chamber.
  2. Try to take the Staff of Armadyl. Nearby Guardians stop you and attack. Defeat the Guardians preventing you from taking it, then pick up the Staff.
  3. The August-2004 guide described these Guardians around levels 36–40. Build 254 instead configures male/female Guardians at 45 and 43; those build values are not treated as proof of the exact September-2004 displays.
  4. Leave through the Shiny key shortcut and travel to Lucien's house north of Varrock.
  5. Speak to Lucien and hand him the Staff. Build 254 removes it from your inventory and completes the quest immediately.

Combat and hazard notes

  • Fire Warrior of Lesarkus: level 84 in both the August-2004 guide and build 254. He attacks with Magic. Build 254 makes the ice-arrow/Ranged restriction absolute.
  • Trapped lever: pulling it before using the 42-Thieving trap-search action causes a fall and 20 damage in build 254.
  • Broken bridge: a total carried weight of 0 kg is still too heavy in build 254; success requires a negative value.
  • Guardians: the target-period guide gives roughly 36–40 for the hostile Guardians on the Lucien route, while build 254 uses 43/45 from later-derived NPC data.
  • Lucien: level 14 in the period walkthrough and build 254. He can only be attacked for the Armadyl ending while the Armadyl pendant is worn.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the Pendant of Lucien? Lucien's build script can replace it while the quest is active if you no longer possess one.
  • Lost the Armadyl pendant? After choosing the Guardians' route, speak to a Guardian again; build 254 can replace it if it is missing.
  • Need more ice arrows? Return to the ice cavern and keep searching. Build 254 re-randomises which of the six quest chests contains arrows after every successful find.
  • Bridge failure is recoverable. You take damage but are not permanently locked out; the period route notes that you can climb out and make another trip.
  • Winelda's payment is one-time quest progress. Once the twenty roots have been handed over, later conversations use the repeat teleport path.
  • Take the Shiny key. It is not needed to trigger completion, but it permanently provides the useful Temple/McGrubor's Wood shortcut.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 10,500 Ranged XP.
  • 8,000 Fletching XP.
  • Boots of Lightness, if collected during the route.
  • Pendant of Lucien, retained from the quest route and used for the Temple of Ikov fear gate.
  • Shiny key, if collected, giving the shortcut between the inner temple and McGrubor's Wood.
  • Armadyl pendant on the Guardians route in build 254; it is given so you can attack Lucien.

There is no golden or ivory statuette reward, no light-beam/mirror puzzle and no generic treasure-room unlock in the September-2004 quest. Those details from the previous local page were unrelated fabrication.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start and requirements: lucien.rs2 starts the quest without an immediate level check, while ikov_journal.rs2 lists 42 Thieving and 40 Ranged. The dungeon lever enforces 42 Thieving and the ice-arrow object has a 40-Ranged requirement.
  • Fear gate: ikov_dungeon.rs2 checks the worn inventory for the Pendant of Lucien when entering the northern gate. Exiting does not require it.
  • Bridge: the implementation fails the crossing when weight >= 0, so the usable threshold is strictly negative. Failure deals 20 damage.
  • Ice arrows: six possible chest coordinates are used. A successful search gives 1–5 arrows and immediately selects another random chest.
  • Trap: the Search-for-traps interaction requires 42 Thieving to set the disabled state. Pulling the live trap drops the player and deals 20 damage.
  • Lesarkus: build 254 configures the Fire Warrior at level 84. The generic NPC combat gate refuses melee and Magic and only permits Ranged attacks while ice arrows are equipped.
  • Winelda: her script requires at least 20 Limpwurt roots in inventory, deletes exactly 20 and advances the quest before teleporting the player.
  • Branching: the Guardians route sets state 70, gives the Armadyl pendant and only permits attacking Lucien while that pendant is worn. The Lucien route instead allows the Staff to be taken after the Guardians no longer block it and deletes the Staff when it is handed to Lucien.
  • Exact completion reward: both build branches award 10,500 Ranged XP, 8,000 Fletching XP and the ^ikov_questpoints = 1 Quest Point constant.
  • NPC-level provenance: the Fire Warrior's level 84 is independently supported by the August-2004 guide. The current Guardian NPC configs explicitly note later OSRS-derived stats and display levels, so build levels 43/45 are kept separate from the period guide's 36–40 values.
  • Reconstruction caution: several quest-journal and dungeon-source comments cite OSRS, RS3, later videos or post-cutoff material. They are authoritative for build-254 behaviour but are not automatically historical evidence for 7 September 2004.
