Imp Catcher

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Imp Catcher
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to Wizard Mizgog in the Wizards' Tower.

Wizard Mizgog kept a set of four enchanted beads close at hand for his experiments, and now they've all been snatched by a swarm of thieving imps. The beads could be scattered anywhere the mischievous little creatures roam. It falls to you to hunt them down, one colour at a time.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Nothing needs to be brought — everything necessary is found during the quest

Recommended

  • A weapon suitable for killing weak imps quickly

Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Wizards' Tower south of Draynor Village and find Wizard Mizgog inside.
  2. Listen as he explains that mischievous imps have stolen four of his magic beads, one each in red, black, yellow, and white.
  3. Agree to track the beads down and set out to find and fight imps wherever you encounter them.
  4. Defeat imps around Runescape's various towns and wilderness fringes, watching their drops for coloured beads.
  5. Keep hunting imps until you've collected the red bead.
  6. Continue until the black bead also drops from one of your kills.
  7. Keep going until you obtain the yellow bead as well.
  8. Finally, hunt down an imp carrying the white bead to complete the set.
  9. Return to Wizard Mizgog in the Wizards' Tower with all four beads in hand.
  10. Hand the beads over to him to complete the quest and receive your reward.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 875 Magic experience
