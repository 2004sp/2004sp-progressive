Wizard Grayzag has summoned hundreds of little imps, and they have stolen many of Wizard Mizgog's possessions. Mizgog only cares about four magical beads — one red, one yellow, one black and one white — and asks you to recover the complete set.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None. Killing imps is one way to obtain the beads, but the quest itself has no combat-level or skill-level requirement.

Required items

  • 1 Black bead.
  • 1 Red bead.
  • 1 White bead.
  • 1 Yellow bead.

The four beads are required at the end rather than at the moment you start the quest. Contemporary 2004 guides explicitly allow obtaining them from other players as well as from imp drops, so they may be collected before or during the quest.

Recommended

  • A weapon if you intend to hunt imps yourself.
  • Four free inventory spaces for one bead of each colour.
  • If trading with other players, consider swapping duplicate bead colours for the ones you are missing instead of continuing to hunt indefinitely.

Before you start

The Wizards' Tower is on the small island south of Draynor Village. Cross the bridge, enter the tower and climb to the top floor to find Wizard Mizgog.

The bead hunt is random if you kill imps. A March-2004 RuneScape 2 guide already recommended buying or trading for missing beads if you did not want to wait for the drops, and a July-2004 guide likewise described buying or trading duplicates as a valid shortcut. There is therefore no need to treat killing four specific imps as a mandatory quest sequence.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Wizard Mizgog

  1. Go to the top floor of the Wizards' Tower, south of Draynor Village, and speak to Wizard Mizgog.
  2. Ask him for a quest. He explains that the neighbouring Wizard Grayzag summoned hundreds of imps.
  3. The imps stole many trivial possessions, but Mizgog wants four magical beads returned: red, yellow, black and white.
  4. Agree to recover them.

Build-254 dialogue note: choose “Give me a quest!” and then the polite “Give me a quest please.” response. The two rude follow-up choices do not set the quest to its started state in this build.

2. Obtain all four coloured beads

  1. Collect one Black bead, one Red bead, one White bead and one Yellow bead.
  2. If you are hunting them yourself, kill imps and check their drops. Mizgog says the summoned imps have spread throughout the kingdom, and contemporary guides note that imps can also be found near the tower.
  3. Expect duplicate colours. You do not need a bead to have dropped after the quest began; the quest only cares that the complete four-colour set is present when you return.
  4. Alternatively, trade with other players for the colours you need. This was already documented as a valid time-saving method in 2004.

3. Return the complete set to Mizgog

  1. Return to Wizard Mizgog on the top floor of the Wizards' Tower with all four different beads in your inventory.
  2. Speak to him. He takes exactly one Black, one Red, one White and one Yellow bead.
  3. Mizgog gives you an Amulet of accuracy and the quest completes.

Combat and bead hunting

  • Combat is optional. If another player supplies the beads, no enemy needs to be killed for quest progress.
  • No exact September-2004 imp combat level is asserted here. The period guides and reconstructed build data are not clean enough to justify turning a non-essential display level into a hard historical claim.
  • Build-254 drop rates: the imp drop script rolls 0–127 and assigns five results to each bead colour. That makes each specific bead 5/128 per eligible imp drop roll, and any one of the four beads 20/128. These are implementation values, not proof of the exact historical September-2004 rates.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Mizgog does not partially take beads. In build 254, if you return with only some colours he tells you to come back with all four.
  • Have all four in your inventory. The build checks the carried inventory, not the bank, when deciding whether the set is complete.
  • Extra duplicates are safe. Completion removes only one bead of each required colour.
  • Already collected the beads? You can start the quest and immediately turn in a pre-collected complete set; the build does not track who killed the imps or when each bead was obtained.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 375 Magic XP for the September-2004 historical guide.
  • 1 Amulet of accuracy. Contemporary March-2004 material describes it as giving +4 weapon accuracy; build 254 gives +4 to Stab, Slash, Crush, Magic and Ranged attack bonuses.

Historical/build difference: two independent RuneScape 2 guides dated March and July 2004 both record 375 Magic XP. Progressive build 254 instead awards 875 Magic XP. This page keeps the target-period reward as the main historical value and documents the build difference below rather than rewriting September 2004 to match the implementation.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest start: Mizgog's script only starts the quest through the polite branch after asking for a quest. The threatening and impatient branches end without changing the quest state.
  • Completion check: build 254 requires all four bead colours in the carried inventory at once. It does not partially consume them, check the bank or track the source of the beads.
  • Bead consumption: completion deletes exactly one Black, one White, one Yellow and one Red bead. Any duplicate beads remain.
  • Imp drops: the build's imp drop table gives each specific coloured bead 5 of 128 random outcomes, for 20 of 128 outcomes across all four colours. Exact historical drop probabilities are not inferred from this reconstructed table.
  • Reward deviation: `quest_imp.rs2` advances Magic by 8,750 internal tenths, which is 875 Magic XP, while target-proximate March and July 2004 RuneScape 2 guides both give 375 Magic XP.
  • Quest points: `^imp_questpoints` is 1.
  • Amulet of accuracy: build object data gives +4 Stab, +4 Slash, +4 Crush, +4 Magic and +4 Ranged attack. The quest completion script adds one amulet directly to the inventory.
  • Post-quest dialogue: build 254's completed-state Mizgog dialogue offers no replacement-amulet shop or paid replacement service, so later versions of that mechanic are not presented as September-2004 content.
