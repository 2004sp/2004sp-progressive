Cook's Assistant

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Cook's Assistant
Access
Free-to-play
Difficulty
Novice
Length
Very short
Quest points
1
Start point
Talk to the Cook in the kitchen of Lumbridge Castle.

The Lumbridge Castle cook is in a full-blown panic: the Duke's birthday is today, and the cake he promised is nowhere near finished. He's missing three basic ingredients and has nobody else to turn to but you. It's a simple emergency, but a good introduction to just how disorganised the castle kitchen really is.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A bucket, for milking a cow
  • An empty pot, for collecting flour

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Lumbridge Castle and head to the ground-floor kitchen to speak with the Cook.
  2. Listen to his panic: the Duke's birthday cake needs an egg, a bucket of milk, and a pot of flour, and he has none of them.
  3. Agree to help and accept the quest.
  4. If you don't already have one, grab an empty bucket and an empty pot; both can be found nearby or bought cheaply from a general store.
  5. Head out to the farmland south of Lumbridge and find a dairy cow, then use your bucket on it to fill it with milk.
  6. Look around the nearby chicken coop for a loose egg lying on the ground and pick it up.
  7. Find a wheat field nearby and pick some grain, then use your pot to collect it.
  8. Cross the river to the windmill north of Lumbridge and climb up to the top floor.
  9. Empty your grain into the hopper and turn the mechanism below to grind it into flour, then scoop the flour into your pot.
  10. Return to the kitchen with the egg, the bucket of milk, and the pot of flour in hand.
  11. Hand all three items to the Cook, who bakes the cake just in time and thanks you for saving him.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • 300 Cooking experience
