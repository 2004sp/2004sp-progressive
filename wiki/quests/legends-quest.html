Legends' Quest

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Legends' Quest
Access
Members
Difficulty
Master
Length
Very long
Quest points
4
Start point
Speak to Radimus Erkle outside the Legends' Guild, south of Ardougne.

Radimus Erkle, master of the Legends' Guild, has heard rumours of an uncharted jungle south of Shilo Village and believes only a true legend could survive what lies within. To earn your place among the guild's elite, you must cut your way through the tangled Kharazi Jungle, prove your mastery of mining, crafting, herblore, and magic against the land's own trials, and confront whatever ancient guardian waits at the heart of it all.

Requirements

  • 56 Quest Points
  • Level 50 Mining
  • Level 50 Crafting
  • Level 45 Magic
  • Level 45 Herblore
  • Level 42 Prayer
  • Completion of Shilo Village
  • Completion of Merlin's Crystal
  • Completion of Heroes' Quest
  • Completion of Dragon Slayer
  • Completion of Family Crest

Items needed

  • A machete or similar tool for cutting through jungle vines
  • Combat equipment suitable for fighting jungle creatures
  • Supplies appropriate to the mining, crafting, herblore, and magic trials

Recommended

  • Food
  • Teleport runes or tablets
  • Antipoison

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Radimus Erkle at the Legends' Guild and confirm you meet his lengthy list of requirements.
  2. Travel south from Shilo Village into the uncharted Kharazi Jungle.
  3. Hack through the thick vines and undergrowth with a machete to carve a path deeper into the jungle.
  4. Deal with the jungle's hostile wildlife and plant life as you press further in.
  5. Locate a jungle-dwelling hermit who has lived among the ruins for years and ask him about the area.
  6. Follow his guidance toward a set of ancient trial chambers hidden within the jungle.
  7. Complete the mining-based trial by extracting the required ore under the chamber's specific conditions.
  8. Complete the crafting-based trial by fashioning the item the chamber demands.
  9. Complete the herblore-based trial by preparing the correct concoction from jungle ingredients.
  10. Complete the magic-based trial by successfully casting the spell the chamber tests you on.
  11. With all four trials cleared, proceed to the innermost sanctum of the ruins.
  12. Face the powerful guardian creature protecting the jungle's secret.
  13. Defeat or outwit the guardian to claim proof of your success.
  14. Return to Radimus Erkle at the Legends' Guild with evidence of what you accomplished.
  15. Receive your Legend's cape and formal recognition as a legend.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • The Legend's cape
  • Full membership and access to the Legends' Guild
  • Substantial experience in Mining, Crafting, Herblore, and Magic
