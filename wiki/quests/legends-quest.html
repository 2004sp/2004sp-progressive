To join the Legends' Guild, Radimus Erkle sends you to map the unexplored Kharazi Jungle, make contact with its people and return with a suitable native trophy. The assignment grows into a long struggle against the demon Nezikchened, involving jungle survival, several skill trials, a rune-and-gem puzzle, sacred water, a choice involving Viyeldi, and the growth of a sacred Yommi tree.

Requirements

Quest points and required quests

  • 107 Quest Points.
  • Heroes' Quest.
  • Family Crest.
  • Shilo Village.
  • Underground Pass.
  • Waterfall Quest.

Those are the five direct quest prerequisites checked for Legends' Quest. Quests required by Heroes' Quest are already part of that earlier chain and are not additional direct Legends' Quest checks.

Required levels

  • 56 Magic
  • 52 Mining
  • 50 Agility
  • 50 Crafting
  • 50 Smithing
  • 50 Strength
  • 50 Thieving
  • 50 Woodcutting
  • 45 Herblore
  • 42 Prayer

Several of these are checked deep into the quest rather than at Radimus's desk. Do not assume that entering the guild compound means you can finish the quest with lower levels.

Required items to bring or obtain

  • Rune axe. This is not just a recommendation in build 254: the mature Yommi tree requires a Rune axe for the final cutting, trimming and carving work.
  • Pickaxe. Any usable pickaxe is sufficient for the cave mining trial. Build 254 also provides a Bronze pickaxe from dwarf remains in the cave as a recovery option.
  • Machete. Needed to cut through Kharazi Jungle. A machete can be taken from the cupboard in Radimus Erkle's office after starting.
  • At least 3 papyrus and charcoal. Each successful map section consumes one papyrus. Mapping can fail and destroy papyrus, charcoal or both, so bring spares.
  • Lockpick. The ancient gate has a 50 Thieving check. Build 254 does not consume the lockpick when the pick attempt fails.
  • 2 gold bars and a hammer. Two bars are needed for the golden bowl. Smithing can fail in build 254, so bringing 4–6 gold bars is safer.
  • 1 unpowered orb, 30 water runes and 3 cosmic runes for the minimum-level Magic solution. At 56 Magic, cast Charge Water Orb on the magic gate. Higher Charge Orb spells also work if you meet their levels and bring their runes.
  • Rune wall: 1 Soul rune, 1 Mind rune, 1 Earth rune and 2 Law runes.
  • Seven cut gems: Opal, Jade, Red topaz, Sapphire, Diamond, Ruby and Emerald.
  • Rope. Used on the lower-cave winch after drinking the bravery potion.
  • 1 vial of water. Used with Snake weed and Ardrigal to make the bravery potion.
  • Snake weed and Ardrigal. These can be gathered during the quest from their Jungle Potion search locations, so they do not need to be banked in advance.

Quest items obtained during the route

  • Radimus notes / completed Kharazi map
  • Bull roarer
  • Book of Binding
  • Golden bowl, blessed golden bowl and hollow reed
  • Yommi seeds and germinated Yommi seeds
  • Bravery potion
  • Three crystal pieces and the heart crystal
  • Black dagger and, on the recommended branch, Holy Force
  • Yommi totem and gilded totem

Recommended

  • Strong melee equipment and plenty of food. There are three Nezikchened fights plus several other hostile encounters and obstacle failures.
  • Prayer potions. Nezikchened can heavily drain Prayer before his fights.
  • Stat-restoring potions. Several ancient obstacles can reduce combat or skill stats after failed attempts.
  • Antipoison. The Kharazi Jungle contains poisonous threats.
  • Extra papyrus, charcoal and gold bars. Mapping and golden-bowl Smithing can destroy supplies on failed attempts even when you meet the required level.
  • Several free inventory spaces. This quest produces many temporary quest objects and is much easier if you bank between major stages.

Before you start

Legends' Quest is long and does not need to be completed in one trip. Shilo Village is the practical banking point for Kharazi Jungle expeditions. Keep Radimus's notes safe, and remember that you need an axe, machete and the map to cut into the jungle before the quest is complete.

Important build-254 warning: cutting your way through dense Kharazi Jungle while carrying water in a golden bowl causes that water to evaporate. Refill sacred water only when you are positioned to use it without hacking through more jungle barriers.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the guild assignment

  1. Travel to the Legends' Guild north-east of Ardougne. The guards check the quest-point and prerequisite-quest requirements before allowing you through to Radimus.
  2. Speak to Radimus Erkle and accept the assignment. He gives you Radimus notes: map the Kharazi Jungle, make contact with friendly natives and return with a suitable native item or trophy.
  3. Search the cupboard in Radimus's office for a machete if you did not bring one.
  4. Prepare at least three papyrus and charcoal, your axe and the rest of your jungle supplies.

2. Enter and map the Kharazi Jungle

  1. Travel to southern Karamja and approach the dense vegetation around the Kharazi Jungle.
  2. Use your machete and axe to cut through. Before quest completion, build 254 refuses jungle entry if you have neither Radimus notes nor a completed copy of the map.
  3. There are three map regions: western, middle and eastern Kharazi Jungle. While standing in each region, use the mapping option on Radimus notes.
  4. You need 50 Crafting, papyrus and charcoal. A successful region consumes one papyrus. Failures can destroy papyrus, charcoal or both, so retry until all three sections are marked complete.
  5. Once all three areas are finished, the notes become a completed Kharazi map.
  6. Show the completed map to a Jungle Forester north of the jungle. He gives you a Bull roarer.

3. Summon Gujuo and find Ungadulu

  1. Return to the Kharazi Jungle and swing the Bull roarer. Gujuo does not necessarily appear on every attempt in build 254; if nothing useful happens, move safely and try again.
  2. Speak to Gujuo. He asks you to rescue the shaman Ungadulu, trapped in caves in the north-west of the jungle.
  3. Find the cave entrance among the rocks and crawl through. The entrance uses a 50 Agility check and can hurt you on failure.
  4. Inside, find Ungadulu trapped within a flaming octagram. Speak to him, then explore the cave for the deeper route and the information needed to break the magical barrier.

4. Pass the ancient trials and rebuild the Book of Binding

The deeper cave contains a chain of skill and item trials. These are genuine requirements, not optional flavour.

  1. Locked ancient gate: use a lockpick with 50 Thieving. Retry if the attempt fails.
  2. Heavy metal doors: force them with 50 Strength.
  3. Boulders: mine through with 52 Mining and a usable pickaxe. If you arrived without one, search the dwarf remains for the build's Bronze-pickaxe recovery.
  4. Crumbled wall: pass the 50 Agility obstacle.
  5. Magic gate: cast a Charge Orb spell while carrying an unpowered orb. At the minimum 56 Magic, use Charge Water Orb, which requires 30 water runes and 3 cosmic runes.
  6. SMELL rune wall: use the runes that spell SMELL by their initials: Soul, Mind, Earth, Law, Law. Wrong runes cause damage.
  7. Seven-gem puzzle: place the seven cut gems on the carved rocks. In build 254, the solution runs around the ring from the north-west clockwise as Opal → Jade → Red topaz → Sapphire → Diamond → Ruby → Emerald.
  8. Completing the gem puzzle restores the Book of Binding. Take it with you.

5. Make and bless the golden bowl

  1. Return to Gujuo and ask about getting through Ungadulu's flames. He explains that you need a vessel made from the metal of the sun.
  2. At an anvil, use 2 gold bars + hammer with 50 Smithing to make a golden bowl. Build 254 can fail the Smithing attempt and consume bars, which is why spare bars are recommended.
  3. Take the bowl to Gujuo. With 42 Prayer, ask him to bless it. A failed blessing can drain Prayer; restore and retry if necessary.
  4. Near the sacred pool, cut a hollow reed from the tall reeds using a knife or your machete.
  5. Use the hollow reed to siphon sacred water into the blessed golden bowl.

6. Free Ungadulu and defeat Nezikchened for the first time

  1. Return to Ungadulu's cave. Avoid cutting more jungle with water in the bowl, or build 254 will evaporate it.
  2. Use the blessed sacred water on the flaming octagram so you can cross to Ungadulu.
  3. Use the Book of Binding on Ungadulu.
  4. Nezikchened appears. Build 254 displays him as level 187 and heavily drains your current Prayer before the fight. Defeat him.
  5. After the demon is driven away, Ungadulu gives you 3 Yommi seeds.
  6. Use the seeds with blessed pure water to germinate them.
  7. Investigate the sacred pool again. It has become polluted/dry, forcing you to find the source beneath the jungle.

7. Make a bravery potion and descend to the lower caves

  1. Speak to Gujuo about the dried sacred pool. He tells you how to make a bravery potion.
  2. Gather Snake weed and Ardrigal from their Jungle Potion search locations if you did not bring them.
  3. Use the herbs with a vial of water to make the bravery potion. The quest requires 45 Herblore.
  4. Go to the lower-cave winch, attach your rope, drink the bravery potion and descend.

8. Defeat the three ancient heroes and restore the heart crystal

  1. Work through the lower cave and fight the three ancient heroes. Build 254 displays them as San Tojalon (106), Irvig Senay (100) and Ranalph Devere (92).
  2. Each drops a different crystal piece: chunk, hunk and lump. Keep all three.
  3. Use the three pieces on the lava furnace. This uses 50 Crafting and produces a heart crystal.
  4. Place the heart crystal on the dragon's-eye / mossy-rock focus to charge it into a glowing crystal.
  5. Place the glowing heart crystal in the heart-shaped recess. The magical force barrier disappears.
  6. Continue to the sacred-source chamber and push the large boulder.

9. Recommended branch: spare Viyeldi

You now meet Echned Zekin, who gives you a black dagger and tells you to kill Viyeldi. The easier route is to refuse to kill Viyeldi.

  1. Take the black dagger back to Ungadulu instead of using it to kill Viyeldi.
  2. Ungadulu gives you Holy Force.
  3. Return to Echned Zekin and use the Holy Force. His disguise is exposed: he is Nezikchened.
  4. Defeat Nezikchened for the second time.

Alternative: you can obey Echned Zekin and kill Viyeldi with the dagger. The quest can still be completed, but build 254 records that choice and makes the final totem encounter harder by forcing you to defeat San Tojalon, Irvig Senay and Ranalph Devere again before Nezikchened. Sparing Viyeldi avoids that extra gauntlet.

10. Restore the sacred water and grow a Yommi tree

  1. After the second Nezikchened fight, access to the sacred source is restored. Refill your blessed golden bowl with pure sacred water.
  2. Go to fertile soil in the Kharazi Jungle and plant a germinated Yommi seed. The planting/growing sequence uses the quest's 45 Herblore and 50 Woodcutting requirements.
  3. Water the sapling promptly with the blessed pure water.
  4. When the Yommi tree matures, use your Rune axe. Build 254 explicitly rejects weaker axes for this stage.
  5. Chop, trim and carve the tree until you obtain a Yommi totem.

11. Replace the corrupted totem and defeat Nezikchened

  1. Take your Yommi totem to the corrupted totem site and use it there.
  2. If you killed Viyeldi earlier, the three ancient heroes return and must be defeated again in sequence.
  3. Nezikchened appears for the third and final fight. Build 254 again displays him as level 187 and can drain a large portion of your Prayer before combat.
  4. Defeat him and finish replacing the corrupted totem.
  5. Speak to Gujuo. He presents you with the gilded totem, the native trophy Radimus asked you to obtain.

12. Return to Radimus and join the Legends' Guild

  1. Return to Radimus Erkle with both the gilded totem and your completed Kharazi map.
  2. Radimus takes the proof of your expedition and admits you into the Legends' Guild proper.
  3. He lets you choose four training rewards. Each selection gives 7,650 XP.
  4. Available choices are Attack, Defence, Strength, Hitpoints, Prayer, Magic, Woodcutting, Crafting, Smithing, Herblore, Agility and Thieving. Build 254 allows the same skill to be selected more than once.
  5. After the fourth selection, Legends' Quest is complete.

Combat encounters and hazards

  • Nezikchened: fought three times. Build 254 displays level 187, matching the level recorded by surviving period guide lineage. His scripted appearances can drain Prayer before combat, so do not enter with exactly enough Prayer for one protection cycle and no restore.
  • Ancient heroes: San Tojalon 106, Irvig Senay 100 and Ranalph Devere 92 are build-254 visible levels. All three are mandatory once for the crystal pieces; killing Viyeldi makes them mandatory a second time at the final totem.
  • Skill failures: mapping, Smithing the bowl, blessing the bowl and several ancient obstacles can fail. Bring spare consumables and enough food to survive repeated attempts.
  • Jungle hazards: hostile wildlife may attack while you use the Bull roarer, and poisonous creatures make Antipoison useful.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Mapping consumes papyrus: three successes mean at least three papyrus. Build failures may destroy papyrus and/or charcoal.
  • Rune axe means Rune axe: a lower-tier axe may get you through ordinary jungle, but it will not finish the mature Yommi-tree stage in build 254.
  • Water evaporates while jungle cutting: if a golden bowl contains water when you hack through dense Kharazi vegetation, build 254 empties the water from it.
  • Bull roarer retries: Gujuo is not guaranteed to appear on every swing; retry rather than assuming you have missed a quest step.
  • Magic gate at minimum level: 56 Magic is sufficient because Charge Water Orb is level 56. Bring an unpowered orb, 30 water runes and 3 cosmic runes.
  • SMELL means five rune uses: Soul, Mind, Earth, Law, Law. Bring two Law runes.
  • Spare Viyeldi for the simpler finale: take the dagger to Ungadulu and use Holy Force on Echned Zekin. Killing Viyeldi is a valid but harder branch.
  • Completed map matters at the end: Radimus expects the gilded totem and completed notes/map before the training reward sequence.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 4 × 7,650 XP in your choice of Attack, Defence, Strength, Hitpoints, Prayer, Magic, Woodcutting, Crafting, Smithing, Herblore, Agility or Thieving — 30,600 XP total. Build 254 permits repeat selections of the same skill.
  • Full access to the Legends' Guild.
  • Access to the Legends' Guild shops, including the ability to buy the Cape of legends. The cape is not handed to you for free by the quest-completion script.
  • Permission to equip the Dragon square shield. In build 254 this additionally requires 60 Defence.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Exact start gate: build 254 uses 107 Quest Points and directly checks Heroes' Quest, Family Crest, Shilo Village, Underground Pass and Waterfall Quest. Its journal lists 56 Magic, 52 Mining, 50 Agility/Crafting/Smithing/Strength/Thieving/Woodcutting, 45 Herblore and 42 Prayer.
  • Mapping: each successful region removes one papyrus. A failed 50-Crafting attempt can destroy nothing, charcoal, papyrus, or both. The three regional flags are western, middle and eastern Kharazi Jungle.
  • Magic gate: the shared Charge Orb spell handler routes Charge Orb spells to the Legends gate. Charge Water Orb is level 56 and costs 30 water + 3 cosmic runes while an unpowered orb is held, directly explaining the quest's minimum Magic requirement.
  • Rune and gem puzzles: the cave tracks Soul, Mind, Earth, Law, Law separately. Its seven gem coordinates form the north-west-clockwise sequence Opal, Jade, Red topaz, Sapphire, Diamond, Ruby, Emerald.
  • Golden bowl: the jungle-cutting script explicitly evaporates ordinary, pure, blessed or blessed-pure water from golden bowls whenever the player hacks through dense jungle.
  • Viyeldi branch: build 254 stores whether Viyeldi was killed. If he was, the final fight reuses three earlier quest bits to track a San Tojalon → Irvig Senay → Ranalph Devere rematch before Nezikchened.
  • Yommi tree: the mature-tree stage checks specifically for a Rune axe rather than accepting any axe the player is otherwise able to use.
  • Training reward: Radimus advances the selected stat by 76500 in the project's tenths-of-XP convention, or 7,650 XP, four times. The quest-point constant is 4.
  • Dragon square shield: build 254's equip handler requires Legends' Quest completion and 60 Defence. Repairing the two shield halves itself is a separate 60-Smithing action.
  • Cape of legends: the build's Legends' Guild shop stocks the cape after guild access. It is a shop unlock, not a free quest item.
  • Reconstruction caveat: several Legends quest source comments cite later videos or OSRS data for exact timings/stats. This guide treats the build source as authoritative for build-254 behaviour while using period evidence for the historical route and requirements.
