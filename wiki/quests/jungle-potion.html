The people of Tai Bwo Wannai have abandoned their village for the jungle. Their witch doctor, Trufitus Shakaya, wants to commune with the gods and discover what fate awaits them. He needs five special jungle herbs for his potion, and he asks you to find them one at a time: Snake Weed, Ardrigal, Sito Foil, Volencia Moss and Rogues Purse.

Requirements

Required quest

  • Druidic Ritual. Contemporary 2004 guides list it as the prerequisite, and build 254 explicitly refuses to start Jungle Potion unless Druidic Ritual is complete.

Required levels

  • No additional skill requirement beyond Druidic Ritual. Completing Druidic Ritual leaves you able to use Herblore, and build 254 sets all five Jungle Potion herbs to require level 3 Herblore to identify.

Required items

  • None to bring in advance. All five quest herbs are obtained during the quest from special searchable scenery.

You do not need a machete, rope or light source for Jungle Potion. The final cave entrance is opened by searching rocks, and the build handles the climb into and out of the cavern directly.

Recommended

  • Antipoison. The Karamja jungle contains poisonous enemies, and a contemporary March-2004 guide specifically recommends buying an Antipoison potion locally before setting out.
  • Food and basic armour if your combat level is low.
  • A weapon is optional. No monster must be killed, but the final herb lies beyond level-48 Jogres.
  • At least one free inventory space whenever you search for a herb.

Before you start

This quest is deliberately sequential. Trufitus asks for one herb at a time, and the special scenery only advances the matching quest stage. After finding each herb, Identify it from your inventory and take the identified herb back to Trufitus before searching for the next one.

Do not rely on ordinary herb drops or pre-collected copies. Build 254 records whether you searched the correct quest location at the correct stage, and Trufitus rejects an identified herb as not fresh if that matching search stage has not been completed.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start the quest with Trufitus

  1. Travel to Tai Bwo Wannai village in southern Karamja and enter Trufitus's main hut.
  2. Speak to Trufitus Shakaya. Asking where everyone is leads into the problem: his people are afraid to remain in the village and have returned to the jungle.
  3. Offer to help. Trufitus explains that he needs a special potion so he can commune with the gods, and that the potion requires rare herbs found only in the deep jungle.
  4. Accept the challenge. If Druidic Ritual is complete, the quest starts and Trufitus asks for the first herb, Snake Weed.

2. Find Snake Weed

  1. From the village, head south-west toward the marshy ground. The period guide describes crossing the muddy land bridge and going slightly west.
  2. Find a Marshy jungle vine near the wet ground and choose Search.
  3. You receive an Unidentified herb. Use its Identify option; it becomes Snake Weed.
  4. Return to Trufitus and give him the Snake Weed. He takes it and gives you the clue for Ardrigal.

3. Find Ardrigal

  1. Head east / north-east from Trufitus toward the coast. Follow the shore until the cliffs come down to meet the sand and you reach a small peninsula with palm trees.
  2. Search the quest Palm tree on the peninsula to obtain an unidentified herb.
  3. Identify it as Ardrigal.
  4. Return to Trufitus and hand it over. He now asks for Sito Foil, which grows where the ground has been blackened by living flame.

4. Find Sito Foil

  1. Go south of Tai Bwo Wannai village to the area around the ever-burning fire.
  2. Look for the searchable Scorched earth just south of the fire area and choose Search.
  3. Identify the herb you find as Sito Foil.
  4. Take it back to Trufitus. He says only two herbs remain and asks for Volencia Moss.

5. Find Volencia Moss

  1. Head south-east of the village toward the mining area. Trufitus's clue says the moss clings to rocks with high metal content in a frequently disturbed environment.
  2. Search the special plain Rock with green moss growing on it.
  3. Identify the herb as Volencia Moss.
  4. Return to Trufitus. He takes the moss and asks for the final herb, Rogues Purse, which grows underground in caverns to the north.

6. Find Rogues Purse

  1. Head north toward the cliffs. The cave entrance is reached by going around the cliffs; contemporary 2004 guidance describes heading east around them and then back west to the top.
  2. Search the Rocks covering the depression. Choose to enter when asked, and you will climb down into the cavern.
  3. Inside, run past or fight the level-48 Jogres. No kill is required for quest progress.
  4. Find the Fungus covered Cavern wall and Search it to obtain the final unidentified herb.
  5. Identify it as Rogues Purse.
  6. Leave the cavern using the Hand holds and return to Trufitus.

7. Finish the quest

  1. Give the identified Rogues Purse to Trufitus.
  2. He thanks you for returning all five herbs and says he can finish the preparations for the potion and commune with the gods.
  3. Trufitus shows you some Herblore techniques. Quest complete.

Combat and jungle hazards

  • No combat is mandatory. Every quest objective is a dialogue, scenery-search or herb hand-in.
  • Level-48 Jogres: the period March-2004 walkthrough warns about them in the final cavern. You can simply run past them to the fungus-covered wall.
  • Poison risk: hostile jungle creatures can poison vulnerable players, which is why period guides recommend Antipoison and basic food/armour.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Gather the herbs in order: Snake Weed → Ardrigal → Sito Foil → Volencia Moss → Rogues Purse. Trufitus advances to the next herb only after accepting the current one.
  • Identify every herb before returning: build 254 gives the quest herbs an inventory Identify action. Trufitus refuses the unidentified versions and tells you they are too dirty to recognise.
  • Use the quest scenery, not drops: the matching search action changes the quest from “get herb” to “found herb”. An identified copy obtained some other way does not substitute for that progression flag.
  • Inventory full: the build does not award the herb or advance the “found” state if you have no free inventory space, so clear a slot and search again.
  • No machete route: Jungle Potion does not send you into the Kharazi Jungle and does not involve cutting dense jungle. Those mechanics belong to other Karamja content, including Legends' Quest.
  • Period progression: completing Jungle Potion is a prerequisite for Shilo Village, which was already available by September 2004.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 775 Herblore XP.

There is no Woodcutting XP, Fishing XP, coin reward or village-favour reward in the September-2004 Jungle Potion reward set.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest-state order: build 254 uses separate “get” and “found” states for each of the five herbs, followed by junglepotion_found_all_herbs and completion. This enforces the one-at-a-time route described by contemporary guides.
  • Start gate: Trufitus only moves the quest to the Snake Weed stage when %druidquest = ^druid_complete. Otherwise the build displays a requirements-failure message.
  • Exact searchable scenery: the quest script binds the herbs to Marshy jungle vine, Palm tree, Scorched earth, Rock and Fungus covered Cavern wall respectively.
  • Identification: all five special herbs are non-tradeable in the build, have an Identify action and require Herblore level 3 to identify. Their configured identification XP is 25 internal tenths, or 2.5 XP each.
  • Fresh-herb enforcement: finding the correct herb through the quest scenery changes the relevant progress state. If the player tries to give Trufitus an identified herb while still on the corresponding “get” state, he rejects it as not fresh and tells the player to pick it personally.
  • Cave handling: the northern cave entrance requires only searching the Rocks and accepting the prompt. Build 254 teleports the player into the cavern; the exit uses Hand holds. No rope, Agility check or light source is consumed.
  • Reward: the completion queue advances Herblore by 7750 in the project's tenths-of-XP convention, giving 775 Herblore XP, and the quest-point constant is 1.
  • Post-cutoff contamination excluded: the Trufitus script also contains dialogue directing the player to Timfraku and explicitly comments that it is the September-2004+ Tai Bwo Wannai Trio behaviour. Tai Bwo Wannai Trio post-dates this wiki's 7 September 2004 cutoff, so that continuation is not presented as part of the target-period Jungle Potion guide.
  • Reconstruction caveat: source comments note that some Jungle Potion scenery animations and sounds may have been added later. This guide relies on the quest-state logic, object interactions and period walkthrough route rather than treating those presentation details as cutoff evidence.
