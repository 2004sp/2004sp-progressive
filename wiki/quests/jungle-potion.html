Jungle Potion

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Jungle Potion
Access
Members
Difficulty
Intermediate
Length
Medium
Quest points
1
Start point
Speak to Trufitus, the witch doctor in Tai Bwo Wannai village on Karamja.

Trufitus, the tribal shaman of Tai Bwo Wannai, is desperate to brew a potion that will let him commune with the spirits of the jungle, but the recipe demands rare and dangerous ingredients scattered throughout the Kharazi jungle. Earning his trust means braving snakes, thick undergrowth, and a rival witch doctor with his own agenda.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A machete, to cut through the jungle vines

Recommended

  • Food
  • Antipoison potion

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Tai Bwo Wannai village on Karamja and speak to Trufitus, the village's witch doctor.
  2. Agree to help him gather the ingredients needed for a powerful jungle potion.
  3. Head out into the surrounding jungle and use your machete to hack through the dense vines blocking your path.
  4. Search the jungle for snake weed, taking care as poisonous snakes may attack while you gather it.
  5. Track down the other rare ingredients Trufitus needs, which are scattered across different parts of the jungle.
  6. Visit the rival witch doctor, Trufitus's estranged brother, and hear his side of their long-standing feud.
  7. Decide how to handle the tension between the two witch doctors as you continue collecting supplies.
  8. Return each ingredient to Trufitus as you find it, checking in on his progress with the brew.
  9. Watch as Trufitus combines the ingredients and successfully completes his jungle potion.
  10. Speak to Trufitus one final time to receive his thanks and complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • Woodcutting experience
  • Fishing experience
  • Improved standing with the Tai Bwo Wannai villagers
