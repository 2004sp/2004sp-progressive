Holy Grail

Quest guide · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Difficulty

Start point
Speak to King Arthur inside Camelot castle after completing Merlin's Crystal.

With Merlin freed and Excalibur in hand, King Arthur sets you on the ultimate Arthurian quest: recovering the fabled Holy Grail. The path leads through a cursed realm ruled by the ailing Fisher King, guarded by a mysterious black knight, and steeped in riddles that only the truest of heart can hope to solve.

Requirements

  • Completion of Merlin's Crystal

Items needed

  • Excalibur
  • A weapon and armour suitable for the black knight guarding the way

Recommended

  • Food
  • Prayer potions

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to King Arthur in Camelot about the location of the Holy Grail.
  2. Set out to find the entrance to the hidden, cursed realm associated with the legend.
  3. Confront the mysterious black knight blocking your path and defeat him in combat.
  4. Enter the blighted lands ruled by the wounded Fisher King.
  5. Speak to the Fisher King and learn of the curse afflicting his realm.
  6. Explore the realm for clues about how to lift the curse and find the Grail.
  7. Solve the puzzles or trials guarding the Grail's resting place.
  8. Locate the chamber where the Holy Grail is kept.
  9. Use Excalibur, or the knowledge you've gathered, to safely claim the Grail.
  10. Return through the cursed lands to the ordinary world.
  11. Bring the Holy Grail back to King Arthur in Camelot.
  12. Speak to King Arthur once more to complete the quest.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points
  • The ability to recharge Excalibur's special attack at a Camelot fountain
  • Access to a new area associated with the Fisher King's realm
  • Combat-related experience
