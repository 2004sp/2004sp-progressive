Sheep Herder

Start point
Speak to Farmer Brumty at his sheep pen near Falador.

Farmer Brumty has a strange request involving his flock of sheep and a shipment bound for the weapon-free island of Entrana. What starts as a simple favour for a farmer turns out to be a cover for smuggling forbidden goods past the monks who guard the island, and you are the one caught in the middle.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • Coins to purchase dye from Aggie the witch in Draynor Village

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Farmer Brumty near his sheep pen outside Falador and agree to help him with his unusual task.
  2. Learn that he needs a sheep dyed a particular colour before it can be shipped out on the next boat.
  3. Travel to Draynor Village and visit Aggie the witch to buy the dye you need for the job.
  4. Return to the sheep pen and use the dye on one of Brumty's sheep to change its colour as requested.
  5. Take the freshly dyed sheep down to Port Sarim and board the ship bound for Entrana.
  6. Once on Entrana, present the sheep and observe as the monks grow suspicious of the shipment.
  7. Discover that the wool has been used to smuggle weapons onto the island, breaking Entrana's strict no-weapons law.
  8. Deal with the fallout of the discovery and clear up the misunderstanding with the monks on the island.
  9. Return to Farmer Brumty to explain what happened and finish the quest.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point
  • A small amount of coins
  • Crafting experience
