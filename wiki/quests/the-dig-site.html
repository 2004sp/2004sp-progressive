The archaeologists at the Dig Site south-east of Varrock will not let an unqualified visitor excavate their best finds. Earn all three Earth Sciences certificates, help the students recover the samples they need, and prove yourself to archaeological expert Terry Balando. Your investigation eventually uncovers a talisman bearing the symbol of Zaros, a buried altar and a blocked chamber that can only be reached by preparing a dangerous chemical compound.

Requirements

Required levels

  • 25 Thieving. Required to pickpocket a Dig Site workman for one student's first rock sample. Workmen are also a convenient source of the specimen brush and ropes.
  • 10 Agility. Build 254 checks this when you lower yourself down either winch shaft.
  • 10 Herblore. Required when adding the later ingredients to the explosive mixture.

Bring before starting

  • 1 cup of tea. The panning guide takes it before he allows you to pan the stream.
  • 2 ropes. Each of the two quest winches needs its own rope in build 254. You can instead pickpocket ropes from Dig Site workmen.
  • 1 empty vial. Used to take the dangerous liquid from the sealed barrel.
  • Pestle and mortar. Used to grind charcoal for the chemical compound.
  • Tinderbox. Used to ignite the compound-covered bricks near the end of the quest.
  • A cut opal, or a chisel. The purple-shirted student asks for an opal before giving her third-exam answer. Panning can produce an uncut opal, which must be cut before the build-254 student script accepts it.

Obtained during the quest

  • Unstamped and stamped recommendation letter. The Examiner gives the first version; Curator Haig Halen at Varrock Museum stamps it.
  • Three rock samples. One is found in a bush, one by panning and one by pickpocketing a workman.
  • Panning tray. Taken from the Dig Site tent.
  • Trowel. Awarded with the level-1 certificate after the first exam.
  • Specimen brush. Pickpocket one from a Dig Site workman if you do not already have one.
  • Specimen jar. Search the sample sacks near the level-3 excavation.
  • Zaros talisman. Dug from a level-3 site after graduation; Terry Balando recognises its ancient symbol.
  • Invitation letter. Terry gives this after inspecting the talisman. Show it to a workman to gain access to the shafts.
  • Arcenia root, chest key, charcoal, ammonium nitrate and nitroglycerin. These become the explosive compound.
  • Stone tablet. Taken from the chamber behind the blasted bricks and shown to Terry to finish the quest.

Recommended

  • Several free inventory spaces. The exams, panning, digging and chemistry stages create many temporary quest items.
  • Food. No fight is required, but skeletons occupy the underground route and the chemical items can inflict heavy self-damage if deliberately dropped.
  • Leather gloves and leather boots if you want to use the level-1 dig areas for ordinary finds. Build 254 enforces both there, although they are not required for the talisman route at the level-3 site.

Before you start

The quest's title in the period material is often simply Digsite Quest. Do not use the modern museum-cleaning route as a substitute: the September-2004 quest is built around three Earth Sciences exams, three student errands, an ancient Zaros talisman and an explosive-compound puzzle.

The first exam is intentionally gated. Once the stamped recommendation has been accepted, the correct answer choices only become available as the students teach them to you. A period walkthrough explicitly has the player fail an early attempt before gathering the three rock samples.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Get the recommendation stamped

  1. Travel to the Exam Centre at the Dig Site south-east of Varrock and speak to the Examiner.
  2. Ask about taking the Earth Sciences exams. She gives you an unstamped recommendation letter.
  3. Go to Varrock Museum and use the letter on Curator Haig Halen. He stamps it.
  4. Return to the Examiner and hand over the stamped recommendation. You are now eligible to work through the three exams.
  5. If you immediately try the first exam, the useful answer choices are still unavailable. The students around the Dig Site teach them after you complete their first errands.

2. Help the three students

  1. Purple-shirted student: search the correct bush near the urns on the raised walkway to find her rock sample, then give it to her.
  2. Orange-shirted student: take the panning tray from the tent, speak to the panning guide at the stream and offer him your cup of tea. Pan and search the filled tray until you obtain the muddy rock sample, then give it to the orange-shirted student.
  3. Green-shirted student: with 25 Thieving, pickpocket a Dig Site workman until you obtain the remaining rock sample, then give it to the green-shirted student.
  4. Keep pickpocketing if you still need a specimen brush or either of your two ropes. Build 254 includes all of these in the workman's successful pickpocket table.
  5. While panning, it is useful to obtain an uncut opal for the third exam. Cut it with a chisel; if it shatters, pan for another.

3. Pass the first Earth Sciences exam

Return to the Examiner. Choose these three answers:

  1. What is Earth Science? — “The study of the earth, its contents, and history.”
  2. Who is eligible to use the Dig Site? — “All that have passed the appropriate Earth Sciences exam.”
  3. What are the proper health and safety points? — “Gloves and boots to be worn at all times; proper tools must be used.”

Passing awards the level-1 certificate and a trowel. Keep the trowel: it is needed for the talisman and the nitroglycerin barrel later.

4. Learn and pass the second exam

  1. Speak to all three students again. No new item errand is required for this round; they teach the second set of answers.
  2. Return to the Examiner and use the following answers:
  • Sample transportation: “Samples taken in rough form; kept only in sealed containers.”
  • Handling finds: “Finds must be carefully handled, and gloves worn.”
  • Rock pick: “Always handle with care; strike cleanly on its cleaving point.”

You receive the level-2 certificate.

5. Learn and pass the third exam

  1. Speak to the students for their final lessons. The purple-shirted student will not give her final answer until you give her an opal.
  2. Return to the Examiner and answer:
  • Sample preparation: “Samples cleaned, and carried only in specimen jars.”
  • Specimen brush: “Brush carefully and slowly using short strokes.”
  • Bones: “Handle bones very carefully and keep them away from other samples.”

Passing awards the level-3 certificate. You are now fully qualified to use the Dig Site's excavation levels, and the Examiner directs you toward impressing the archaeological expert.

6. Find the Zaros talisman

  1. Search the sample sacks near the level-3 excavation until you obtain a specimen jar.
  2. Make sure you are carrying the trowel, specimen jar and specimen brush.
  3. Use the trowel on soil in a level-3 dig area. Build 254 rolls the site's normal loot table, so the quest item may take several attempts.
  4. Keep digging until you uncover the strange Zaros talisman.
  5. Take the talisman to Terry Balando, the archaeological expert in the Exam Centre. He recognises the symbol of Zaros and gives you an invitation/recommendation letter authorising access to the private shafts.
  6. Use that letter on a Dig Site workman. This advances the quest to the mineshaft-permit stage.

7. Explore both winch shafts

  1. Use one rope on the western winch. Build 254 permanently records that rope as attached for your quest state.
  2. Operate the winch and lower yourself down. The descent requires 10 Agility.
  3. Pick up an Arcenia root. You will need it as the final ingredient of the chemical compound.
  4. Return to the surface. Attach your second rope to the north-eastern/private winch and descend.
  5. Speak to the workman below. Say that you have been invited to research there, ask where to find a chest key, and keep politely begging until he gives you the chest key.
  6. Return to the surface. The key is for the locked quest chest near the panning/tent area.

8. Collect and identify the chemical ingredients

  1. Use the chest key on the locked chest by the Dig Site tent and search the opened chest for unidentified chemical powder.
  2. West of the tent, find the sealed barrel. Use your trowel on it to lever the lid open.
  3. Use an empty vial on the opened barrel to obtain the orange unidentified liquid.
  4. With a specimen jar in your inventory, search the nearby specimen tray until you find charcoal. Grind it with the pestle and mortar.
  5. Take both unidentified chemicals to Terry Balando. He identifies the powder as ammonium nitrate and the liquid as nitroglycerin.
  6. Optional: search the Exam Centre bookcases for the book on chemicals. It describes the same four-part mixture used by the quest.

9. Make the compound and blast the bricks

  1. Use ammonium nitrate with nitroglycerin. This creates the first chemical mixture.
  2. Add the ground charcoal. Build 254 requires 10 Herblore for the chemistry sequence.
  3. Add the Arcenia root. You now have the finished chemical compound.
  4. Return down the western winch and follow the cavern toward the blocked southern passage.
  5. Use the chemical compound on the bricks blocking the chamber.
  6. Use your tinderbox on the compound-covered bricks. Your character runs clear and the explosion removes the blockage.

10. Recover the stone tablet

  1. Enter the newly opened chamber and take the stone tablet from the altar area.
  2. Reading it identifies the chamber as an altar of the “dread lord Zaros”.
  3. Climb back to the surface and take the stone tablet to Terry Balando.
  4. Show him the tablet. His recognition of the buried Zaros altar triggers quest completion.

Hazards and useful notes

  • No boss fight: combat is not a quest requirement. Period guides only recommend being able to run past the skeletons encountered underground.
  • Chemicals are dangerous: in build 254, deliberately dropping the unidentified liquid, identified nitroglycerin or later mixtures destroys the item and deals progressively larger self-damage. Keep the chemistry items in your inventory until they are used.
  • Two ropes really are separate: build 254 stores independent rope-attached bits for the normal and private winches. Attaching one does not satisfy the other.
  • Need another specimen brush? The workman pickpocket table can supply one. Jagex specifically fixed specimen-brush availability the day after the quest's original 2003 release.
  • Need an opal? Panning can yield uncut opals. The build-254 student checks for the cut opal object, so cut an uncut one before offering it.
  • Talisman not appearing? Keep digging the level-3 soil with the trowel while carrying the specimen jar and specimen brush. Build 254 gives the talisman through the normal random dig table rather than guaranteeing it on the first attempt.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 15,300 Mining XP.
  • 2,000 Herblore XP.
  • 2 gold bars.
  • Access to the Dig Site's panning and excavation activities through the qualifications and permissions earned during the quest.

There is no 4-Quest-Point reward, generic experience lamp, survey-calibration section or trapped-archaeologist rescue in the September-2004 quest. Those claims in the previous local page were unrelated fabrication.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: quest_itexam progresses through recommendation stamping, first/second/third exams, impressing the expert, mineshaft permission, applying the compound, removing the blockage and final tablet completion.
  • Requirements: itexam_journal.rs2 lists 10 Agility, 10 Herblore and 25 Thieving. The winch script enforces 10 Agility, the workman pickpocket supplies the required first-exam sample at 25 Thieving, and itexam_chemistry.rs2 enforces 10 Herblore during mixture preparation.
  • Exam gating: the student scripts unlock the correct answer flags. The Examiner then checks those learned states before each Earth Sciences certificate is awarded.
  • Student errands: build 254 uses three distinct first-exam samples: one from a workman pickpocket, one from panning and one from the Dig Site bush. The purple-shirt student's final errand checks for a cut opal.
  • Level-3 digging: area_digsite.rs2 requires a trowel, specimen jar and specimen brush. The Zaros talisman is a random level-3 dig-table result while it has not yet been shown to the expert.
  • Winches: the normal and private winches have separate attached-rope bits and each consumes one rope. Both routes check for level 10 Agility before lowering the player into the caverns.
  • Chemistry: Terry Balando identifies the quest powder as ammonium nitrate and the orange liquid as nitroglycerin. The recipe is ammonium nitrate + nitroglycerin, then ground charcoal, then Arcenia root.
  • Drop hazard: build 254 deals 25 damage for dropping the unidentified liquid, 35 for nitroglycerin, then 45, 55 and 65 damage as the mixture advances through later stages.
  • Final chamber: the finished compound must be used on digblastbrick, followed by a tinderbox. The resulting state change opens the altar route and the stone tablet explicitly names Zaros.
  • Exact completion reward: quest_itexam.rs2 awards 15,300 Mining XP, 2,000 Herblore XP and 2 gold bars; ^itexam_questpoints is 2.
  • Reconstruction caution: some implementation comments cite later OSRS/RS3 references to reconstruct missing details. The guide therefore treats build-254 mechanics as implementation evidence and uses period sources separately for September-2004 historical claims.
