Start point
Visit the Digsite Exam Centre north of Varrock and speak to the Examiner about sitting the archaeology test.

North of Varrock, archaeologists have uncovered a dig site hinting at a lost civilisation buried beneath the fields. Before you can so much as touch a trowel, you must prove your knowledge by passing a written examination, after which the site's overworked diggers will put you to real use. What begins as simple fieldwork soon turns into a rescue when an old tunnel gives way underground.

Requirements

  • None

Items needed

  • A pot
  • A trowel
  • A specimen brush
  • A specimen jar

Recommended

  • Food
  • Spare inventory space for finds

Walkthrough

  1. Head to the Exam Centre near the dig site and take the archaeology exam from the Examiner to earn your digging permit.
  2. Once you pass, collect the basic digging tools if you do not already have them and head onto the site itself.
  3. Help the archaeologists by digging in the marked pits and unearthing pottery shards, bones, and other artefacts.
  4. Use your specimen brush to clean dirty finds so they can be properly identified.
  5. Take your identified finds to Curator Haig Halen at the Varrock Museum for cataloguing.
  6. Return to the dig site and speak with the lead archaeologist, Terry Balando, about the deeper excavation.
  7. Assist with calibrating equipment used to survey the ruins, following the instructions of the site's specialists.
  8. Investigate reports of a second, older set of ruins beneath the visible dig, and enter the passages that lead there.
  9. Explore the unstable tunnels carefully, as parts of the old ruin are prone to collapse.
  10. Deal with the emergency when a cave-in traps one of the archaeologists below ground, and help free them.
  11. Report your findings back to the surface team once the immediate danger has passed.
  12. Speak to Curator Halen one final time to wrap up the excavation and claim your reward.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points
  • An antique lamp granting experience in a skill of your choice
  • Full access to dig for treasure at the Dig Site
  • A stepping stone toward Legends' Quest
