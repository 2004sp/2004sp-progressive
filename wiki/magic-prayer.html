3. Magic & Prayer

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

Magic — standard spellbook

21 non-combat spells are defined in magic_spells.dbrow: Enchant levels 1–5, the six teleports available by 2004 (Varrock, Lumbridge, Falador, Camelot, Ardougne, Watchtower), 4 elemental orb-charges, Low/High Alchemy, Superheat Item, Bones to Bananas, Telekinetic Grab, and God Spell (Charge). Combat spells (the Strike/Bolt/Blast/Wave lines) live separately in skill_combat/configs/magic/magic_combat_spells.dbrow. Ancient Magicks (2005) are correctly absent.

Complete for the period

Prayer — standard prayer book

prayers.dbrow defines exactly the classic 15: Thick Skin through Protect Item. Real RuneScape added 3 more — Retribution, Redemption, and Smite — alongside the Priest in Peril quest on 29 June 2004, unlocked at the Paterdomus altar. That quest is fully implemented here, but none of those 3 prayers exist in this repo.
