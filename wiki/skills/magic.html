Magic is a free-to-play combat and utility skill built around rune-powered spells. At the 7 September 2004 cutoff, the standard spellbook already covered elemental combat, stat reduction, binding, alchemy, enchanting, teleports, orb charging, Telekinetic Grab, Iban Blast, the three god spells and Charge. Members gain the higher combat waves and several utility/special spells, while much of the core spellbook remains free-to-play.

Correction from the old page: build 254 contains only the six ordinary teleports to Varrock, Lumbridge, Falador, Camelot, Ardougne and the Watchtower. There is no level-61 Magic unlock and no Trollheim Teleport entry in the ref-254 spell database. The previous Magic guide's Trollheim row has therefore been removed.

How Magic experience works

Combat spells give two kinds of Magic XP. Casting first awards the spell's fixed base XP from the spell database. If a damaging spell lands, build 254 then awards additional combat XP from the damage: normally 2 Magic XP per point of credited damage. When the build treats the cast as defensive Magic, damage instead gives about 1.33 Magic XP + 1 Defence XP per point. Ordinary damaging Magic also awards 1.33 Hitpoints XP per point of credited damage.

  • Fixed spell XP: every spell row has a configured base experience value. The project stores experience internally in tenths, so a value such as 55 is displayed here as 5.5 Magic XP.
  • Damage XP: successful damaging combat spells call the general combat-XP routine after damage is capped to the target's remaining Hitpoints. Target-specific combat-XP multipliers can therefore alter the damage-derived portion.
  • Splashing: the fixed spell XP and runes are handled when the spell is cast, before the hit roll; damage-derived Magic and Hitpoints XP require credited damage.
  • Membership and level checks: build 254 checks the spell's members flag and required Magic level before casting.
  • Rune checks: the spell database supplies up to three rune requirements. The shared Magic handler accounts for an equipped staff that supplies a matching elemental rune before it checks or deletes runes.
  • Worn-item gates: special spells can require a specific equipped staff. Iban Blast and each of the three god spells use this mechanism in build 254.

Training methods

Magic can be trained through combat or through repeatable utility spells. This guide records exact per-cast and per-damage rules from build 254 instead of importing modern XP/hour routes whose rates depend on supplies, targets, travel and player behaviour.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Two spell databases: ordinary utility/enchant/teleport/orb spells are in scripts/skill_magic/configs/magic_spells.dbrow; combat spells are in scripts/skill_combat/configs/magic/magic_combat_spells.dbrow.
  • Complete non-combat set: the ref-254 utility database contains 21 entries: five enchantments, six teleports, four orb charges, Low and High Alchemy, Superheat Item, Bones to Bananas, Telekinetic Grab and Charge.
  • No Trollheim spell in ref 254: the six teleport entries stop at Watchtower. The level-up enum also has no level-61 Magic unlock.
  • Wind Strike starts at level 1: it is present in the combat-spell database but is not listed as a later level-up milestone because a new character can cast it from the starting Magic level.
  • Special combat spells are real build content: Iban Blast, Saradomin Strike, Claws of Guthix and Flames of Zamorak are explicit ref-254 combat-spell rows with staff requirements. The previous local Magic page omitted them.
  • Fixed XP is explicit: the shared Magic procedure advances the Magic skill by the spell row's configured experience value when a spell is cast.
  • Damage XP is separate: successful damaging spells then call the general combat-XP procedure. Normal Magic gets 2 Magic XP per credited damage; defensive Magic gets about 1.33 Magic XP plus 1 Defence XP, with Hitpoints XP awarded separately.
  • Staff rune substitution is implemented: the shared requirement routine looks up the equipped staff and zeroes matching rune counts before inventory checks and rune deletion.

Cutoff and scope notes

Do not extend this spellbook with later content. The guide describes the standard spell set implemented at ref 254 and the 7 September 2004 snapshot. Trollheim Teleport and later alternative spellbook content are not presented as target-period Magic unlocks.

  • Quest requirements stay beside the spell: Ardougne and Watchtower Teleports are not advertised as level-only unlocks, and Iban Blast / god spells retain their staff gates.
  • Members status is per spell: the Strike/Bolt/Blast lines are F2P while Wave spells are members-only; utility membership is likewise taken from each ref-254 spell row rather than inferred from level.
  • Project deviations are labelled: the Imp Catcher and Watchtower reward differences are intentionally separated into historical-target and Progressive-build values.
  • No invented XP/hour: combat target multipliers, hit chance, damage, rune supply, travel and casting rhythm all affect practical rates, so this page gives exact build XP rules rather than unsupported hourly estimates.

  • Ref-254 combat spell data: Content/scripts/skill_combat/configs/magic/magic_combat_spells.dbrow supplies the elemental combat progression, binding/debuff spells, Crumble Undead, Iban Blast, god spells, rune costs, base XP and maximum hits.
  • Ref-254 level-up list: Content/scripts/levelup/configs/levelup_unlocks.enum supplies the Magic milestone levels and confirms there is no level-61 Magic unlock.
  • Ref-254 Magic requirements/XP: Content/scripts/skill_magic/scripts/magic.rs2 checks membership, level, runes and worn-item requirements, applies staff rune substitution and awards the configured base spell XP.
  • Ref-254 combat casting: Content/scripts/skill_combat/scripts/player/player_magic.rs2 consumes runes and awards base spell XP before the hit roll, then routes successful damaging spells through the combat-XP procedure.
  • Ref-254 damage-derived XP: Content/scripts/skill_combat/scripts/combat.rs2 defines normal and defensive Magic's damage-XP splits and the separate Hitpoints award.
  • Local quest cross-checks: the already-audited Witch's Potion, Imp Catcher, The Grand Tree, Watchtower, Underground Pass and Legends' Quest guides provide the quest rewards, scroll/staff gates and documented build deviations used here.
