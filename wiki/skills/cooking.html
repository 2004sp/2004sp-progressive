Cooking is a free-to-play production skill used to turn raw meat, fish and prepared ingredients into food. Successful cooking normally gives Cooking XP and an edible item; failure normally produces a burnt item instead. By 7 September 2004 the skill already covered ordinary fish and meat, pies, stews, pizzas, cakes, wine, members-only snails and eels, gnome cuisine, Big Chompy Bird Hunting food, and high-level catches through manta ray.

Mechanics

Basic cooking loop: use a supported raw item on a fire, range or oven. Build 254 checks the item's required Cooking level and membership flag, consumes the raw item, then rolls success from the item's cooking data. A success gives the cooked product and its listed XP; a normal failure gives the corresponding burnt product and no Cooking XP.

  • Current Cooking level matters: the generic cooker calls the build's Cooking skill roll with the player's current level. Temporary boosts can therefore affect success checks, although this page lists normal base-level unlocks rather than advertising boosted unlocks.
  • Fire versus range: many raw meats and fish can be cooked on either. Some database rows provide a better range-specific success roll, while prepared foods such as bread, pies, pizza and cake require a range or oven rather than an ordinary fire.
  • Lumbridge Cook-o-matic 100: after completing Cook's Assistant, the castle cook permits use of his range. Build 254 gives supported foods a separate Cook-o-matic success roll, making it preferable to an ordinary range where that override exists.
  • Cooking gauntlets: after Family Crest, Caleb can enchant the steel gauntlets into Cooking gauntlets. At the target date and in build 254, their special reduced-burn roll is specifically implemented for lobster, swordfish and shark.
  • Wine: grapes and a jug of water make unfermented wine. It later resolves into wine or bad wine; successful fermentation awards 110 Cooking XP in build 254.
  • No intrinsic damage: ordinary cooking failures waste food but do not damage the player. Risk comes from where ingredients are obtained or where a player chooses to cook, not from the basic Cooking action itself.

2004scape implementation notes

Why this page is larger than the old one: levelup_unlocks_cooking.rs2 only contains milestone popup messages and is not a complete Cooking guide. Build 254 also has a fuller skill-guide row, a table-driven generic Cooking database, separate inventory recipe scripts, Cook-o-matic overrides, Cooking-gauntlet overrides, gnome cooking scripts and quest-specific cooking flows. This audit uses those sources together instead of treating the popup list as exhaustive.

  • Generic food data: cooking_generic.dbrow supplies required levels, XP, burnt products and success-roll variants. The generic cooking.rs2 handler enforces membership and level checks before processing a cook.
  • Range and special-range behaviour: the generic handler can replace the ordinary success roll with range-specific or Lumbridge Cook-o-matic values when the database row provides them.
  • Cooking gauntlets: the handler uses a gauntlet-specific roll only when one exists in the row; the period-supported targets in this build are lobster, swordfish and shark.
  • Gnome recipes: the checkout contains battas, crunchies, bowls and cocktails, but several reconstructed gnome level restrictions are absent or uncertain. Their existence is documented without fabricating exact unlock levels.
  • Chocolate-cake conflict: the cake script's own historical-source comment says adding chocolate should not give XP, but the current code executes stat_advance(cooking, 300), which is 30 Cooking XP in this project's x10 skill scale. The table above records the build behaviour and flags the conflict rather than silently choosing one.

Cutoff exclusion — Tai Bwo Wannai Trio: the checkout also contains Tai Bwo Wannai Trio and karambwan-related Cooking code, but that quest was released on 14 September 2004, one week after this wiki's 7 September 2004 snapshot. Karambwan and the quest's Cooking reward are therefore intentionally excluded from target-period unlocks and training.

Source notes

  • Historical anchors: Cooking is an original RuneScape skill dated to 4 January 2001; the Cooks' Guild is an early 2001 feature; gnome food and cocktail content predates the cutoff; Big Chompy Bird Hunting was released in May 2004; and Tai Bwo Wannai Trio is dated 14 September 2004, after the target snapshot.
  • Build-254 Cooking implementation: scripts/skill_cooking/scripts/cooking.rs2, scripts/skill_cooking/configs/cooking_source/cooking_generic.dbrow, scripts/player/configs/skill_guide.dbrow, scripts/levelup/scripts/levelup_unlocks_cooking.rs2, and the recipe scripts under scripts/skill_cooking/scripts/cooking_inv/.
  • Special systems: cooking_guild.rs2, the gnome-cooking scripts, quest_cook.rs2, crest_caleb.rs2, and the Big Chompy cooking script supply the guild, Cook-o-matic, gauntlet and quest-specific behaviour.
  • Quest reward cross-checks: the already-audited local Cook's Assistant, Gertrude's Cat, Heroes' Quest, Big Chompy Bird Hunting and Family Crest guides agree with the build findings used above.
