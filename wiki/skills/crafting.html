Crafting turns raw materials into equipment and useful objects. At the 7 September 2004 cutoff that includes pottery, wool and flax spinning, ordinary leather, studded leather, gem cutting, gold and silver jewellery, members-only glassblowing and battlestaves, and RuneScape 2's four colours of dragonhide armour. The old local page reproduced only the level-up popup list; this audit also checks the build's actual action/configuration scripts and period evidence, which exposes several missing level-1 activities and one real build conflict.

Mechanics

Crafting is a collection of separate production systems: most actions require a specific tool or facility, check the player's current Crafting level, consume the input, create the product and then award the configured XP. Membership is enforced separately for members-only materials and processes.

  • Pottery: soften clay with water, form the soft clay on a potter's wheel, then fire the unfired item in a pottery oven. In build 254, forming checks the item's level and is guaranteed once started; firing can still crack the item. A cracked item is lost and awards no firing XP.
  • Leather: use a needle on tanned leather while carrying thread. Ordinary leather and hard leather are free-to-play; coifs, studded leather and dragonhide crafting are members-only in build 254. The build tracks thread use and consumes one reel after five completed leather crafts.
  • Spinning: wool can be spun into a ball of wool from level 1 for 2.5 XP. Members can spin flax into bow strings from level 10 for 15 XP.
  • Gem cutting: a chisel cuts gems. Sapphire, emerald, ruby and diamond cuts do not have a failure roll in the 254 script. Members-only opal, jade and red topaz can be crushed on a failed roll, giving reduced XP instead of the cut gem.
  • Jewellery: use a gold bar at a furnace with the correct mould; gem jewellery also needs the relevant cut gem. Silver-symbol/sickle casting uses a silver bar and the matching mould. Amulets and symbols are cast unstrung, then a ball of wool strings them for an additional 4 Crafting XP.
  • Glass: members heat soda ash and a bucket of sand in a furnace to make molten glass, then use a glassblowing pipe. Build 254 implements beer glasses, vials and unpowered orbs only.
  • Battlestaves: members attach a charged elemental orb to a battlestaff. Charging the orb is a Magic action; Crafting supplies the final staff-making level and XP.
  • Cape dyeing: recolouring a supported cape consumes the cape and dye and awards 2.5 Crafting XP; mixing two dyes itself does not award Crafting XP in the 254 script.

2004scape implementation notes

The level-up popup is not the skill guide. levelup_unlocks_crafting.rs2 omits several working actions — including level-1 gloves, wool spinning, opal cutting, beer glasses, ruby necklaces, unholy symbols and silver sickles. This audited page derives capability from the real recipe/action data and uses the popup as one evidence source rather than the sole source.

  • Sapphire-necklace conflict: the 254 level-up script says level 22, matching the historical requirement. The 254 jewellery.struct recipe data stores 20, and the crafting action reads that struct directly, so the build currently permits the necklace two levels early. The main table preserves the 2004 level and records the implementation divergence.
  • Pottery: the 254 pottery script gives XP when forming the unfired item, then independently rolls success when firing. Softening clay itself gives no Crafting XP in this implementation.
  • Semiprecious gems: opal, jade and red topaz have explicit success rolls. A failed cut produces crushed gemstone and awards roughly one quarter of the normal XP under the project's tenths-of-XP scale.
  • Thread consumption: the leather script increments a per-player counter and consumes one reel after five successful leather crafts, rather than consuming one thread item per craft.
  • Members gates: flax spinning, glass, battlestaves, coifs, studded leather, dragonhide, semiprecious gems and dragonstone routes all have explicit members checks or members-only inputs in the 254 content.
  • Quest-specific Crafting: the checkout also contains specialised Crafting actions inside quests such as Shilo Village, Dwarf Cannon, Big Chompy Bird Hunting and Family Crest. These are documented through their quest guides rather than misrepresented as general training unlocks.

Cutoff exclusions: the old local page had already drifted beyond the target date. Snelms were added on 14 October 2004, and The Fremennik Trials was released on 2 November 2004; both are after the wiki's 7 September 2004 snapshot. Tag 254 likewise has no snelm script and no Fremennik level-up message. Later Crafting additions such as bracelets, snakeskin armour and expanded glassblowing are also outside this page's target period.

  • Historical cutoff checks: RuneScape update history places semiprecious gems before the cutoff, RuneScape 2 dragonhide in March 2004, snelms on 14 October 2004, and The Fremennik Trials on 2 November 2004.
