Agility is a members-only skill used to cross obstacles, take shortcuts, enter specialised training courses, and improve the rate at which run energy recovers. In the September 2004 game, training centres on the Gnome Stronghold, Barbarian Outpost, Wilderness course, Gnome Ball, the Yanille Agility Dungeon, and the newly released Brimhaven Agility Arena.

Mechanics

Obstacle success: successful obstacles award Agility XP. Many harder obstacles can be failed; failure can move you away from the route and may deal damage, so food is useful at the Barbarian, Wilderness, Yanille, and Brimhaven activities. Higher Agility improves success on checks that use the skill.

  • Run energy: higher Agility reduces the time needed to recover run energy. Jagex strengthened this effect on 8 June 2004; the same Agility level then reduced restore time by roughly 50% more than before that update.
  • Boosts: an Agility potion temporarily raises Agility by 3. It was introduced with the Brimhaven update on 27 July 2004.
  • Course progress: the three lap courses award a completion bonus only when their obstacles are completed in the expected sequence. Missing or failing part of a route can prevent the lap bonus.
  • Danger: the Wilderness course is in deep Wilderness. In addition to obstacle damage, other players are a period-authentic danger.

Brimhaven Agility Arena

The Brimhaven Agility Arena was released on 27 July 2004. Cap'n Izzy No-Beard charges 200 coins to enter the bamboo hut in north-east Brimhaven. Inside, a flashing arrow marks an active ticket dispenser; the target changes about once per minute. The first successful tag in a sequence primes the ticket system, and subsequent consecutive successful tags award Agility Arena tickets.

The arena contains low-, medium-, and high-level obstacles. The build's level-up script identifies 20 Agility for medium obstacles and 40 Agility for high obstacles. A lower-level player can still enter, but may be forced onto longer routes when an obstacle is above their level.

Agility potion

Period recipe: at 34 Herblore, combine Toadflax with Toad's legs to make an Agility potion, receiving 80 Herblore XP. Drinking a dose temporarily raises Agility by 3 levels. See the Herblore guide for the wider potion table.

2004scape implementation notes

Build 254 implements course progress with per-player state and uses current Agility for many obstacle checks, so temporary boosts can affect numerous non-quest obstacles. Failure rolls and damage are scripted obstacle by obstacle rather than using one universal chance or damage formula. Where the cache-driven generic-shortcut data could not be tied to a period location with high confidence, this guide does not invent a location name.

Post-cutoff Agility content is intentionally excluded. In particular, this page does not import rooftop courses, graceful equipment, skillcapes, Karamja diary bonuses, the Ape Atoll course, or later extended courses.
