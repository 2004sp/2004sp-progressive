Agility is used for obstacle courses and shortcuts. The source's level-up messages identify the main Agility Arena thresholds and the two higher-level course entrances below.

Notes

The overall Agility implementation looks substantial, but the main audit still marks it unverified because its obstacle set was not diffed obstacle-by-obstacle against a historical list. Milestones come from levelup_unlocks_agility.rs2.
