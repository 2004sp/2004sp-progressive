Hitpoints is the character's health skill. Damage reduces current Hitpoints and healing restores them up to the current maximum. The build defines no separate equipment, area, or activity unlocks at particular Hitpoints levels.

Level unlocks

Members shows whether that specific unlock requires membership. This skill has no discrete content-unlock levels, so the progression effect is listed instead.

10–99No discrete content unlock; each base level raises the character's maximum Hitpoints.No

Notes

The character starts at level 10 Hitpoints. The build's levelup_unlocks_hitpoints list is empty.
