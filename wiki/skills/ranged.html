Ranged is a combat skill used with bows, the early crossbow, thrown weapons, and the dwarf multicannon. This guide uses the 7 September 2004 cutoff and has been checked against the build-254 server scripts and configs.

How Ranged combat works

Ranged attacks use the player's Ranged level together with the equipped weapon and armour bonuses. In build 254, the effective Ranged level starts from the current Ranged level plus the normal combat calculation baseline, then the selected attack style can add its own bonus. Equipment's Ranged attack bonus affects accuracy, while the weapon's Ranged strength value contributes to maximum damage.

StyleCombat effectXP from normal damage
Accurate+3 effective Ranged.4 Ranged XP and 1.33 Hitpoints XP per point of damage.
RapidNo +3 stat bonus; attacks one tick faster than the weapon's normal attack delay.4 Ranged XP and 1.33 Hitpoints XP per point of damage.
Longrange+3 effective Defence and +2 tiles of attack range, capped at 10 tiles.2 Ranged XP, 2 Defence XP, and 1.33 Hitpoints XP per point of damage.

The target build does not apply a Ranged-boosting prayer to the combat calculation, so later prayer-based Ranged boosts should not be backported into this guide.

Ammunition and weapon compatibility

Ordinary bows require arrows, the Ogre bow requires Ogre arrows, and the period crossbow uses bolts. Build 254 also checks the ammunition tier against the bow: ammunition with a higher level requirement than the bow is rejected.

Implementation and cutoff notes

  • Willow and Maple bows are members-only at this cutoff. Build 254 marks both tiers as members items. Their later free-to-play expansion happened after the September 2004 cutoff, so the old page's F2P labels were removed.
  • The target build contains the original basic Crossbow and bolts. The later metal crossbow and bolt progression is outside this guide's cutoff and is not listed.
  • The Ranging Guild entry script requires 40 base Ranged and the guild content is members-only.
  • Horror from the Deep is not included in the quest-XP table because it was released on 17 November 2004, after this wiki's 7 September 2004 cutoff.
  • The level-up interface is useful for unlock messaging, but item configs and level-requirement scripts are authoritative here for membership and secondary Defence/quest gates.
