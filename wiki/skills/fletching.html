Fletching is the members-only skill used to turn logs and ammunition components into bows, arrows, throwing darts, tipped bolts and quest-specific ogre arrows. The target-period skill is more than the level-up popup list: several useful products depend on another skill, a quest unlock or a separate supply chain. This guide uses a RuneScape 2 Fletching guide updated on 14 July 2004 as the main period community reference and checks it against the project's build-254 recipes.

How Fletching works

Members-only creation: the build-254 cutting, bow-stringing, arrow, dart, bolt and ogre-arrow routines all reject Fletching actions on a free-to-play map. A July-2004 RuneScape 2 guide likewise describes Fletching as a members skill. Finished ranged equipment could still have free-to-play uses; that does not make the act of Fletching free-to-play.

  • Cutting logs: use a knife on normal logs to choose arrow shafts, a shortbow or a longbow. Higher-tier logs are cut into their matching shortbow or longbow. Build 254 makes 15 arrow shafts from one normal log.
  • Stringing bows: use a bow string on the matching unstrung bow. If you make the string yourself, flax must first be spun on a spinning wheel; the July-2004 guide records level 10 Crafting for spinning flax into bow string.
  • Making arrows: normal logs become shafts; feathers make headless arrows; arrowheads finish the ammunition. Making your own metal arrowheads also requires the appropriate Smithing level, but traded arrowheads can be used directly.
  • Making darts: combine feathers with metal dart tips. Historically, the ability to make throwing darts is taught by Tourist Trap; the quest itself requires 10 Fletching and 20 Smithing.
  • Tipping bolts: the 2004 system upgrades ordinary bolts rather than fletching modern crossbow stocks. Opal, pearl and barbed bolt tips have their own Fletching thresholds and supply sources.
  • Ogre arrows: Big Chompy Bird Hunting introduces the separate Achey-log, wolfbone and four-feather ogre-arrow chain at level 5 Fletching.

The cutoff-period bolt system is much smaller than later crossbow/Fletching systems. The July-2004 guide says ordinary bolts are not made through Fletching; Fletching upgrades them with Opal, Pearl or Barbed tips. No invented XP/hour: a period-correct hourly rate depends on log supply, bank route, flax/string supply, trading and player pace. This guide keeps verified action XP and a dated training route rather than importing modern efficiency figures.

2004scape implementation notes

Build-254 core: the actual recipe scripts are under scripts/skill_fletching/. The guide was checked against the build-254 log cutting, bow stringing, arrows, darts, bolts and ogre-arrow scripts plus their database rows. The build's level-up popup is not a complete recipe list.

  • Level-up popup is incomplete: build 254's levelup_unlocks_fletching.rs2 lists the bow tiers and Iron-to-Rune arrows, but omits Bronze arrows, arrow shafts, headless arrows, darts, tipped bolts and ogre arrows even though their recipes exist elsewhere.
  • Tourist Trap gate is missing from generic dart-making: the historical July-2004 guide explicitly requires Tourist Trap before making darts, and the audited quest teaches the ability. Build 254's generic make_darts routine checks membership and Fletching level but does not check the quest state, so a player who obtains dart tips can mechanically fletch them without the historical unlock.
  • Opal/Pearl bolt levels differ: the July-2004 table records levels 17 and 33. Build 254 uses 11 and 41 respectively; Barbed bolts agree at 51.
  • Bow XP conflict: the period guide records 55 total XP for an Oak longbow, while build 254 awards 50. All other ordinary bow totals in the same table agree with the build after tenths are combined.
  • High-tier arrow XP conflicts: the period guide records 165 XP for the full 15-Adamant-arrow chain and 225 for Rune. Build 254 gives 170 and 207.5 respectively. Bronze through Mithril agree.
  • Iron-dart XP conflict: the July-2004 table records 32 XP; build 254 awards 38 XP for ten. The other dart XP values in that period table match the build.
  • Big Chompy Crafting gate: build 254 requires 5 Crafting when chiselling Wolf bones and awards Crafting XP alongside Fletching XP. The contemporary quest requirements list only 5 Fletching, 30 Cooking and 30 Ranged, so this is kept as a build-specific extra requirement.

Cutoff and uncertainty notes

Do not let later Fletching expansions leak backward into September 2004. Recipe data from a newer branch or a modern wiki is not automatically valid for build 254 or the target date.

  • The Fremennik Trials requirement is post-cutoff: the old local Fletching page included a level-25 Fremennik Trials note copied from a later level-up script. The quest was released on 2 November 2004 and is excluded from the 7 September guide.
  • Later crossbow construction is excluded: the cutoff-period evidence used here supports tipping ordinary bolts, not the much larger later system of fletching crossbow stocks, limbs and modern bolt families.
  • Javelin production is not promoted to a verified cutoff unlock: retrospective references place javelin items in early RuneScape 2 and later Fletching guides give javelin-making levels, but the dated July-2004 Fletching table used here does not list javelin production and build 254 contains no corresponding Fletching recipe. Without stronger period evidence, this guide does not guess the crafting method backward.
