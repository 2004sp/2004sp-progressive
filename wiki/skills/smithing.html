Smithing turns ore from Mining into metal bars at furnaces and then works Bronze, Iron, Steel, Mithril, Adamantite and Runite bars into equipment at anvils. On 7 September 2004 the core skill was free-to-play, while several ammunition, quest and special-item recipes required members access or additional quest progress.

How Smithing works

Two main loops: smelt ore at a furnace to make bars, then use a metal bar on an anvil while carrying a hammer to choose an item. Silver and Gold bars are primarily used by Crafting; they are smelted with Smithing but do not open the ordinary metal-equipment anvil interface.

  • Smelting checks your current Smithing level. The furnace also checks that all required ores and Coal are present. Bronze needs Copper + Tin; Steel and the higher base metals consume progressively more Coal.
  • Iron is unreliable without assistance. In build 254 an ordinary Iron-ore smelt has a 50% failure chance. Wearing a Ring of forging on a members world guarantees the Iron bar while consuming a ring charge.
  • Anvil work requires a hammer. Build 254 checks the selected recipe's Smithing level, the number of bars in your inventory and any membership or quest gate before consuming bars.
  • Anvil XP is based on bars used, not the finished item. A Bronze bar is worth 12.5 Smithing XP when worked at an anvil, Iron 25, Steel 37.5, Mithril 50, Adamantite 62.5 and Runite 75. A five-bar platebody therefore gives five times the metal's per-bar XP.
  • Some recipes have extra conditions. Dart tips require the lesson from The Tourist Trap; claws require Death Plateau completion; cannonballs require members access, 35 Smithing and the ammo mould obtained through Dwarf Cannon.
  • Quest smithing can use special objects instead of the normal anvil table. Examples include Elemental Workshop metal, Family Crest's perfect gold, the Legends' Quest golden bowl and the Dragon square shield repair.

Training at the cutoff

Smithing training in 2004 depended heavily on how the player obtained ore and bars. The reliable facts are the XP per bar/action and the available methods; this guide does not project modern banking routes, Blast Furnace rates or modern XP/hour figures backward into September 2004.

LevelMethodAccessXPRequirements / practical notes
1+Smelt Bronze and smith Bronze itemsFree-to-play6.2 build XP per bar smelted; 12.5 XP per Bronze bar workedCopper + Tin for smelting; hammer + anvil for items. The normal Bronze equipment table starts with dagger and axe at level 1.
AnyThe Knight's SwordFree-to-play12,725 Smithing XP on completionNo Smithing level gate. Mining the required Blurite yourself needs 10 Mining; the reward can skip a large part of early Smithing training.
15+Smelt Iron / smith IronFree-to-play12.5 XP per successful smelt; 25 XP per bar workedBuild 254's ordinary Iron smelting can fail 50% of the time, so ore cost is less predictable than later metals.
30+Smelt Steel / smith SteelFree-to-play17.5 XP per smelt; 37.5 XP per bar workedEach Steel bar needs 1 Iron ore + 2 Coal. Members with Dwarf Cannon can instead cast one Steel bar into four cannonballs for the same 37.5 Smithing XP per bar.
40+Smelt Gold with Goldsmithing gauntletsMembers56.2 XP per ordinary Gold bar in build 254Requires the Avan version of the Family Crest gauntlets. The target-period evidence conflict is documented above.
50+Higher metal smelting and anvil workFree-to-playMithril 30/50; Adamantite 37.5/62.5; Runite 50/75 XP per smelt/anvil barCoal costs rise to 4, 6 and 8 per ore. Which metal is practical depends on ore supply, banking and whether the finished item is useful or saleable.

Item choice does not change anvil XP per bar. Within one metal tier, making a five-bar platebody awards the same total anvil XP as spending those five bars across five one-bar items. The difference is inventory output, clicking and what product you want.

2004scape implementation notes

Core implementation: Content/scripts/skill_smithing/configs/smelting/smelting.struct defines normal bar levels, ingredients and XP; configs/smithing/smithing.dbrow and smithing.struct define anvil recipes, bar counts and per-bar XP; and the smelting/smithing scripts enforce the action rules and quest gates.

  • Iron smelting: the build removes the ore, then performs a 50% success/failure roll unless a Ring of forging is worn on a members world.
  • Goldsmithing gauntlets: build 254 multiplies ordinary Gold-bar smelting XP by 2.5. This is kept separate from the older July-2004 guide value noted above.
  • Current level checks: furnace and anvil scripts use the player's current Smithing stat. Temporary Smithing boosts can therefore satisfy checks while active.
  • Doric's anvils: the build rejects use before Doric's Quest completion. Other anvils do not inherit that quest gate.
  • Dart lesson: the anvil script tests Tourist Trap's learned-darts state before it accepts any dart-tip recipe.
  • Claw layer: the entire claw column is hidden until Death Plateau completion even if the player already has the numerical Smithing level.
  • Steel nails: the active build database has a level-34 Steel-nail recipe producing 2 nails per Steel bar. The old local guide omitted it because it relied too heavily on level-up messages.
  • Special anvils/furnaces: build 254 also contains area-specific access checks such as the Fremennik furnace/anvil restriction and quest-specific dispatches for the golden bowl, Elemental ore and the Dragon square shield.

Cutoff exclusions

Do not use later Smithing systems as September-2004 training advice. The target guide stops at mechanics and recipes available by 7 September 2004.

  • Blast Furnace: later content and not a target-period furnace/training method.
  • Smithable metal pickaxes: they are not part of build 254's audited Smithing recipe table and are not projected backward into this guide.
  • Later crossbow-component smithing and later metal/equipment tiers: omitted unless they belong to a verified cutoff quest interaction.
  • Modern Smithing activities, guild-like workshops and modern XP/hour routes: not used as evidence for 7 September 2004.
