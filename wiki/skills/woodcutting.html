Woodcutting gathers logs from trees for Firemaking and Fletching. The build follows early-era behaviour where axes themselves do not have Woodcutting-level requirements; the main gates are tree types and one quest requirement.

Level unlocks

Members shows whether that specific unlock requires membership. Mixed level-up messages are split into separate rows so every unlock has a clear Yes or No.

Notes

The source comments that axe level requirements were not part of the original period behaviour, so they are deliberately not presented as unlock milestones here. Milestones come from levelup_unlocks_woodcutting.rs2.
