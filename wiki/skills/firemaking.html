Firemaking burns logs with a tinderbox. Its progression is a simple ladder of log types, with Maple and Magic logs belonging to members content in this period.

Level unlocks

Members shows whether that specific unlock requires membership. Mixed level-up messages are split into separate rows so every unlock has a clear Yes or No.

LevelUnlockMembers
15Burn Oak logs.No
30Burn Willow logs.No
45Burn Maple logs.Yes
60Burn Yew logs.No
75Burn Magic logs.Yes

Notes

The level-up cases come from levelup_unlocks_firemaking.rs2. The Members column describes access to the log/content, not whether Firemaking itself can be trained on a members account.
