The Brimhaven Agility Arena is a members-only Agility activity beneath the bamboo hut in north-east Brimhaven. Cap'n Izzy No-Beard admits players for 200 coins. Inside, a flashing arrow points to one active ticket dispenser at a time. Reach and tag consecutive active dispensers to earn Agility arena tickets, then exchange those tickets with Pirate Jackie the Fruit for Agility experience, Toadflax, Snapdragon, or a Pirate's hook.

Historical/build split: Jagex's 27 July 2004 release announcement states that there is no Agility requirement to enter, that the target changes every minute, and that the first successful tag in a streak gives no ticket while each following consecutive tag does. Build 254 implements that same core loop, but its paid-entry flag is never cleared, so the build appears to charge a character only once instead of the historical 200 coins per visit.

Getting there

The entrance is the bamboo hut in north-east Brimhaven. Speak to Cap'n Izzy No-Beard outside the ladder. He explains the arena and takes the entrance fee.

  • From East Ardougne: use Captain Barnaby's ship at the East Ardougne docks. The route to Brimhaven costs 30 coins and predates the target snapshot. From the Brimhaven dock, walk north-east to the bamboo hut.
  • From Port Sarim: pay 30 coins to sail to Musa Point, then walk west across northern Karamja into Brimhaven and continue to the north-east hut. This is longer but period-valid.

How to start and play

  1. Talk to Cap'n Izzy No-Beard or use his Pay option. Historically, pay 200 coins for that visit.
  2. Climb down the ladder. An arena overlay appears and a flashing-arrow hint points to the currently active ticket dispenser.
  3. Cross the platform network by clicking its obstacles. Routes vary in difficulty, and some obstacles require 20 or 40 Agility.
  4. Click Tag on the dispenser under the flashing arrow before the target moves.
  5. The first correct tag starts a streak but gives no ticket. Tag the next active dispenser successfully to receive one Agility arena ticket. Continue tagging every target in sequence for one ticket per further successful target.
  6. If you miss a target, your streak is broken. The next successful target primes the streak again; the following consecutive success is the next one that awards a ticket.
  7. When finished, climb the ladder in the north-east area back to Brimhaven and trade tickets with Pirate Jackie the Fruit.

Target timing, scoring and multiplayer behaviour

Historically the flashing arrow changes to another platform every minute. Build 254 picks one of the 24 dispenser coordinates, refuses to repeat the current index, and schedules the next change at map_clock + 98, which is the implementation's near-one-minute cycle. The same selected dispenser is used for everyone in the arena, while each player's streak/ticket eligibility is held in that player's arena state.

  • There is no round win condition: the activity runs continuously. Your practical success condition is reaching consecutive active dispensers.
  • No teams: players share the target but do not share tickets, streaks or rewards.
  • Leaving: climbing out clears the personal target timer/overlay and streak bits. Tickets already in the inventory remain yours.
  • Re-entering: historically another 200-coin fee is due for another visit. Build 254's persistent paid bit is a deviation and does not charge again after the first payment.
  • Death: the arena is not scripted as a safe-death activity. If obstacle damage kills the player, normal build-254 death/item-loss handling applies.

Build damage formula: most damaging failures in game_agilityarena call the common expression calc((stat(hitpoints) * 5 / 100) + 2). This guide leaves that source formula explicit rather than guessing at a different display-unit interpretation. Falling below the platforms is recoverable: use a nearby climbing rope to return to the top level.

Tickets and rewards

The period currency is the stackable, untradeable Agility arena ticket. Tickets are retained when you leave and can be saved for larger XP bundles. Larger bundles give more Agility XP per ticket.

Pirate Jackie the Fruit, beside the arena entrance, opens the Brimhaven Agility Arena Ticket Exchange. This is a fixed exchange interface rather than a stock-based coin shop: there are no stock quantities or restock timers.

There are no random grab-bag rewards, no coin payout, no reward cooldown, and no success-round requirement beyond having enough tickets. The exchange can be repeated whenever you have the tickets.

Post-cutoff exclusions: Wergali is not a September-2004 reward; it was introduced years later and is absent from build 254. Modern Karamja diary bonuses, vouchers, amylase packs, graceful recolours and later reward-system changes are also outside this guide's cutoff.
