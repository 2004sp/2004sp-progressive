2004scape Progressive is a Lost City–based recreation of RuneScape 2 as it stood at revision 254, dated 7 September 2004 by the project's own setup notes, with a bot-AI and hiscores mod ("Progressive") layered on top. This page audits what's implemented, partial, or missing versus the real game on that date.

content/pack/*.pack (obj/npc/loc/seq/etc.) are text tables extracted wholesale from the real 254 cache — 3,169 items, 1,174 NPC names, 3,855 scenery objects — so the game's definitions are already essentially complete by construction. The meaningful question is behavior: which of those definitions have a script behind them — a quest chain, a dialogue tree, a shop till, a drop table, a working minigame — versus sitting inert. That's what this audit spent its effort on, read directly from the file tree and cross-checked against a verified chronological list of every quest RuneScape had shipped by 7 September 2004.

On “524”: the project's own server.json and start.js only support revisions 225, 244, 245.2, 254, 274, and 377-wip — there's no 524. This wiki assumes 254 was the intended target.
