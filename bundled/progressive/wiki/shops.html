5. Shops & economy

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

The generic shop engine (dynamic buy/sell pricing, haggle multipliers, stock depletion/restock, member-item gating, even a hardcoded Al Kharid silk-selling exception) is fully implemented in shop/scripts/shop.rs2. On top of it, roughly 74 distinct shops are stocked across area configs — general stores, every weapon/armour specialty shop, magic/rune shops, gem/jewelry shops, food/herblore/fletching/crafting shops, and regional oddities (Gnome Stronghold's shops, the Bedabin Camp shop, the April Fools' horse shop). 99 NPCs are tagged as shop keepers, roughly matching the shop count once buyer/seller pairs are accounted for.
