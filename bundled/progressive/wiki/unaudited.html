13. Previously unaudited subsystems

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

The audit above walks content/scripts/quests, skill_*, areas, and minigames against a real quest/feature chronology — a gameplay-content completeness audit. It never opened several other folders that exist in the repo. This section closes that gap: a follow-up pass specifically targeting everything the original methodology would have walked past.

Findings from the completed quest and skill guide audit

The later 58 quest/miniquest-guide and 19-skill guide audit did uncover additional inaccuracies and build-specific divergences that were outside this page's original subsystem pass. The table below records the strongest source-backed findings. A Build issue marks behaviour that is directly inconsistent with the verified quest/skill requirement or with the source's own intended flow; a Conflict means the build and surviving period evidence disagree, but the audit could not prove which historical value changed first.

AreaGuideVerdictAudit finding
Quest completionBiohazardBuild issueKing Lathas only reaches the quest-completion queue through the “I don't understand...” dialogue branch; the dismissive branch ends without completing the quest. Separately, the rope-ladder route does not actually check that the player is wearing the gas mask required by the period route.
Quest instancingDemon SlayerBuild issueThe flushed sewer key is spawned as an ordinary world-visible item rather than a per-player quest object, and Delrith is also a shared world NPC whose quest-state changes can affect other players. The guide audit confirmed both as cross-player state/instancing problems.
Quest rewardDoric's QuestBuild issueThe completion queue begins before the script adds the 180 coins. The source itself warns that the coin grant can be missed even though quest completion still triggers, so interruption at the wrong point can produce an incomplete reward.
Quest routeErnest the ChickenBuild issueThe Search-side bookcase-opening route into Draynor Manor's hidden basement path is explicitly described by the source as “borked” under the current zone search. The audited guide warns players about this rather than presenting the historical route as reliably functional.
Quest skill gateObservatory QuestBuild issueThe verified period requirement is 10 Crafting, but build 254's lens-making interaction removes molten glass and creates the lens with no Crafting-level check.
Quest / FletchingBig Chompy Bird HuntingExtra gateBuild 254 adds an unsupported level-5 Crafting requirement when chiselling wolf bones for ogre arrows and awards Crafting XP alongside Fletching XP. Contemporary quest requirements support 5 Fletching, 30 Cooking and 30 Ranged, not this extra Crafting gate.
FletchingFletchingMissing gateThe period skill/quest evidence makes The Tourist Trap the unlock for throwing-dart manufacture, but build 254's generic dart routine checks membership and Fletching level only. A player with dart tips can therefore fletch darts without completing the quest. The build level-up popup is also incomplete, omitting several recipes that are implemented elsewhere.
HerbloreHerbloreMissing gateGeneric herb-identification and potion-brewing routines do not check Druidic Ritual completion even though it is the verified historical entry route. The audit also retained several period/build numeric disagreements, including Tarromin identification 10 vs 11, Torstol 77 vs 75, Anti-firebreath 68 vs 69, and Weapon poison 137.5 vs 127.5 XP.
FishingFishingConflictThe 14 July 2004 RuneScape 2 guide records 30 Fishing XP for a Lava eel while build 254 gives 60. Big-net Seaweed is recorded as 1 XP in the period guide, while build 254's tenths-of-XP convention makes its stat_advance(fishing, 1) call 0.1 XP. These remain explicit unresolved period/build conflicts rather than silently choosing one value.
CookingCookingBuild issueThe cake script's own historical-source comment says adding chocolate to a cooked cake should give no additional Cooking XP, but the current code executes stat_advance(cooking, 300), awarding 30 Cooking XP.

Scope note: these are the notable inaccuracies/deviations exposed while rewriting and source-checking the public quest and skill guides; they are not a claim that every period/build disagreement is a code bug. Where contemporary evidence and build 254 merely disagree on a number, the row is deliberately labelled Conflict. Runtime bugs that already have a dedicated entry may also be cross-referenced on Known bugs & rough edges.

The one substantive finding from this pass: ClientGameProt.ts declares a full family of "anticheat" wire opcodes (ANTICHEAT_OPLOGIC1–9, ANTICHEAT_CYCLELOGIC1–7) with corresponding model classes (AnticheatOpLogic.ts, AnticheatCycleLogic.ts) — but a repo-wide search found no handler or codec that actually processes them. The wire format exists; nothing decodes or acts on it. Separately, rate limiting exists only for login attempts (World.ts, gated by NODE_RATELIMIT_ADDRESS_LOGIN / NODE_RATELIMIT_DEVICE_LOGIN) — there's no rate limiting on gameplay packets (drops, trades, etc.), and no transactional/locking protection around item-container mutations that would guard against duplication races. This is documentation only: no code was changed to address it. Wiring up real anti-cheat/anti-dupe protection is a deep, high-risk systems change that deserves its own dedicated review rather than a fast pass alongside a documentation audit.
