7. Minigames

A dedicated page from the 2004scape Progressive build 254 coverage audit.

The project's published feature-complete tracker counts six minigames: Duel Arena, Gnomeball, Party Room, Shooting Range, Fishing Trawler and Treasure Trails. game_agilityarena is also present in the audited build source, but belongs to the Agility activity coverage rather than that 6/6 project count. The historical labels matter: the Party Room was still in Seers' Village in September 2004, and Brimhaven Agility Arena had been released on 27 July 2004. Treasure Trails had been available since 5 May 2004. Castle Wars is correctly absent from this target: it was released on 13 December 2004, after build 254's 7 September cutoff.
