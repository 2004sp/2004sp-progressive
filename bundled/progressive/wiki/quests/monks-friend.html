Brother Omad has been unable to sleep because Brother Androe's young son will not stop crying after thieves stole his favourite blanket. Recovering it solves only the first problem: Omad is also trying to organise the child's birthday party, and Brother Cedric has disappeared while collecting the wine.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • No prerequisite quests, Quest Point requirement or hard skill levels.
  • No mandatory combat. A March-2004 RuneScape 2 route places level-14 thieves and a level-26 head thief in the blanket cave, but you do not need to defeat them to take the blanket.

Items and preparation

Bring for the quickest route

  • 1 jug of water — consumed when you sober Brother Cedric.
  • 1 normal log — consumed when Cedric repairs his broken cart. Oak, willow and other log types do not substitute for the ordinary Logs item in build 254.

Obtained during the quest

  • Child's blanket — taken from the thieves' cave and handed to Brother Omad.

Optional

  • Axe — only needed if you want to cut the normal log during the quest. The quest itself does not check a Woodcutting level or require an axe.
  • Food and armour — useful for a low-combat player running through the thieves' cave, but not quest requirements.
Preparation tip: bringing the jug of water and one normal log before speaking to Cedric makes the second half very short. If you need to make the water during the quest, fill an empty jug at a sink before returning to him.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Offer to help Brother Omad

  1. Go to the monastery south of Ardougne Zoo and speak to Brother Omad.
  2. Ask what is wrong. He explains that Brother Androe's son has been crying since thieves stole the child's favourite sleeping blanket.
  3. Choose the option to help recover the blanket. Omad says the thieves hide in a secret cave in the forest beneath a ring of stones.

2. Recover the child's blanket

  1. Travel north-west from the monastery toward the Gnome Battlefield and look for a ring of stones just east of the battlefield.
  2. Approach the stone ring after accepting Omad's request. The hidden entrance reveals a ladder; climb down into the thieves' cave.
  3. Move through the cave to the room containing the Child's blanket and take it.
  4. You may fight the thieves or simply run past them. Killing them does not advance the quest.
  5. Climb back out and return to Brother Omad.

3. Return the blanket

  1. Speak to Brother Omad with the Child's blanket in your inventory.
  2. He takes the blanket and is relieved that the child can sleep again.
  3. Speak to Omad again. He now explains that he is organising the child's first birthday party and that Brother Cedric has not returned with the wine.
  4. Offer to look for Cedric. In build 254, choose “Where should I look?” and agree to find him; this is the dialogue that advances the quest to the Cedric stage.

4. Sober Brother Cedric

  1. Head north to north-west of the monastery until you find Brother Cedric beside his broken cart.
  2. Talk to him. Cedric is drunk and remembers that Omad needs the wine, but first asks for a jug of water.
  3. Talk to Cedric again while carrying the jug of water. He drinks it and the jug of water is consumed.

5. Help Cedric repair the cart

  1. After drinking the water, Cedric explains that his cart still needs repairing.
  2. Choose “Yes, I'd be happy to!” when he asks for help.
  3. Speak to him with 1 normal log in your inventory.
  4. Cedric takes the log, repairs the cart and tells you to return to Brother Omad while he brings the wine back.

6. Return to Brother Omad and party

  1. Return to the monastery and speak to Brother Omad.
  2. Tell him that Cedric is on his way.
  3. Omad gives you 8 Law runes and starts the birthday celebration.
  4. After the party sequence, the quest completes and you receive the Woodcutting experience and Quest Point reward.

Combat and danger notes

  • No enemy must be killed. The blanket is the objective in the thieves' cave.
  • Target-proximate RS2 evidence records level-14 thieves and a level-26 head thief. Low-combat players can bring food and run through rather than fight.
  • There is no combat requirement around Brother Cedric. His stages are purely the jug-of-water and log hand-ins.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 2,000 Woodcutting XP.
  • 8 Law runes.

Monk's Friend gives no coin reward and no permanent Prayer-restoration service in build 254. Those claims from the previous local page were not part of the implemented completion reward.

Useful recovery notes

  • Cedric only gives generic drunk dialogue? Return to Omad after handing in the blanket, talk to him again about the party and explicitly agree to look for Cedric.
  • Need another log? Obtain one ordinary Logs item. Build 254 checks that exact item rather than any log type.
  • Need water? Bring or refill a jug of water and speak to Cedric again. The water hand-in and the later log hand-in are separate quest stages.
  • Left the blanket cave? Revisit the ring of stones while the blanket stage is active; build 254 dynamically reveals the ladder when you approach the quest entrance.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Internal quest name: build 254 calls the quest state drunkmonk, but the player-facing quest remains Monk's Friend.
  • Quest state order: Omad's request → blanket returned → agree to find Cedric → Cedric asks for water → water given → agree to repair cart → normal log given → return to Omad.
  • Exact Cedric items: the build removes one jug_water and later one ordinary logs item. There is no axe or Woodcutting-level check in either hand-in.
  • Completion: the completion script adds exactly 8 Law runes, advances Woodcutting by 2,000 XP and awards 1 Quest Point. The numeric 250 passed to the completion interface is not an additional XP reward.
  • Historical alignment: the core build route and exact reward match the March-2004 RuneScape 2 guide used for this audit. A July-2004 community FAQ instead mentions a bucket of water and gives only an approximate, much larger XP figure; the target-proximate March route plus build source support the jug and 2,000-XP values used here.
