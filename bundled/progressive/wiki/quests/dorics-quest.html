Doric is willing to let you use his anvils, but first he wants a supply of basic mining materials. Bring him six clay, four copper ore and two iron ore and he will reward you for the errand and give you permanent permission to use the anvils at his home.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None. Jagex's December 2003 quest manual lists no minimum requirements for Doric's Quest.
  • 15 Mining is needed only if you choose to mine the two iron ore yourself. It is not a quest-start requirement.
  • Clay and copper can be mined from level 1 in build 254.

Required items

Doric must receive all of the following at the same time:

  • 6 clay — consumed. Build 254 expects the ordinary Clay item, not Soft clay.
  • 4 copper ore — consumed.
  • 2 iron ore — consumed.

You may obtain the materials before starting the quest. If you already have the exact quantities, start the quest and speak to Doric again immediately to finish.

Recommended / obtainable during the quest

  • Any usable pickaxe if you intend to mine the materials yourself. The preserved February 2004 route obtains a Bronze pickaxe from a table in Barbarian Village before travelling to the Dwarven Mine.
  • Build 254 does not give you a pickaxe when Doric starts the quest. If you arrive at the Dwarven Mine without one, the build's mine shops stock Bronze pickaxes.
  • No food, armour or weapon is required for the quest itself. Stay near the northern Dwarven Mine route described below if you want to avoid unnecessary deeper-mine danger.

Before you start

The fastest completion is to bring six clay, four copper ore and two iron ore with you. Doric only checks your inventory when you return; the ore does not have to be mined after the quest begins.

If you are gathering everything yourself, bring a pickaxe and make sure you have at least 15 Mining for the iron. The preserved 2004 route uses the northern entrance to the Dwarven Mine on Ice Mountain, north-east of Falador.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Find Doric north of Falador. Leave Falador by the north-western side and follow the road north. Doric lives at the anvils just east of the gate leading toward Taverley.
  2. Ask to use his anvils. Talk to Doric and choose the conversation about using his anvils. He explains that he needs more materials and will let you use the anvils if you help him.
  3. Agree to fetch the materials. Doric asks for exactly 6 clay, 4 copper ore and 2 iron ore. Accept the errand. If those items are already in your inventory, talk to him again and skip to the finishing step below.

Getting a pickaxe if needed

  1. Bring your own pickaxe if possible. Unlike later versions of the quest, the preserved February 2004 guide tells the player to obtain a pickaxe separately, and build 254's Doric script does not hand one out.
  2. Period route: if you do not have one, the 2004 guide records a Bronze pickaxe on a table in Barbarian Village. Pick it up, then travel back toward Ice Mountain.
  3. Build-254 fallback: the Dwarven Mine's reconstructed shops also stock Bronze pickaxes, so you can buy one there if necessary.

Mine the materials

  1. Enter the Dwarven Mine from Ice Mountain. Go to the northern entrance north-east of Falador and climb down into the mine.
  2. Mine 2 iron ore and 4 copper ore. From the northern entrance, head south to the nearby iron and copper rocks. Iron requires 15 Mining; copper requires level 1.
  3. Mine 6 clay. Continue a little farther south to the clay rocks and mine six pieces of ordinary clay. Clay requires level 1 Mining.
  4. Check the quantities before leaving. You need all 6 clay, 4 copper ore and 2 iron ore in your inventory together. Extra pieces are not necessary.

Finish

  1. Return to Doric. Leave the Dwarven Mine and go back to Doric's anvils north of Falador.
  2. Tell him you have everything. With all twelve required materials in your inventory, talk to Doric. He takes exactly six clay, four copper ore and two iron ore.
  3. Quest complete. Doric rewards you with 180 coins, 1,300 Mining experience, 1 Quest Point, and permission to use his anvils whenever you want.

Useful notes and recovery

  • No combat is required: the quest has no mandatory enemy or boss. You only need to gather or acquire the materials and return them to Doric.
  • No partial hand-in: build 254 checks for all three material totals before taking anything. If you are missing even one required piece, Doric reminds you of the full list and takes nothing.
  • Materials can be pre-collected: there is no requirement that you personally mine them or obtain them after accepting the quest.
  • Ordinary clay: build 254 checks the item named Clay. Soft clay does not satisfy the quest inventory check.
  • Doric's anvils before completion: build 254 blocks smithing at Doric's anvils while the quest is unstarted or in progress. Attempting to use them can also open the relevant Doric quest dialogue if he is nearby.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1,300 Mining experience.
  • 180 coins.
  • Permanent permission to use Doric's anvils.

The September-2004 reward does not include a pickaxe. The pickaxe is only a tool for obtaining the materials if you choose to mine them yourself.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Exact material check: build 254 requires at least 6 clay, 4 copper_ore and 2 iron_ore, then deletes exactly those quantities. The quest can therefore be completed with materials collected before it was started.
  • Mining levels: the build's mining table requires level 1 for clay, level 1 for copper and level 15 for iron. The quest itself has no skill-level gate.
  • Reward: the completion queue advances Mining by 13000 in the project's tenths-of-XP convention, which is 1,300 Mining XP. The quest-point constant is 1, and Doric gives 180 coins.
  • Coin handout edge case: the Doric source deliberately queues quest completion before adding the 180 coins and contains a comment warning that the coins can be missed even though completion still triggers. Avoid interrupting the completion conversation. If the quest completes without the coins, the post-quest dialogue does not contain a replacement handout.
  • Anvil unlock: the Smithing script rejects Doric's anvils at quest states 0 and 10, then allows normal anvil use once the quest is complete.
  • No start pickaxe: build 254's Doric start script does not add a Bronze pickaxe. This agrees with the preserved February-2004 route, which tells the player to obtain one separately, and differs from later quest versions that hand one to the player.
  • Reconstructed dialogue: the build's Doric quest source explicitly cites Jagex's 16 January 2006 Agility Pyramid update and says it assumes that update changed the older dialogue. Because that source is post-cutoff, its exact dialogue wording is not used as proof of September-2004 historical dialogue.
  • Journal title typo: build 254's reconstructed quest journal currently displays Dorics' Quest; period Jagex material and this guide use the correct title Doric's Quest.
