The Grand Tree is dying, and King Narnode Shareen fears that someone has deliberately poisoned its roots with Daconia rocks. Carry a bark sample to the ancient gnome Hazelmere, investigate royal adviser Glough, infiltrate a Karamja shipyard, expose an invasion plan and descend beneath the tree to stop the saboteur before the gnome stronghold is lost.

Requirements

Required level

  • 25 Agility. Build 254 checks this when you climb into the upper part of Glough's tree house to solve the twig puzzle.

Combat requirement

  • You must defeat a level 172 Black Demon beneath the Grand Tree. Bring equipment and supplies appropriate for a long, dangerous fight.

Bring before starting

  • No quest item is required to begin. Leave at least 2 free inventory spaces when accepting the quest so King Narnode can give you the bark sample and translation book.
  • 1,000 coins, conditionally. After the Karamja shipyard section, Femi can smuggle you back through the closed stronghold gate. In build 254 she only charges 1,000 coins if you previously refused to help her move the boxes by the entrance; otherwise she helps for free. Players who can enter by another route, such as the Tree Gnome Village spirit tree, do not need to pay her.

Obtained during the quest

  • Bark sample and translation book. Given by King Narnode when the quest begins.
  • Hazelmere's scroll. Received after Hazelmere examines the bark.
  • Glough's journal. Found by searching the cupboard in Glough's home.
  • Lumber order. Obtained from the shipyard foreman, either by answering his questions or defeating him.
  • Glough's key. Given by Anita.
  • Invasion plans. Taken from Glough's locked chest.
  • T, U, Z and O twigs. King Narnode gives all four after seeing the invasion plans. You need 4 free inventory spaces to receive them.
  • Daconia rock. Found by searching the roots beneath the tree after the Black Demon is defeated.

Recommended

  • Good food, armour and a reliable weapon. The level 172 Black Demon is the quest's mandatory boss.
  • Several free inventory spaces. The quest repeatedly hands you documents, keys and other untradeable items.
  • A way to leave Karamja quickly after obtaining the lumber order, if available in your account's progression.

Before you start

This quest predates the September-2004 cutoff by almost two years. Its period route is the original Grand Tree investigation: translate Hazelmere's warning, expose Glough's shipyard operation, spell TUZO with four twigs and defeat the Black Demon beneath the tree. The balloon transport system is not part of this quest.

Keep the translation book while returning Hazelmere's message to the king. The dialogue is a translation test: choosing the wrong lines does not advance the quest, so use the exact sequence below.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Take the bark sample to Hazelmere

  1. Enter the Grand Tree in the Tree Gnome Stronghold and speak to King Narnode Shareen.
  2. Ask what is troubling him and agree to help. Make sure you have 2 empty inventory slots.
  3. The king takes you below the tree and explains that the roots are dying. He gives you a bark sample and a translation book.
  4. Travel to Hazelmere, who lives on the small island east of Yanille and south of the Khazard Fight Arena.
  5. Talk to Hazelmere. He examines the bark and gives you a scroll written in the old gnome language.

2. Translate Hazelmere's warning

  1. Return to King Narnode Shareen with the scroll and translation book.
  2. When he asks what Hazelmere's message says, choose the following answers in order:
  • “None of the above.”
  • “None of the above.”
  • “A man came to me with the King's seal.”
  • “I gave the man Daconia rocks.”
  • “And Daconia rocks will kill the tree!”

The warning reveals that someone used the king's seal to obtain poisonous Daconia rocks. Narnode sends you to speak to his adviser Glough.

3. Investigate Glough and the human prisoner

  1. Go to Glough's tree house near the Grand Tree and tell him what happened. He immediately blames humans.
  2. Return to King Narnode. Glough has now arrested a human who was carrying three Daconia rocks. Ask the king for permission to question the prisoner.
  3. Go to the upper level of the Grand Tree and talk to Charlie, the imprisoned human.
  4. Charlie explains that Glough employed him to collect Daconia rocks and suggests searching Glough's home.
  5. Return to Glough's house, open his cupboard and search it to find Glough's journal.
  6. Read the journal if you wish, then confront Glough. His guards arrest you and place you in the cell with Charlie.

4. Escape by glider and reach the Karamja shipyard

  1. Talk to Charlie in the cell. He tells you that Glough has dealings with a shipyard on Karamja and gives you the password clue.
  2. King Narnode releases you but still believes Glough. He orders you out of the stronghold and directs you to the gnome glider at the top of the tree.
  3. Speak to the gnome pilot and choose the quest flight to Karamja. The glider crash-lands west of the shipyard.
  4. Run east to the Ship Yard gate.
  5. Tell the shipyard worker “Glough sent me.” Give the password in three parts: Ka — Lu — Min.

5. Get the lumber order from the foreman

Find the Foreman on the southern dock. Build 254 supports two routes:

  • Talk your way through: answer his security questions with “Sadly his wife is no longer with us!”, “worm holes”, and “Anita.” He reveals the invasion operation and gives you the lumber order.
  • Fight him: wrong answers can turn the foreman hostile. Defeating him also produces the lumber order and advances the quest.

6. Return to the stronghold

  1. Travel back to the Tree Gnome Stronghold. At this stage, the main gate refuses entry to humans.
  2. If using Femi, speak to her outside the entrance. She hides you in her cart and takes you inside.
  3. If you previously refused to help Femi lift her boxes, she demands 1,000 coins. If you helped her—or never triggered that refusal state—build 254 lets her smuggle you in for free.
  4. If you have another valid way into the stronghold, you can use it instead.
  5. Show the lumber order to King Narnode. He remains unconvinced that Glough is responsible.

7. Obtain the invasion plans

  1. Return to Charlie. He tells you that Glough's girlfriend Anita may know where a key is kept.
  2. Find Anita in her house in the north-west part of the stronghold, west of the swamp. Talk to her and receive Glough's key.
  3. Go back to Glough's house and use the key on his locked chest.
  4. Search the opened chest to obtain the invasion plans.
  5. Take the plans to King Narnode. He still wants stronger proof and gives you four lettered twigs: T, U, Z and O. You need 4 free inventory spaces.

8. Solve the TUZO pillar puzzle

  1. Return to Glough's house and climb into its upper tree section. Build 254 requires 25 Agility here.
  2. You will find four pillars. Place the four twigs from left to right so that they spell TUZO.
  3. The exact build-254 order is T — U — Z — O. When all four are correct, you hear an ancient pulley mechanism and the trapdoor unlocks.
  4. Prepare for the boss fight before descending. Open the trapdoor and climb down.

9. Defeat the level 172 Black Demon

  1. After the underground confrontation, Glough summons a level 172 Black Demon.
  2. Defeat the demon. It is mandatory for quest progression.
  3. Continue through the cave and find King Narnode Shareen.
  4. Talk to him. A guard has found Glough buried beneath Daconia rocks, and the king finally accepts that the adviser was responsible.
  5. Narnode asks you to find the final Daconia rock still poisoning the tree.

10. Find the last Daconia rock

  1. Search the Grand Tree roots in the underground area.
  2. Build 254 randomly assigns the correct root position for each player, so keep searching roots until you find the Daconia rock.
  3. Take the rock to King Narnode Shareen.
  4. Hand it over to complete The Grand Tree.

Hazards and useful notes

  • The Black Demon is not optional: build 254 does not advance past the underground confrontation until the level 172 demon has been defeated.
  • Wrong translation answers are harmless: they simply fail to advance the Hazelmere-message stage. Repeat the conversation and use the exact five-line sequence above.
  • The shipyard password is Ka-Lu-Min. Incorrect gate answers can lead to a hostile response rather than entry.
  • The foreman has two valid outcomes: correct security answers avoid combat, while defeating him also yields the lumber order.
  • Femi's 1,000-coin fee is conditional: the build-254 script charges only when your earlier Femi state records that you refused to help with her boxes.
  • Need replacement quest items? King Narnode's dialogue supports replacement of important lost items at the relevant stages, including the starting translation materials and twig set.
  • The final root is player-specific: there is no single universally correct root in build 254. Search until the Daconia rock appears.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points.
  • 18,400 Attack XP.
  • 7,900 Agility XP.
  • 2,150 Magic XP.
  • Access to the gnome glider transport network. Build 254 enables the ordinary pilot travel dialogue after quest completion.
  • Access through the Grand Tree root door to the mine beneath the tree.
  • Use of the Tree Gnome Stronghold spirit tree in build 254. Its local script checks completion of The Grand Tree before allowing that tree's travel dialogue.

The experience rewards are fixed. There is no choice between Attack, Strength, Defence or Agility XP.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: grandtree progresses from start through Hazelmere, Glough, Charlie, the journal, jail release, shipyard order, invasion plans, twig puzzle, Black Demon and Daconia search, then reaches ^grandtree_complete = 160.
  • Agility check: quest_grandtree.rs2 checks stat(agility) < 25 when the player climbs the tree section above Glough's house.
  • Translation: King Narnode's quest dialogue requires the exact interpretation that a man used the king's seal to obtain Daconia rocks and that those rocks will kill the tree.
  • Shipyard: the gate password is Ka-Lu-Min. The foreman route supports either the correct three-answer interrogation or combat, with both paths producing the lumber order.
  • Femi: femi.rs2 charges exactly 1,000 coins only when %femi_help = 1, the state set by refusing her earlier box-lifting request. Otherwise the quest-stage cart route is free.
  • Twig puzzle: the four pillar checks require T, U, Z and O on consecutive positions from left to right. The completed arrangement advances the quest and unlocks the trapdoor.
  • Boss: grandtree_blackdemon is configured with visible combat level 172. The quest advances only after that demon is defeated.
  • Daconia search: the implementation chooses one root from a coordinate enum for the player's final rock. Searching the assigned root while in the final search state produces the quest item.
  • Exact XP: the completion queue awards 7,900 Agility XP, 18,400 Attack XP and 2,150 Magic XP; ^grandtree_questpoints is 5.
  • Permanent access: the gnome-pilot script unlocks ordinary glider travel after ^grandtree_complete, the Grand Tree root door opens after completion, and the stronghold spirit-tree script separately checks Grand Tree completion.
  • Reconstruction caution: some Content comments reference later OSRS or video evidence when reconstructing missing details. This guide uses build-254 scripts for implementation behaviour and keeps post-2004 transport additions out of the historical walkthrough.
