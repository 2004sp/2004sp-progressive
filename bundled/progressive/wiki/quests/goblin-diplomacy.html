Goblin Diplomacy

A rumour in Port Sarim says the goblins north of Falador are close to war over the colour of their armour. General Bentnoze wants red, General Wartface wants green, and the player is left to test new colours until the generals discover that the old brown armour was preferable after all.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • No skill-level requirement. Making and mixing the required dyes has no Crafting-level check in build 254.
  • No mandatory combat. Goblin mail can be obtained before starting and is tradeable in build 254. The contemporary April-May 2004 route obtains three sets by killing goblins.

Required items

  • 3 Goblin mail. One is dyed orange, one is dyed blue, and the third is handed in unchanged as the original brown armour. All three are consumed during the quest.
  • 1 orange dye. If making it yourself, Aggie first makes one red dye and one yellow dye, which you combine.
  • 1 blue dye.

If making the dyes yourself

  • 3 redberries + 5 coins for one red dye from Aggie.
  • 2 onions + 5 coins for one yellow dye from Aggie.
  • 2 woad leaves + 5 coins for one blue dye from Aggie.
  • 20 coins to buy two woad leaves at once from Wyson the gardener in Falador Park. Offering 15 coins only gives one leaf in build 254.
  • This makes 35 coins for Aggie's three dye fees plus Wyson's two-leaf offer, before any extra money you may spend buying the other ingredients.

Obtained during the quest

  • Goblin mail can be obtained as a goblin drop. The target-period April-May 2004 guide uses drops rather than the convenient crate supplies familiar from later Old School guides.
  • Redberries were available from the Port Sarim food shop in the contemporary route, or could be gathered near Varrock.
  • Onions can be gathered from the field south of Falador near Rimmington.
  • Woad leaves can be bought from Wyson in Falador Park.

Recommended

  • Prepare all three mail sets before returning to Goblin Village: one orange, one blue and one unchanged brown. The generals demand them in that order.
  • Gather the three Goblin mail before making a long dye run. The period walkthrough gets them from goblin drops, so the time needed is variable.
  • Leave at least one inventory space free for the gold-bar reward.

Before you start

You may gather the mail and dye ingredients before starting. If making the dyes yourself, an efficient preparation set is 3 Goblin mail, 3 redberries, 2 onions, 35 coins, then buy two woad leaves from Wyson. Aggie will turn the ingredients into red, yellow and blue dyes; combine red with yellow to make orange.

Do not use the modern Old School start point as your historical reference. For the September-2004 route, the quest begins in The Rusty Anchor at Port Sarim, and build 254 deliberately preserves that older start.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Hear the rumour in Port Sarim

  1. Go to The Rusty Anchor in Port Sarim and speak to the bartender.
  2. Choose "Not very busy in here today, is it?"
  3. The bartender tells you that the goblins up by the mountain are arguing over the colour of their armour and that the dispute could turn into a war.
  4. Your quest journal now tells you to investigate the Goblin Village.

2. Offer to solve the generals' argument

  1. Travel north through or around Falador to Goblin Village, north of Doric's hut.
  2. Speak to General Bentnoze or General Wartface. Bentnoze argues for red armour while Wartface argues for green.
  3. Choose the conversation option offering to pick an armour colour for them.
  4. The generals ask you to bring them orange goblin armour first.

3. Obtain three sets of Goblin mail

  1. You need three sets in total.
  2. The contemporary April-May 2004 route tells players to kill goblins until three Goblin mail drops are obtained. Build 254 also includes Goblin mail on goblin drop tables.
  3. Keep one set unchanged. This is the brown armour that will be requested last.

4. Make red, yellow and blue dye

  1. If you do not already have the dyes, collect 3 redberries and 2 onions.
  2. In Falador Park, talk to Wyson the gardener and ask for woad leaves. Offer 20 coins; he gives you 2 woad leaves.
  3. Go to Aggie in Draynor Village.
  4. Ask her to make red dye: she consumes 3 redberries and 5 coins.
  5. Ask her to make yellow dye: she consumes 2 onions and 5 coins.
  6. Ask her to make blue dye: she consumes 2 woad leaves and 5 coins.

5. Prepare orange, blue and brown Goblin mail

  1. Use the red dye on the yellow dye, or vice versa. The two bottles are consumed and become one orange dye.
  2. Use the orange dye on one Goblin mail. This consumes the dye and produces Orange goblin mail.
  3. Use the blue dye on a second Goblin mail. This consumes the dye and produces Blue goblin mail.
  4. Leave the third Goblin mail unchanged. It is the original brown set.

6. Give the generals the orange armour

  1. Return to General Bentnoze or General Wartface in Goblin Village.
  2. With the orange mail in your inventory, continue the quest dialogue.
  3. The generals take 1 Orange goblin mail and reject it because the colour does not look right.
  4. They ask you to bring blue armour next.

7. Give the generals the blue armour

  1. Speak to either general again with 1 Blue goblin mail.
  2. They take the armour but decide that it is still wrong.
  3. Bentnoze suggests something lighter, and the generals ask to try brown armour.

8. Give them the original brown armour

  1. Speak to either general with the final unchanged Goblin mail.
  2. The generals take it, decide that brown is actually rather nice, and end their argument.
  3. The quest completes during this conversation. There is no separate final turn-in after the brown armour is accepted.

Combat and hazards

  • No enemy has to be defeated as a quest-state requirement.
  • The period route obtains the three Goblin mail pieces from goblin drops, so low-level players may still choose to fight goblins. Because the item can be obtained beforehand or from another player in build 254, combat itself is not a hard requirement.
  • There are no boss fights, dangerous dungeon routes or failure penalties specific to this quest.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Started at the generals and nothing advances: go to the Port Sarim bartender first. Build 254 requires the bartender rumour to set the quest to its started state.
  • Wrong armour colour: the hand-in order is fixed: orange, then blue, then original brown.
  • Dyeing the mail: both the dye and one brown Goblin mail are consumed when making the orange or blue quest mail.
  • No Crafting level is needed: Aggie's dye creation, mixing red with yellow, and dyeing the Goblin mail have no level gate in build 254.
  • Need two woad leaves: offer Wyson 20 coins in one conversation. His 15-coin offer yields only one leaf in build 254.

Rewards

  • 5 Quest Points.
  • 200 Crafting XP.
  • 1 gold bar.

No additional September-2004 permanent unlock is asserted here. Later quests that use Goblin Diplomacy as a prerequisite are outside this guide's target-date reward set.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Historical start preserved: build 254's journal explicitly starts Goblin Diplomacy with the Port Sarim bartender. The source comments note that the Old School version was reworked to start elsewhere.
  • Quest states: the build tracks bartender rumour → offer to find a new colour → orange handed in → blue handed in → brown handed in → complete.
  • Exact hand-ins: the quest script deletes one Orange goblin mail, one Blue goblin mail and one ordinary Goblin mail in sequence. There is no generic colour-matching puzzle.
  • Dye recipes: Aggie consumes 3 redberries + 5 coins for red, 2 onions + 5 coins for yellow, and 2 woad leaves + 5 coins for blue. Red + yellow produces orange with no level check.
  • Woad leaves: Wyson gives one leaf for 15 coins or two for 20 coins. The two-leaf 20-coin choice is the efficient quest route.
  • Goblin mail: build 254's goblin drop tables include ordinary Goblin mail. Their exact probabilities are implementation data reconstructed using later references, so this guide does not present those rates as proven September-2004 history.
  • Completion reward: `stat_advance(crafting, 2000)` is 200 Crafting XP under the project's tenths-of-XP convention; the script adds exactly 1 gold bar, and the global quest constant awards 5 Quest Points.
