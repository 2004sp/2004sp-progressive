Veronica and her fiancé Ernest became lost while travelling. Ernest went into the nearby manor to ask for directions, but an hour later he still had not returned. Inside Draynor Manor, Professor Oddenstein has accidentally transformed him into a chicken, and three missing machine parts must be recovered before Ernest can be restored.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None.

Required items

  • Nothing has to be brought. The fish food, poison, spade and all three machine parts can be obtained in or around Draynor Manor during the quest.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Fish food and poison — combined to make poisoned fish food.
  • Spade — used on the compost heap to uncover the closet key.
  • Oil can, pressure gauge and rubber tube — Professor Oddenstein's three missing machine parts.

Recommended

  • A little food if you are very low level. A skeleton occupies the locked room containing the rubber tube, although you do not have to kill it.
  • Several free inventory spaces so you can carry the quest items without repeated trips.

Before you start

The historical quest can be completed entirely with items found at Draynor Manor. It is efficient to speak to Professor Oddenstein first, then collect the three missing parts while working back through the house and grounds.

Build-254 warning: the current quest source marks the secret-bookcase opening routine as broken. Historically you search the western bookcase to enter the hidden room leading to the oil-can basement. In build 254 that Search-side opening is a known progression blocker. The lever on the hidden-room side is coded separately and works as the way back out once that side has been reached; it is not a historical alternative entrance.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start with Veronica. Go to the gates immediately south of Draynor Manor and speak to Veronica. She explains that she and Ernest became lost, Ernest entered the manor to ask for directions, and he has been gone for about an hour. Agree to look for him.
  2. Find Professor Oddenstein. Enter Draynor Manor and climb to the top floor. Speak to Professor Oddenstein and ask about Ernest. He admits that he tested his pouletmorph machine on Ernest and accidentally turned him into a chicken. The machine cannot reverse the transformation because gremlins have hidden three parts: an oil can, rubber tube and pressure gauge.
  3. Pick up the fish food. Go down one floor from the Professor. In the south-east room, pick up the fish food.
  4. Pick up the poison. Return to the ground floor and go to the western side of the manor. Pick up the poison, then use it on the fish food to make poisoned fish food.
  5. Open the historical secret passage. In the western ground-floor room south of the poison room, search the bookcase on the western wall. Historically this opens a hidden room with a ladder down to the basement. Build 254 currently has a known bug in this Search-side bookcase-opening routine; see the warning above.
  6. Enter the oil-can basement. Once inside the hidden room, climb down the ladder. The basement is a door-and-lever puzzle using six levers labelled A through F. Leaving by the ladder resets the lever arrangement, so complete the sequence in one visit.
  7. Solve the lever puzzle. From the starting room, use this sequence:
    1. Pull A and B, then go through the north-east door.
    2. Pull D, then go through the south-west door followed by the south door.
    3. Pull A and B again, then go through the north-west, west and north doors.
    4. Pull E and F, then go east through two doors.
    5. Pull C, then go through the north-west door and the west door.
    6. Pull E again, then go east, south, south and finally west.
    Pick up the oil can. Return to the ladder. Historically, after climbing back up, pull the lever on the eastern wall of the hidden room to open the way back into the manor.
  8. Get a spade. On the ground floor, pick up the spade from the eastern side of the manor if you do not already have one.
  9. Dig through the compost heap. Go outside and around to the west side of the manor grounds. Use the spade on the compost heap. You find a small, smelly key used for the locked closet inside the manor.
  10. Recover the pressure gauge. Go to the fountain in the south-west corner of the manor grounds. Use the poisoned fish food on the fountain to kill the piranhas, then search the fountain and take the pressure gauge. In build 254, searching before poisoning the fish costs 1 Hitpoint and does not give the gauge.
  11. Recover the rubber tube. Return inside. Use the compost-heap key on the locked door to the small room behind the main staircase. A skeleton is inside with the rubber tube. You do not need to kill it: take the tube and leave if you can safely do so.
  12. Return all three parts to Professor Oddenstein. Carry the oil can, pressure gauge and rubber tube back to the Professor on the top floor. He takes the parts, repairs the machine and turns the chicken back into Ernest.
  13. Quest complete. Ernest thanks you and gives you 300 coins. The quest completes during this conversation with Professor Oddenstein and Ernest; you do not need to escort Ernest outside or return to Veronica.

Combat and failure notes

  • Rubber-tube room: a skeleton is present, but defeating it is not a quest objective. Low-level players can take the tube and leave rather than stopping to fight.
  • Fountain: do not repeatedly search it before poisoning the piranhas. Build 254 inflicts 1 Hitpoint of damage on an unsuccessful bare-handed attempt.
  • Lever puzzle reset: leaving the basement by the ladder resets its levers. If you lose your place, leave, re-enter and repeat the full sequence from the start.
  • Lost key: in build 254 the compost heap can provide the closet key again while the quest is unfinished if you no longer have one.
  • Lost ordinary quest items: fish food, poison and the manor item spawns can be collected again as needed. The quest does not require a one-time external item purchase.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 300 coins.

There is no skill-XP reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Secret-bookcase blocker: `quest_haunted.rs2` contains an explicit source comment that the Search-side bookcase-opening routine is "borked" because of its current zone-search implementation. This can stop normal access to the oil-can basement. The nearby reverse-side lever uses a separate path that the source notes is working.
  • Three-part hand-in: Professor Oddenstein checks for the oil can, pressure gauge and rubber tube together. When all three are present, he removes them and immediately runs the transformation/completion sequence.
  • Pressure gauge: poisoned fish food is required before the fountain yields the gauge. An early search causes 1 Hitpoint of damage.
  • Closet key: using a spade on the compost heap gives the key while the quest is unfinished and the player does not already have one.
  • Reward: the completion code adds exactly 300 coins, and the quest-point constant awards 4 Quest Points. No skill XP is advanced.
  • Finish point: completion is handled by Professor Oddenstein's dialogue after the three parts are returned. Veronica has post-quest dialogue, but she does not complete the quest.
