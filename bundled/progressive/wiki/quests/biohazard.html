Elena is free, but the Mourners have confiscated the distillator she needs to test plague samples. You must find another way into West Ardougne, recover her equipment, smuggle chemical samples through Varrock and ask her old mentor Guidor to discover what is really happening.

Requirements

Required quests

  • Completion of Plague City.

Required levels

  • None.

Required items

  • Gas mask — obtained during Plague City. Bring it and wear it when crossing into West Ardougne. If you no longer have one, another can be obtained from the cupboard in Edmond and Alrena's house.
  • Priest gown top and bottom — both pieces must be worn to get past Guidor's wife. If you do not already own them, a contemporary May 2004 guide records the full set for 12 coins from the fancy-clothes shop north of Guidor in Varrock.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Pigeon cage and bird feed, from Jerico's house.
  • Rotten apple, beside the Mourner headquarters in West Ardougne.
  • Doctors' gown, from Nurse Sarah's cupboard after the Mourners become ill.
  • Key, obtained from the Mourner encounter inside the headquarters.
  • Distillator, found in a crate behind the locked gate upstairs in the Mourner headquarters.
  • Plague sample, liquid honey, sulphuric broline and Ethanea, given by Elena after the distillator is returned.
  • Touch paper, given by the Chemist in Rimmington.

Recommended

  • A weapon, armour and some food for the Mourner fight. There is no listed combat-level requirement.
  • At least four free inventory spaces when returning the distillator to Elena, because she gives you three chemical vials and a plague sample.
  • 12 coins if you intend to buy the priest-gown set near Guidor rather than bringing one.

Before you start

Biohazard involves repeated travel between East Ardougne, West Ardougne, Rimmington and Varrock. The important restriction is the plague sample: period guides warn that the quest samples are too fragile for teleporting. In build 254, normal spell teleports and spirit-tree travel specifically destroy the plague sample. Bank it before using those transports, or travel normally while carrying it.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start with Elena. Speak to Elena in her house in East Ardougne. She explains that the Mourners confiscated her distillator and that the old tunnel into West Ardougne has been filled in. Agree to retrieve the distillator. She tells you to speak to Jerico, a friend of her father who communicates with people across the wall.
  2. Speak to Jerico and collect the bird supplies. Jerico lives just south of East Ardougne's northern bank and next to the chapel. Talk to him about getting across the wall. Before leaving, search the cupboard in his house for bird feed and take a pigeon cage from the backyard.
  3. Meet Omart. Go to the southern end of the wall separating East and West Ardougne. Omart is waiting near the wall, with Kilron on the western side. Omart has a rope ladder but says the nearby Mourner watchtower must be distracted first.
  4. Distract the watchtower. Go to the small watchtower immediately north of Omart. Use the bird feed on the watchtower, then open the pigeon cage while standing beside it. The pigeons swarm the tower and occupy the Mourners.
  5. Cross into West Ardougne. Return to Omart. He calls to Kilron, who throws the rope ladder over the wall. Wear your gas mask and cross while the guards are distracted.
  6. Poison the Mourners' stew. Head to the Mourner headquarters in the north-east of West Ardougne. Pick up a rotten apple beside the building, get into the rear yard and use the rotten apple on the cauldron. The Mourners soon suffer food poisoning and call for a doctor.
  7. Get a Doctors' gown. Go south-west to Nurse Sarah's house. Search her cupboard and take the Doctors' gown, then wear it before returning to the Mourner headquarters.
  8. Enter the headquarters and get the key. The disguise gets you inside. Go upstairs and speak to the sick Mourner. The conversation turns hostile and a Mourner attacks you. Defeat the required Mourner and take the key. A contemporary May 2004 walkthrough describes fighting both Mourners in this room before receiving the key; build 254 awards the key when its quest Mourner dies.
  9. Recover the distillator. Use the key on the locked metal gate upstairs. Search the crates in the storage area until you find Elena's distillator.
  10. Leave West Ardougne. Go to the south-east part of West Ardougne and speak to Kilron to cross the wall back to East Ardougne.
  11. Return the distillator to Elena. Elena runs a test, but the touch paper does not react. She gives you a plague sample and three reagents: liquid honey, sulphuric broline and Ethanea. She asks you to obtain fresh touch paper from the Chemist in Rimmington and then take everything to her former mentor Guidor in Varrock.
  12. Protect the plague sample during travel. Do not teleport while carrying it. If you want to use a teleport for the long trip, bank the plague sample first, travel, then withdraw it again before continuing. Build 254 destroys the plague sample on normal magic teleports and spirit-tree crossings.
  13. Get touch paper from the Chemist. Travel to the Chemist's house west of Rimmington. When he tries to close the conversation, tell him that you are Elena's friend. When asked why you need touch paper, say it is for Guidor. Do not tell him you are carrying a plague sample: in the period route and build 254 he confiscates the sample if you reveal it.
  14. Give each reagent to the correct errand boy. The Chemist warns that Varrock guards are searching travellers, so his three helpers must carry the chemical vials for you. Give Hops the sulphuric broline, Chancy the liquid honey, and DeVinci the Ethanea. They arrange to meet you at the Dancing Donkey Inn in south-east Varrock.
  15. Recover from a wrong hand-off if necessary. The helpers are unreliable with the wrong vial: Hops may drink it, Chancy may sell it and gamble the proceeds, and DeVinci may use it in a painting. If you lose a reagent this way, return to Elena for a replacement before attempting Guidor's test.
  16. Enter Varrock without the chemical vials. Travel to Varrock carrying the plague sample and touch paper but not the three reagents. The guard at the eastern gate searches you. In build 254 he confiscates any of the three reagent vials that you carry through the checkpoint.
  17. Collect the three vials. Go to the Dancing Donkey Inn in south-east Varrock and speak to Hops, Chancy and DeVinci. Each returns the correctly assigned vial. Once you have the vials back, do not leave through the guarded eastern gate and then try to re-enter with them.
  18. Disguise yourself as a priest. Guidor's house is just south-east of the inn. Equip both halves of the priest gown before trying to enter his room. Guidor's wife wants a priest to see her ill husband and allows you through while you are wearing the complete outfit. If you still need the clothes, buy the set from the nearby fancy-clothes shop.
  19. Have Guidor analyse the sample. Speak to Guidor and explain that Elena sent you. You need all five test items in your inventory: the plague sample, liquid honey, sulphuric broline, Ethanea and touch paper. Guidor uses them and discovers that the sample is not toxic: there is no plague.
  20. Report to Elena. Return to East Ardougne and tell Elena what Guidor discovered. She concludes that the matter is bigger than the two of you and tells you to confront King Lathas.
  21. Confront King Lathas. Find King Lathas upstairs in the East Ardougne palace. Tell him that Guidor proved the plague is a hoax. Hear his explanation about King Tyras and the danger west of the mountains. When the conversation finishes, Biohazard is complete. Build-254 players should read the implementation warning below before choosing Lathas's dialogue response.

Combat and failure notes

  • Mourner encounter: combat is mandatory inside the Mourner headquarters. The exact target-date combat level is not presented as certain here: reconstructed March-to-late-2004 data lists headquarters Mourners at levels 24 and 12, while the later/current quest fight and build 254 use level 13.
  • Lost distillator: return to the upstairs crate while you are at the appropriate quest stage and search again.
  • Lost test items: Elena can replace missing liquid honey, Ethanea, sulphuric broline and the plague sample after she has first given them to you.
  • Chemist takes the sample: if you tell him that you are carrying the plague, return to Elena for another plague sample, then use the Guidor dialogue route next time.
  • Wrong reagent given to a helper: after seeing the result in Varrock, return to Elena to replace the lost vial and repeat that hand-off correctly.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • 1,250 Thieving XP.
  • Permission to enter King Lathas's Combat Training Camp north-west of Ardougne.
  • The ability to use the main gate into and out of West Ardougne.
  • Progression to Underground Pass, which requires Biohazard.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Gas-mask check: preserved versions of Biohazard require the gas mask when crossing the wall, and the mask is a period item obtained in Plague City. Build 254's rope-ladder script currently does not check whether you are wearing it. The historical walkthrough above still tells you to bring it.
  • Mourner level: build 254's quest Mourner is combat level 13. Historical reconstruction for the March-to-late-2004 Mourner set instead records the two headquarters variants at levels 24 and 12, so level 13 is treated as an implementation value rather than silently projected back onto 7 September 2004.
  • Teleport breakage: a May 2004 walkthrough warns that the quest vials/samples break when teleporting. Build 254's teleport and spirit-tree scripts specifically delete the plague sample, not the three reagent vials. Avoid teleporting with the plague sample in either case.
  • King Lathas completion choice: in build 254, choose “I don't understand...” when Lathas begins explaining why the plague was faked. That branch reaches the quest-completion queue. The dismissive “Well I've wasted enough of my time here.” branch currently ends the conversation without completing the quest.
  • Training-camp dummies: after the quest, build 254 allows six successful melee hits on the camp's training dummy, awarding 50 Attack XP each for 300 Attack XP total. This is a post-quest camp feature, not part of Biohazard's 1,250 Thieving-XP quest reward.
