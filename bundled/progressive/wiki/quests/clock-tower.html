The Clock Tower south of Ardougne has broken down, leaving the townspeople without a reliable timepiece. Brother Kojo asks you to recover four coloured cogs from the cellars below and fit each one onto the matching spindle on a different level of the tower.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None.
  • There is no hard skill-level requirement and no enemy has to be killed.

Required items

  • 1 bucket of water — used to cool the black cog and turned into an empty bucket. You can obtain the bucket and water during the quest near Brother Cedric, north-east of the Clock Tower.
  • Ice gloves are a period-valid alternative to the bucket of water if you already own them; they are not required if you use the bucket.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Red cog — recovered from the south-eastern cellar route.
  • White cog — recovered after poisoning the dungeon rats.
  • Blue cog — recovered through the separate ladder north-east of the tower.
  • Black cog — recovered from the fire room after cooling it.
  • Rat poison — found along the white-cog route and consumed when used on the rats' food trough.

Recommended

  • Food and armour if you are low level. The April 2004 period guide records three level 48 ogres in the red-cog room; you only need to run past them and pick up the cog.
  • Keep at least one free inventory slot. More importantly, the quest only allows you to carry one cog at a time, so each cog must be fitted before you collect another.

Before you start

You do not need to bring a bucket from a bank. The period route provides one near Brother Cedric and a nearby well, which is convenient to collect before taking the black cog. No combat is mandatory, but the red-cog route passes through ogres, so low-level players should bring food.

The four coloured floor tiles in the main cellar junction act as directions: red points south-east, white north-west, blue south-west, and black north-east. The cogs can be done in different orders, but the route below follows the preserved 2004 guide and avoids carrying conflicts.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start with Brother Kojo. Enter the Clock Tower south-west of Ardougne Zoo and talk to Brother Kojo. He explains that the clock has broken and asks you to retrieve four cogs from the cellar. Agree to help. He warns that the cogs are too heavy to carry together, so you must move them one at a time.
  2. Enter the cellar. Climb down the ladder in the tower. Continue into the central room containing four coloured tiles. Each tile indicates the general direction of the matching cog.

Red cog

  1. Follow the red route south-east. From the coloured-tile room, take the south-eastern passage marked by the red tile.
  2. Run through the ogre room. At the end of the route is the red cog with three ogres. The preserved April 2004 guide identifies them as level 48. You do not need to fight them: run in, pick up the red cog, and return the way you came.
  3. Fit the red cog. Return to the tower ladder and climb to the ground floor. Use the red cog on the red clock spindle beside the ladder. The cog is consumed by the repair.

White cog

  1. Follow the white route north-west. Return to the cellar's coloured-tile room and take the north-western passage marked by the white tile.
  2. Pick up the rat poison. Continue north-west until you find rat poison. Take one, then continue east/north-east toward the cage containing dungeon rats.
  3. Open the rat cage. Near the cage are two levers controlling its gates. Pull the levers so that you can pass through both gates and reach the food trough inside.
  4. Poison the food. Use the rat poison on the food trough. The rats swarm to the poisoned food, panic, and die. Their struggle loosens the western gate.
  5. Take the white cog. Go through the loosened western gate, pick up the white cog, and use the nearby ladder to return outside.
  6. Fit the white cog. Re-enter the Clock Tower and climb to its top floor. Use the white cog on the white clock spindle.

Blue cog

  1. Inspect the blue route. Return to the cellar's coloured-tile room and take the south-western passage marked by the blue tile. You will reach a room where the blue cog is visibly locked inside a cage with no usable door from this side.
  2. Return to the surface. Use the ladder in this part of the dungeon to get outside. Head north-east toward the wooden cart where Brother Cedric from Monk's Friend can be found.
  3. Collect the bucket of water. Pick up the bucket south of the cart and use it on the nearby well. Keep this bucket of water for the black cog later.
  4. Find the second cellar entrance. Climb down the ladder just east of the cart, south of the zoo. Follow the long passage until you reach an odd-looking wall, then push through it.
  5. Take the blue cog. The hidden route brings you into the cage from the other side. Pick up the blue cog, then climb the ladder out.
  6. Fit the blue cog. Return to the Clock Tower, climb to the first floor above ground level, and use the blue cog on the blue clock spindle.

Black cog

  1. Follow the black route north-east. Go back down to the coloured-tile room and take the north-eastern passage marked by the black tile.
  2. Reach the fire room. Follow the passage until you find the black cog surrounded by fire. The room also contains weak giant spiders; the period guide records them as level 2.
  3. Cool the black cog. Use your bucket of water on the black cog. The water cools it enough to handle. If you brought ice gloves instead, wear them before trying to pick it up.
  4. Take and fit the black cog. Pick up the cooled black cog, return to the main cellar ladder, and use it on the black clock spindle in the basement.

Finish

  1. Return to Brother Kojo. Once all four cogs have been placed on their matching spindles, climb back to Brother Kojo. He hears the repaired clock working, thanks you, and gives you the promised reward.

Puzzle, combat and recovery notes

  • One cog at a time: you cannot pick up a second cog while another cog is in your inventory. Fit the cog you are carrying before returning for another.
  • Wrong spindle: each cog only fits the matching colour. Red goes on the ground floor, blue on the first floor, white on the top/second floor, and black in the basement.
  • White-cog cage: the levers get you into the rat enclosure, but the final western gate only becomes passable after the rats eat the poisoned food.
  • Blue cog: the apparent dead end is intentional. The cog's cage is entered through the separate ladder north-east of the Clock Tower, near Brother Cedric and the zoo.
  • Hot black cog: trying to pick it up while hot fails. Cool it with a bucket of water, or wear ice gloves.
  • Ogres: no ogre has to be killed. The historical April 2004 guide records level 48 ogres in the red-cog room, so running past them with food is the safe low-level approach.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 500 coins.

There is no Crafting XP reward. The old local page's Crafting-experience reward and unspecified extra rewards were not supported by the period guide or build-254 completion script.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: build 254 will not let you pick up a cog before speaking to Brother Kojo, and it prevents carrying more than one cog at a time.
  • Black cog: build 254 accepts a bucket of water or worn ice gloves. Using the water cools the cog and leaves an empty bucket; pick the cog up after cooling it.
  • Rat puzzle: using rat poison on the food trough triggers the dungeon rats' scripted deaths and marks the western gate as loosened, allowing access to the white cog.
  • Spindles: each coloured cog is checked against its matching spindle. A cog used on the wrong colour is rejected rather than advancing the quest.
  • Reward: the build-254 completion script gives exactly 500 coins, while the quest-point constant gives 1 Quest Point. No skill XP is granted.
  • Ogre level caution: the preserved April 2004 guide records level 48 ogres in this quest. The current Content repository's generic ogre definition is level 53, so the server's displayed danger may differ from the period guide depending on the dungeon spawn definition.
