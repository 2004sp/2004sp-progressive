The Observatory professor's telescope has been damaged by the neighbouring goblins. He needs a new wooden tripod, bronze tube and glass lens before he can use it again. After supplying the repair materials, you must recover the stolen lens mould from the goblin-filled dungeon, make the replacement lens, reach the Observatory dome and look through the repaired telescope to discover what the stars have in store for you.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • 10 Crafting. This is the requirement listed by both Jagex's 2003 quest manual and the August 2004 RuneScape 2 guide. Build 254 currently does not enforce the level when the lens is made; see the implementation notes below.
  • No prerequisite quest and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No mandatory monster kill on the August 2004 RuneScape 2 route. A level-42 Goblin guard is at the locked gate and can attack you, but the period guide specifically recommends running past it rather than requiring a kill.

Items and preparation

Bring

  • 3 wooden planks. The professor takes all three for the new tripod.
  • 1 bronze bar. The professor takes it for the telescope tube.
  • 1 molten glass. The professor temporarily takes it, then returns it after you recover the lens mould so you can make the lens yourself.

Obtained during the quest

  • Keep key from a chest in the Observatory Dungeon.
  • Lens mould hidden under sacks beyond the locked gate.
  • Lens, made by using the molten glass with the lens mould.
  • Build 254 also has a dungeon chest containing Antipoison(1); another chest can release a poisonous spider.

Recommended

  • Food and armour if you are low combat. The level-42 Goblin guard can retaliate while you pass the gate.
  • A weapon is optional rather than required. You may fight the guard, but the target-period RS2 guide gives running past it as the practical route.
  • A poison cure is useful if you intend to search extra dungeon chests. You can avoid unnecessary chests when following the direct route.
  • At least one free inventory space when returning the lens mould to the professor in build 254, because he gives the molten glass back to you at that point.
You can bring the three repair materials before starting. The professor still accepts them in order — three planks, then the bronze bar, then the molten glass — so speak to him again after each hand-in. The assistant is useful for hints but is not required if you already know where to get the materials.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Offer to repair the telescope

  1. Travel to the Observatory reception south-west of Ardougne. This is the square reception building below the Observatory hill.
  2. Speak to the Observatory professor and ask to look through the telescope.
  3. He explains that the nearby goblins tampered with it and that several parts need replacing.
  4. Agree to help. The professor asks first for three wooden planks for a new tripod.

2. Supply the telescope parts

  1. Speak to the professor with 3 wooden planks. He takes them and asks for bronze for the tube.
  2. Speak to him again with 1 bronze bar. He takes it and asks for molten glass for the lens.
  3. Speak to him again with 1 molten glass. He takes it, then realises that his lens mould is missing.
  4. The professor and assistant suspect the goblins. Your next job is to find the mould in the caverns below the Observatory.

3. Enter the Observatory Dungeon

  1. Use the ladder in the Observatory reception to climb down into the dungeon. The assistant warns you that goblins have taken over the cavern.
  2. Follow the passage into the dungeon and head toward the rooms on the south-eastern side.
  3. Search the chest in the south-eastern room to obtain the Keep key.
  4. Do not search every chest unless you need to. In build 254 one chest contains a one-dose Antipoison, one is empty, and another releases a poisonous spider and poisons the player.

4. Unlock the gate and recover the lens mould

  1. Return through the dungeon to the locked keep gate.
  2. Use the Keep key on the gate. On the first successful unlock, the key is discarded because it is no longer needed.
  3. A level-42 Goblin guard is positioned at the gate. Be ready for it to attack. Fighting is optional on the August-2004 RS2 route; you can move through quickly instead.
  4. Beyond the gate, search the sacks on the western side. Under the sacks you find the stolen Lens mould.
  5. Leave the dungeon and return to the Observatory professor in the reception building.

5. Make the replacement lens

  1. Speak to the professor with the Lens mould. He is pleased that you recovered it but admits that his own Crafting is not good enough to finish the job.
  2. He gives your molten glass back. In build 254, make sure you have a free inventory slot before this conversation can advance.
  3. Use the molten glass with the Lens mould to make a Lens.
  4. Speak to the professor again and give him the finished Lens. He repairs the telescope, takes the mould back if you still have it, and tells you to meet him at the Observatory.

6. Reach the Observatory dome

  1. Climb back down the reception ladder into the dungeon.
  2. Work through the cavern toward the three short dead-end passages leading toward the Observatory side.
  3. Take the southernmost passage and climb the ladder at its end. This brings you up near the Observatory dome.
  4. Climb into the dome and find the professor beside the repaired telescope.

7. Look through the telescope

  1. Use the Telescope while the professor is nearby.
  2. You see one of 12 constellations. The professor identifies it and gives the constellation's associated bonus or item.
  3. You also receive the fixed quest rewards: 2,250 Crafting XP, 1 uncut sapphire and 2 Quest Points.
  4. The quest completes during this telescope scene. There is no separate return trip to the reception professor for completion.

Constellation reward

Your star reward is random and is awarded in addition to the fixed quest rewards.

  • Aries: 875 Attack XP.
  • Taurus: a Super strength potion (build 254 gives Super strength(1)).
  • Gemini: a Black two-handed sword.
  • Cancer: an Amulet of defence.
  • Leo: 875 Hitpoints XP.
  • Virgo: 875 Defence XP.
  • Libra: 3 Law runes.
  • Scorpio: 1 Weapon poison.
  • Sagittarius: a Maple longbow.
  • Capricorn: 875 Strength XP.
  • Aquarius: 25 Water runes.
  • Pisces: 3 cooked tuna.

Fixed rewards and post-quest benefits

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 2,250 Crafting XP.
  • 1 uncut sapphire.
  • One random constellation reward from the list above.
  • After completion, speak to the Observatory assistant for a one-time jug of wine.
  • Completion also allows you to seek the Spirit of Scorpius at his nearby grave. In build 254 he can give an unholy symbol mould and bless a strung unholy symbol.

Useful recovery notes

  • Lost the Keep key: search the key chest again. Build 254 refuses to give a duplicate while you already have one.
  • Gate will not open: use the Keep key on it from the locked side. The first unlock permanently advances the build's gate state and consumes the key.
  • Cannot find the lens mould: it is under the sacks beyond the Goblin guard's gate, not a drop from the guard.
  • Professor will not accept the next material: the repair hand-ins are sequential. Give 3 planks first, then the bronze bar, then the molten glass.
  • Professor will not take the mould in build 254: make at least one free inventory slot so he can return the molten glass to you.
  • Lens mould missing after the quest: this is expected. The professor takes it back when you hand in the completed lens if it is still in your inventory.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Core route: build 254 implements the same main sequence as the August-2004 RS2 guide: 3 planks → bronze bar → molten glass → dungeon key → lens mould → player-made lens → Observatory telescope.
  • 10 Crafting is not enforced: the period quest requirement is 10 Crafting, but build 254's lens-crafting interaction simply removes one molten glass and adds one Lens. There is currently no Crafting-level check.
  • Goblin guard: build 254 defines the gate guard as level 42 with 43 Hitpoints. Opening the gate while it is nearby makes it retaliate, but the script does not require killing it before passage.
  • Dungeon chests: build 254 has fixed chest results: one Keep key chest, one one-dose Antipoison chest, one empty chest and one poisonous-spider trap. These are implementation details; the public route only depends on obtaining the key.
  • Lens mould: build 254 checks whether you already possess one before giving another from the sacks. The professor does not delete the mould at the mould hand-in; he returns the molten glass first, then removes the mould later when you submit the finished lens.
  • Constellation: before completion build 254 selects one of 12 constellations at random when the telescope is used. The selected reward is then followed by 2,250 Crafting XP, an uncut sapphire and 2 QP.
  • Post-completion telescope: after the quest build 254 displays Scorpio through the telescope and does not award another constellation reward.
  • Assistant reward: the build gives one jug of wine the first time you speak to the assistant after quest completion, then records that it has been claimed.
  • Scorpius: build 254 gates the Spirit of Scorpius's useful dialogue behind Observatory Quest completion. He can supply an unholy symbol mould and bless an appropriate strung symbol.
  • Dialogue provenance: several build-source comments say exact dialogue was reconstructed from RuneScape Classic, later guides or later game versions because direct pre-rework RS2 dialogue was unavailable. The historical walkthrough therefore relies on the period route and objective evidence rather than treating every build dialogue line as a verbatim September-2004 source.
