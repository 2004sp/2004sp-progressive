A boy near Taverley has kicked his ball over the hedge into the garden of a frightening old witch. Retrieving it means breaking into her house, defeating her unusual security system, slipping through the garden without being spotted, and surviving the experiment guarding the shed.

September 2004 date lock: this route follows the RuneScape 2 layout implemented by 2004scape build 254 and corroborated by surviving period material. Older Classic-style instructions that conflict with the build are not used for the walkthrough.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • No skill requirement.
  • No prerequisite quest and no Quest Point requirement.
  • You must defeat four consecutive forms of the Witch's experiment. The final form is level 53.

Required items

  • 1 cheese. It is consumed while luring the mouse from its hole.
  • Leather gloves. Wear them before handling the electrified basement gate.

Recommended

  • Combat equipment suitable for four fights in succession.
  • Food, especially if level 53 combat is dangerous for your character.
  • A few free inventory spaces for the keys, diary, magnet and ball obtained during the quest.

Quest items obtained on the way

  • House key — hidden under the flower pot beside the front door.
  • Witch's diary — found upstairs and used to understand the security system.
  • Magnet — found in the basement cupboard.
  • Shed key — hidden in a secret compartment in the garden fountain.
  • Ball — guarded by the Witch's experiment in the shed.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to the Boy and enter the house

  1. Speak to the Boy outside Witch's House near Taverley. Agree to retrieve the ball that he kicked over the hedge.
  2. Go to the front door and search/look under the flower pot beside it. You find the house key.
  3. Use the key to enter the house.

2. Read the Witch's diary

  1. Go upstairs to the bedroom and find the Witch's diary.
  2. Read it. The diary describes the Witch's security precautions and gives the clues for getting through the back door and finding the shed key.
  3. Keep the diary while you continue through the house.

3. Get the magnet from the basement

  1. Go down into the basement.
  2. Equip your leather gloves before operating the electrified gate. Trying to handle it bare-handed causes damage.
  3. Pass the gate and search the cupboard to obtain a magnet.
  4. Return to the main floor.

4. Use the mouse to unlock the back door

  1. Find the small room with the mouse hole.
  2. Use your cheese on the mouse hole. The cheese is consumed and draws the mouse out.
  3. Use the magnet on the mouse. The magnet is consumed and the mouse trips the mechanism that unlocks the back door.
  4. Go through the unlocked back door into the garden.

5. Sneak past Nora T. Hagg and get the shed key

  1. The Witch, Nora T. Hagg, patrols the garden. Use the hedges as cover and move when she is facing away.
  2. Make your way to the fountain and search it. Its secret compartment contains the shed key.
  3. Continue using the hedges for cover as you approach the shed. If the Witch spots you, she can confiscate quest items such as the shed key or ball and throw you back outside, so avoid crossing her line of sight.

6. Defeat the Witch's experiment

  1. Use the shed key to enter the shed.
  2. Try to take the ball. The Witch's experiment appears and prevents you from taking it.
  3. Defeat all four forms in sequence:
    1. Level 19 glob.
    2. Level 30 spider.
    3. Level 42 bear.
    4. Level 53 wolf.
  4. After the level 53 wolf dies, take the ball.

7. Return the ball

  1. Leave the shed and sneak back through the garden, again keeping out of Nora T. Hagg's sight.
  2. Return to the Boy outside Witch's House with the ball.
  3. Give him the ball to complete the quest.

Useful notes and recovery

  • The basement gate hurts you? Equip leather gloves before interacting with the electrified gate.
  • The mouse will not help? Use one cheese on the mouse hole first, then use the basement magnet on the mouse that appears.
  • The back door is still locked? Complete the cheese-and-magnet mouse sequence before trying to enter the garden.
  • The Witch spots you in the garden? You are thrown back outside and she can remove the shed key or ball. Re-enter the house, return to the garden and reacquire anything she took.
  • Caught after defeating the experiment? In build 254 the defeated-experiment state is retained. If the Witch confiscates the ball, you can return to the shed and take it again without defeating all four forms a second time.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 6,325 Hitpoints XP.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest progression: build 254 tracks starting the Boy's request, obtaining the magnet, unlocking the mouse-controlled back door, reading the diary, defeating the experiment and returning the ball.
  • RuneScape 2 house route: the build hides the house key beneath the flower pot, uses the cheese-and-magnet mouse puzzle for the back door and hides the shed key in the fountain. Those mechanics are used here instead of conflicting Classic-style instructions preserved in some older guide text.
  • Garden stealth: Nora T. Hagg actively patrols the garden. Being seen triggers her ejection routine and can remove the shed key or ball.
  • Four-form experiment: the build-visible forms are level 19 glob, level 30 spider, level 42 bear and level 53 wolf. The walkthrough deliberately does not publish copied modern combat statistics that appear in internal comments.
  • Reward package: build 254 awards 4 Quest Points and 6,325 Hitpoints XP. It does not award Defence XP or coins.
