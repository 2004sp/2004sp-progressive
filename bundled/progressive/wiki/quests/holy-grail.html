King Arthur has learned that the Holy Grail has entered RuneScape and sends you to recover it. Merlin points you toward a holy island and the retired Sir Galahad; their clues lead to the Fisher King's dying realm, where the Grail cannot be claimed until you help restore the land by finding the king's missing son, Sir Percival.

Requirements

Required quest

  • Completion of Merlin's Crystal. Holy Grail is only offered after you have become a Knight of the Round Table.

Required level

  • 20 Attack — required to equip Excalibur. In build 254 the Black Knight Titan will not stay defeated unless Excalibur is equipped when the defeat is resolved.

Required item

  • Excalibur from Merlin's Crystal. It is not consumed. If you lost it, build 254 lets the Lady of the Lake replace it for 500 coins after you have already proved yourself worthy.

Obtained during the quest

  • Holy table napkin from Galahad.
  • 2 Magic whistles in Draynor Manor. Take two: one is given to Sir Percival and the other is needed to return to the Fisher Realm.
  • Grail bell outside the Grail Castle during the first Fisher Realm visit.
  • Magic gold feather from King Arthur after the Fisher King asks you to find Percival.
  • Holy grail from the restored Grail Castle.

Recommended

  • Good melee armour, a decent melee weapon and food for the Black Knight Titan.
  • 43 Prayer and Prayer-restoring supplies if you want Protect from Melee for the Titan. This was already a recommendation in an August-2004 walkthrough, not a hard quest requirement.
  • A way to handle poison if the poison scorpions on the western Karamja route are dangerous at your combat level.
  • Several free inventory spaces for the napkin, whistles, feather and Grail.

Before you start

You will visit Entrana early in the quest, so do not try to take your combat weapons and armour onto the island. A simple approach is to begin with little equipment, complete the Entrana and Galahad clue stages, collect the two Magic whistles from Draynor Manor, and then bank/regear before travelling to Brimhaven for the Titan.

Keep both Magic whistles. Sir Percival needs one later. The whistle only transports you when used at the correct Karamja realm-crossing point or from inside the Fisher Realm.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Accept King Arthur's quest

  1. Speak to King Arthur in Camelot Castle. After Merlin's Crystal is complete, ask whether he has another quest for you.
  2. Choose the dialogue accepting the search for the Holy Grail.
  3. Arthur tells you to speak to Merlin, who has a workshop beside the castle library.

2. Ask Merlin where to search

  1. Go upstairs in Camelot Castle and enter Merlin's workshop/study beside the library.
  2. Tell Merlin that King Arthur sent you to look for the Grail.
  3. Merlin reasons that the Grail is likely to be in a holy place and suggests a nearby holy island. He also recommends speaking to Sir Galahad, who once returned from a Grail quest.

3. Visit Galahad, then travel to Entrana

  1. Find Galahad in his house west of McGrubor's Wood. He now lives as Brother Galahad.
  2. Ask him about the Holy Grail. He describes the Grail Castle and explains that he left the realm without taking the Grail.
  3. Bank weapons and armour that the Entrana monks will reject.
  4. Travel to Entrana from Port Sarim.

4. Speak to the High Priest and the Crone

  1. At Entrana's church, speak to the High Priest and say that you are searching for the Holy Grail.
  2. A Crone interrupts. She says the Fisher King is in pain and gives the main travel clue: go to where the six stone heads face and blow a Magic whistle.
  3. Ask her about the whistle. She explains that the whistles are in a haunted manor in Misthalin, but they can only be perceived while carrying something from the Fisher King's realm.

5. Get Galahad's Holy table napkin

  1. Return to Galahad west of McGrubor's Wood.
  2. Ask for an item from the realm of the Fisher King, or follow his Grail dialogue until he mentions the small cloth he brought back.
  3. Galahad gives you a Holy table napkin. Keep it in your inventory for the next step.

6. Take two Magic whistles from Draynor Manor

  1. Go to Draynor Manor north of Draynor Village.
  2. Climb to the top floor and go to the room at the far southern end.
  3. With the Holy table napkin in your inventory, the quest's Magic whistles become obtainable there.
  4. Take two Magic whistles. Do not leave with only one unless you are prepared to come back for another before speaking to Percival.

7. Prepare for the Black Knight Titan

  1. Retrieve your combat equipment and food.
  2. Make sure you have Excalibur. If it is missing, visit the Lady of the Lake in Taverley; build 254 can replace it for 500 coins after Merlin's Crystal.
  3. Use melee for the Titan. Build 254 gives it extremely high Ranged and Magic defence, and the quest's defeat check requires Excalibur to be equipped when the Titan is defeated.

8. Cross into the Fisher Realm

  1. Travel to Brimhaven on Karamja.
  2. Head far west toward the poison scorpions, then north.
  3. Continue past the horseshoe-shaped cluster of gold rocks until you reach a raised platform on stilts.
  4. Stand underneath the platform and blow a Magic whistle. This is the realm-crossing point indicated by the stone heads.

9. Defeat the Black Knight Titan

  1. Head west in the bleak Fisher Realm until the Black Knight Titan blocks your route.
  2. Fight it in melee. A preserved August-2004 guide specifically notes that the fight can be flinched.
  3. Have Excalibur equipped for the finishing blow / defeat. In build 254, if the Titan reaches its defeat routine without Excalibur worn, it heals back to full and declares itself invincible.
  4. Once the Titan is defeated correctly, continue deeper into the realm.

10. Ring the Grail bell and meet the Fisher King

  1. Cross the bridge toward the small peninsula and speak to the Fisherman to the south-east.
  2. Ask how to enter the castle. He tells you to ring one of the bells outside.
  3. Go to the Grail Castle. A Grail bell is outside the wall on the north-west side.
  4. Take and ring the Grail bell. A Grail Maiden brings you inside.
  5. Climb the central staircase and find the Fisher King in the eastern room.
  6. Ask about his health. He explains that his life is failing and asks you to find his son and heir, Percival, a Knight of the Round Table.

11. Ask King Arthur about Sir Percival

  1. Blow a Magic whistle from the Fisher Realm to return to Karamja.
  2. Return to Camelot and speak to King Arthur about Sir Percival.
  3. Arthur explains that Percival disappeared while questing for the golden boots of Arkaneeses.
  4. He gives you a Magic gold feather. Blowing on it points toward Percival.

12. Free Sir Percival at Goblin Village

  1. Travel to Goblin Village, north of Falador.
  2. The feather leads to the eastern hut. Investigate the sacks inside and open the quest sack.
  3. Sir Percival climbs out and explains that goblins trapped him.
  4. Tell him that the Fisher King is his father and is dying.
  5. If you have a Magic whistle, the player gives one whistle to Percival and explains how to use it. This advances the quest. Keep your second whistle.

13. Return to the restored Fisher Realm

  1. Return to the same western Karamja platform and blow your remaining Magic whistle.
  2. The realm is now green and restored, the Black Knight Titan is gone, and Percival has become king.
  3. Enter the Grail Castle normally. You may speak to King Percival upstairs for his thanks.

14. Take the Holy Grail

  1. From the castle's lower level, make your way to the eastern tower.
  2. Climb the staircase and then the ladder to the upper room.
  3. Take the Holy grail. The quest now allows it to be removed because the realm has been restored.

15. Return the Grail to King Arthur

  1. Leave the Fisher Realm with your whistle and return to Camelot Castle.
  2. Speak to King Arthur while carrying the Holy grail.
  3. Arthur accepts the Grail and the quest completes.

Combat encounter and hazards

  • Black Knight Titan: build 254 displays level 120. The preserved August-2004 guide is internally inconsistent, giving level 120 in its requirement list but level 126 in the preparation step. Because those target-proximate lines conflict, this page does not pretend that one exact September-2004 display value is independently settled.
  • Required weapon condition: use melee and have Excalibur equipped when the Titan is defeated. Build 254 heals the Titan to full if Excalibur is not worn at the defeat check.
  • Ranged/Magic: build 254 gives the Titan +1000 Ranged and Magic defence, so melee is the practical method.
  • Flinching: an August-2004 RuneScape 2 guide already describes the Titan as flinchable, making this a period-valid tactic.
  • Western Karamja: the route to the realm-crossing point passes poison scorpions. Low-level players should bring food and, if needed, a period-appropriate way to deal with poison.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost Excalibur: in build 254, the Lady of the Lake can replace it for 500 coins after you have progressed beyond the original Excalibur test.
  • Titan healed itself: you did not have Excalibur equipped at the build's defeat check. Re-equip it and defeat the Titan again. Galahad also has special advice after this failure state.
  • Cannot see/get the whistles: carry the Holy table napkin in your inventory when using the relevant Draynor Manor room/door. Build 254 does not count a banked napkin for this spawn check.
  • Only took one whistle: obtain another before trying to send Percival to his father. Percival consumes one whistle, and you still need one to travel back to the restored realm.
  • Lost Holy table napkin after failing the Titan: build 254 gives Galahad a replacement dialogue if neither your inventory nor bank contains one at that failure stage.
  • Magic gold feather: it points toward Percival's sacks. In build 254 the actual sack-opening state does not additionally require the feather to be present, so the feather functions as guidance rather than a second code gate.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 15,300 Defence XP.
  • 11,000 Prayer XP.

There is no coin reward. Holy Grail also does not award an Excalibur special-attack recharge service; that claim in the previous local guide was not part of this quest's completion reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start gate: King Arthur only offers Holy Grail after Merlin's Crystal is complete. No additional quest or Quest Point requirement is checked.
  • 20 Attack: build 254's equipment requirement script sets Excalibur at 20 Attack. This agrees with the preserved August-2004 RuneScape 2 guide.
  • Titan level conflict: build 254 displays the Black Knight Titan as level 120. The August-2004 guide says level 120 in its requirements and 126 in the later walkthrough; Jagex's December-2003 Classic-era manual used level 146. These are kept separate rather than silently mixing Classic, period-RS2 and reconstructed build values.
  • Titan defeat logic: the build checks the worn equipment at defeat time. If Excalibur is absent from the weapon slot, the Titan restores itself to full health and the quest records a failed-defeat stage. With Excalibur worn, the kill proceeds.
  • Titan defences: build config uses +1000 Magic defence and +1000 Ranged defence, reinforcing the period guide's melee requirement.
  • Whistle spawn: the Draynor Manor interaction requires the Holy table napkin in the inventory and spawns two Magic whistles when the player has fewer than two in inventory. It does not check a banked napkin or banked whistles.
  • Whistle locations: the build only accepts the whistle at the small Karamja realm-crossing zone or from inside the Fisher Realm. Elsewhere it reports that the whistle makes no noise.
  • Percival search: once the Fisher King has asked for Percival, opening the correct Goblin Village sacks is state-gated but not additionally gated on carrying the Magic gold feather. The feather's build function is directional guidance.
  • Restored-realm warp: the quest source itself comments that the post-Percival teleport coordinate places the player inside a tree on the reconstructed map because the original tree position differs from later Old School. This is a build/map defect; this audit did not invent an unverified workaround.
  • Exact completion reward: the build removes the Holy grail, awards 11,000 Prayer XP and 15,300 Defence XP, and grants 2 Quest Points.
