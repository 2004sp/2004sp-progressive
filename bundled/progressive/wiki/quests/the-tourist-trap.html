Tourist Trap

Quest guide · September 2004 target · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Tourist Trap

Irena's daughter Ana went south into the Kharidian Desert as a tourist and never returned. Her tracks lead to the heavily guarded Desert Mining Camp, where prisoners are forced to work as slaves. To bring Ana home you must get inside the camp, disguise yourself as a slave, bargain with the Bedabin nomads, steal Captain Siad's technical plans, build a prototype throwing dart, and smuggle Ana out in a mining barrel.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • 10 Fletching. This is checked when attaching feathers to the prototype dart tip.
  • 20 Smithing. This is checked when forging the prototype dart tip.
  • Ability to defeat the level-47 Mercenary Captain. In build 254 he fights with a scimitar using slash-style melee attacks and has 80 Hitpoints.
  • No prerequisite quest and no Quest Point requirement.

Items and preparation

Bring

  • 1 Shantay pass. You need it to enter the desert through Shantay Pass. More are only needed if you leave north and later re-enter.
  • Desert shirt, desert robe and desert boots. You trade all three pieces to a mining slave for his complete slave disguise.
  • 1 hammer. Required at the experimental anvil.
  • At least 1 bronze bar. Required for the prototype dart tip. Bring spares because a failed Smithing attempt can consume the bar.
  • At least 10 feathers. Required for the prototype dart. Bring spare sets of 10 because a failed Fletching attempt can consume all ten.
  • 20 coins for the period Mercenary-Captain route, which bribes the four nearby mercenaries before the duel.
  • A weapon for the Mercenary Captain. Armour is useful for the fight, but remove your weapon and armour before entering the Mining Camp.

Obtained during the quest

  • Metal key after defeating the Mercenary Captain; it opens the Mining Camp gate.
  • Slave shirt, slave robe and slave boots from the male slave.
  • Bedabin key from Al Shabim.
  • Technical plans from Captain Siad's chest.
  • Prototype dart tip and then a prototype dart.
  • Tenti pineapple from Al Shabim.
  • Empty mining barrel and later Ana in a barrel.
  • Cell door key from Captain Siad's desk is optional but useful if you are jailed on the surface.
  • Wrought iron key from Ana after she reaches Irena; it opens the locked post-quest mine gate.

Recommended

  • Several full waterskins for desert heat.
  • Food for the Mercenary Captain, desert travel and accidental guard encounters.
  • 2–3 bronze bars and 20–30 feathers if your Smithing and Fletching are close to the minimum levels. Build 254 preserves the old failure rolls that can waste materials.
  • Plenty of free inventory space. The route gives you keys, plans, slave clothes, the prototype, pineapple and a large barrel.
Do not rely on later desert transport. The target date is 7 September 2004. Follow the quest on foot between Shantay Pass, the Desert Mining Camp and the Bedabin Camp; later magic-carpet conveniences are outside this guide's historical cutoff.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start with Irena at Shantay Pass

  1. Travel south of Al Kharid to Shantay Pass, obtain a Shantay pass and go through the gate into the desert.
  2. Speak to Irena, who is crying just south of the pass.
  3. Ask what is wrong. Her daughter Ana went travelling in the desert and failed to return.
  4. Agree to look for Ana. Irena mentions that Ana was wearing a red silk scarf when she left.

2. Follow Ana's tracks to the Mining Camp

  1. Head south-west into the desert and inspect the footprints in the sand.
  2. A closer search shows five sets of tracks: one lighter set and four made by heavier booted people.
  3. Continue south-west until you reach the Desert Mining Camp. The guarded gate and Mercenary Captain are outside.
  4. The September-2004 route uses these footprints as the clue. Do not look for the later red-scarf cactus used by post-2006 versions.

3. Isolate and defeat the Mercenary Captain

  1. Use Watch on the Mercenary Captain and speak to him. Flatter him and offer to do something for him.
  2. He orders you to deal with a supposed bandit leader called Al Zaba Bhasim.
  3. Walk west to the Bedabin Camp and speak to Al Shabim. The Bedabin leader makes it clear that Al Zaba Bhasim does not exist.
  4. Return to the Mining Camp and speak to the four mercenaries around the Captain. Follow the dialogue about the Captain not fighting his own battles and bribe each guard for 5 coins.
  5. Speak to the Captain again and confront him over the fictitious bandit. Provoke him into fighting you.
  6. Defeat the level-47 Mercenary Captain. The other bribed mercenaries stay out of the duel.
  7. Take the Metal key. In build 254 it is placed directly into your inventory when the scripted Captain kill is credited.

4. Enter the Mining Camp without being arrested

  1. Unequip your weapon and armour before entering. Guards inside the camp can recognise armed or armoured outsiders and throw them in jail.
  2. Use the Metal key on the main gate and enter.
  3. For insurance, go upstairs in Captain Siad's building and search his desk for the optional Cell door key.
  4. Keep your combat gear unequipped while moving through the camp. In build 254, a wielded pickaxe is specifically exempt from the weapon check.

5. Trade clothes with the male slave

  1. Find the male slave near the eastern side of the surface camp who talks about escaping.
  2. Tell him you are new and encourage his escape plan.
  3. Offer to remove his chains. The attempt uses a Thieving success roll but there is no fixed Thieving-level requirement; retry if it fails.
  4. Trade your desert shirt, desert robe and desert boots for his slave shirt, slave robe and slave boots.
  5. Wear all three slave pieces.

6. Learn what the underground guard wants

  1. While wearing the full slave outfit, push through the large doors into the underground mine.
  2. Follow the mine passage until guards block the way to the deeper cavern.
  3. Speak politely to a guard and ask whether you can mine somewhere else.
  4. He agrees to let you through only if you bring him a Tenti pineapple.
  5. Return to the surface and leave the Mining Camp, still keeping your Metal key and slave clothes.

7. Make a deal with Al Shabim

  1. Walk west to the Bedabin Camp and ask Al Shabim for a Tenti pineapple.
  2. He wants Captain Siad's technical plans in return and gives you a Bedabin key for the chest that contains them.
  3. Return to the Desert Mining Camp and enter with your Metal key.

8. Distract Captain Siad and steal the plans

  1. Go upstairs to Captain Siad's room.
  2. Search the bookcase. You notice that several books are about sailing; this unlocks the reliable distraction topic.
  3. Talk to Captain Siad and ask to chat. Bring up his books, then his interest in sailing.
  4. Flatter him by saying you could tell by the cut of his jib. He becomes distracted talking about sailing.
  5. While he is distracted, open his chest with the Bedabin key and take the Technical plans.

9. Forge the prototype throwing dart

  1. Return to Al Shabim and show him the Technical plans. Agree to build the weapon.
  2. Show the plans to the guard at the nearby private tent so that he lets you enter.
  3. At the experimental anvil, use a bronze bar while carrying the hammer and Technical plans.
  4. Choose to follow the plans. You need 20 Smithing. A failed build-254 Smithing roll destroys that bronze bar, so use another if necessary.
  5. When successful, you receive a Prototype dart tip.
  6. Use 10 feathers on the tip. You need 10 Fletching. A failed build-254 Fletching roll destroys the 10 feathers; retry with another set.
  7. When successful, you make the Prototype dart.

10. Receive the Tenti pineapple and enter the deep mine

  1. Give the Prototype dart to Al Shabim.
  2. He teaches you how to make throwing darts and gives you the Tenti pineapple. Build 254 also gives six bronze darts here if you are not already carrying any bronze darts.
  3. Return to the Mining Camp, put on the full slave outfit and enter the underground mine.
  4. Give the guard the Tenti pineapple. Ordinary pineapple pieces do not substitute for the special Tenti pineapple.
  5. Pass into the deeper cavern.

11. Reach Ana by mine cart

  1. Near the mine cart, search the barrels until you can take one empty mining barrel.
  2. Search the mine cart and choose to squeeze into it. This uses an Agility success roll but has no fixed Agility requirement.
  3. If the build-254 roll fails, you take 2 damage and can immediately try again.
  4. After the cart carries you to the other section, follow the passage to Ana.

12. Put Ana in the barrel

  1. Talk to Ana and tell her that you want to get her out.
  2. Use the safe dialogue route: suggest sneaking out, say that you managed to sneak past the guards, and when she asks how you passed the gate choose "It's a trade secret!".
  3. Do not boast that the guards are rubbish or tell nearby guards that you used a key. Those branches can expose you and send you to the punishment area.
  4. Use the empty barrel on Ana. She protests, but you squeeze her into it.
  5. Use Ana in a barrel on the mine cart, then use the cart yourself to return to the barrel-loading area.
  6. Search the nearby barrels to recover Ana in a barrel.

13. Move Ana up the lift

  1. Take Ana's barrel to the underground lift/winch.
  2. Use the barrel on the lift or speak to the nearby guard while carrying it. Let the guard help with the heavy barrel.
  3. Ana starts talking from inside. Cover for her by complimenting the guard; in build 254 the successful response is "I said you were very gregarious!".
  4. The guard puts the barrel onto the lift.
  5. Return through the mine to the surface camp and go to the winch in the south-west corner.
  6. Operate the winch to haul the barrel up, then search the raised barrel and take Ana in a barrel back into your inventory.

14. Put Ana on the surface cart and persuade the driver

  1. Use Ana in a barrel on the large surface mine cart near the Mine cart driver.
  2. Talk to the driver and begin with the compliment about his nice cart.
  3. Follow the joke chain: the wagon-wheel joke, "One good turn deserves another", "Fired... no, shot perhaps!", and "In for a penny, in for a pound."
  4. Choose "Well, you see, it's like this...", warn him about a prison riot, then say "You can't leave me here, I'll get killed." This is the no-payment escape route preserved by the June-2004 guide and implemented in build 254.
  5. Search the cart and choose to get on. The driver takes the cart through the gates and you jump off outside with Ana's barrel.

15. Return Ana to Irena and choose your XP

  1. Carry Ana in a barrel north through the desert to Shantay Pass. Do not try to walk out of the Mining Camp's front gate with her barrel; use the cart escape.
  2. At Shantay Pass, Ana is released from the barrel and appears near Irena.
  3. Speak to Ana before she leaves. In build 254 she gives you the Wrought iron key if you do not already have one.
  4. Speak to Irena. She offers 4,650 XP twice, each choice being Fletching, Agility, Smithing or Thieving.
  5. You may choose the same skill twice. After the second choice the quest completes and you receive 2 Quest Points.

Combat encounter

  • Mercenary Captain — level 47. Build 254 gives him 80 Hitpoints and slash-style melee combat with a scimitar.
  • The period route first turns his nearby mercenaries against him with 5-coin bribes, allowing the Captain to be fought by himself.
  • Your weapon and armour are useful for this duel, but unequip them before stepping into the Mining Camp or the guards can identify you as an intruder.

Rewards and permanent unlocks

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 4,650 XP in each of two choices from Agility, Fletching, Smithing and Thieving. The same skill may be selected twice for 9,300 XP total in that skill.
  • Ability to make throwing darts. Al Shabim teaches this when you hand him the prototype.
  • Wrought iron key, opening the locked deeper section of the Desert Mining Camp mine.
  • Full slave outfit retained for access to the mine.
  • Build 254 can also give 6 bronze darts when the prototype is handed in, provided you are not already carrying bronze darts.

Useful recovery notes

  • Lost the Metal key: the Captain can be killed again for another one; build 254 can also replace it from Captain Siad's desk in relevant states.
  • Jailed on the surface: the Cell door key from Siad's desk opens the cell door. The build also implements the old weak-window-bar and rocky-escape route, although source comments mark parts of that reconstruction as guessed.
  • Jailed in the underground punishment mine: mine 15 punishment rocks and give all 15 to the guard in one go to be released.
  • Lost the Bedabin key or Technical plans: return to the relevant quest NPC/state before continuing rather than trying to substitute a normal key or plans.
  • Prototype attempt failed: obtain another bronze bar or another set of 10 feathers. Failure can consume the materials in build 254.
  • Ana exposed: if guards discover her, she can be returned to the deeper mine and you may need to repeat the barrel transport sequence.
  • No inventory space for Ana's barrel: clear at least one slot before searching the destination barrels; build 254 refuses to give the barrel back while your inventory is full.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest title: build 254's journal/completion text uses Tourist Trap; the source explicitly notes that the name changes to The Tourist Trap at a later point. This page uses the build/target-period title while retaining the established filename.
  • Requirements: the build journal lists 10 Fletching and 20 Smithing. There is no fixed Thieving or Agility level gate; those parts use repeatable success rolls instead.
  • Captain: build 254 defines the Mercenary Captain as level 47 with 80 Hitpoints, a scimitar and slash-style melee. His quest death script supplies the Metal key directly.
  • Captain shortcut: build dialogue contains shorter provocation branches that can start the duel without reproducing every step of the June-2004 Al Zaba Bhasim / guard-bribe route. The historical walkthrough above keeps the contemporary route; the shortcut is a build convenience.
  • Camp equipment checks: while the quest is incomplete, periodic guard checks can jail a player wearing armour or wielding a non-pickaxe weapon. Pickaxes are explicitly exempt.
  • Slave disguise: all three pieces — slave shirt, robe and boots — must be worn to pass the large mine doors in build 254.
  • Prototype failures: the build deliberately preserves material loss on failed Smithing and Fletching rolls. A Smithing failure consumes one bronze bar; a Fletching failure consumes ten feathers.
  • Mine cart: the build uses an Agility roll but no minimum level. A failed squeeze attempt deals 2 damage. This differs from later guides that quote much larger post-Hitpoints-scale damage numbers.
  • Post-2006 scarf cactus excluded: the build source notes that the extra old tracks were later replaced by a cactus; its target-period implementation uses the sand footprints and does not add the reworked scarf-cactus interaction.
  • Bronze darts: Al Shabim's build script gives six bronze darts only when you are carrying none. Dart-making knowledge is unlocked regardless.
  • Cart-driver payment: build 254 supports both a 100-coin bribe branch and the no-payment prison-riot dialogue branch. The June-2004 guide preserves the latter, so 100 coins are not a hard quest requirement here.
  • Wrought iron key: after the Shantay-Pass rescue state, Ana gives the key if it is missing. Captain Siad's desk also has later recovery logic for the key.
  • Reward code: Irena awards 46,500 internal XP units per choice, which is 4,650 displayed XP in the build's tenths-based XP storage, then the completion script awards 2 Quest Points.
  • Reconstructed details: several jail/guard comments in the source explicitly say their exact pre-rework behaviour or animation timing is guessed from RSC/later evidence. Those uncertain details are kept out of the main route unless they are needed as a recovery option.
