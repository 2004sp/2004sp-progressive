Sir Vyvin's squire has lost a ceremonial family sword that has passed through the knight's family for five generations. The blade was made by the nearly vanished Imcando dwarves, so an ordinary replacement will not do. Track down an Imcando smith, earn his trust with his favourite pie, steal a picture of the original sword and brave the ice dungeon for the rare blurite needed to forge a convincing replica.

Requirements

Required level

  • 10 Mining. Blurite rocks require level 10 Mining in build 254.

Combat requirement

  • No enemy has to be killed. You must, however, pass aggressive level 57 Ice Warriors and level 49 Ice Giants in the build-254 ice dungeon to reach the blurite rocks.

Bring before starting

  • 1 redberry pie. Thurgo will not agree to help until you give him one.
  • 2 iron bars. Thurgo consumes both when he makes the replacement sword.
  • A pickaxe. Any pickaxe you can use is sufficient for the blurite rock.

You can obtain these supplies after starting, but bringing them in advance removes several extra trips.

Obtained during the quest

  • Portrait. Taken from Sir Vyvin's cupboard after the squire tells you where to find a picture of the sword.
  • 1 blurite ore. Mine it from the rocks deep in the ice dungeon beneath the cliffs near Thurgo.
  • Blurite sword. Thurgo makes it from the portrait's design, 1 blurite ore and 2 iron bars.

Recommended

  • Food and defensive equipment. Fighting is optional, but the route to the blurite rocks runs through aggressive enemies.
  • At least 3 free inventory spaces when collecting the final materials so you can carry the blurite ore and two iron bars together.
  • If you want to keep an extra blurite sword, bring a total of 4 iron bars and mine 2 blurite ore. See the optional note below before completing the quest.

Before you start

The September-2004 route is the old Imcando-dwarf quest, not a simple mine-and-return task. You must speak to Reldo, give Thurgo a redberry pie, obtain Sir Vyvin's portrait, then provide the smith with 2 iron bars and 1 blurite ore.

The cave is the only dangerous part. Build 254 does not require you to defeat either the Ice Warriors or Ice Giants, so lower-level players can run past them, mine the ore and leave.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Offer to replace the lost sword

  1. Go to the courtyard of the White Knights' Castle in Falador and talk to the Squire.
  2. Ask about his trouble. He explains that he has lost Sir Vyvin's sword while carrying it from Lumbridge.
  3. Offer to make a new sword. The squire explains that it is a five-generation family heirloom originally made by the Imcando dwarves.
  4. Ask whether the dwarves could make another. He suggests speaking to Reldo, the palace librarian in Varrock.
  5. Agree to track down an Imcando dwarf to begin the quest.

2. Ask Reldo about the Imcando dwarves

  1. Travel to Varrock Palace and find Reldo in the palace library.
  2. Ask him what he knows about the Imcando dwarves.
  3. Reldo says that a few may still live on the southern peninsula of Asgarnia, near the cliffs, and that they are especially fond of redberry pie.

3. Give Thurgo a redberry pie

  1. Travel south of Port Sarim to the southern Asgarnian peninsula. Thurgo lives beside the cliffs near the entrance to the ice dungeon.
  2. Talk to Thurgo. If you ask for a special sword immediately, he refuses.
  3. Offer him your redberry pie. He eats it and becomes much friendlier.
  4. Ask again whether he can make a special sword for a Falador knight.
  5. Thurgo agrees to try, but he needs to know exactly what the original sword looked like. He asks you to bring him a picture.

4. Learn where Sir Vyvin keeps a portrait

  1. Return to the Squire in Falador Castle.
  2. Tell him that the Imcando dwarf needs a picture of the sword.
  3. The squire remembers a portrait of Sir Vyvin's father that shows the sword. Sir Vyvin keeps it in a cupboard in his room.

5. Take the portrait without Sir Vyvin stopping you

  1. Go upstairs in the White Knights' Castle to Sir Vyvin's room on the eastern side.
  2. Find the cupboard in his room and wait until Sir Vyvin is not standing next to you. Build 254 blocks the search if he is within one tile.
  3. Open and search the cupboard to take the portrait.
  4. If Vyvin catches you, he only prevents the search; move or wait until he has walked farther away and try again.

6. Show the portrait to Thurgo

  1. Return to Thurgo with the portrait.
  2. Tell him you found a picture of the sword. He takes and studies the portrait.
  3. Thurgo now gives the exact material list: 2 iron bars for the sword and 1 blurite ore for its decorative hilt.
  4. He tells you that blurite can be found beneath the nearby cliff and warns you about the Ice Giants below.

7. Mine blurite in the ice dungeon

  1. Enter the ice dungeon near Thurgo with your pickaxe, food and any defensive equipment you are using.
  2. Head deeper into the cave toward the icy section containing the blurite rocks.
  3. You will pass aggressive level 57 Ice Warriors and level 49 Ice Giants in build 254. You do not need to kill them.
  4. Mine 1 blurite ore. The rock requires 10 Mining.
  5. If you plan to keep a spare blurite sword, mine 2 blurite ore instead.
  6. Leave the dungeon and return to Thurgo.

8. Have Thurgo forge the replacement

  1. Make sure you are carrying at least 1 blurite ore and 2 iron bars.
  2. Talk to Thurgo about the sword.
  3. He takes 1 blurite ore and 2 iron bars, then hands you the finished blurite sword.

Optional: keep a second blurite sword

Build 254 allows Thurgo to make another sword before quest completion whenever you are at the final material stage and do not have a blurite sword in your inventory. If you brought 4 iron bars and mined 2 blurite ore, bank the first sword—or briefly place it on the ground—then speak to Thurgo again with the second ore and the other two bars. He will forge another. Retrieve the first sword before continuing. Banking is safer than leaving an item on the ground.

9. Return the sword to the squire

  1. Take a blurite sword back to the Squire in Falador Castle.
  2. Keep the sword in your inventory. If it is equipped, the squire asks you to unequip it; if it is in your bank, he asks you to fetch it.
  3. Talk to the squire and hand over one sword to complete The Knight's Sword.

Hazards and useful notes

  • The squire lost the sword; he did not break it. This is explicit in the build-254 starting dialogue.
  • Reldo is a required quest stage. Thurgo's Imcando dialogue is not active until you have asked Reldo about the surviving dwarves.
  • The redberry pie is mandatory. Thurgo refuses to make a special sword before you give him the pie.
  • Sir Vyvin only needs to be away from the cupboard area. Build 254 checks whether he is within one tile when you search; there is no combat or punishment for being caught.
  • You do not have to fight in the ice dungeon. Running past the enemies is enough if you can survive the trip.
  • Lost portrait: while you are at the portrait stage, the cupboard can supply another portrait if one is in neither your inventory nor your bank.
  • Lost replacement sword: before quest completion, Thurgo can forge another if you again supply a blurite ore and two iron bars and are not carrying a blurite sword.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 12,725 Smithing XP.

There is no coin reward in build 254. A spare blurite sword is optional and must be arranged before you hand the quest sword to the squire.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: %squire progresses from the initial conversation through Reldo, the redberry pie, Thurgo's picture request, the portrait and the blurite-material stage, then reaches ^squire_complete = 7.
  • Mining requirement: the quest journal advertises 10 Mining, and the build-254 blurite-rock table independently requires rock_level = 10.
  • Thurgo sequence: his area script only routes into the quest conversation after the Reldo stage. The quest script consumes exactly 1 redberry pie before he will discuss making the sword.
  • Portrait: the Falador cupboard script only provides the portrait from the relevant quest stage onward. The vyvin_distracted check rejects the search while Sir Vyvin is within one tile.
  • Exact recipe: Thurgo checks for at least 1 blurite ore and 2 iron bars, deletes those exact quantities and adds one faladian_sword (displayed as Blurite sword).
  • Optional duplicate: Thurgo's pre-completion check only looks for a blurite sword in the player's inventory. Moving the first sword out of the inventory and supplying another ore plus two more iron bars therefore permits a second sword before completion.
  • Cave levels: build 254 configures Ice Warriors at visible combat level 57 and Ice Giants at 49. A contemporary April-2004 guide listed the same Ice Warriors at 57 but Ice Giants at 53, so the giant's displayed build level is an implementation-era discrepancy rather than silently being rewritten as period evidence.
  • Exact reward: completion awards 1 Quest Point and advances Smithing by 127250 internal XP units, equal to 12,725 Smithing XP.
  • No combat gate: no quest script checks for an Ice Warrior or Ice Giant kill. The enemies are a traversal hazard only.
