Caroline has stopped hearing from her husband Kent and their son Kennith, who are staying on an offshore fishing platform. Holgart can take you out to investigate, but what first looks like a broken-boat problem becomes stranger when the fishermen begin behaving as though something else is controlling them.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • 30 Firemaking. Near the end of the quest you must dry a pair of damp sticks and rub them together to light a torch. This is the quest's only hard skill requirement.
  • No prerequisite quest and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No combat is required. The sea slugs and controlled fishermen are hazards rather than enemies you must defeat.

Required items

  • 1 swamp paste. Holgart needs it to repair his rowboat before he can take you to the Fishing Platform. You may obtain it before starting; the period recipe is swamp tar mixed with a pot of flour and then heated over a fire.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Unlit torch — supplied by Bailey after you learn why the fishermen will not let you back upstairs.
  • Broken glass — found on the Fishing Platform and used as a lens to dry the sticks.
  • Damp sticks — found on the Fishing Platform; dry them with the broken glass, then rub the dry sticks together while carrying the torch.

Recommended

  • A few pieces of food if your Hitpoints are very low. There is no mandatory fight, but build 254 can deal small fixed damage if you mishandle a sea slug or try to force your way past the fishermen without the lit torch.
  • At least three free inventory spaces for the torch, broken glass and sticks you collect on the platform.

Before you start

Bring the swamp paste with you if possible. It is the only external quest item you need, and having it ready avoids an extra trip before Holgart's boat can sail.

The important late-quest mechanic is heat. Do not try to fight the fishermen or pick up the sea slugs. Bailey's torch, the broken glass and the damp sticks provide the intended way through the platform.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Caroline

  1. Go to the coast east of Ardougne, south of the Legends' Guild, and speak to Caroline.
  2. She explains that her husband Kent works on an offshore fishing platform and sometimes takes their son Kennith with him.
  3. Caroline has heard nothing from them for a week and asks you to check the platform.
  4. Agree to help. She tells you that her friend Holgart can take you there.

2. Repair Holgart's rowboat

  1. Speak to Holgart nearby and ask him to take you to the Fishing Platform.
  2. His rowboat needs repairing. Give him 1 swamp paste.
  3. Once the boat is usable, sail with Holgart to the Fishing Platform.

3. Find Kennith and learn that Kent is missing

  1. On the platform, head west and speak to Bailey.
  2. Bailey tells you that Kennith is hiding upstairs and that Kent left several days ago to seek help.
  3. Go to the ladder on the north-eastern side of the platform and climb to the upper level.
  4. Head west to the building where Kennith is hiding. Speak to him through the wall/opening.
  5. Kennith is frightened and will not simply leave. For now, return to Holgart.

4. Find Kent on the small island

  1. Tell Holgart that Kent is missing and ask to look for him.
  2. Holgart takes you to a small nearby island where Kent is stranded.
  3. Speak to Kent. He explains the strange events at the Fishing Platform and the danger posed by the sea slugs.
  4. During the encounter a sea slug attempts to affect you; Kent prevents it from taking control.
  5. Return to the Fishing Platform with Holgart.

5. Learn the sea slugs' weakness

  1. Try to climb back to the upper platform. The fishermen now block the way.
  2. Return to Bailey and explain what happened.
  3. Bailey tells you that the sea slugs dislike heat and gives you an unlit torch.
  4. Find the broken glass and the damp sticks on the platform.

6. Make a fire and light Bailey's torch

  1. Use the broken glass with the damp sticks. The glass focuses sunlight onto them and turns them into dry sticks.
  2. Make sure the unlit torch is in your inventory.
  3. Rub the dry sticks together. You need 30 Firemaking.
  4. When the sticks catch, they light Bailey's torch. In build 254 the lighting attempt can fail even when you meet the level requirement; simply rub the sticks together again.
  5. With the lit torch, return to the ladder. The fishermen are afraid of the heat and allow you upstairs.

7. Free Kennith with the crane

  1. Return to Kennith and speak to him. He still cannot get out of the building safely.
  2. Go around the outside and find the loose wooden panel.
  3. Kick the loose panel. The rotten wood breaks away, creating an opening large enough for Kennith.
  4. Speak to Kennith again so he is ready to escape.
  5. Use the nearby fishing crane. Kennith climbs onto the fishing net, and the crane lowers him into the rowboat below.

8. Return to Caroline

  1. Return to Holgart and sail back to shore.
  2. Speak to Caroline.
  3. She tells you that Kennith has explained what happened and that Kent has also been rescued and returned home.
  4. Caroline gives you the reward and the quest completes.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Do not pick up a sea slug. In build 254, trying to pick one up makes it bite your hand for 3 damage and you immediately drop it.
  • Fishermen blocking the ladder? You need the lit torch. In build 254, attempting the ladder without it at the relevant stage causes the fishermen to hit you with a fishing rod for 4 damage.
  • Dry sticks will not light? You need 30 Firemaking. If you meet the requirement but the attempt only smokes and goes out, retry; build 254 uses a success roll.
  • Loose panel does nothing? Speak to Kennith first. The panel only opens at the correct rescue stage.
  • Crane does not rescue Kennith? After opening the panel, speak to Kennith again before rotating the crane.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 7,175 Fishing XP.
  • Oyster pearls.
  • Continued access to the Fishing Platform through Holgart's boat route.
  • No Crafting XP and no coin reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 tracks Caroline's start, speaking to Holgart, repairing the boat, finding Kennith, sailing to Kent, speaking to Kent, lighting the torch, opening Kennith's panel, preparing the crane escape, saving Kennith, and completion as a twelve-state progression.
  • Start and finish: Caroline is both the quest starter and the completion NPC. Her completion script awards 7,175 Fishing XP, 1 Quest Point and one Oyster pearls reward item.
  • Firemaking gate: rubbing the dry sticks checks for 30 Firemaking. Build 254 also applies a success roll, so a qualifying player may need more than one attempt before the sticks catch.
  • Swamp-paste preparation: build 254 supports combining swamp tar with a pot of flour to create raw swamp paste, returning the empty pot. The quest's period route requires finished swamp paste for Holgart's boat.
  • Sea slug hazard: using the ordinary Pick-up interaction on a quest sea slug inflicts 3 damage and leaves the slug behind.
  • Platform ladder: after the Kent stage, trying to climb without the lit torch causes the controlled fishermen to strike the player for 4 damage. Carrying the lit torch lets the player pass.
  • Kennith escape: the loose-panel and crane handlers are explicitly state-gated. The intended sequence is talk to Kennith, kick the panel, talk to him again, then rotate the crane.
  • Historical agreement: the build's core Caroline/Holgart/Kent/Kennith route, 30-Firemaking torch puzzle and reward values closely match the surviving August-2004 guide and the 2003 Jagex quest manual.
