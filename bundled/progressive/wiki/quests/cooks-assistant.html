The cook in Lumbridge Castle has forgotten to buy the ingredients for the Duke of Lumbridge's birthday cake. He asks you to bring him milk, an egg and flour before the celebration.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None.
  • There is no skill-level, combat or quest-point requirement.

Required items

You need the following three ingredients when you return to the Cook. All of them, including the containers needed to collect them, can be obtained during the quest.

  • 1 bucket of milk — fill an empty bucket by using it on a cow.
  • 1 egg — pick one up at the chicken farm north-east of Lumbridge.
  • 1 pot of flour — mill one piece of grain at the windmill north-west of Lumbridge, then collect the flour in an empty pot.

Containers obtainable during the quest

  • 1 empty pot — the preserved 2004 route obtains one in Lumbridge Castle before going to the windmill.
  • 1 empty bucket — the preserved 2004 route obtains one from the farmhouse by the chicken farm before going to the cows.

Recommended

  • Nothing is required beyond the ingredients. There is no combat and no dangerous area on the normal route.
  • If you already have a bucket of milk, egg or pot of flour, you may bring them with you and skip that gathering step.

Before you start

You do not need to gather the ingredients in a particular order. The route below follows the preserved 2004 guide: make the flour at the windmill, collect the egg at the chicken farm, then milk a cow nearby. Keep one empty inventory slot for each ingredient you still need.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Talk to the Cook. Enter Lumbridge Castle and go to the kitchen on the ground floor. Speak to the Cook and ask what is wrong. He explains that it is the Duke's birthday and he has forgotten to buy ingredients for the cake.
  2. Agree to help. Tell him you will help. He asks for milk, an egg and flour. The quest is now in progress.

Pot of flour

  1. Get an empty pot. If you did not bring one, take an empty pot in Lumbridge Castle.
  2. Go to the windmill. Leave Lumbridge and head north-west to the windmill. The preserved August 2004 guide places a grain field immediately west of it.
  3. Pick one grain. Take a piece of grain from the field, enter the windmill and climb to the top floor.
  4. Fill the hopper. Use the grain on the hopper, then operate the hopper control/lever so the grain is ground and sent down the chute.
  5. Collect the flour. Return to the ground floor and use your empty pot on the flour bin to obtain a pot of flour.

Egg

  1. Go to the chicken farm. Head to the farm north-east of Lumbridge.
  2. Pick up an egg. An egg spawns near the chickens. Pick up one egg and keep it for the Cook.

Bucket of milk

  1. Get an empty bucket. If you did not bring one, enter the farmhouse beside the chicken area and take an empty bucket.
  2. Go to the cows. Leave the farm and head east to the nearby cow field.
  3. Milk a cow. Use the empty bucket on a cow. The bucket becomes a bucket of milk.

Finish

  1. Return to the Cook. Go back to Lumbridge Castle's kitchen with one bucket of milk, one egg and one pot of flour in your inventory.
  2. Hand over all three ingredients. Talk to the Cook. He takes the milk, egg and flour, thanks you for saving the birthday cake, and completes the quest.

Recovery and useful notes

  • Partial ingredients are safe: if you speak to the Cook before you have everything, he tells you which ingredients are still missing. He does not take the ingredients until you have all three.
  • Flour: putting grain in the hopper is not enough by itself; operate the hopper control, then collect the flour from the bin downstairs with an empty pot.
  • Milk: an empty bucket is consumed and replaced by a bucket of milk when you milk the cow.
  • No special quest variants: build 254 checks for the ordinary egg, bucket of milk and pot of flour items.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 300 Cooking experience.
  • Permission to use the Cook's range in Lumbridge Castle.

No coins, fish or other later-version rewards are part of the September 2004 reward set.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Exact completion check: build 254 requires one egg, one bucket of milk and one pot of flour in your inventory at the same time. It then removes exactly one of each.
  • Reward: the completion script grants exactly 300 Cooking XP; the quest-point constant gives 1 Quest Point.
  • Cook's range: build 254 blocks the special Lumbridge kitchen range before quest completion. After completion it can apply the range's dedicated improved cooking-success values to foods that define them, matching the quest journal's description of a higher-quality range that burns food less often.
  • Mill: the build's general windmill script requires grain in the hopper, operation of the hopper control, and an empty pot at the flour bin. This matches the preserved 2004 walkthrough.
  • Cow milking: build 254 converts one empty bucket directly into one bucket of milk when used on a cow.
  • Quest-name display: period Jagex material and the public guide name the quest Cook's Assistant, while build 254's reconstructed journal/completion text currently displays The Cooks' Quest.
