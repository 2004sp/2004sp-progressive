Duke Horacio has found a strange air talisman and asks you to take it to the head wizard at the Wizards' Tower. Sedridor recognises it as the missing clue in research he has been conducting with Aubury of Varrock. Acting as courier between the two wizards helps them reconnect the long-lost secrets of Rune Essence, elemental altars and rune creation.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • None. Rune Mysteries has no prerequisite quest, Quest Point requirement, skill check or combat requirement.

Required items

  • Nothing has to be brought. Duke Horacio supplies the air talisman, Sedridor supplies the research package, and Aubury supplies the research notes.

Items handled during the quest

  • Air talisman: Duke Horacio gives it to you. Sedridor takes it for study and returns it when the quest is completed.
  • Research package: Sedridor gives it to you for delivery to Aubury; Aubury consumes it.
  • Research notes: Aubury gives these after examining Sedridor's package; Sedridor consumes them at completion.

Recommended

  • At least one free inventory space. The quest continually replaces one courier item with the next.
  • No weapon, armour, food, runes or pickaxe are required for the quest itself.

Before you start

The route is a simple courier triangle between Lumbridge Castle, the Wizards' Tower south of Draynor Village, and Aubury's rune shop in south-east Varrock. You do not visit the Rune Essence Mine or mine an essence sample during the quest. Access to that mine is part of the reward.

The release date needs a small historical distinction: Rune Mysteries existed in the RuneScape 2 beta that began on 1 December 2003, but the project's normal live-game quest chronology places it at the permanent RuneScape 2 launch on 29 March 2004. Either way, it is safely inside the 7 September 2004 target.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Take Duke Horacio's air talisman

  1. Go upstairs in Lumbridge Castle and speak to Duke Horacio.
  2. Ask whether he has any quests for you.
  3. He explains that one of his subjects found a strange talisman and asks you to take it to the head wizard at the Wizards' Tower.
  4. Choose "Sure, no problem." Duke Horacio gives you 1 Air talisman and the quest begins.

2. Give the talisman to Sedridor

  1. Travel to the Wizards' Tower south of Draynor Village.
  2. Go down into the basement and speak to the head wizard, Sedridor.
  3. Tell him that Duke Horacio sent you and that you have a strange talisman for the head wizard.
  4. Hand over the Air talisman. Sedridor realises that it may be the missing piece of his research into rune stones.
  5. Agree to help with his research. He gives you a Research package and asks you to deliver it to Aubury, the rune-shop owner in Varrock.

If you initially decline Sedridor's follow-up task, simply speak to him again when you are ready; the quest waits at that stage.

3. Deliver the research package to Aubury

  1. Travel to Varrock and find Aubury's rune shop in the south-east part of the city.
  2. Speak to Aubury while carrying the Research package and choose the delivery option, or use the package on him.
  3. You hand over the package. Aubury says the research is extremely important and asks for a few moments to examine it.
  4. Speak to Aubury again. In build 254 this second conversation is required after the package hand-in.
  5. Aubury combines Sedridor's work with his own discoveries and gives you Research notes to take back to Sedridor.

4. Return Aubury's notes to Sedridor

  1. Return to the Wizards' Tower basement.
  2. Speak to Sedridor while the Research notes are in your inventory.
  3. Sedridor explains the rediscovery: Rune Essence can be reached by a guarded teleport spell, talismans can reveal long-lost elemental altars, and Rune Essence can be bound into runes at those altars.
  4. He identifies Duke Horacio's talisman as the key to the Air Altar.
  5. You hand Sedridor Aubury's Research notes. He returns the Air talisman to you and the quest completes.

There is no essence-mining quest step

Do not look for an essence sample before completion. The September-2004 route ends when Aubury's notes are returned to Sedridor. The ability to ask approved wizards to teleport you to the Rune Essence Mine is the reward that follows. Mining essence and using the Air talisman at the Air Altar belong to Runecraft training after the quest, not to the Rune Mysteries walkthrough.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the Air talisman before delivery? Speak to Duke Horacio. Build 254 can replace it while Rune Mysteries is still in progress if it cannot find another one owned by your character.
  • Lost Sedridor's Research package? Return to Sedridor. At the delivery stage he can magically retrieve and replace the missing package.
  • Delivered the package but received nothing? Speak to Aubury a second time. Build 254 deliberately separates the package hand-in from receiving his Research notes.
  • Lost Aubury's Research notes? Speak to Aubury again. He says that he made duplicates and supplies another copy.
  • Notes are in the bank? Withdraw them before speaking to Sedridor for completion; he requires the notes in your inventory for the final hand-in.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • Your Air talisman is returned, allowing you to locate and enter the Air Altar.
  • Access to the Rune Essence Mine through the approved wizard teleport network. The target-period route explicitly names Sedridor and Aubury.
  • Practical access to Runecraft. Contemporary 2004 skill guidance treats Rune Mysteries as the prerequisite before using Runecraft because the quest provides normal Rune Essence Mine access and explains the altar/talisman system.
  • No coins and no skill XP are awarded by the quest-completion script.
  • Quest progression: the preserved period Priest in Peril guide lists Rune Mysteries as a prerequisite, although build 254 does not directly test that quest flag when starting Priest in Peril.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state sequence: build 254 tracks not started → Duke's talisman → talisman handed to Sedridor → Research package received → package handed to Aubury → Research notes received → complete.
  • Exact item flow: Duke adds one Air talisman; Sedridor deletes it and later adds one Research package; Aubury deletes the package and on the next conversation adds Research notes; Sedridor deletes those notes and adds an Air talisman back before completion.
  • Recovery: Duke can replace the talisman while the quest is in progress, Sedridor can replace the missing Research package at its delivery stage, and Aubury can replace lost Research notes.
  • Rune Essence Mine gate: the shared essence-mine teleport routine explicitly rejects players who have not completed Rune Mysteries. After completion, Sedridor and Aubury can teleport free-to-play players there.
  • Members' teleporters: build 254 also enables Rune Essence teleports from Wizard Cromperty in Ardougne, Wizard Distentor in the Wizards' Guild and Brimstail in the Tree Gnome Stronghold once Rune Mysteries is complete and the player can reach those members locations.
  • Runecraft edge case: the altar-crafting script itself does not test the Rune Mysteries quest flag. If a player somehow obtains Rune essence and a valid talisman before completion, the build's altar logic relies on Runecraft level/membership checks rather than the quest state. Normal essence-mine access is still quest-gated, and the build also refuses to assign genie-lamp XP to Runecraft before completion.
  • Reward: the completion queue awards 1 Quest Point and no skill XP or coins. Its completion-screen text says "Mysteries Of The Runes Quest", while the quest journal and player-facing title are Rune Mysteries.
  • Reconstruction caveat: the build journal comments cite later screenshots/videos and mark one intermediate journal line as a best guess. Core item/state mechanics are authoritative for build 254, but reconstructed dialogue wording is not treated as direct September-2004 evidence.
