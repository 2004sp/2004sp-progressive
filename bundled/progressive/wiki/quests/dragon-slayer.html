The Guildmaster knows a man who can sell Rune plate mail, but Oziach will only sell armour fit for a hero to someone who proves worthy of it. His test is to kill Elvarg, the dragon of Crandor. Reaching her is the real challenge: the route requires three pieces of an old map, a repaired ship, a captain willing to sail through the reefs, and protection against dragon fire.

Requirements

Required quests and quest points

  • 32 Quest Points. You must be able to enter the Champions' Guild to speak to the Guildmaster and begin the quest.
  • No specific prerequisite quest. Any combination of period quests that gives the required 32 Quest Points is sufficient.

Required levels

  • No universal numeric skill requirement. You must, however, be capable of surviving Melzar's Maze and defeating Elvarg.
  • 33 Magic is required only if you choose to kill Wormbrain and use Telekinetic Grab on his map piece. It is not required if you buy the piece from him for 10,000 coins.

Required items

  • 2,000 coins — consumed when buying the Lady Lumbridge in Port Sarim.
  • 3 wooden planks — consumed while repairing the Lady Lumbridge.
  • 12 steel nails — consumed at four nails per plank. This is the pre-rework quantity used by the target-period route and build 254, not the later 90-nail version.
  • 1 hammer — required for the ship repairs; not consumed.
  • 1 Wizard's mind bomb — consumed by the magic door beneath Ice Mountain.
  • 1 silk — consumed by the same door.
  • 1 lobster pot — consumed by the same door.
  • 1 unfired bowl — consumed by the same door.
  • Wormbrain route — choose one: bring 10,000 coins to buy his map piece, or use combat through the jail bars and then cast Telekinetic Grab on the dropped piece. In build 254 that spell requires 33 Magic, 1 air rune and 1 law rune.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Maze key: Oziach gives this when you ask about the map piece hidden in Melzar's Maze.
  • Three map pieces: one comes from Melzar's Maze, one from the chest beneath Ice Mountain, and one from Wormbrain.
  • Crandor map: made by using one map piece on another once all three pieces are in your inventory.
  • Anti-dragon shield: obtained from the Duke of Lumbridge after Oziach tells you about dragon-fire protection. It is not consumed and should be equipped against Elvarg.
  • Coloured maze keys: dropped by the relevant enemies inside Melzar's Maze and consumed when their matching doors are opened.

Recommended

  • Your best practical combat gear and a large amount of food. The quest contains several mandatory fights in Melzar's Maze and the final Elvarg fight.
  • A one-handed weapon for Elvarg so the anti-dragon shield can stay equipped.
  • Enough spare coins that buying the Wormbrain piece is an option. The 10,000-coin route avoids making 33 Magic a requirement.
  • Open the Crandor-to-Karamja secret passage before fighting Elvarg. It gives you a much easier return route if you die or leave the island.

Before you start

The quest can be done in several trips, and the three map pieces may be collected in any order after Oziach has explained them. For a smooth run, have the four Oracle-door items, the ship-repair materials, 2,000 coins, combat supplies, and either 10,000 extra coins or the Telekinetic Grab requirements ready before beginning the long travel section.

Do not rely on modern Dragon Slayer guides for material quantities or the ending. The September-2004/pre-rework route uses 3 planks and 12 steel nails, and the quest finishes when Elvarg dies; there is no Elvarg-head hand-in to Oziach.

Detailed walkthrough

Starting the quest

  1. Enter the Champions' Guild. The guild is south-west of Varrock. You need at least 32 Quest Points to enter.
  2. Speak to the Guildmaster. Ask where you can get a Rune plate mail body. He tells you about his friend Oziach, who lives near Edgeville, and the quest begins.
  3. Visit Oziach west of Edgeville. Ask about buying Rune plate mail and tell him the Guildmaster sent you. Oziach says the armour is only for heroes and challenges you to kill Elvarg on Crandor.
  4. Ask Oziach for every piece of information. Ask where the dragon is, where all three map pieces are, and where to obtain protection from dragon breath. He identifies Melzar's Maze, the Oracle on Ice Mountain, a goblin thief, and the Duke of Lumbridge. When you ask about the first map piece, Oziach gives you the Maze key.

Map piece: the Oracle and the Dwarven Mine

  1. Speak to the Oracle on Ice Mountain. Ask about the lost map piece. Her riddle describes four objects: a drink used by a mage, worm string made into sheet, a small crustacean cage, and a bowl that has not seen heat.
  2. Bring the four answers. You need exactly 1 Wizard's mind bomb, 1 silk, 1 lobster pot and 1 unfired bowl.
  3. Enter the Dwarven Mine beneath Ice Mountain. Make your way to the magic door in the mine's north-eastern area. You must have spoken to the Oracle before the door will accept the items.
  4. Open the magic door. The four items glow and are consumed. The door then becomes passable for this quest state.
  5. Search the chest beyond it. Open and search the chest to receive one-third of the Crandor map.

Map piece: Wormbrain

  1. Go to Port Sarim jail. Oziach's clue points to a goblin from Goblin Village; General Bentnoze can identify the thief as Wormbrain, but that detour is not required once the quest has unlocked this branch.
  2. Choose how to obtain the map piece. Wormbrain is locked behind the jail bars, so there are two practical routes.
  3. Payment route: talk to Wormbrain about the map piece and agree to his price of 10,000 coins. He takes the coins and gives you the piece directly.
  4. Telekinetic Grab route: attack Wormbrain through the bars with a suitable ranged or magic attack. When he dies, the map piece drops inside the cell. Cast Telekinetic Grab on it; build 254 requires 33 Magic, 1 air rune and 1 law rune for the spell.

Map piece: Melzar's Maze

  1. Travel to Melzar's Maze north-west of Rimmington. Use the Maze key Oziach gave you on the entrance door.
  2. Get the red key. Kill the quest giant rat that drops a red key. Use it on the red door toward the north-west and continue upward.
  3. Get the orange key. On the ghost floor, kill the quest ghost that drops an orange key. Use it on the correct orange door among the eastern/north-eastern doors and continue through the maze.
  4. Get the yellow key. Kill the quest skeleton that drops a yellow key. Use it on the south-western yellow door, then follow the ladders downward.
  5. Get the blue key. Kill the quest zombie that drops a blue key. Use it on the north-western blue door.
  6. Defeat Melzar the Mad. Melzar drops the magenta key. Use it on the magenta door to continue.
  7. Defeat the Lesser demon. The demon drops the green key. Use it on the green door to reach the final chest.
  8. Search the chest. It contains the Melzar's Maze map piece. There is no Silverlight reward in this quest and no potion/equipment-matching puzzle; those were errors in the previous local draft.

Assemble the Crandor map

  1. Put all three map pieces in your inventory. You need the Melzar's Maze piece, the Dwarven Mine piece and the Wormbrain piece.
  2. Use one map piece on another. With all three present, they are joined into a single Crandor map showing a safe route through the reefs.

Get the anti-dragon shield

  1. Go to Lumbridge Castle. Find the Duke of Lumbridge upstairs.
  2. Ask for dragon-fire protection. Once Oziach has told you about the shield, a dialogue option appears asking for a shield that protects against dragon breath. The Duke gives you an anti-dragon shield.
  3. Keep the shield. Equip it for Elvarg. In build 254 it reduces Elvarg's dragon-fire maximum from 70 damage to 10, making it the single most important defensive item in the fight.

Buy and repair the Lady Lumbridge

  1. Speak to Klarense at Port Sarim. He is beside the damaged ship Lady Lumbridge. Ask to buy it and pay 2,000 coins.
  2. Board your ship and go below deck. Inspect the large hole in the hull.
  3. Repair the hull three times. Use a wooden plank on the hole while carrying a hammer and steel nails. Each plank consumes 4 steel nails. After 3 planks and 12 nails in total, the ship is seaworthy.

Recruit Ned and sail

  1. Speak to Ned in Draynor Village. Ask the old sailor whether he can take you to Crandor. He agrees if you can provide a seaworthy ship.
  2. Make sure the ship is repaired and the Crandor map is complete. Then use the Crandor map on Ned, or continue the Dragon Slayer conversation and hand it to him. He takes the map and agrees to meet you on the Lady Lumbridge.
  3. Return to Port Sarim and board the Lady Lumbridge. Find Ned aboard the ship and tell him you are ready.
  4. Sail to Crandor. The voyage ends badly: the Lady Lumbridge crashes against the rocks on Crandor, leaving you stranded on the island.

Crandor and Elvarg

  1. Leave the wreck and cross Crandor. Head toward the dungeon entrance and descend into the caves beneath the island.
  2. Open the secret Karamja passage before the boss. Find the hidden wall/door connecting the Crandor dungeon to the Karamja volcano dungeon. Open it from the Crandor side. The passage remains remembered and is your safest recovery route if you later need to return.
  3. Prepare at Elvarg's gates. Equip the anti-dragon shield, a one-handed weapon and your best armour, and heal before entering. Build 254's Elvarg is combat level 83; surviving period references disagree on the exact early-RS2 displayed level, so this number is presented as the build value.
  4. Enter and defeat Elvarg. Keep the anti-dragon shield equipped for the entire fight. Elvarg uses melee and dragon fire; bring enough food to handle repeated damage.
  5. Quest complete. In the pre-rework version represented by the September-2004 target and build 254, Elvarg's death completes the quest immediately and moves you out of the lair. You do not cut off her head and you do not return to Oziach or the Guildmaster for a final hand-in.

Combat and recovery notes

  • Melzar's Maze: build 254 uses level 6 Giant rat, level 19 Ghost, level 22 Skeleton, level 25 Zombie, level 43 Melzar the Mad and level 82 Lesser demon for the quest sequence. These are implementation values; the public route does not assume every one is independently proven for 7 September 2004.
  • Elvarg: build 254 uses combat level 83 and 120 Hitpoints. The anti-dragon shield reduces the build's dragon-fire maximum from 70 to 10. Do not remove it during the fight.
  • Lost Maze key: before the map is handed to Ned, Oziach can supply another Maze key if you have none in your inventory or bank.
  • Lost map pieces: while you are still in the map-gathering stage, the relevant chest/Wormbrain routes can supply missing pieces again. Recombine all three into a new Crandor map.
  • Lost anti-dragon shield: the Duke of Lumbridge can provide another when the Dragon Slayer shield dialogue is available and you are not already carrying or wearing one.
  • Death after reaching Crandor: if you opened the secret wall, return through the Karamja volcano dungeon. Build 254 also supports asking Ned to sail again, but the Lady Lumbridge is damaged by the crash and must be repaired again before another voyage.
  • Other players at Elvarg: build 254's completion logic requires the player's kill credit/finishing hit. If another player interferes with the shared boss, make sure your own character receives the kill before assuming the quest will complete.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 18,650 Strength experience.
  • 18,650 Defence experience.
  • Permission to wear Rune platebody armour, provided you also meet its normal 40 Defence requirement.
  • Permission to wear the green dragonhide body, provided you also have 40 Ranged and 40 Defence.
  • Access to Oziach's armour shop after completion. He stocks Rune platebodies and green dragonhide bodies.
  • Persistent Crandor/Karamja shortcut access once the secret wall has been discovered.
  • Dragon Slayer completion also satisfies one of the major prerequisites for Heroes' Quest.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Pre-rework ending: build 254 queues completion directly from Elvarg's death, awards 18,650 Strength XP and 18,650 Defence XP, sets the quest complete and moves the player out of the lair. There is no Elvarg-head item or post-kill Oziach hand-in. This also agrees with surviving descriptions of the original quest before the later Dragon Slayer rework.
  • Ship material quantity: the build consumes exactly 3 wooden planks and 12 steel nails, four nails per plank. Several pre-rework community guides independently preserve the same quantity. The modern/later 90-nail requirement is deliberately excluded from the historical walkthrough.
  • Wormbrain alternatives: build 254 lets the player buy the piece for exactly 10,000 coins. The alternative Telekinetic Grab spell is free-to-play, level 33 Magic and costs one air rune plus one law rune. This is why 33 Magic is not treated as a universal quest requirement.
  • Elvarg values: the build config uses combat level 83, 120 Hitpoints and a dragon-fire base maximum of 70. Wearing the anti-dragon shield subtracts 60 from that maximum. The exact displayed combat level in surviving early-RS2 references is inconsistent, so the public guide labels 83 as a build value rather than presenting it as independently settled September-2004 history.
  • Equipment unlocks: build 254 separately checks Dragon Slayer completion when equipping a Rune platebody or green dragonhide body. Rune platebody also needs 40 Defence; green dragonhide body needs 40 Ranged and 40 Defence.
  • Oziach shop: at normal base stock, build 254's shop formula starts a Rune platebody at 84,500 coins and a green dragonhide body at 10,140 coins. Shop prices change with stock, so those are implementation starting prices rather than fixed historical reward values.
  • Reconstructed dialogue: the Dragon Slayer source itself notes that the quest was later reworked and that portions of its dialogue were reconstructed from RuneScape Classic, archived guides and later video evidence. This audit therefore uses build code strongly for state, quantities and rewards, but does not treat every reconstructed dialogue line as direct proof of September-2004 wording.
