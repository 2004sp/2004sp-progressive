Fight Arena

Combat
Three mandatory arena enemies; build 254 displays levels 63, 44 and 137. General Khazard (112) is optional.

Lady Servil's husband and son have been kidnapped by General Khazard and forced into his Fight Arena. To rescue them, you must infiltrate the compound in a stolen Khazard uniform, get a prison guard drunk, free Jeremy Servil and then survive a sequence of arena battles. General Khazard himself attacks after the final mandatory fight, but defeating him is not required to complete the quest.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests, skill levels or Quest Point requirement.
  • You must be able to defeat the Khazard Ogre, Khazard Scorpion and Bouncer. Surviving 2004 sources disagree on their exact displayed combat levels; build 254 uses 63, 44 and 137 respectively.
  • General Khazard is not a required kill. Once Bouncer is dead, the Servils are released and you may escape rather than fight the General.

Required items

  • 5 coins — build 254 charges five coins for the Khali brew. You must first learn about the drink from the prison guard before the barman offers it.
  • Combat equipment and enough food for three fights in succession. The quest supplies the Khazard disguise and cell keys, but it does not supply your combat gear.

Recommended

  • Ranged or Magic equipment. Contemporary 2004 guidance specifically recommends fighting the required monsters from safe positions where possible.
  • Extra food and prayer supplies if Bouncer is dangerous for your combat level.
  • An emergency teleport is useful before the arena sequence, although the quest's scripted progression can restrict ordinary exits during the mandatory fights.

Before you free Jeremy

Do your final banking before unlocking Jeremy's cell. In build 254, freeing him advances the quest directly into the first arena encounter. You are then captured after the Ogre fight and sent back into the arena for the Scorpion and Bouncer, so this is not a good point to discover that your food or runes are still in the bank.

The exact September-2004 combat displays are not safely recoverable from the surviving guides: an April 2004 walkthrough records 55/44/132 for the mandatory enemies, while a July 2004 FAQ records 58/46/122. The walkthrough below therefore uses enemy names for the historical route and labels build-254 levels separately.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start with Lady Servil

  1. Travel south of Ardougne to the Fight Arena settlement and speak to Lady Servil near the north-west edge of the camp.
  2. Ask about her trouble and offer to help. She explains that General Khazard's men abducted her husband, Sir Justin Servil, and their son Jeremy Servil and intend to make them fight in the arena.

2. Steal a Khazard guard disguise

  1. Head east to the north-easternmost house in the Fight Arena settlement.
  2. Open and search the guard chest inside. You receive a Khazard helmet and Khazard armour.
  3. Wear both pieces. The disguise is what lets you move through the guarded prison area without immediately being treated as an outsider.

3. Find Jeremy and learn who has the keys

  1. Go south to the prison building while wearing the Khazard helmet and armour.
  2. Find Jeremy Servil in the cells on the eastern side and speak to him.
  3. Tell him the uniform is a disguise and that you are there to help. Jeremy explains that a guard keeps hold of the cell keys.

4. Get the guard drunk

  1. Find the prison guard who complains that he wants a decent drink. Continue the conversation until he mentions Khali brew. Keep the Khazard disguise equipped.
  2. Leave the prison and go to the nearby Fight Arena bar west of the jail.
  3. Talk to the Khazard barman and buy one Khali brew. Build 254 charges 5 coins, and the option only appears after the relevant guard conversation.
  4. Return to the guard and speak to him with the Khali brew in your inventory. He drinks it, becomes drunk and hands over the Khazard cell keys.

5. Prepare, then free Jeremy

  1. Before touching Jeremy's cell door, equip the combat gear you intend to use and make sure your food, runes or ammunition are ready.
  2. Return to Jeremy and use the Khazard cell keys on his cell door, or speak to him while carrying the keys.
  3. Jeremy is freed and runs ahead because his father has already been taken into the arena. Build 254 moves the quest straight into the first fight sequence.

6. Save Justin from the Khazard Ogre

  1. Inside the arena, Jeremy points out that his father is in immediate danger.
  2. Kill the Khazard Ogre attacking Justin Servil. Build 254 displays the Ogre as level 63; surviving 2004 walkthroughs give lower and conflicting values.
  3. Continue the Servil/General dialogue after the Ogre dies. General Khazard claims the two men as his property and agrees to release them only if you stay and fight for him.
  4. You are taken to a prison cell.

7. Talk to Hengrad and fight the Khazard Scorpion

  1. Speak to Hengrad in the cell. He tells you that Khazard has kept him as a prisoner and fighter since childhood.
  2. A guard arrives and escorts you back into the arena.
  3. Kill the Khazard Scorpion. Build 254 displays it as level 44.

8. Defeat Bouncer

  1. After the Scorpion dies, General Khazard calls for his pet Bouncer.
  2. Kill Bouncer. Build 254 displays Bouncer as level 137. This is the final mandatory kill and the dangerous part of the quest for a low-level account.
  3. When Bouncer dies, Khazard is furious but keeps his agreement: the Servils are free to leave.

9. Escape General Khazard — or fight him if you want

  1. General Khazard now turns on you personally. Build 254 displays him as level 112.
  2. You do not need to kill him. Run out of the arena and return to Lady Servil once you can get clear.
  3. If you choose to stay and defeat him, the quest still completes normally. Build 254 has only this single optional Khazard fight; the later transformed second phase introduced by the 2009 quest rework is outside the September-2004 target.

10. Return to Lady Servil

  1. Go back to Lady Servil. This works whether you escaped General Khazard or killed him.
  2. She confirms that Justin and Jeremy are safe, thanks you for rescuing them and gives you the quest reward.

Combat encounters

  • Khazard Ogre: mandatory. Build 254 level 63.
  • Khazard Scorpion: mandatory. Build 254 level 44.
  • Bouncer: mandatory. Build 254 level 137 and the principal combat check.
  • General Khazard: optional. Build 254 level 112. You can leave once Bouncer is dead and still finish the quest.
  • Historical-level warning: the NPC level values above describe build 254, not claimed exact September-2004 displays. Surviving 2004 guides conflict strongly enough that the historical numbers should remain marked uncertain.

Useful notes and recovery

  • The barman will not sell Khali brew yet: return to the prison and finish the drink conversation with the correct guard first.
  • Lost the cell keys before freeing Jeremy: in build 254, the drunk guard can provide a spare set while you are still at the pre-arena quest stage and wearing the disguise.
  • Lost one or both Khazard armour pieces: the guard chest checks your inventory and worn equipment and can replace missing pieces. It does not check the bank, an implementation quirk worth remembering before deliberately fetching replacements.
  • Nothing happened after the Ogre: speak to Justin or Jeremy to continue the post-fight sequence if the automatic dialogue did not start.
  • Do not confuse later guides with the 2004 route. Sleeping guards, stealing keys from a table, the renamed Sammy Servil and General Khazard's transformed second phase belong to later revisions. Build 254 preserves Jeremy and the older direct key hand-over route.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 12,175 Attack XP.
  • 1,000 coins.
  • Thieving XP: build 254 awards 2,175 Thieving XP. A preserved April-2004 guide instead lists 3,375, while a July-2004 FAQ only gives a rough higher estimate. Because those period sources conflict, 2,175 is presented here as the implementation value rather than an unquestioned September-2004 historical figure.
  • You keep the Khazard helmet and Khazard armour obtained during the quest; build 254 does not remove them on completion.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: the build tracks the complete old route: start → obtain armour → learn about Khali brew → receive keys → free Jeremy/Ogre fight → jail → Scorpion → Bouncer → Servils freed → optional General kill → Lady Servil completion.
  • Five-coin brew: the Khazard barman script removes exactly 5 coins and gives one Khali brew. The purchase option is gated behind the earlier drunk-guard conversation.
  • Keys: after drinking the brew, the guard directly adds the Khazard cell keys to your inventory. The later "guard falls asleep, take the keys from the table" presentation is not how build 254 implements this stage.
  • Mandatory fights: Ogre, Scorpion and Bouncer deaths each advance the quest. Bouncer's death releases/spawns General Khazard and triggers the agreement that frees the Servils.
  • General Khazard: the build sets the Servils-free state before Khazard attacks. Lady Servil accepts either that state or the state reached by killing him, which proves the General is optional for quest completion. There is no transformed second phase.
  • Build combat displays: the quest NPC configs currently use 63 for the Ogre, 44 for the Scorpion, 137 for Bouncer and 112 for General Khazard. Several config comments explicitly say values were reconstructed from later OSRS data or guessed where the old game changed, so these numbers are implementation evidence rather than direct 2004 historical proof.
  • Reward: the completion script grants 1,000 coins, 12,175 Attack XP and 2,175 Thieving XP; the global quest constant supplies 2 Quest Points. The old local page incorrectly listed 12,175 coins and omitted both skill-XP rewards.
  • Historical Thieving-XP conflict: the preserved LostHQ guide entered in March 2004 and updated 19 April 2004 lists 3,375 Thieving XP. Build 254 and later legacy/Old School data use 2,175. No stronger pre-7-September-2004 source found during this audit resolves when or whether that value changed, so the discrepancy remains documented instead of being silently normalised.
