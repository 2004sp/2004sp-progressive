Ceril Carnillean wants a stolen suit of family armour recovered from a cult hiding south of Ardougne. When you confront Clivet at the cult's sewer entrance, he offers a second version of the story and a real choice: remain loyal to the Carnilleans and break the cult's operation, or join the Hazeel Cult and help resurrect Lord Hazeel. Both branches use the same five sewer valves and raft, but their objectives and endings are very different.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests, skill levels or Quest Point requirement.
  • No combat is required on the Hazeel Cult route.
  • If you side with Ceril, you must defeat Alomone. A guide last updated 8 July 2004 records him as level 13, and build 254 also displays level 13.

Required items

  • Nothing must be brought to start the quest.
  • On the cult route, Clivet supplies the poison and the Mark of Hazeel, while the mansion contains the chest key and Scroll of Hazeel.
  • On the Carnillean route, Alomone drops the stolen Carnillean armour when defeated.

Recommended

  • Weapon, armour and a little food if taking the Carnillean route. Alomone is a low-level opponent, but the hideout also contains cultists.
  • Several free inventory spaces for quest items, especially on the cult route.
  • Choose your branch deliberately. Clivet's decision is the defining choice of the quest; once you accept or reject his offer, the quest proceeds down that side.

Before you choose a side

The quest was designed around two different endings. Jagex's original release announcement explicitly advertised the choice between the Hazeel Cult and Ceril Carnillean. Neither route is a hidden failure state: both can complete the quest and award the main completion reward.

The walkthrough below gives the shared opening first, then separates the Carnillean route and Hazeel Cult route. The five-valve solution is the same whichever side you choose.

Shared opening

1. Speak to Ceril Carnillean

  1. Go to the Carnillean Mansion in southern Ardougne, west of Ardougne Zoo, and speak to Ceril Carnillean.
  2. Ask what is wrong. Ceril explains that strange cultists stole a suit of Carnillean family armour months ago and have broken into the house several more times.
  3. Agree to recover the armour. Ceril points you toward a cult hideout in the forest south of the mansion.

2. Find Clivet and make the branch choice

  1. Travel south from the mansion, cross the nearby bridge and find the cave entrance in the forest.
  2. Speak to Clivet, the cultist at the entrance.
  3. Let him explain the cult's account of Hazeel and the Carnillean family's history.
  4. You now choose:
    • Side with Ceril: refuse to join the cult. Clivet escapes on the sewer raft.
    • Side with the Hazeel Cult: agree to help. Clivet gives you poison and orders you to prove your loyalty by poisoning the Carnillean family's food.

Sewer-valve solution

Both routes eventually need the raft inside Clivet's cave. The raft only reaches the cult hideout when all five surface valves are set correctly. A contemporary guide last updated on 8 July 2004 gives the following locations and directions:

  1. Valve on the south wall of the Carnillean Mansion — turn RIGHT.
  2. Valve directly south of the bridge — turn RIGHT.
  3. Valve directly north of the cave entrance, by the fence — turn RIGHT.
  4. Valve south of Ardougne Zoo — turn LEFT.
  5. Valve reached by following the zoo fence south toward the iron rocks — turn RIGHT.

Once all five are correct, enter the cave and board the raft. Do not change the valves again unless you need to repair an incorrect setting.

Carnillean route

3A. Refuse Clivet and follow the raft

  1. When Clivet asks you to join the cult, refuse. He jumps onto the raft and disappears into the sewer system.
  2. Set the five surface valves using the solution above.
  3. Return to the cave and board the raft. With the water flow set correctly, it carries you to the cult hideout.

4A. Defeat Alomone and recover the armour

  1. Find Alomone, leader of the Hazeel Cult, and speak to him.
  2. Demand the stolen Carnillean armour. The confrontation turns into combat.
  3. Kill Alomone and take the Carnillean armour he drops. The July 2004 guide and build 254 both record/display him as level 13.
  4. During this stage you also learn that Butler Jones is working with the cult.

5A. Return the armour and accuse Jones

  1. Use the raft to leave the hideout and return to Ceril Carnillean.
  2. Give Ceril his armour and tell him that Jones is involved.
  3. Ceril refuses to accept the accusation without evidence. In build 254 this produces a deliberately false-looking completion screen and only 5 coins; the quest is not actually finished yet.

6A. Search Jones' cupboard

  1. Go upstairs in the Carnillean Mansion and find Butler Jones' cupboard.
  2. Open it and search the cupboard. You uncover poison and a cult amulet linking Jones to the Hazeel Cult.
  3. The evidence exposes Jones. The historical route finishes by presenting the proof to Ceril; in build 254 the search itself launches the Ceril/Jones/Guard confrontation and then completes the quest.

Hazeel Cult route

3B. Poison the Carnillean meal

  1. Accept Clivet's offer. He gives you poison.
  2. Return to the Carnillean Mansion and climb down into the basement/kitchen area.
  3. Use the poison on the cooking range/stove. The poison is consumed.
  4. The attempt does not kill a Carnillean: the family's dog Scruffy eats the poisoned meal instead.

4B. Get the Mark of Hazeel and reach the hideout

  1. Return to Clivet. He accepts your attempt as proof of loyalty and gives you the Mark of Hazeel, described in period guidance as a golden amulet/symbol.
  2. Wear the mark for the period route and set the five sewer valves using the solution above.
  3. Enter the cave and take the raft to the cult hideout.

5B. Speak to Alomone

  1. Find Alomone and speak to him. Because you joined the cult, you do not need to fight him.
  2. He explains that the ritual to restore Hazeel requires a powerful Scroll of Hazeel hidden somewhere inside the Carnillean Mansion.
  3. He also confirms that Butler Jones is the cult's agent in the house.

6B. Find the chest key and hidden room

  1. Take the raft back out and return to the Carnillean Mansion. A contemporary 2004 route keeps the Mark of Hazeel equipped and speaks to Jones while searching the house.
  2. Go down to the basement and search the storage crates until you obtain the old chest key.
  3. Go upstairs to Jones' area and knock at the suspicious wall to reveal the hidden passage.
  4. Follow the secret route to the locked chest.
  5. Use the chest key on the chest, then search it to obtain the Scroll of Hazeel.

7B. Resurrect Hazeel

  1. Return to Clivet's cave. Do not disturb the valve settings.
  2. Ride the raft back to the cult hideout and speak to Alomone with the scroll.
  3. Alomone performs the restoration ritual. Hazeel appears from the coffin and thanks you for making his return possible.
  4. The July 2004 guide and build 254 display Hazeel as level 296, but this is a story/cutscene appearance: you do not fight Hazeel.
  5. Hazeel gives you ancient coins and leaves to attend to his affairs in the north. The quest completes.

Combat and hazards

  • Carnillean route: Alomone is the only required kill. Contemporary July-2004 guidance and build 254 both give him level 13.
  • Hazeel Cult route: no quest-state combat is required.
  • Hazeel: level 296 is a displayed story NPC value in the cult ending, not a boss fight.
  • The sewer hideout contains other cultists. On the anti-cult route they can be hostile, so low-level players should still bring basic food.

Useful notes and recovery

  • The raft stops early: one or more valves are wrong. Build 254 checks the five valve states in sequence and can strand the raft on intermediate sewer islands depending on how many early settings are correct.
  • Lost the cult-route poison before using it: build 254 lets Clivet replace it during the poisoning stage if you do not have one in your inventory.
  • Do not mistake the 5-coin screen for completion: on the build's Carnillean route, returning the armour is intentionally only a fake ending. You still need evidence from Jones' cupboard.
  • The Mark of Hazeel is not a fence unlock. The previous local guide's claim that it grants access to a stolen-goods buyer is unsupported and has been removed.
  • Optional period exploit: the July 2004 guide records a method of dropping the first Carnillean armour and killing Alomone again to obtain a spare before turning one set in. This is not a normal quest reward and should not be relied upon as build-254 behaviour.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 2,000 coins as the main completion reward.
  • Thieving XP is historically disputed. The preserved RuneHQ/LostHQ guide last updated 8 July 2004 lists 1,000 Thieving XP, while build 254 awards 1,500 Thieving XP and later legacy references also use 1,500. No stronger pre-7-September-2004 evidence found during this audit proves which value was live on the target date, so the disagreement is left explicit.
  • Hazeel Cult route: you retain the Mark of Hazeel in build 254; period guidance also has the player carrying the symbol through the ending.
  • Carnillean route: the armour is normally returned to Ceril. A contemporary guide documents an extra-armour exploit, but this is not treated as an intended reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Branch state: build 254 records the choice made with Clivet. The good side proceeds through Alomone's armour drop and the evidence against Jones; the evil side proceeds through poison, Mark of Hazeel, Scroll of Hazeel and the resurrection ceremony.
  • Valve logic: the raft only reaches the hideout after all five valve bits are correct. Four configured valves are correct when turned right and one (`sewervalve3`) is correct when turned left. The public walkthrough uses the July-2004 guide's physical-location directions rather than assuming the internal config index is the player's geographic numbering.
  • Carnillean route: killing Alomone while on the good side drops Carnillean armour. Giving it to Ceril removes the armour, awards 5 coins and shows a fake “kind of completed” screen. Searching Jones' cupboard at the later stage triggers the real exposure scene and completion.
  • Cult route: using the supplied poison on the Carnillean range consumes it; Clivet later gives the mark; Alomone advances the quest to the scroll search; a secret wall in the mansion becomes usable; a crate supplies the chest key; the hidden chest supplies the Scroll of Hazeel; Alomone then runs the resurrection sequence.
  • Build reward: the real completion script gives 2,000 coins, 1,500 Thieving XP (`stat_advance(thieving, 15000)`) and 1 Quest Point. Therefore the build's Carnillean route can total 2,005 coins because of the earlier five-coin fake payment, while the cult route receives the normal 2,000-coin completion amount.
  • Quest items: the completion script deletes a Scroll of Hazeel from inventory but does not remove the Mark of Hazeel. The chest-unlock script also does not consume the chest key, so build 254 can leave quest-item leftovers that should not be mistaken for historical reward design.
  • Reconstruction warning: the build source itself notes that Hazeel Cult was heavily changed in later Old School RuneScape and that parts of the reconstruction rely on older videos, RuneScape Classic material and later references. Build dialogue and NPC config values are therefore implementation evidence, not automatically direct proof of every September-2004 detail.
