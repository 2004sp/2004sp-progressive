Edmond's daughter Elena crossed into quarantined West Ardougne to help plague victims and has not returned. Edmond asks you to help him tunnel beneath the city wall, find Elena and bring her home. At this point in the 2004 storyline, the supposed plague is still treated as a real threat; the investigation into what is actually happening continues in Biohazard.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • No skill-level requirement. The 2,425 Mining XP is a reward, not a Mining requirement.
  • No mandatory combat. Any hostile creatures encountered while collecting supplies can be avoided; there is no quest fight in West Ardougne.

Required items

  • 1 dwellberries. Alrena consumes them to finish the gas mask. The period route points players to McGrubor's Wood to the north.
  • 4 bucketfuls of water. Four separate uses are required to soften Edmond's garden soil. Bringing four filled buckets is fastest, although empty buckets can be refilled.
  • 1 spade. Used after the fourth bucketful of water; it is not consumed.
  • 1 rope. Consumed when tied to the sewer-pipe grill.
  • 1 bucket of milk. Consumed while making Bravek's hangover cure.
  • 1 chocolate bar and a pestle and mortar to make chocolate dust. If you already have chocolate dust, the build accepts that directly.
  • 1 snape grass. Added to the chocolate milk to finish the hangover cure.

Obtained during the quest

  • Gas mask from Alrena after giving her the dwellberries. A spare can be taken from the cupboard after she prepares it.
  • Picture of Elena from the table in Edmond and Alrena's house. Take it before entering West Ardougne; Jethick needs it to identify Elena.
  • Turnip-growing book from Jethick, used to gain entry to the Rehnison house.
  • Scruffy note from Bravek, containing the hangover-cure recipe.
  • Warrant from Bravek after curing his hangover and explaining that the Mourners will not listen.
  • Small key from a barrel inside the plague house, used to unlock Elena's cell.

Recommended

  • Bring all external supplies before starting the tunnel. The quest has no bank-sensitive stage, but this avoids repeated trips between Ardougne and supply locations.
  • Take Elena's picture before you descend. Without it, Jethick cannot identify her and the investigation stalls.
  • Keep the gas mask equipped when using the sewer pipe. Build 254 explicitly checks the head slot before allowing passage into West Ardougne.

Before you start

Gather dwellberries, four bucketfuls of water, a spade, rope, bucket of milk, chocolate bar, pestle and mortar, and snape grass. The hangover cure can be prepared later when Bravek gives you the recipe. No quest, skill or combat level is required.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Edmond and Alrena

  1. Find Edmond in his garden in East Ardougne, north of Ardougne Castle and immediately east of the wall around West Ardougne.
  2. Ask what has happened to his daughter and agree to help find Elena. Edmond explains that she crossed into West Ardougne to help plague victims and has not returned.
  3. Speak to Alrena, Edmond's wife. Give her 1 dwellberries; she crushes them into a paste and gives you a gas mask.
  4. Inside their house, take the picture of Elena from the table. Keep it in your inventory for Jethick.

2. Soften the garden and enter the sewer

  1. Speak to Edmond again after receiving the gas mask. He explains that the planned route into West Ardougne is through the sewers beneath his garden.
  2. Use 4 bucketfuls of water on the mud patch in the garden. Each use empties one bucket; after the fourth, the soil is soft enough to dig.
  3. Use the spade on the softened mud patch. The ground collapses and you fall into the Ardougne sewer; Edmond follows.

3. Remove the sewer-pipe grill

  1. Follow the sewer south-west to the large pipe leading toward West Ardougne.
  2. Try the grill. It is too secure to pull off alone.
  3. Use 1 rope on the grill. The rope is consumed when you tie it in place.
  4. Speak to Edmond. Together you pull the grill loose.
  5. Wear the gas mask in your head slot, then climb through the sewer pipe into West Ardougne.

4. Ask Jethick about Elena

  1. Once inside West Ardougne, find Jethick near the town square.
  2. Tell him you are looking for a woman from East Ardougne and name Elena.
  3. When he asks what she looks like, the picture of Elena lets him recognise her.
  4. Jethick says Elena had been staying with the Rehnison family in the small timber house on the north side of the city. He also gives you a borrowed book to return to them.

5. Question the Rehnison family

  1. Go north to the Rehnisons' timber house and try the door.
  2. Explain that you are a friend of Jethick's and are returning his book. Ted lets you in and takes the book.
  3. Speak to Ted or Martha Rehnison. They explain that Elena had been staying with them but disappeared while preparing to leave.
  4. Go upstairs and speak to their daughter Milli. She saw men seize Elena and drag her into a mossy, windowless building in the south-east corner of West Ardougne.

6. Learn how to enter the plague house

  1. Go to the south-east plague house and try its western door. A Mourner warns you away because the black cross marks the building as plague-infected.
  2. Explain that you believe a kidnap victim is inside and insist on checking. Ask how to get clearance.
  3. The Mourner tells you that clearance would have to come from the Head Mourner or Bravek, the city warder.

7. Get access to Bravek

  1. Go to the large Civic Office on the north side of the town square.
  2. Speak to the Clerk. Ask who is through the inner door and press the point that the matter is urgent.
  3. The Clerk calls to Bravek and grants access to his room.
  4. Build-254 quirk: Bravek must be close enough for the Clerk's call to find him. If the Clerk calls out but the quest does not advance, wait until Bravek is nearer the door and try the urgent dialogue again.

8. Make Bravek's hangover cure

  1. Speak to Bravek. Tell him the matter is important, then ask what is in his hangover cure.
  2. He gives you a scruffy note. The recipe is: bucket of milk, ground chocolate, then snape grass.
  3. If you brought a chocolate bar, use a pestle and mortar on it to make chocolate dust.
  4. Use the chocolate dust on the bucket of milk to make chocolate milk.
  5. Use snape grass on the chocolate milk to make the hangover cure.
  6. Give the cure to Bravek. When he asks what you wanted, explain that the Mourners will not listen to you. He gives you a warrant to enter the plague house.

9. Sneak into the plague house

  1. Return to the plague house in the south-east corner and use the western door again.
  2. Present Bravek's warrant to the Mourner.
  3. The Mourner turns away to consult the Head Mourner. Use the opening to slip inside the building.

10. Find the key and free Elena

  1. Inside the plague house, search the barrel near the staircase to obtain a small key.
  2. Go downstairs. Elena is being held behind a locked door.
  3. Use the small key on the door and speak to Elena.
  4. Elena confirms that she has no plague symptoms and says the infected-house story appears to have been a cover for her kidnapping. She leaves for East Ardougne and tells you to see Edmond for your reward.

11. Return to Edmond

  1. Return to the central manhole in West Ardougne and retrace the sewer route back toward Edmond's garden.
  2. Speak to Edmond. Elena has already returned safely.
  3. Edmond thanks you and the quest completes immediately.

Combat and hazards

  • No quest combat: no guard, Mourner or plague creature needs to be fought at any stage.
  • McGrubor's Wood: if you gather your own dwellberries there, hostile guard dogs make the supply run more dangerous than the quest itself. The objective is simply to obtain the berries, not to kill anything.
  • Gas-mask check: build 254 will not let you pass through the sewer pipe into West Ardougne unless the gas mask is actually worn.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the gas mask: after Alrena makes your first one, a spare is available from the cupboard in Edmond and Alrena's house during the quest.
  • Forgot Elena's picture: return to the house in East Ardougne and take it from the table before trying Jethick again.
  • Bravek will not answer the Clerk: in build 254 the Clerk only advances the quest if Bravek is found within roughly seven tiles. Wait for him to move close enough, then repeat the urgent dialogue.
  • Lost the warrant: after Bravek has been cured, speaking to him again can re-open the permission dialogue and replace the warrant when you no longer have one.
  • Lost the small key: search the plague-house barrel again while you are still on the rescue stage.
  • Do not look for the Biohazard reveal here. Plague City ends with Elena safely home; the wider truth about the plague belongs to the sequel, Biohazard.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 2,425 Mining XP.
  • Ardougne teleport scroll. Reading it permanently teaches the Ardougne Teleport spell.
  • Gas mask retained. The mask obtained from Alrena remains useful for the West Ardougne route.
  • Story progression: Plague City is the prerequisite for Biohazard.

The scroll can be read without 51 Magic; casting Ardougne Teleport requires 51 Magic. In build 254 the spell also requires 2 water runes and 2 law runes.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest requirements: the build-254 journal explicitly states that there are no quest requirements, matching the period official manual and August-2004 guide.
  • Gas mask: Alrena consumes exactly 1 dwellberries and gives one gas mask. The journal records a spare in the cupboard. The sewer pipe checks the worn head slot before allowing entry to West Ardougne.
  • Garden route: the mud-patch script requires exactly 4 bucketfuls of water; every use returns an empty bucket. A spade only breaks through once the fourth water stage has been reached.
  • Sewer grill: using rope on the grill consumes 1 rope; Edmond's next dialogue removes the obstruction and opens the pipe route.
  • Jethick / Rehnisons: Jethick only identifies Elena when the picture is present. He gives the borrowed book, and Ted consumes it as the condition for letting the player into the Rehnison house.
  • Civic Office proximity: the Clerk's script calls npc_find for Bravek within 7 tiles before advancing the state. The August-2004 guide independently warns players to wait until Bravek is close enough.
  • Hangover cure: build 254 uses the period recipe exactly: chocolate dust + bucket of milk, then snape grass. There is no Cooking or Herblore level check on these quest-item combinations.
  • Plague-house entry: the west door is the scripted quest entrance. With the warrant and the nearby Mourner present, the door sequence lets the player sneak inside while the Mourner turns away.
  • Small key: the plague-house barrel gives the cell key during the post-Bravek stage and can replace it while that stage remains active.
  • Completion: Edmond queues completion after Elena is freed. The build removes temporary quest items, adds 1 Ardougne teleport scroll, awards 2,425 Mining XP, and the global quest constant awards 1 Quest Point.
  • Teleport scroll: reading it after quest completion consumes the scroll and changes the quest state to the learned-scroll state. Build 254's spell data requires 51 Magic, 2 water runes and 2 law runes.
  • Scroll recovery: before the scroll has been read, Edmond can replace a lost copy if none is in the player's inventory or bank. Once learned, the scroll is intentionally gone.
