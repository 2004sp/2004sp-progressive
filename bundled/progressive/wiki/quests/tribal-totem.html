Lord Handelmort of Ardougne has taken a sacred totem from the Rantuki tribe and placed it in his private collection. Kangai Mau wants it stolen back. The job is a short burglary built around misdirecting Wizard Cromperty's experimental teleport block, discovering Handelmort's security code, and using 21 Thieving to spot a trapped staircase inside the mansion.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • 21 Thieving. Jagex's 30 April 2002 release announcement explicitly says level 21 is needed to complete the quest.
  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • No mandatory combat. Guard dogs are present around Handelmort Mansion, but no monster kill advances the quest.

Items and preparation

Required items

  • None brought from outside the quest. The address label, guide book and tribal totem are obtained during the route.

Obtained during the quest

  • Address label. Peeled from a parcel addressed to Lord Handelmort at the R.P.D.T. depot.
  • Guide book. Optional clue item whose Ardougne history section gives Lord Handelmort's full name: Lord Francis Kurt Handelmort.
  • Tribal Totem. Recovered from the upstairs chest in Handelmort Mansion and handed to Kangai Mau.

Recommended

  • Travel money if you are using the boats between Karamja and Ardougne rather than another period-appropriate route.
  • A little food if your Hitpoints are low. There is no required fight, but a failed stair trap in build 254 damages you and drops you into Ardougne Sewers.
  • Several free inventory spaces for the address label, guide book if you take it, the totem and the five swordfish reward.
Reward evidence conflict: the surviving RuneHQ/LostHQ guide last updated 2 August 2004 lists 1,250 Thieving XP. Build 254 awards 1,775 Thieving XP, which is also the value preserved by later versions. Because no stronger target-date source found in this audit settles when or why the values diverged, the historical XP is left explicitly disputed instead of silently replacing the August-2004 figure with the build value.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Accept Kangai Mau's burglary

  1. Travel to Brimhaven on Karamja and enter The Shrimp & Parrot.
  2. Speak to Kangai Mau of the Rantuki tribe.
  3. Ask about his mission or say that you are looking for adventure.
  4. He explains that Lord Handelmort, an Ardougne explorer and collector, took the Rantuki tribal totem and placed it in his house.
  5. Agree to get the totem back. There are no further level or item checks at the start.

2. Learn how Handelmort's mansion is protected

  1. Travel to East Ardougne and find Handelmort Mansion just west of the marketplace.
  2. Speak to Horacio Dobson, Lord Handelmort's gardener, outside the mansion.
  3. Ask who he is, then ask whether he gardens around the back of the house as well.
  4. Horacio lets slip that the security-door combination is Lord Handelmort's middle name.

This conversation is the intended clue, but build 254 does not store a separate Horacio quest flag. If you already know the code, the later lock will still accept it.

3. Redirect Wizard Cromperty's teleport block

  1. Find Wizard Cromperty in his blue-roofed building in north-eastern East Ardougne.
  2. Ask what he has invented. He describes a teleportation block that lets him teleport something directly to the location of a matching block.
  3. Ask to be teleported and accept the risk. Before the parcel has been redirected, Cromperty sends you to the R.P.D.T. depot near Ardougne's southern side.
  4. At the depot, investigate the crate addressed to Lord Handelmort. Peel off its Address label.
  5. Find the crate containing Cromperty's teleport block, originally labelled for the Wizards' Tower in Misthalin.
  6. Use the Address label on the teleport-block crate. You cover the Wizards' Tower address with Handelmort Mansion's address.
  7. Speak to an R.P.D.T. employee and ask when the crate is going to be delivered. The employee agrees to deliver it immediately.

4. Discover the door code: KURT

If you want to solve the clue rather than use the answer directly, pick up the Guide book from a nearby Ardougne house and read its historical section. It names the mansion's owner as Lord Francis Kurt Handelmort. Combined with Horacio's clue, the security code is therefore:

K — U — R — T

The surviving guide last updated 2 August 2004 explicitly warns that the RuneScape 2 password is KURT, not the older RuneScape Classic password BRAD.

5. Teleport into Handelmort Mansion

  1. Return to Wizard Cromperty.
  2. Ask about his invention again, ask to be teleported and accept.
  3. Because the R.P.D.T. has now delivered the relabelled block, you arrive inside Handelmort Mansion.
  4. Move through the interior to the combination-locked western door.
  5. Set the four letters to KURT and submit the combination. A wrong combination does not consume anything; simply try again.

6. Disarm the trapped stairs

  1. Beyond the combination door, right-click the stairs and choose Investigate before climbing them.
  2. With at least 21 Thieving, you identify the trap and remember its position.
  3. Now climb the stairs safely.

Do not skip Investigate. In build 254, climbing before finding the trap drops you through the floor into Ardougne Sewers and damages you. You can climb back out and have Cromperty teleport you into the mansion again.

7. Recover the Tribal Totem

  1. On the upper floor, move into the room containing the quest chest.
  2. Open the chest, then search it.
  3. You receive the Tribal Totem.
  4. Leave the mansion and return to Brimhaven.

8. Return the totem to Kangai Mau

  1. Speak to Kangai Mau in The Shrimp & Parrot while carrying the Tribal Totem.
  2. Tell him you recovered it.
  3. He takes the totem and rewards you with 1 Quest Point, five cooked swordfish and the quest's Thieving XP reward.

Failure and recovery

  • Lost address label: before the teleport crate has been relabelled, build 254 lets you investigate the Handelmort parcel again and take another label if you no longer have one.
  • Stair trap triggered: the trap drops you into Ardougne Sewers. Exit the sewer, return to Cromperty and teleport back into the mansion. Once build 254 has recorded that you investigated the stairs, the trap remains disabled for you.
  • Lost Tribal Totem: before completion, the mansion chest can supply another totem when none is in your inventory or bank.
  • No combat recovery state: there is no boss or mandatory NPC kill to repeat.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 5 cooked swordfish. Both the August-2004 guide and build 254 support this reward.
  • Thieving XP — disputed historically. The surviving guide last updated 2 August 2004 says 1,250 XP; build 254 awards 1,775 XP. The public guide does not treat either number as conclusively proven for 7 September 2004 without stronger period evidence.
  • No permanent target-period unlock is asserted. The quest's core reward is the Quest Point, Thieving experience and swordfish.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state: %totemquest runs through not started, started, crate marked, crate delivered, teleported and complete.
  • Journal requirement: totem_journal.rs2 lists exactly Level 21 Thieving and starts the quest with Kangai Mau in The Shrimp & Parrot.
  • Address label: quest_totem.rs2 gives the label from the Handelmort parcel while the quest is active, then consumes it when used on Cromperty's teleport crate.
  • R.P.D.T. delivery: the employee advances the state only after the teleport crate has been relabelled. Cromperty then sends the player to the mansion instead of the depot.
  • Door code: the lock directly checks the four stored letter positions for KURT. Horacio's dialogue says the combination is Handelmort's middle name, and the Ardougne guide book gives the full name Lord Francis Kurt Handelmort.
  • Stair requirement: the Investigate option checks stat(thieving) < 21. On success it permanently records the trap as known for that character.
  • Trap failure: climbing without disarming it teleports the player into Ardougne Sewers and deals roughly 20% of current Hitpoints plus 1 in build 254.
  • Totem recovery: the chest gives a totem whenever the player has none in inventory or bank, allowing replacement before hand-in.
  • Completion: Kangai Mau removes the totem only from the teleported quest state and queues the reward.
  • Build reward: completion grants 1,775 Thieving XP, 5 swordfish and 1 Quest Point. This differs from the 1,250 Thieving XP recorded by the August-2004 RuneHQ/LostHQ guide.
  • RS2 availability: Jagex's 21 January 2004 update explicitly listed Tribal Totem among the quests uploaded to RuneScape 2 before the March launch, so the Ardougne RS2 route is safely inside the September cutoff.
