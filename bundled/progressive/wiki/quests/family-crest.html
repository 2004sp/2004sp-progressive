Dimintheis of the Fitzharmon family has lost his estate because he can no longer produce the family crest. His three sons — Caleb, Avan and Johnathon — split the crest between them after arguing over their inheritance. You must find all three brothers, earn or recover their fragments, restore the crest and return it to their father.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • 40 Mining — needed to mine the two pieces of perfect gold ore.
  • 40 Smithing — needed to smelt perfect gold ore into bars.
  • 40 Crafting — the ruby necklace is the highest Crafting check in Avan's section.
  • 59 Magic — needed for Fire Blast, the highest of the four elemental Blast spells.
  • You must be able to survive the perfect-gold mine and defeat Chronozon. His exact September-2004 combat display is disputed by surviving period material; build 254 displays level 170.

Required items

  • 1 cooked shrimp, 1 cooked salmon, 1 cooked tuna, 1 cooked bass and 1 cooked swordfish — all five are consumed by Caleb. You may buy them; Fishing and Cooking levels are not quest requirements.
  • 1 pickaxe you can use — needed to mine the perfect gold and not consumed.
  • 2 cut rubies — one for the ring and one for the necklace; both are consumed.
  • 1 ring mould and 1 necklace mould — needed at a furnace and not consumed.
  • 1 regular antipoison/cure-poison potion — talk to Johnathon first, then use the potion on him. Build 254 accepts the normal 1-, 2-, 3- or 4-dose antipoison items and consumes the item used.
  • Runes for Wind Blast, Water Blast, Earth Blast and Fire Blast. With no elemental staff and no splashes, one cast of each costs 4 death runes, 13 air runes, 3 water runes, 4 earth runes and 5 fire runes in total. Bring extra runes because only successful hits weaken Chronozon.

Recommended

  • Good armour, food and a combat weapon for the hellhounds in the perfect-gold mine and for the Wilderness Dungeon.
  • A second antipoison for yourself. Poison spiders are encountered on the route to Chronozon.
  • Extra Blast-spell runes. Splashes consume runes but do not count toward Chronozon's four required elemental hits.
  • Prayer supplies if the dungeon enemies or Chronozon are dangerous for your combat level.
  • Bank between the Avan and Johnathon sections if needed. The quest does not require you to carry every item for every stage at once.

Before you start

The quest covers a large part of the members' world: Varrock, Catherby, Al Kharid, the Dwarven Mine, a dungeon east of Ardougne, the Jolly Boar Inn and the members' Wilderness Dungeon beneath Edgeville. The August-2004 walkthrough classed the quest as long, so preparing the stage-specific items before each trip saves considerable backtracking.

The three brothers must effectively be progressed in order. In particular, after Caleb gives you his fragment, keep talking to him until he tells you that Avan is treasure-hunting in the desert; otherwise the Gem Trader does not offer the Family Crest dialogue in build 254.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Dimintheis in Varrock

  1. Go to the small house in south-east Varrock, close to the east bank, and speak to Dimintheis.
  2. Ask why a nobleman is living there and continue the conversation about his family crest. He explains that without the crest he cannot prove his family's rights to its estate.
  3. Agree to help. Dimintheis tells you that his son Caleb was last heard of working as a chef beyond White Wolf Mountain.

2. Caleb — bring the five cooked fish

  1. Travel to Catherby. Find Caleb, the chef in a house just north-east of the bank.
  2. Ask whether he is Caleb Fitzharmon and tell him that his father wants the family crest restored. Caleb explains that he and his two brothers split the crest into three pieces.
  3. Caleb will trade his fragment for the ingredients for his fish salad: one cooked shrimp, salmon, tuna, bass and swordfish.
  4. Speak to Caleb with all five cooked fish in your inventory. He removes one of each and hands you a crest fragment.
  5. Do not leave before getting the next clue. Ask what happened to the rest of the crest and continue asking about his brothers until Caleb tells you that Avan was searching for treasure or gold in the desert.

3. Find Avan through the Al Kharid Gem Trader

  1. Go to the Gem Trader in Al Kharid. Ask about Avan Fitzharmon.
  2. The trader describes a nobleman in a yellow cape who headed into the desert toward the gold and scorpions.
  3. Go north of Al Kharid to the Scorpion Pit/Chasm mining area. Find the well-dressed Man near the gold rocks and speak to him. In build 254 his visible NPC name remains simply Man; dialogue reveals that he is Avan Fitzharmon.
  4. Avan wants a ruby ring and ruby necklace made from exceptionally pure "perfect gold". He directs you toward a dwarf named Boot for a clue to the gold's location.

4. Ask Boot about perfect gold

  1. Enter the Dwarven Mine beneath Ice Mountain, north of Falador.
  2. Find Boot the Dwarf in the western/south-western part of the mine and ask him about very high-quality gold.
  3. Boot says the best gold he knows of is underground east of Ardougne and warns that it is difficult to reach.

5. Solve the perfect-gold lever dungeon

  1. Travel to East Ardougne and head south-east to the dungeon entrance east of the city. Descend the ladder.
  2. The perfect-gold room is protected by doors controlled by three levers: one on the north wall, one in the south room, and one inside the north room.
  3. Pull the north-wall lever up.
  4. Enter the south room and pull its lever up.
  5. Return to the north-wall lever and pull it down.
  6. Enter the north room and pull the lever inside it up.
  7. Leave the north room and pull the north-wall lever up again.
  8. Return to the south room and pull its lever down. The final route into the perfect-gold cage is now unlocked.
  9. Enter the cage and mine two perfect gold ores. The August-2004 route records level-122 hellhounds in this mine. Fighting them is not a quest objective; if practical, tank or avoid them long enough to mine both ores.

6. Make Avan's perfect-gold jewellery

  1. Take the two perfect gold ores to a furnace. Smelt both into perfect gold bars. This requires 40 Smithing.
  2. Have two cut rubies, the ring mould and necklace mould with you.
  3. Use the perfect gold bars at the furnace to make one ruby ring and one ruby necklace. The necklace requires 40 Crafting; the ring requires 34.
  4. Return to Avan in the Scorpion Pit. Give him both perfect-gold ruby items. He gives you the second crest fragment and tells you about his brother Johnathon, a demon hunter recovering at a tavern near the Wilderness.

7. Cure Johnathon at the Jolly Boar Inn

  1. Go to the Jolly Boar Inn north-east of Varrock and climb upstairs. Find Johnathon Fitzharmon in one of the upstairs rooms.
  2. Talk to Johnathon first. He is badly poisoned and can barely speak.
  3. Use a regular antipoison/cure-poison potion on Johnathon. He recovers and explains that he lost his crest fragment while fighting Chronozon the blood demon.
  4. Ask how to defeat Chronozon. Johnathon tells you that the demon must be struck by each of the four elemental Blast spells before it can be killed.

8. Defeat Chronozon

  1. Prepare for the Wilderness Dungeon. Bring combat equipment, food, poison protection and enough runes for repeated Blast casts.
  2. Enter Edgeville Dungeon and travel into its members' Wilderness section. The period route passes thugs, chaos druids, skeletons, black demons and poison spiders before reaching Chronozon's chamber near the Earth Obelisk.
  3. Fight Chronozon and successfully hit him with Wind Blast, Water Blast, Earth Blast and Fire Blast. The order does not matter. A splash does not set the corresponding build-254 quest bit.
  4. Build 254 displays Chronozon as level 170. Surviving pre-cutoff material gives conflicting older combat levels, so the guide does not claim 170 as the certain historical September-2004 display.
  5. Do not try to finish him before all four successful Blast hits. In build 254 he regenerates instead of dying if any of the four elemental Blast requirements is still missing.
  6. Once all four elements have successfully hit, kill Chronozon and pick up Johnathon's crest fragment.

9. Reassemble the crest and finish

  1. Have all three crest fragments together. Use one fragment on another. Build 254 combines them once all three pieces are in your inventory and produces the complete Family crest.
  2. Return to Dimintheis in south-east Varrock with the completed crest.
  3. Tell him you recovered it. He takes the crest and rewards you with Steel gauntlets, completing the quest.

Combat encounters

  • Perfect-gold mine hellhounds: the August-2004 guide records them as level 122. They guard the ore but do not need to be killed for quest state; survival long enough to mine two ores is sufficient.
  • Wilderness Dungeon: the route includes aggressive enemies and poison spiders. Bring food and antipoison rather than treating the trip as a safe walk.
  • Chronozon: build 254 displays level 170. Four successful hits — Wind Blast, Water Blast, Earth Blast and Fire Blast — must be registered before he can die. Without all four, his death logic restores him instead.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Gem Trader has no Avan option: return to Caleb and continue the crest/brother dialogue until he explicitly tells you Avan went treasure-hunting in the desert.
  • Lost a crest fragment: the brother who supplied it can replace the relevant fragment once the quest has advanced far enough and neither your inventory nor bank contains it.
  • Lever confusion: examine each lever to see whether it is up or down. The build-valid sequence is north up → south up → north down → north-room up → north up → south down.
  • Chronozon keeps regenerating: at least one Blast element has not successfully hit. Recast the missing elements; the message Chronozon weakens... appears when a successful Blast registers.
  • Lost gauntlets after the quest: Dimintheis can return the same gauntlet type you permanently selected, or Steel gauntlets if you never chose an enhancement.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1 pair of Steel gauntlets.
  • Permanent choice of one gauntlet enhancement:
    • Cooking gauntlets from Caleb — reduce the chance of burning supported seafood, including lobster, swordfish and shark in build 254.
    • Goldsmithing gauntlets from Avan — build 254 multiplies Smithing XP from smelting ordinary gold bars by 2.5.
    • Chaos gauntlets from Johnathon — build 254 adds 3 to the maximum hit of the four elemental Bolt spells.
  • Family Crest becomes a prerequisite for Legends' Quest.

Choose the enhancement carefully. The later system that lets players pay to swap gauntlet enchantments was added after this wiki's 7 September 2004 cutoff. In build 254, once one of the three enhancement bits is chosen, the other brothers no longer offer a different specialisation.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Requirements: build 254's quest journal explicitly lists 40 Mining, 40 Crafting, 40 Smithing, 59 Magic and the ability to defeat a level-170 demon. The 40 Crafting check comes from the ruby necklace; the perfect-gold smelt requires 40 Smithing.
  • Chronozon's combat level: the build NPC config uses visible level 170. A dated August-2004 community guide contains conflicting level-121 and level-139 references, while an official 2003 manual described the requirement as defeating a level-121 demon. The public guide therefore labels 170 as the build value rather than rewriting the historical uncertainty.
  • Earth vs Air Obelisk: the August-2004 route places Chronozon by the Earth Obelisk, matching the dungeon location. Build 254's Johnathon dialogue and quest journal instead say Air Obelisk. Treat that text as an implementation dialogue error; the walkthrough uses the period-correct Earth Obelisk.
  • Blast mechanic: build 254 only calls the Chronozon weakening routine after the normal magic hit roll succeeds. Wind, Water, Earth and Fire Blast each set their own bit; splashes do not count. If Chronozon reaches his death routine without all four bits, he heals instead of dropping the crest.
  • Lever sequence: the preserved 14 August 2004 walkthrough says the south-room lever is pulled "down" on the second step, but its own note warns that the order/status may be reversed. Build 254's door conditions confirm the functional sequence as north up, south up, north down, north-room up, north up, south down.
  • Perfect gold: build 254 detects the quest mine by coordinates; gold rocks inside that room yield untradeable perfect gold ore. Two ores become two perfect gold bars, which the normal jewellery interface converts into the special perfect ruby ring and necklace variants.
  • Quest reward: completion gives Steel gauntlets and 1 Quest Point. There is no completion Mining XP, Smithing XP or coin reward. The old local guide's skill-XP/coins/decorating-service claims were unsupported and have been removed.
  • Quest-point constant bug: the Family Crest completion call currently passes ^squire_questpoints instead of ^crest_questpoints. Both constants are presently 1, so the displayed/awarded result remains correct, but the code reference is wrong.
  • Gauntlet choice: a source comment in Caleb's build script records the paid 25,000-coin switching feature as an addition from 30 August 2005. Build 254 deliberately makes the enhancement choice permanent, matching the pre-cutoff "choose one" guidance.
