Lost City

Lost City

A band of adventurers in Lumbridge Swamp is trying to locate the legendary city of Zanaris. Their least discreet member lets slip that a nearby leprechaun knows the secret. Following that lead takes you to Entrana, where you must enter a one-way dungeon, defeat the guardian of the Dramen tree and make the staff that turns an ordinary swamp shed into the doorway to the Lost City.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • 31 Crafting to carve a Dramen branch into a Dramen staff.
  • 36 Woodcutting to cut the Dramen tree.
  • Enough combat ability to defeat the Tree Spirit with Entrana's equipment restrictions. The December 2003 Jagex quest manual records the spirit as level 95, and surviving 2004 guide evidence agrees. Build 254 instead displays a level-101 Tree Spirit.

There is no prerequisite quest or Quest Point requirement to start Lost City. The Crafting and Woodcutting levels are completion requirements rather than checks at the adventurer camp.

Items and preparation

Required or effectively required

  • An axe you can use for the special swamp tree that hides Shamus. Bank it before sailing to Entrana because the monks reject axes as weapons.
  • A knife for carving a Dramen branch into a staff. Build 254 permits the knife on the trip to Entrana.
  • At least one free inventory space when cutting the Dramen tree.

Recommended for Entrana

  • Good food and useful potions. You cannot bring ordinary combat equipment, so supplies matter more than usual.
  • Combat runes if you plan to use Magic. Runes are not rejected by build 254's Entrana search.
  • Teleport runes if your Magic level allows a mainland teleport. The Entrana dungeon ladder is one-way; otherwise you must use the dungeon's exit into the Wilderness.
  • Materials that only become weapons or armour after arrival can be useful if you know the relevant Crafting/Fletching route. Do not turn them into prohibited equipment until after the monks have transported you.
Build-254 Entrana warning: the Port Sarim monk script rejects a broad set of weapon and armour categories in both inventory and worn slots. That includes axes, pickaxes, bows, staves, shields, body/leg armour, helmets, gloves and capes. If the monk refuses you, bank the flagged equipment and try again.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Get the adventurers to reveal their secret

  1. Go to the adventurer camp in Lumbridge Swamp and talk to the Warrior.
  2. Ask “What are you camped here for?”. He accidentally mentions Zanaris.
  3. One reliable dialogue route is “Who's Zanaris?” → “If it's hidden how are you planning to find it?” → “Looks like you don't know either.”
  4. The Warrior loses his temper and lets slip that they are searching for a leprechaun hiding in a tree nearby. This is the point at which build 254 marks Lost City as started.

2. Find Shamus

  1. Search the nearby swamp trees for the unusual tree used by the quest. It has the quest-specific chopping interaction rather than behaving like an ordinary tree.
  2. Use your axe on it. Shamus the leprechaun appears and complains that you are chopping his house.
  3. Talk to Shamus and tell him you want to find Zanaris.
  4. He explains that the doorway to Zanaris is the shed in Lumbridge Swamp, but it only works while you have a Dramen staff.
  5. Shamus tells you that a Dramen staff is carved from a branch of the Dramen tree in a cave on Entrana, and that the monks at Port Sarim run a ship there.

3. Prepare for Entrana

  1. Bank the axe you used to find Shamus and all normal weapons and armour.
  2. Keep your knife, food, potions, runes and any permitted materials you intend to turn into combat gear after reaching the dungeon.
  3. Go to the Entrana monks at Port Sarim. Agree to be searched and to sail to Entrana.
  4. If build 254 refuses passage, check for easy-to-miss restricted equipment such as a cape, gloves, pickaxe, staff or bow.

4. Enter the one-way Entrana dungeon

  1. From the Entrana dock, travel east beyond the church, cross north over the bridge, then continue west to the dungeon ladder.
  2. Attempt to climb down. A Cave monk warns that the evil below interferes with Prayer, that the ladder does not provide a return route and that the dungeon's exit leads into the Wilderness.
  3. Accept the risk and descend. Expect your Prayer to be reduced on entry.
  4. If you brought components for combat equipment, finish making that equipment only after you are safely below the monks' search area.

5. Obtain an axe inside the dungeon

  1. Because you could not bring an axe onto Entrana, kill the zombies near the dungeon entrance until one drops an axe.
  2. Build 254 gives the Entrana zombie a particularly common Bronze axe drop, with an Iron axe also possible. Either works for the Dramen tree if you meet the Woodcutting requirement.
  3. Continue deeper through the dungeon. Run past or deal with the other hostile creatures rather than wasting supplies unless you need to.
  4. Reach the chamber containing the Dramen tree in the southern part of the dungeon.

6. Defeat the Tree Spirit

  1. Make sure you are ready before attempting to chop the Dramen tree. You need 36 Woodcutting.
  2. On the first chopping attempt after speaking to Shamus, the Tree Spirit appears and attacks.
  3. Defeat it using whatever legal equipment, Magic or supplies you managed to bring or create after entering Entrana.
  4. Historical level note: the period Jagex manual and 2004 guide evidence identify this encounter as level 95. Build 254's reconstructed NPC is visibly level 101, with 85 Hitpoints, 90 Attack, 95 Strength and 80 Defence. Treat 101 as the build's behaviour, not as proof that September 2004 displayed the same level.
  5. When the spirit dies, build 254 advances the quest and tells you that the tree can now be chopped.

7. Make the Dramen staff

  1. Chop the Dramen tree again. With 36 Woodcutting and a free inventory slot, you receive a Dramen branch.
  2. It is sensible to cut several branches before leaving. Once the Tree Spirit has been defeated, build 254 does not make you fight it again for each additional branch.
  3. Use your knife on a branch. At 31 Crafting, it becomes a Dramen staff.
  4. Carve spare branches into spare staffs if you have room. They save a return trip if you later lose your staff.

8. Leave Entrana and return to Lumbridge Swamp

  1. If you brought a usable mainland teleport, this is the safest way out after obtaining your staff.
  2. Otherwise use the dungeon's magic exit door/portal. The Cave monk explicitly warns that this route exits into the Wilderness, so be prepared to leave the area promptly.
  3. Return to Lumbridge Swamp and locate the small shed Shamus described.

9. Enter Zanaris and complete the quest

  1. Wield the Dramen staff. Merely having it in your inventory is not enough for build 254's shed-door check.
  2. Open the shed door while wearing the staff.
  3. The world shimmers and you are teleported directly into Zanaris.
  4. Lost City completes immediately on entry. There is no final conversation with Shamus, the adventurers or a fairy NPC.

Combat and Entrana notes

  • The historical “unarmed” wording is about the Entrana restriction. You are not required to punch the Tree Spirit bare-handed. The route deliberately provides ways to obtain or create combat equipment after the monks' search.
  • Prayer is unreliable as your only plan. The Cave monk warns that the dungeon interferes with Prayer, and build 254 runs a Prayer-reduction action when you descend.
  • You must obtain an axe after arrival. Build 254's Port Sarim search explicitly treats axes as prohibited weapons, while the Entrana zombie drop table supplies a Bronze axe.
  • The dungeon is a one-way commitment. Once you climb down, you cannot climb back to Entrana's surface. Bring enough food to finish the Tree Spirit or an emergency teleport to retreat.

Rewards and unlocks

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • Access to Zanaris through the Lumbridge Swamp shed while wielding a Dramen staff.
  • Permission to wield the Dragon longsword and Dragon dagger. In build 254 both also require 60 Attack.
  • Access to Jukat's Dragon Sword Shop in Zanaris, which stocks the Dragon longsword and Dragon dagger in build 254. The market itself has an additional period-accurate entry cost: the Door man charges one cut diamond per entry from the outside.
  • Progression toward Heroes' Quest. Build 254 directly checks Lost City completion as one of the Heroes' Quest prerequisites.

No skill XP or coin reward is granted by Lost City in build 254. The old page's Woodcutting/Crafting XP reward and its claim that Dragon equipment is forged at a Zanaris furnace were incorrect.

Useful recovery notes

  • Lost your Dramen staff? You can return to Entrana, go back through the dungeon and cut more Dramen branches. Build 254 only summons the Tree Spirit at the pre-defeat quest state, so a completed character does not have to repeat that boss fight.
  • Make spares on the first trip. Cutting several branches after the spirit dies is quicker than repeating the Entrana journey later.
  • Forgot to wield the staff? The shed behaves as an ordinary shed. Equip the Dramen staff and use the door again.
  • Zanaris market tax: the diamond is consumed when the Door man admits you from outside. Leaving the market is free; entering again requires another diamond.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start state: build 254 sets %zanaris to its started state only when the Warrior accidentally reveals the leprechaun clue. Merely speaking to the other campers does not start the quest.
  • Shamus tree: the special swamp tree first runs the normal Woodcutting axe checker, then spawns Shamus if he is not already nearby. Shamus advances the quest after explaining the shed, Dramen staff, Entrana cave and Port Sarim monks.
  • Skill checks: the Dramen tree checks 36 Woodcutting; cutting the branch with a knife checks 31 Crafting. These agree with the period Jagex manual.
  • Tree Spirit discrepancy: build 254 defines the Tree Spirit with visible level 101, 85 Hitpoints, 90 Attack, 95 Strength and 80 Defence. The config comments explicitly say the stats/visible level match OSRS data, while direct period evidence records level 95. The guide therefore preserves 95 as the historical target and documents 101 as a build difference.
  • Entrana equipment check: the Port Sarim monk scans inventory and worn slots for many armour and weapon categories. In this build capes are also rejected, an important practical difference from some later guide advice.
  • Entrana axe recovery: the Entrana zombie drop table gives a Bronze axe on 50 of 128 random outcomes, so the intended axe recovery is very common in build 254.
  • Quest completion trigger: the Lumbridge Swamp shed checks for a worn Dramen staff. If the quest is at the staff-made state, crossing the door queues completion and teleports the player to Zanaris.
  • Rewards: the completion script sets the quest complete and awards 3 Quest Points. It contains no skill-experience grant and no inventory coin grant.
  • Dragon equipment: build 254's equip handlers require Lost City plus 60 Attack for the Dragon dagger, poisoned Dragon dagger and Dragon longsword. Jukat's Zanaris shop stocks the dagger and longsword.
  • Marketplace: build 254's Door man removes one diamond before opening either guarded market entrance from outside. This matches the market tax documented for the 2002–2006 Door man.
