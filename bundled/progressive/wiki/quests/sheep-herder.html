Four plague-infected sheep have escaped near Ardougne. Councillor Halgrive wants them rounded up, poisoned and disposed of before the disease can spread. The quest is short and has no combat or skill requirements, but herding each sheep into the quarantine pen takes careful positioning.

Requirements

Required levels and quests

  • No skill requirements.
  • No prerequisite quests and no Quest Point requirement.
  • No combat is required. The diseased sheep are handled with quest items rather than fought.

Required items

  • 100 coins. Doctor Orbon charges this for the two-piece plague suit.

Items obtained during the quest

  • Poisoned sheep feed — supplied by Councillor Halgrive when he sends you to deal with the escaped sheep.
  • Plague jacket and plague trousers — bought together from Doctor Orbon for 100 coins. Wear both pieces while working around the quarantine enclosure.
  • Cattle prod — obtained from the shed beside the plague-sheep enclosure. Equip it before trying to herd the sheep.
  • Four different sets of sheep bones — one from each diseased sheep after it has been poisoned inside the pen.

Recommended

  • Several free inventory spaces. You will be carrying the feed and plague-suit pieces and may hold several distinct sheep-bone items before incinerating them.
  • Patience with the herding step. The cattle prod moves a sheep away from your character, so approach from the side opposite the direction you want it to travel.

Before you start

Bring 100 coins to East Ardougne. Everything else required for the quest can be obtained during the quest itself.

The four diseased sheep are tracked separately. Treat each one as its own objective: herd it into the quarantine pen, poison it with Halgrive's feed, collect its bones and eventually burn those bones in the nearby incinerator.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Councillor Halgrive

  1. Go to East Ardougne and find Councillor Halgrive south of the church.
  2. Ask about the problem and agree to help with the escaped plague-infected sheep.
  3. Halgrive gives you poisoned sheep feed and tells you to speak to Doctor Orbon before approaching the sheep.

2. Buy and wear the plague suit

  1. Find Doctor Orbon at the Ardougne church.
  2. Tell him that Halgrive sent you about the plague sheep.
  3. Pay 100 coins for the protective suit. You receive a plague jacket and plague trousers.
  4. Wear both pieces. Build 254 checks the full suit when you use the quarantine area, so merely carrying the clothing is not enough.

3. Meet Farmer Brumty and get the cattle prod

  1. Travel north from Ardougne to the fenced plague-sheep enclosure.
  2. Speak to Farmer Brumty by the enclosure to learn how the escaped sheep are being contained.
  3. Take the cattle prod from the nearby shed.
  4. Equip the cattle prod and keep the plague jacket and trousers equipped while herding.

4. Herd all four diseased sheep into the pen

The escaped sheep can be distinguished by their differently coloured faces. They are scattered around the countryside north of Ardougne: one is close to the northern city wall, one is east of the pen, one is to the north-west, and another is farther to the north-east toward the Fishing Guild.

  1. Find one of the four diseased sheep.
  2. Stand behind it relative to the direction you want it to move. When you prod it, it moves away from you.
  3. Use the equipped cattle prod to nudge the sheep toward the quarantine enclosure one step at a time.
  4. Work around obstacles and line the sheep up with the enclosure gate. Continue prodding until it is safely inside.
  5. Repeat the process for the other three diseased sheep. Each colour is tracked independently; all four must eventually be dealt with.

5. Poison the sheep and collect their bones

  1. Once a diseased sheep is inside the pen, use the poisoned sheep feed on it.
  2. The sheep dies, leaving its own set of sheep bones. Pick them up.
  3. Do the same for all four sheep. The bone items are distinct even though they serve the same purpose, so make sure no colour is missed.

6. Incinerate all four sets of bones

  1. Use each set of sheep bones on the special incinerator beside the quarantine area.
  2. Each set is consumed separately. Burn all four; destroying only some of the bones is not enough to finish the job.

7. Return to Councillor Halgrive

  1. Return to Councillor Halgrive in East Ardougne.
  2. Once all four sheep have been poisoned and all four bone sets incinerated, tell him the work is complete.
  3. Halgrive awards 3,100 coins and 4 Quest Points, completing the quest.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Cannot use the enclosure properly? Wear both the plague jacket and plague trousers. Build 254 checks the equipped suit rather than just your inventory.
  • Prod does not herd the sheep? Equip the cattle prod, then approach from the opposite side of the direction you want the sheep to move.
  • A sheep is awkwardly positioned? Move around it and change the angle of your next prod. Build 254's current sheep script does not automatically send a misplaced sheep back to its starting point.
  • Quest will not finish? Four separate sheep states are tracked. Make sure every diseased sheep was poisoned inside the pen and that every corresponding bone item was used on the incinerator.
  • No combat supplies are required. There is no mandatory enemy or boss encounter.

Rewards

  • 4 Quest Points.
  • 3,100 coins.
  • No skill XP reward.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest progression: build 254 first sends the player from Halgrive to Doctor Orbon, then advances to the sheep-disposal stage. Four separate bit ranges track the four diseased sheep through their individual states.
  • Protective clothing: the quarantine gate checks that the plague jacket and plague trousers are worn. This is an equipment check, not merely an ownership check.
  • Herding: the diseased-sheep handler moves a sheep away from the player when it is prodded, which is why positioning on the opposite side of the intended route matters.
  • Four separate remains: each sheep produces its own bone object. The incinerator has separate handlers for all four and advances the matching sheep state as each set is burned.
  • Completion gate: Halgrive only completes the quest after all four tracked sheep have reached their disposed state. His reward script gives exactly 3,100 coins and queues 4 Quest Points.
  • No automatic reset in the current build: the sheep timer handles ambient behaviour such as bleating but does not implement a timed return to the original spawn. This guide therefore does not rely on later-guide advice about waiting for a badly placed sheep to reset.
  • Historical scope: some reconstruction comments in the diseased-sheep script refer to later material when discussing uncertain movement behaviour. Those comments are useful for implementation context but are not treated here as evidence for September 2004 timing details.
