A ghost is haunting the graveyard beside Lumbridge church. Father Aereck asks you to find his hermit friend Father Urhney, who provides an Amulet of Ghostspeak so you can discover what is preventing the spirit from resting in peace.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels

  • None. A Skeleton appears during the skull recovery, but no combat level or skill level is required and you can run away after taking the skull.

Required items

  • Nothing needs to be brought. Father Urhney supplies the Ghostspeak amulet and the skull is obtained during the quest.

Recommended

  • A little food if you are a very low-level player. The skull is guarded by a level-13 Skeleton in the target-proximate March 2004 route and build 254.
  • One free inventory space for the Ghostspeak amulet and another for the skull.

Before you start

The quest is a short trip between Lumbridge, Lumbridge Swamp and the Wizards' Tower. You do not need to bank for any special equipment. Father Urhney's shack is in the eastern part of Lumbridge Swamp, south of Lumbridge; the Wizards' Tower is on the island south of Draynor Village.

The official December 2003 quest manual and the surviving March 2004 RuneScape 2 guide both call the mission Medium. The 2003 manual still describes the older Classic-era level-19 Skeleton, while the March 2004 RS2 guide and build 254 use level 13; the page therefore uses the RS2 value for the target-period route.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Speak to Father Aereck

  1. Go to the Lumbridge church, immediately south-east of Lumbridge Castle, and speak to Father Aereck.
  2. Choose the option saying you are looking for a quest.
  3. He explains that a ghost is haunting the church graveyard and asks you to get rid of it.
  4. Aereck recommends speaking to Father Urhney, an expert on ghosts who is living as a hermit in a shack in Lumbridge Swamp.

2. Get the Ghostspeak amulet from Father Urhney

  1. Leave Lumbridge and make your way into Lumbridge Swamp. Father Urhney's small shack is in the eastern depths of the swamp.
  2. Talk to Father Urhney and tell him that Father Aereck sent you.
  3. Explain that Aereck has a ghost haunting his graveyard.
  4. Urhney cannot leave his meditation, but he gives you an Amulet of Ghostspeak. Wear it before talking to the ghost.

3. Find out why the ghost cannot rest

  1. Return to the Lumbridge graveyard beside the church.
  2. Enter the small graveyard building and open the coffin if necessary to make the Restless ghost appear.
  3. With the Ghostspeak amulet equipped, speak to the ghost.
  4. Work through the conversation until he explains that a warlock stole his skull.
  5. He believes the thief came from the large tower by the sea to the south-west: the Wizards' Tower.

If you talk to the ghost without the amulet, you only hear ghostly noises and cannot advance this part of the quest.

4. Recover the skull from the Wizards' Tower

  1. Travel to the Wizards' Tower on the island south of Draynor Village.
  2. Enter the tower and climb down into the basement.
  3. Find the skull in the basement and take it.
  4. A level-13 Skeleton appears and attacks when the skull is taken.
  5. You may kill the Skeleton or simply leave with the skull. Defeating it is not a quest-state requirement.

5. Put the skull back in the coffin

  1. Return to the Lumbridge graveyard.
  2. Open the ghost's coffin if it is closed.
  3. Use the skull on the open coffin. Do not try to hand it directly to the ghost.
  4. The skull is returned to the corpse, the ghost vanishes and thanks you, and the quest completes immediately.

No final visit to Father Aereck is required. The reward is triggered at the coffin as soon as the skull is replaced.

Combat encounter

  • Skeleton — level 13 in the March 2004 RuneScape 2 guide and build 254.
  • Location: Wizards' Tower basement, appearing when the quest skull is taken.
  • Fight required? No. You can take the skull and run away.
  • Historical note: Jagex's December 2003 Classic-era manual still listed a level-19 Skeleton. Because the quest had already moved into RuneScape 2 by the September 2004 target, the closer March 2004 RS2 evidence is used for the player route.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost the Ghostspeak amulet? In build 254, Father Urhney can give another one once you have reached the amulet stage and no longer own one.
  • Lost or destroyed the skull? Build 254 lets you take the quest skull again from the Wizards' Tower basement if you no longer have one in your inventory or bank.
  • Do not use the skull on the ghost. The build explicitly rejects this because the item passes through him; use it on the open coffin.
  • The Ghostspeak amulet remains useful after the quest. It is also relevant to later ghost-related content, including the members quest Nature Spirit.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 1,125 Prayer XP.
  • Continued use of the Ghostspeak amulet, with replacement available from Father Urhney in build 254 if you no longer own one.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest states: build 254 progresses from Father Aereck -> Father Urhney -> ghost conversation -> skull obtained -> completion at the coffin. There is no final Aereck hand-in.
  • Ghostspeak requirement: the ghost only gives intelligible quest dialogue when the Ghostspeak amulet is worn. Father Urhney supplies it and can replace it when the player no longer owns one.
  • Skeleton: `skull_skeleton` is visibly level 13 in build 254 with 18 Hitpoints. Picking up the skull spawns it and immediately lets the player take the skull, so killing it is optional.
  • Skull recovery: the build prevents duplicate skull pickup when one is already in inventory or bank. If the skull is destroyed or otherwise lost, the basement pickup can be used again while the quest remains unfinished.
  • Coffin completion: the skull must be used on the open coffin. Using it on the closed coffin tells the player to open it first; using it on the ghost fails because it passes through him.
  • Reward: the completion queue awards 1,125 Prayer XP and `^priest_questpoints`, which is 1 Quest Point.
  • Reconstruction caveat: the quest source notes that The Restless Ghost was reworked on 22 May 2006 and that parts of the build reconstruction use RuneScape Classic material and a 2005 video. Exact build dialogue therefore is not treated as direct September-2004 evidence; the route and rewards are cross-checked against the March 2004 RuneScape 2 guide and the 2003 official manual.
