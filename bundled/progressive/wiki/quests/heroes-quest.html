Achietties will only admit proven adventurers to the Heroes' Guild. After meeting the quest-point and prerequisite-quest requirements, you must bring her three trophies: an Entranan Firebird feather, a cooked lava eel and a master thief's armband. The armband trial continues your Shield of Arrav gang allegiance and requires cooperation with a player from the opposite gang.

Requirements

Required quests and Quest Points

  • 55 Quest Points.
  • Completion of Shield of Arrav.
  • Completion of Lost City.
  • Completion of Merlin's Crystal.
  • Completion of Dragon Slayer.

Those are the four quests Achietties checks by name in build 254. Druidic Ritual and Scorpion Catcher are not separate Heroes' Quest start gates. Historically, training Herblore normally followed Druidic Ritual, but Heroes' Quest itself does not add it to Achietties' four-quest test.

Required levels

  • 50 Mining — required to clear the rockslide leading toward the Ice Queen.
  • 25 Herblore — required to make Blamish oil for the oily fishing rod.
  • 53 Fishing — required to catch a raw lava eel. Build 254 checks your current Fishing stat, so a period-valid temporary boost can satisfy the action.
  • 53 Cooking — required to cook the lava eel.

Required player cooperation

You need a second player who completed Shield of Arrav in the opposite gang and can progress the complementary Heroes' Quest branch. A Phoenix Gang player must kill Grip, while a Black Arm Gang player must infiltrate Scarface Pete's mansion, obtain the keyring and loot the candlesticks. Build 254 tracks those roles with separate quest states, so do not plan on simply swapping jobs.

Required items

  • 1 pickaxe you can use — needed for the level-50 Mining rockslide and not consumed.
  • 1 ordinary fishing rod — converted into an oily fishing rod.
  • Fishing bait — at least one per lava-eel catch attempt; bring several.
  • 1 harralander and 1 vial of water — used with the Blamish snail slime supplied by Gerrant to make Blamish oil.
  • Black Arm Gang branch only: a black full helm, black platebody and black platelegs, all worn when presenting the ID papers at Scarface Pete's mansion.
  • Phoenix Gang branch only: suitable Ranged or Magic equipment for killing Grip from the Phoenix-side position.

Obtained during the quest

  • Ice gloves from the Ice Queen.
  • Blamish snail slime from Gerrant in Port Sarim.
  • Jail key from the Taverley Dungeon Jailer and Dusty key from Velrak the explorer.
  • Gang-specific Brimhaven items including ID papers, the miscellaneous key, Grip's keyring and Pete's candlesticks.
  • The three final hand-in items: Entranan Firebird feather, cooked lava eel and master thief's armband.

Recommended

  • Good combat equipment and food for the Ice Queen and the Taverley Dungeon.
  • Prayer supplies if the Ice Queen or dungeon route is dangerous for your combat level.
  • An anti-dragon shield can make the blue-dragon section of Taverley Dungeon safer; Dragon Slayer is already a prerequisite.
  • Several free inventory spaces. The Brimhaven branch passes keys and candlesticks between two players.

Before you start

The three trophies can be gathered in any order, but the master-thief task is much easier if you arrange your opposite-gang partner before travelling to Brimhaven. Both players should have started Heroes' Quest and reached the appropriate gang dialogue before attempting the mansion sequence.

The Firebird stage includes a trip to Entrana. Obtain the Ice gloves first, then bank weapons and armour that the Entrana monks will not allow on the island. The Ice gloves themselves are the item that lets you pick up the hot feather.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Apply to the Heroes' Guild

  1. Travel to the Heroes' Guild north of Taverley and speak to Achietties outside the entrance.
  2. Choose the option to apply to join the guild.
  3. If you have at least 55 Quest Points and have completed Shield of Arrav, Lost City, Merlin's Crystal and Dragon Slayer, Achietties accepts your application.
  4. She asks you to bring three items: an Entranan Firebird feather, a master thief's armband and a cooked lava eel.

Entranan Firebird feather

2. Obtain the Ice gloves

  1. Take a pickaxe and combat gear to White Wolf Mountain. Find the rockslide leading into the Ice Queen's cavern system.
  2. Mine through the rockslide. Build 254 requires 50 Mining and a usable pickaxe.
  3. Navigate the cavern route to the Ice Queen and defeat her.
  4. Pick up the Ice gloves she drops. Keep them for the Entrana stage.

3. Kill the Firebird on Entrana

  1. Bank prohibited weapons and armour, then travel from Port Sarim to Entrana with the Ice gloves.
  2. Find the Entrana Fire Bird on the island and kill it. Build 254 displays the Fire Bird as level 2.
  3. Wear the Ice gloves before taking the feather.
  4. Pick up the Entranan Firebird feather. In build 254 an unprotected attempt burns you and refuses the pickup.

Cooked lava eel

4. Make an oily fishing rod

  1. Go to Gerrant's fishing shop in Port Sarim and ask how to catch a lava eel.
  2. Gerrant gives you Blamish snail slime and explains that an ordinary fishing rod needs a fireproof coating.
  3. Put the harralander into the vial of water to make the unfinished harralander mixture.
  4. Add the Blamish snail slime at 25 Herblore to make Blamish oil.
  5. Use the Blamish oil on an ordinary fishing rod. The oil and rod are consumed and you receive an oily fishing rod plus the empty vial.

5. Get a Dusty key in Taverley Dungeon

  1. Enter Taverley Dungeon.
  2. Reach the jail area and kill the Jailer to obtain a Jail key.
  3. Use the Jail key to free Velrak the explorer.
  4. Ask Velrak whether he knows anywhere good to explore, then accept his offer to try the deeper dungeon. He gives you a Dusty key.
  5. Use the Dusty key on the locked deeper gate and continue toward the lava-eel fishing area.

6. Catch and cook the lava eel

  1. At the lava fishing spot, have the oily fishing rod and fishing bait in your inventory.
  2. Fish at level 53 Fishing to catch a raw lava eel. Build 254 checks your current Fishing stat at the moment you fish.
  3. Cook the raw lava eel at level 53 Cooking. Build 254's cooking data makes the lava eel a guaranteed successful cook once the level check is met.
  4. Keep the resulting cooked lava eel for Achietties.

Master thief's armband

Your path depends on which gang you joined during Shield of Arrav. The two branches meet at Scarface Pete's mansion in Brimhaven and must be performed together.

7A. Black Arm Gang player — prepare the mansion infiltration

  1. Speak to Katrine in the Black Arm Gang headquarters in south-west Varrock. Ask whether there is any way to earn the rank of master thief.
  2. She sends you after Scarface Pete's valuable candlesticks in Brimhaven and tells you to contact the Black Arm operatives there.
  3. Go to the Black Arm hideout on Palm Street in Brimhaven. When Grubor challenges you, choose “Four leaved clover.”
  4. Speak to Trobert. He gives you ID papers so you can impersonate Hartigen, Scarface Pete's new deputy.
  5. Wear a black full helm, black platebody and black platelegs, then approach the mansion and present the ID papers to Garv.
  6. Inside, speak to Grip and ask whether there is anything you can do. He gives you a miscellaneous key.
  7. Give the miscellaneous key to your Phoenix Gang partner. They need it to reach the attack position used for the Grip ambush.

7B. Phoenix Gang player — open the secret approach

  1. Speak to Straven in the Phoenix Gang headquarters in Varrock and ask how to obtain a master thief's armband.
  2. Straven sends you to the Shrimp and Parrot in Brimhaven and gives you the secret word “gherkin”.
  3. Speak to Alfonse the waiter and choose “Do you sell Gherkins?”
  4. Go around to Charlie the cook behind the restaurant. Follow his dialogue to learn about the secret way into Scarface Pete's mansion.
  5. Use the secret panel/side route and the miscellaneous key supplied by your Black Arm partner to reach the position from which Grip can be attacked when he is lured into view.

8. Work together to steal the candlesticks

  1. Black Arm player: once both players are ready, search the appropriate mansion cupboard to draw Grip into the ambush position.
  2. Phoenix player: attack and kill Grip from the Phoenix-side position. Build 254 requires the Phoenix-route player to receive the Grip-kill progress; a Black Arm player cannot substitute for this role.
  3. Black Arm player: obtain Grip's keyring, open the treasure room and search the chest. The chest supplies two Pete's candlesticks — one for each partner.
  4. Black Arm player: keep one candlestick and give the second to the Phoenix player.
  5. Black Arm player: return your correctly looted candlestick to Katrine. She gives you a master thief's armband.
  6. Phoenix player: return the candlestick received from your partner to Straven. Because you personally killed Grip, he recognises the theft and gives you a master thief's armband.

Do not swap the branch jobs. Build 254 checks whether the Black Arm player progressed through the mansion/chest sequence and whether the Phoenix player killed Grip. Katrine and Straven reject a candlestick if the relevant personal quest state is missing.

9. Return all three trophies to Achietties

  1. Have the Entranan Firebird feather, cooked lava eel and master thief's armband together in your inventory.
  2. Return to Achietties outside the Heroes' Guild.
  3. Tell her you have all the required items. Build 254 removes one of each trophy and completes the quest.
  4. The Heroes' Guild doors are now available to you.

Combat encounters and hazards

  • Ice Queen: required for the Ice gloves. A preserved 2004 quest guide records her as level 102, while build 254 currently displays level 111. The walkthrough therefore does not silently present the build number as certain September-2004 history.
  • Entrana Fire Bird: build 254 displays level 2. The important mechanic is the feather: wear Ice gloves before picking it up.
  • Taverley Dungeon Jailer: must be killed if obtaining your own Jail key to free Velrak. The deeper route also passes dangerous dungeon monsters and the blue-dragon area.
  • Grip: build 254 displays level 22. The Phoenix Gang player must kill him while the Black Arm partner handles the mansion-side trigger and treasure access.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Achietties will not start the quest: check both the 55-Quest-Point gate and all four named prerequisite quests. The build start check does not substitute Druidic Ritual or Scorpion Catcher for any of them.
  • The rockslide will not move: you need 50 Mining and a pickaxe your Mining level can use.
  • The feather burns you: equip the Ice gloves before trying to take it.
  • Ordinary fishing gear at the lava: build 254 destroys unsuitable normal fishing tools used on the lava. Make and use the oily fishing rod.
  • Velrak will not give a Dusty key: first free him from the jail, then ask about somewhere good to explore and accept the offer.
  • Katrine or Straven rejects the candlestick: your own branch state is wrong. The Black Arm player must be the mansion looter; the Phoenix player must be the one credited with killing Grip.
  • Lost master thief's armband: after reaching the relevant armband state, build 254 lets Katrine or Straven replace it when you no longer possess one.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 3,075 Attack XP.
  • 3,075 Defence XP.
  • 3,075 Strength XP.
  • 3,075 Hitpoints XP.
  • 2,075 Ranged XP.
  • 2,725 Fishing XP.
  • 2,825 Cooking XP.
  • 1,575 Woodcutting XP.
  • 1,575 Firemaking XP.
  • 2,275 Smithing XP.
  • 2,575 Mining XP.
  • 1,325 Herblore XP.
  • Permanent access to the Heroes' Guild.
  • Access to the guild weapon shop, which stocks Dragon battleaxes and Dragon maces in build 254.

The completion script does not award coins or a Dragon battleaxe as a free item. The Dragon battleaxe is used as the quest-completion interface icon and is available through the guild shop after entry.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start gate: build 254 checks at least 55 Quest Points plus completion of Shield of Arrav, Lost City, Merlin's Crystal and Dragon Slayer. The 50 Mining, 25 Herblore, 53 Fishing and 53 Cooking requirements are enforced later by the actions that need them rather than by Achietties' start routine.
  • Official-period skill list: Jagex's 2003 quest manual already records 55 Quest Points, 50 Mining, 25 Herblaw, 53 Fishing and 53 Cooking for Heroes' Quest. The RuneScape 2/build route preserves the same numerical skill requirements; the skill is called Herblore in the September-2004 target.
  • Ice Queen level: a preserved 2004 RuneScape 2 walkthrough records level 102, while the current build NPC config displays level 111. This is kept as a historical/implementation conflict instead of silently rewriting the period value.
  • Firebird feather: build 254 only allows the quest feather to be taken after Heroes' Quest has started and while Ice gloves are worn. An unprotected pickup attempt damages the player and leaves the feather behind.
  • Lava eel: build 254 requires current Fishing level 53, an oily fishing rod and fishing bait. Its cooking row requires 53 Cooking and has guaranteed success, so a raw lava eel cannot burn once the level requirement is met.
  • Blamish oil: the build recipe is harralander in a vial of water plus Blamish snail slime, with a 25 Herblore check. Gerrant supplies the slime during the quest, and using the finished oil on a normal fishing rod creates the oily fishing rod.
  • Opposite-gang cooperation: the two gang branches are stateful. Black Arm progression controls the Hartigen disguise, mansion access and candlestick chest; Phoenix progression controls the secret approach and Grip kill. The final gang leaders verify those personal states before granting an armband.
  • Exact completion reward: the build awards 3,075 Attack, Defence, Strength and Hitpoints XP; 2,075 Ranged; 2,725 Fishing; 2,825 Cooking; 1,575 Woodcutting and Firemaking; 2,275 Smithing; 2,575 Mining; 1,325 Herblore; and 1 Quest Point.
  • Guild shop: the build's Heroes' Guild stock contains Dragon battleaxes and Dragon maces. Access to the guild entrance itself is gated by Heroes' Quest completion.
