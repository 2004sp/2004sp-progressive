Nature Spirit

Quest guide · September 2004 target · 2004scape Progressive build 254

Nature Spirit
Access
Members

13 July 2004
Period length
Medium (release-day community guide)
Quest points
2
Start NPC
Drezel
Start location
Paterdomus mausoleum / underground passage at the River Salve
Required quests
Priest in Peril; The Restless Ghost

Mandatory combat
Defeat 3 Ghasts; level 30 in the release-day guide and build 254

Drezel is worried about a druid named Filliman Tarlock, who lives in the dangerous Mort Myre Swamp beyond the River Salve. When you find Filliman, the problem is stranger than expected: he has died without realising it. You must help him accept what happened, complete a three-part nature ritual, transform him into a Nature Spirit, and then prove that the nearby Ghasts can be kept under control.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • Completion of Priest in Peril.
  • Completion of The Restless Ghost.
  • 18 Crafting on the release-day route, which has you make the Silver sickle yourself. Build 254 accepts a pre-made Silver sickle and therefore lists 18 Crafting as recommended rather than an absolute quest gate.
  • Combat ability to defeat three level-30 Ghasts. There is no fixed player Combat-level requirement.

Items and preparation

Bring

  • Amulet of ghostspeak. You need it equipped to communicate properly with Filliman.
  • Silver bar + Sickle mould if following the period self-crafting route. The mould can instead be bought during the quest from Dommik's Crafting Shop in Al Kharid.
  • Weapon and armour suitable for three level-30 Ghasts.

Obtained during the quest

  • Washing bowl and Mirror at Filliman's camp.
  • Filliman's journal from the grotto.
  • Druidic spell scroll, later turned into a used spell scroll after casting it.
  • Mort Myre fungus grown with the spell and later with the blessed sickle's Bloom ability.
  • Blessed silver sickle and Druid pouch from the Nature Spirit.

Recommended

  • Food for the bridge, swamp and Ghasts. In build 254 an attacking Ghast can rot a food item, so do not rely on carrying only one piece.
  • Prayer points before the final swamp section. Build 254's blessed-sickle Bloom action consumes Prayer points.
  • A little spare inventory space. The quest repeatedly gives you temporary items and swamp produce.
No antipoison is required for the quest. The important Mort Myre hazards are Ghasts and, in build 254, the swamp-decay effect. The old guide's poison warning was not part of the verified route.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Ask Drezel about Filliman Tarlock

  1. Go beneath Paterdomus to the underground passage where Priest in Peril finishes and speak to Drezel.
  2. Ask whether there is anything else you can help with. Drezel explains that a druid called Filliman Tarlock was sent into Mort Myre and has not been heard from.
  3. Agree to look for him. Continue through the holy barrier and into Morytania.

2. Find Filliman's camp in Mort Myre

  1. Pass through the gate into Mort Myre Swamp and head south-west toward the small circular island containing Filliman's grotto.
  2. Cross the broken bridge to the island. There is no hard Agility requirement; failure can hurt you, so low-level players should keep enough Hitpoints to absorb a bad crossing.
  3. At the camp, take the Washing bowl from the bench. A Mirror is revealed beneath it; take the Mirror as well.
  4. Search the grotto to find Filliman's journal.

3. Convince Filliman that he is a ghost

  1. Equip your Amulet of ghostspeak.
  2. Try to enter or interact with the grotto. Filliman Tarlock's spirit appears.
  3. Speak to him. He does not initially understand that he is dead.
  4. Use the Mirror on Filliman to prove that he has no reflection.
  5. Give him Filliman's journal. Reading it helps him understand what happened and decide that he should become a Nature Spirit.
  6. Continue the conversation and agree to help. Filliman gives you a Druidic spell scroll and explains that his transformation requires three things: something from nature, something with faith, and something of the spirit-to-become freely given.

4. Receive Drezel's blessing

  1. Return to Drezel beneath Paterdomus.
  2. Explain what Filliman is trying to do and ask Drezel to bless you.
  3. This blessing makes you the "something with faith" part of the ritual. Drezel does not bless the Silver sickle at this stage.
  4. Return to Mort Myre and Filliman's camp.

5. Grow the "something from nature"

  1. Find a suitable rotten log in Mort Myre near Filliman's island.
  2. Use the Cast option on the Druidic spell scroll while standing near the log.
  3. The spell makes swamp growth bloom. Pick the Mort Myre fungus that appears.
  4. The spell scroll becomes a used spell scroll. Its significance is the final clue: it is the "something of the spirit-to-become freely given" because Filliman freely gave it to you.

6. Arrange the three-part ritual

  1. Return to the three stones beside Filliman's grotto.
  2. Place the Mort Myre fungus on the western stone.
  3. Place the used spell scroll on the eastern stone.
  4. Stand on the southern stone yourself. Drezel's blessing means you supply the faith component.
  5. Speak to Filliman and tell him that you have solved the puzzle.
  6. Enter the grotto when instructed. Filliman performs the ritual and completes his transformation into a Nature Spirit.

7. Make and bless the Silver sickle

  1. Speak to the Nature Spirit. He now asks you to bring him a Silver sickle so that he can prepare you to fight Ghasts.
  2. If you do not already have one, travel to Dommik's Crafting Shop in Al Kharid and buy a Sickle mould.
  3. With 18 Crafting, use a Silver bar on a furnace while carrying the mould and make a Silver sickle.
  4. Return to the Nature Spirit and give him the Silver sickle.
  5. He blesses it, creating a Blessed silver sickle, and gives you an empty Druid pouch.

8. Fill the Druid pouch

  1. Keep the Blessed silver sickle available in your inventory and use its Bloom ability near suitable rotten logs in Mort Myre.
  2. For the simplest route, grow and pick three Mort Myre fungi.
  3. Fill the Druid pouch with the three fungi. This gives enough uses for the three quest Ghasts.
  4. The release-day route also allows other nature produce such as budding branches/stems and golden pears; three fungi are simply the most straightforward option.

9. Kill three Ghasts

  1. Walk through Mort Myre with the filled Druid pouch until a Ghast attacks or use the pouch on one.
  2. The pouch's nature harvest makes the otherwise ghostly Ghast visible and attackable.
  3. Kill it, then repeat until you have defeated three Ghasts.
  4. In the release-day guide and build 254 the visible Ghast is combat level 30.

10. Return to the Nature Spirit

  1. After the third Ghast, return to Filliman in the grotto.
  2. Tell him that all three Ghasts have been defeated.
  3. Filliman can now establish his holy sanctuary / Altar of Nature and the quest completes here.
  4. Do not return to Drezel to finish. Drezel has progress dialogue, but Filliman is the completion NPC.

Rewards

  • 2 Quest Points.
  • 3,000 Crafting XP.
  • 2,000 Defence XP.
  • 2,000 Hitpoints XP.
  • Blessed silver sickle.
  • Druid pouch and the ability to use nature harvests to make Ghasts visible and fight them.
Prayer XP is not a Nature Spirit completion reward. Build 254 awards 30 Prayer XP for each credited visible-Ghast kill, so the three quest kills can produce 90 Prayer XP incidentally. The completion script itself awards only the Crafting, Defence and Hitpoints XP listed above.

Useful recovery notes

  • Cannot understand Filliman: make sure the Amulet of ghostspeak is equipped.
  • Lost the Druidic spell before the ritual: Filliman's dialogue can supply another spell scroll while you are on the relevant quest stage.
  • Fungus rejected by the ritual stone in build 254: grow and pick a fresh Mort Myre fungus after reaching the correct ritual stage. The build tracks this quest pickup rather than accepting an arbitrary old fungus for the nature stone.
  • Lost the blessed sickle later: once the sickle-blessing stage has been reached, build 254 lets you bless a replacement plain Silver sickle at the grotto's holy water, provided you do not already have a blessed one.
  • Bridge crossing hurts: there is no hard Agility gate. Heal and try again.
  • Drezel says you have killed all three: return to Filliman. Drezel does not perform the final completion.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Core route and rewards: build 254 implements Drezel → Filliman → mirror/journal → Drezel blessing → bloom fungus → three-stone ritual → Silver sickle → Druid pouch → three Ghasts → Filliman, and awards 3,000 Crafting, 2,000 Defence, 2,000 Hitpoints and 2 Quest Points.
  • 18 Crafting is not a hard build gate: the build journal places it under recommended requirements. Filliman checks for a finished Silver sickle, so a pre-made one bypasses the 18-Crafting crafting action. The 13 July 2004 release-day route instead lists 18 Crafting and instructs the player to make the sickle.
  • Sickle mould: build 254's Al Kharid Crafting Shop stock includes the Sickle mould, matching Filliman's hint about a distant sandy place.
  • Bridge: build 254 uses an Agility success roll rather than a required level. A failed jump can deal 2–7 damage; successful and failed crossings also give small amounts of Agility XP.
  • Ritual item strictness: the nature stone checks for the quest-stage Mort Myre fungus pickup. The spirit stone is more permissive than the clean release-day route and can accept the spell scroll in either its fresh or used form at the relevant state.
  • Swamp decay: outside Filliman's protected camp, build 254 can periodically deal 1–3 damage while you are in Mort Myre without a Blessed silver sickle. Carrying or wearing the blessed sickle prevents this build-specific decay effect.
  • Bloom Prayer cost: build 254 currently removes a random 1–7 Prayer points when the blessed sickle successfully blooms the swamp. The source marks parts of this reconstruction as arbitrary/TODO, so the exact random cost is implementation detail rather than a claimed September-2004 historical mechanic.
  • Druid pouch charges: build 254 values fungus at 1 nature-harvest charge, a budding stem at 2 and a pear at 3. One pouch charge is consumed when an invisible Ghast is materialised.
  • Ghasts: build 254's visible Ghast is level 30 with 45 Hitpoints. An invisible Ghast can rot food in your backpack or drain 1–3 Hitpoints; each credited visible-Ghast kill awards 30 Prayer XP.
  • Nature altar: after completion the grotto becomes an Altar of Nature. In build 254 it recharges Prayer up to two points above the player's base Prayer level and can be used to bless a replacement Silver sickle after the blessing stage.
