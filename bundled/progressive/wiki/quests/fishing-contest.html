The dwarves beneath White Wolf Mountain do not let strangers use their underground passage. One of them offers a bargain: win the annual Hemenster Fishing Contest and bring back its trophy, and the dwarves will treat you as a friend and let you use the tunnel whenever you like.

Requirements

Required quests and levels

  • No prerequisite quests or Quest Point requirement.
  • 10 Fishing. Jagex's 2003 quest manual and a March-2004 guide both list level 10 as the minimum. Build 254 lets you begin the dialogue below 10, but its Hemenster fishing spots refuse to work until you reach level 10.
  • No mandatory combat. The red-vine-worm route passes level-44 guard dogs in McGrubor's Wood, so low-level players should be ready to run past them.

Required items

  • 1 fishing rod. Build 254 specifically checks the ordinary fishing rod used for bait fishing.
  • 1 spade. Used to dig red vine worms in McGrubor's Wood; it is not consumed.
  • 1 clove of garlic. Consumed when placed in the pipe beside the Sinister Stranger's fishing spot.
  • 5 coins. Bonzo charges exactly five coins to enter the contest in build 254.
  • Enough bait for three contest catches. The straightforward route is to collect 3 red vine worms. Build 254 has a looser judging check: one red vine worm can produce the Giant Carp and two ordinary fishing baits can fill the remaining two catches, but three red vine worms avoids relying on that implementation quirk.

Obtained during the quest

  • Fishing Contest Pass — supplied by the Mountain Dwarf after you accept the quest.
  • Red vine worms — dig at the red vines in McGrubor's Wood with a spade. Build 254 gives one worm per dig and lets you gather more.
  • Fishing trophy — awarded by Bonzo when you win; the dwarf takes it when the quest is completed.

Recommended

  • Food and light armour if level-44 guard dogs are dangerous to you.
  • Bring the garlic before paying Bonzo so the contest can be completed in one visit.
  • Keep at least three free inventory spaces for contest fish and quest items.

Before you enter Hemenster

The important trick is not better ordinary bait. Grandpa Jack can explain that the best fish are near the pipes and that red vine worms from McGrubor's Wood are especially good bait. A suspicious entrant called the Sinister Stranger occupies that pipe-side spot, and his dialogue reveals that he cannot stand garlic. Place garlic in the nearby pipe and he moves away, allowing Bonzo to assign you the useful spot.

A surviving March-2004 guide describes Bonzo's first assigned place as beside an oak tree, while build 254 dialogue/journal calls it the willow-tree spot. The exact tree species does not affect the route: it is simply the inferior tree-side fishing position you receive before moving the Sinister Stranger.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Start at White Wolf Mountain

  1. Speak to the Mountain Dwarf guarding either entrance to the underground passage beneath White Wolf Mountain.
  2. Ask about using the tunnel and continue the conversation until the dwarf explains that only friends may pass safely.
  3. Offer to help. The dwarf wants the first-prize trophy from the annual fishing competition at Hemenster, north of the Fishing Guild.
  4. You receive a Fishing Contest Pass. Keep it for the gatekeeper at Hemenster.

2. Learn the winning bait trick

  1. Travel toward Hemenster and speak to Grandpa Jack if you want the in-game clue route.
  2. Ask him about fishing. He explains that the best fish are by the pipes and points you toward red vine worms in McGrubor's Wood.
  3. This conversation is useful but not required by build 254; you can collect the worms and perform the garlic trick without first speaking to him.

3. Collect red vine worms in McGrubor's Wood

  1. Go to McGrubor's Wood, west of Seers' Village and south-west of Hemenster. The main gate is locked.
  2. Walk around to the loose railing on the north side of the wood and squeeze through it.
  3. Be careful of the guard dogs. Build 254 displays them as level 44, matching the danger level recorded by contemporary 2004 guidance.
  4. Go to the red vines on the western side of the wood and use your spade on them.
  5. Collect 3 red vine worms for the simplest build-254 contest route. Each dig gives one worm and you can repeat the action.

4. Enter the Fishing Contest

  1. Go north to Hemenster and speak to Morris at the contest gate.
  2. Show your Fishing Contest Pass. Build 254 checks that you possess the pass but does not consume it.
  3. Inside, speak to Bonzo, the judge, and pay the 5-coin entry fee.
  4. Bonzo assigns you a tree-side fishing position. Do not rely on that position to win; the valuable carp are at the pipe-side spot currently occupied by the Sinister Stranger.

5. Move the Sinister Stranger with garlic

  1. Find the Sinister Stranger beside the fishing spot near the pipes. Talking to him is optional, but he strongly hints that he dislikes garlic.
  2. Use one clove of garlic on the nearby wall pipe.
  3. The garlic is consumed, the smell drives the Stranger away, and Bonzo allows you to use the pipe-side fishing position.

6. Catch the winning fish

  1. Stand at the pipe-side fishing spot with your fishing rod and red vine worms.
  2. Bait the spot. In build 254, a red vine worm at this particular spot catches a Raw giant carp.
  3. Make three catches. Build 254's contest counter judges the result after the third fish.
  4. Using three red vine worms gives three Giant Carp catches and is the cleanest route. The build's hand-in logic actually only checks that at least one Giant Carp is present, so one red worm plus two ordinary fishing baits can also win; treat that as an implementation quirk, not the intended historical strategy.
  5. After the third catch, Bonzo judges the fish and awards you the Fishing trophy.

7. Return the trophy to the dwarf

  1. Return to either Mountain Dwarf at White Wolf Mountain with the Fishing trophy.
  2. The dwarf takes the trophy, accepts you as a friend and permanently opens the underground shortcut to you.
  3. The quest completes immediately.

Combat and hazards

  • No enemy must be killed.
  • McGrubor's Wood guard dogs: level 44 in build 254 and in the contemporary warning used for this guide. Run past them or bring food if needed.
  • White Wolf Mountain: normal travel around the mountain can also expose a low-level player to aggressive creatures. Starting from whichever tunnel entrance is safer for your route avoids unnecessary crossings.

Useful notes and recovery

  • Lost your Fishing Contest Pass: build 254's Mountain Dwarf supplies another one while the quest is in progress if no pass is found in your inventory or bank.
  • Forgot the garlic: leave before committing to your catches, obtain a clove, then return. The Stranger's displacement is controlled by the garlic-pipe state rather than by Grandpa Jack dialogue.
  • Used ordinary bait at the wrong spot: build 254 produces ordinary low-value fish rather than a Giant Carp. The pipe-side position plus a red vine worm is the important combination.
  • Lost the contest: if the judging reaches the end without a Giant Carp, the Stranger wins and the build resets the competition. You must pay Bonzo another 5 coins to enter again.
  • Quit the contest: build 254 can reset your contest progress when you confirm that you are leaving, so expect to pay the entry fee again.
  • Lost the trophy before returning to the dwarf: Bonzo can replace it after you have won. His build-254 replacement check only looks at the inventory, not the bank, so banking the original can allow another to be issued.

Rewards

  • 1 Quest Point.
  • 2,437 Fishing XP.
  • Permanent access to the underground passage beneath White Wolf Mountain. The tunnel lets you move safely between the two sides instead of taking the dangerous surface route.
  • No coin reward. The five-coin contest fee is an expense, and the Fishing trophy is handed to the dwarf rather than kept.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Historical level requirement versus start script: Jagex's 2003 manual and the March-2004 guide both state a 10 Fishing minimum. Build 254's Mountain Dwarf dialogue does not block a sub-10 account from starting, but `hemenster_fishing.rs2` blocks the actual contest fishing below 10. The public requirement remains 10 Fishing.
  • Starter naming: build 254's NPC config displays Mountain Dwarf. Historical databases disagree over exactly when the later Austri/Vestri naming should be applied to the RS2-era NPCs, so this guide uses the period-safe generic name supported directly by the build and older quest material.
  • Contest fee: Bonzo removes exactly 5 coins when the player enters.
  • Three-catch counter: the build advances the competition through three catches before automatic judging. Red vine worms are preferred over ordinary fishing bait whenever both are carried.
  • Catch table: at the pipe-side spot, red vine worm produces Raw giant carp while ordinary bait produces Raw sardine. At the other contest spot, red vine worm produces Raw sardine and ordinary bait produces Raw shrimp.
  • Loose win check: Bonzo's build script declares the player the winner if any Raw giant carp remains at judging time. That means the implementation can be won with one Giant Carp plus two ordinary catches even though gathering three red vine worms is the straightforward intended route.
  • Garlic: using garlic on the pipe removes one clove and moves the Sinister Stranger away. The action can be staged independently of Grandpa Jack's optional clue dialogue.
  • Tunnel unlock: the White Wolf Mountain tunnel stairs only permit the through-route once Fishing Contest is complete.
  • Reward: the quest completion script advances Fishing by 24,370 internal units, which is 2,437 XP under the project's tenths-of-XP convention; the global quest constant awards 1 Quest Point.
