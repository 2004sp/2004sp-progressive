Sir Amik Varze needs an unknown face to infiltrate the Black Knights. They are threatening Falador with a powerful secret weapon, so your job is to enter their fortress, discover what they are preparing, and sabotage it before it can be used.

Requirements

Required quests

  • None.

Required levels and quest points

  • 12 Quest Points are required to start the quest.
  • There is no hard skill-level requirement.
  • You must be able to survive or evade aggressive level 33 Black Knights inside the fortress. No Black Knight has to be killed to complete the quest.

Required items

  • 1 bronze medium helmet — not consumed. It must be worn with the iron chainbody to pass the guarded fortress entrance.
  • 1 iron chainbody — not consumed. It must be worn with the bronze medium helmet to pass the guarded fortress entrance.
  • 1 normal cabbage — consumed when it is dropped into the potion. Do not use a cabbage from Draynor Manor: the witch specifically needs the magical Draynor cabbage, while an ordinary cabbage is what ruins the potion.

Items obtainable during the quest

  • A normal cabbage can be picked from the cabbage patch north of the Monastery, east of Ice Mountain and close to the fortress.

Recommended

  • Food if your combat level is low. Several Black Knights are aggressive and can attack while you follow the interior route.
  • Your normal combat armour and weapon. The guard disguise is needed for the entrance; in build 254 you can change back into stronger gear after getting inside.
  • Take a second normal cabbage if you are worried about accidentally eating the first one.

Before you start

Get the bronze medium helmet and iron chainbody before travelling to the fortress. For the cabbage, the period route uses the patch just north of the Monastery, east of Ice Mountain. Pick an ordinary cabbage there rather than taking one from Draynor Manor.

The fortress is on the north side of Ice Mountain. The dangerous part of the quest is the interior rather than a required boss fight: period guides warn about aggressive level 33 Black Knights, so lower-level players should bring food and be prepared to run between ladders and doors.

Detailed walkthrough

  1. Start with Sir Amik Varze. Go to the White Knights' Castle in Falador and climb to the top floor of the western tower. Speak to Sir Amik Varze and ask for a quest. If you have at least 12 Quest Points, he explains that the Black Knights are demanding money and land and claim to possess a powerful new secret weapon. Agree to infiltrate their fortress, learn what the weapon is, and sabotage it.
  2. Collect a normal cabbage. Travel toward the Monastery east of Ice Mountain. Just north of the Monastery is a cabbage patch. Pick one or two ordinary cabbages. Do not substitute a Draynor Manor cabbage.
  3. Put on the guard disguise. Travel west/north-west to Black Knight's Fortress on the north side of Ice Mountain. Equip the bronze medium helmet and iron chainbody. The guard at the southern/central entrance will refuse you if either piece is missing; his dialogue identifies these two pieces as the proper guard uniform.
  4. Enter through the guarded door. Open the sturdy door on the south side of the fortress while wearing both disguise pieces. Immediately inside, push the wall to reveal the secret passage and climb the ladder.
  5. Climb to the upper route. From the secret passage, climb the next ladder to reach the top floor. Take the ladder down on the south-east side, then open the door to the east and the next door beyond it. Climb the ladder there. You should reach a room containing two cannons and a Black Knight.
  6. Reach the Zamorak altar room. Climb down the ladder on the east side of the cannon room. You arrive in the room with a Zamorak altar. Open the door onward; an aggressive Black Knight can attack you here. Fight only if you want to — running past is sufficient.
  7. Reach the listening grill. Climb the ladder on the south-west side of the altar area into the small room above. Use Listen-at on the grill.
  8. Learn how to sabotage the weapon. You overhear a Black Knight, the resident witch and Greldo the goblin. The witch explains that her invincibility potion is almost complete and needs a specially grown cabbage from Draynor Manor. She also reveals the crucial weakness: any ordinary cabbage would completely wreck the potion.
  9. Return to the fortress's main floor. Go back down through the route you used to reach the grill until you are back near the entrance area, then push through the secret wall again to return to the main section.
  10. Enter the meeting room. Open the door to the east. A Fortress Guard warns that the Black Knights are holding an important meeting and will kill anyone who enters. Choose the option saying that you do not care and are going in anyway. The door opens and Black Knights can attack you.
  11. Reach the hole above the cauldron. Run through the meeting area and climb the ladder. From the upper floor, head east and then south. Push the wall there to find a hidden space containing a hole in the floor.
  12. Sabotage the potion. Use your normal cabbage on the hole. It falls into the cauldron below, causing the potion to froth and fail. If you have not listened at the grill first, the quest will not accept this as the sabotage step.
  13. Return to Sir Amik. Leave the fortress and go back to the White Knights' Castle in Falador. Speak to Sir Amik Varze. Tell him that you ruined the Black Knights' invincibility potion; he confirms that their demands have been withdrawn and pays your reward.

Combat and failure notes

  • Black Knights: period quest guidance identifies the dangerous interior knights as level 33 and recommends food/equipment for running past them. Killing them is not a quest requirement.
  • Guard disguise: both the bronze medium helmet and iron chainbody must be worn to enter through the guarded fortress door. A generic black-coloured item is not a valid disguise.
  • Wrong cabbage: a Draynor Manor cabbage is the magical ingredient the witch actually wants. Build 254 explicitly rejects that cabbage as the wrong sort for sabotage. Get an ordinary cabbage instead.
  • Ate your cabbage: simply pick another ordinary cabbage and return to the hole. There is no need to restart the quest.
  • Cabbage does nothing: listen at the grill first. The sabotage only advances the quest after you have overheard the witch explain the potion's weakness.

Rewards

  • 3 Quest Points.
  • 2,500 coins.

There is no skill-XP reward. Later quests that eventually used Black Knight's Fortress as a prerequisite are outside this guide's September 2004 target unless separately documented for their own release date.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Start gate: build 254 checks for at least 12 Quest Points before Sir Amik will offer the mission, matching the period guide and quest journal.
  • Exact disguise check: the guarded entrance requires a bronze medium helmet in the head slot and an iron chainbody in the torso slot. The old local guide's suggestion that any black item could serve as a disguise was incorrect.
  • Quest-state order: build 254 requires you to listen at the grill before the ordinary cabbage can sabotage the potion. The normal cabbage advances the quest; the Draynor Manor magic cabbage is explicitly rejected.
  • Aggression: entering the altar-side route and the meeting room can force nearby aggressive Black Knights onto the player in build 254. Bring food if you are low level rather than assuming you can stand safely in those rooms.
  • Reward: the completion script gives exactly 2,500 coins and the quest-point constant gives 3 Quest Points, matching the preserved period guide.
