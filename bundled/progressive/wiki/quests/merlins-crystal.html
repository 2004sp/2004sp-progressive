King Arthur will admit you to the Knights of the Round Table if you can rescue Merlin, who has been trapped inside a giant crystal. The search leads from Camelot to Keep Le Faye, where Morgan Le Faye reveals that freeing Merlin requires Excalibur, a black candle, bat bones and the binding words hidden at a Wilderness Chaos Temple.

Requirements

  • Members' world.
  • No prerequisite quest, Quest Point requirement or hard skill level.
  • Enough combat ability to defeat Sir Mordred. A target-proximate April 2004 RuneScape 2 guide and build 254 both identify him as level 39.

20 Attack is not required to complete Merlin's Crystal. Build 254 requires 20 Attack only to wield Excalibur. The quest's crystal interaction can use Excalibur directly from your inventory.

Items and preparation

Bring or collect before the ritual

  • 1 bread — consumed when you give it to the beggar during the Lady of the Lake's test.
  • 1 insect repellent — used on a beehive to make it safe to collect wax; build 254 does not consume it.
  • 1 tinderbox — used to light the black candle; not consumed.
  • 1 set of bat bones — required for the summoning ritual. You can bring them or kill a giant bat near Keep Le Faye. Build 254 does not remove the bones during the successful ritual.
  • Weapon, armour and food for Sir Mordred. These are recommendations rather than coded quest-item requirements.

Obtained during the quest

  • Empty bucket — a bucket can be taken from a crate inside Keep Le Faye if you did not bring one.
  • Bucket of wax — use insect repellent on a beehive, then use an empty bucket on it. The candle maker consumes the wax.
  • Black candle — the Catherby candle maker exchanges a bucket of wax for one; light it with a tinderbox.
  • Excalibur — obtained from the Lady of the Lake after completing her Port Sarim test.
Wilderness warning: the binding words used by this September-2004/build-254 route are at the Chaos Temple in roughly level 12 Wilderness north of Varrock. Bank valuables you do not need before making that trip.

Detailed walkthrough

1. Ask King Arthur to make you a knight

  1. Go to Camelot Castle and speak to King Arthur.
  2. Ask about joining the Knights of the Round Table.
  3. Arthur says that you can prove yourself by rescuing Merlin, who has been trapped inside a crystal.

2. Question Sir Gawain, then Sir Lancelot

  1. Find Sir Gawain in Camelot and ask about Merlin. He suspects Morgan Le Faye and tells you that her stronghold to the south is guarded by renegade knights led by Sir Mordred.
  2. Next speak to Sir Lancelot. He explains that the keep's main entrance is difficult to breach, but that supplies are delivered from Catherby.
  3. Do these conversations in this order. Build 254 only enables the delivery-crate route after the Gawain and Lancelot stages have both been completed.

3. Smuggle yourself into Keep Le Faye

  1. Travel to Catherby.
  2. Find the delivery crate behind the candle shop, west of the bank.
  3. Choose to hide inside the crate. The delivery is loaded onto Arhein's ship and carried to Keep Le Faye.
  4. Climb out when the crate arrives. The keep's front doors are locked from the outside, so the crate is the intended entry route.
  5. If you need an empty bucket, take one from the bucket crate inside the keep before leaving.

4. Fight Sir Mordred and bargain with Morgan Le Faye

  1. Climb through the keep to the upper floor and confront Sir Mordred.
  2. Fight him until Morgan Le Faye appears and begs you to spare her son.
  3. Choose the bargain equivalent to “Tell me how to untrap Merlin and I might.”
  4. Morgan explains the complete method: summon the spirit Thrantax at the magic symbol near Camelot by dropping bat bones while carrying a lit black candle, bind the spirit with words found on a Chaos altar, then use Excalibur to shatter Merlin's crystal.
Do not refuse Morgan's bargain. Choosing to finish Mordred does not advance the quest to the ritual stage. If that happens, return and trigger the Mordred/Morgan encounter again, then take the bargain.

5. Get bat bones and return to Catherby

  1. If you did not bring bat bones, kill a giant bat near Keep Le Faye and take its bones.
  2. You can use the keep's return delivery crate to travel back to Catherby, or leave by another available route after opening your way out.

6. Make and light the black candle

  1. Speak to the candle maker in Catherby after learning Morgan's ritual. Ask for a black candle. He agrees only if you bring him a bucket full of wax.
  2. If needed, obtain insect repellent from the house north of Catherby bank.
  3. Go to the beehives west of Catherby.
  4. Use the insect repellent on a hive. Then use an empty bucket on the treated hive to obtain a bucket of wax.
  5. Return to the candle maker. He takes the wax and gives you an unlit black candle.
  6. Use a tinderbox on the candle so you have a lit black candle.

7. Prove yourself to the Lady of the Lake and obtain Excalibur

  1. Travel to the Lady of the Lake by the lake south-east of Taverley and ask for Excalibur.
  2. She refuses to hand over the sword immediately and tells you that she must test whether you are worthy.
  3. Take 1 bread to the jeweller's shop in Port Sarim.
  4. When you enter, a beggar appears and asks for food. Give him the bread.
  5. The beggar reveals herself as the Lady of the Lake and rewards your generosity with Excalibur.

You do not need 20 Attack for this quest step. Keep Excalibur in your inventory if you cannot wield it.

8. Learn the binding words at the Wilderness Chaos Temple

  1. Bank unnecessary valuables, then travel to the Chaos Temple north of Varrock in roughly level 12 Wilderness.
  2. Find the quest altar and use its Check interaction.
  3. You discover an inscription at the bottom of the altar:

Snarthon Candtrick Termanto

Remember the spelling exactly. Build 254 records that you have learned the words when you check this altar after Morgan's instructions.

9. Summon and bind Thrantax

  1. Return to Camelot and find the magic symbol/pentagram just north-east of Camelot Castle.
  2. Make sure the lit black candle is in your inventory and that you have learned the altar inscription.
  3. Stand on the symbol and drop the bat bones. Thrantax appears.
  4. The three incantations are shown in a random order. Select Snarthon Candtrick Termanto.
  5. Thrantax is bound and grants your request. Merlin's crystal can now be shattered with Excalibur.
Wrong incantation: build 254 deletes your lit black candle and makes Thrantax attack. The summoned spirit is visibly level 92 in the build. Escape or defeat it, make and light another black candle, then repeat the ritual with the correct words.

10. Shatter Merlin's crystal

  1. Return to Camelot Castle and climb to Merlin's giant crystal on the upper level of the south-east tower.
  2. Use Excalibur on the crystal. In build 254 the sword does not have to be equipped; using the inventory item is enough.
  3. The crystal shatters and Merlin is freed. He tells you to report back to King Arthur.

11. Report to King Arthur

  1. Speak to King Arthur again.
  2. Tell him that Merlin is free. Arthur welcomes you as a Knight of the Round Table and the quest completes.

Combat and danger notes

  • Sir Mordred is the mandatory fight. The April-2004 RS2 guide and build 254 both use combat level 39.
  • Build-254 renegade knights are level 37. They guard Keep Le Faye, but defeating them is not the quest objective.
  • Thrantax is only a punishment fight. The level-92 spirit attacks if you choose either wrong incantation. The correct words avoid that fight entirely.
  • The Chaos Temple step enters the Wilderness. Carry only what you need for the altar trip and keep the exact incantation written down before returning to Camelot.

Rewards and unlocks

  • 6 Quest Points.
  • Excalibur. You obtain it during the quest and keep it afterward.
  • Knight of the Round Table status.
  • Progression to Heroes' Quest. Build 254 directly checks Merlin's Crystal as one of its named prerequisites.
  • Access to Holy Grail. King Arthur only offers that quest after Merlin's Crystal is complete.

Merlin's Crystal awards no skill XP and no coins in build 254. The completion script awards 6 Quest Points and displays Excalibur, but contains no stat-advance or coin grant.

Useful recovery notes

  • Lost your route into Keep Le Faye? After speaking to Gawain and Lancelot, use the Catherby delivery crate again. The front door is deliberately locked from outside.
  • Need another black candle after a wrong chant? Return to the Catherby candle maker, ask for another black candle and repeat the wax process. Build 254 permits this while the quest remains at Morgan's ritual stage.
  • Lost Excalibur? The Lady of the Lake can put you through the acquisition path again while the quest is still at the relevant stage. Build 254 also has a later replacement route costing 500 coins once the player has progressed far enough.
  • Cannot wield Excalibur? You do not need to. Use the sword directly on Merlin's crystal from the inventory.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Quest state order: build 254 advances through Arthur → Gawain → Lancelot → Morgan's bargain → Thrantax bound → Merlin freed → Arthur completion. The delivery crate and later ritual interactions are state-gated to this order.
  • Mordred level: Jagex's December-2003 RuneScape Classic manual describes a level-58 enemy, but the target-proximate April-2004 RuneScape 2 guide gives level 39 and build 254 also displays Sir Mordred as level 39. The player guide therefore uses 39 for the RS2 target.
  • Chaos altar location: build 254 places the quest-specific thrantaxaltar object in the Wilderness Chaos Temple at approximately 3239,3608, matching the map's Chaos Temple label. Modern routes that send you to a different Chaos altar do not match this build.
  • Ritual: the correct phrase is always Snarthon Candtrick Termanto, although its position in the three-choice menu is random. A wrong choice removes one lit black candle and makes level-92 Thrantax retaliate. The successful path does not delete the bat bones or lit candle in the current script.
  • Excalibur: its normal equipment handler requires 20 Attack, but Merlin's crystal uses an item-on-location interaction and therefore does not require the sword to be worn.
  • Completion: using Excalibur on the bound crystal frees Merlin; speaking to Arthur then sets the quest complete and awards 6 Quest Points. The numeric 250 passed to the completion interface is not a skill-XP reward.
