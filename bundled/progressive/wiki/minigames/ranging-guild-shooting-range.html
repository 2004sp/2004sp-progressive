The Ranging Guild's target competition is a members-only shooting activity inside the guild east of Hemenster. Pay the Competition Judge 200 coins, receive 10 bronze arrows, equip your own bow, and fire ten shots at the competition targets. Each shot can score 0, 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100 points and awards Ranged XP equal to half of the points scored. When all ten shots are finished, the judge converts the final score into one Archery ticket for every 10 points. Tickets can then be spent with the Ticket Merchant on the six fixed rewards documented below.

Historical/build split: the guild and competition existed before the 7 September 2004 cutoff. Build 254 reconstructs the period competition and deliberately preserves the old Ticket Merchant interaction style: the NPC still has a Talk-to option rather than the later mid-2005 Trade option. The build's target-hit probability is reconstructed from later documented behaviour, so the exact random accuracy formula is a project implementation detail rather than a proven September-2004 server formula.

Getting there

The Ranging Guild is in Kandarin, east of Hemenster, between Seers' Village and East Ardougne. Enter through the guild door beside the Ranging Guild Doorman. The target competition and its Competition Judge are on the eastern side of the guild; the Ticket Merchant is in the western side.

  • From Seers' Village: walk south-west from the village toward Hemenster, then continue to the guild immediately east of Hemenster. This is a straightforward period-valid route.
  • From East Ardougne: leave the city to the north-west and follow the road toward Hemenster and Seers' Village; the Ranging Guild is east of Hemenster before reaching Seers' Village.
  • From Camelot: Camelot Teleport was already available by the target date. From Camelot, walk west into Seers' Village and then south-west to the guild. This is optional convenience, not an activity requirement.

How to start and play

  1. Bring 200 coins and an equipable bow into the Ranging Guild.
  2. Talk to the Competition Judge and agree to enter. The judge removes 200 coins and gives you 10 bronze arrows.
  3. Equip your bow and the supplied bronze arrows.
  4. Stand behind the shooting line/haystack. Build 254 refuses a shot if you are fewer than five tiles from the target.
  5. Use Fire-at on a target. The game consumes one bronze arrow, rolls a target region, adds the corresponding score and Ranged XP, and opens the target-view interface showing the result and running score.
  6. Repeat until all 10 shots have been fired. The Competition Judge can tell you your current score during the attempt.
  7. After the tenth shot, talk to the judge. If the judge is nearby when you try to fire again, build 254 can also trigger the payout from that interaction.
  8. Your total score is divided by 10 using integer division. You receive that many Archery tickets, and the attempt's score/count state is reset so you can pay for another round.

Target scoring and Ranged XP

Where you click on the target does not select the ring. Build 254 rolls the result from the player's Ranged attack value and then maps it to one of the target regions below. A miss gives neither points nor XP.

Because every non-zero score is a multiple of ten, the final conversion is exact for normal play: Archery tickets = total score ÷ 10. The theoretical maximum from ten bull's-eyes is 1,000 points, 500 Ranged XP and 100 Archery tickets for one 200-coin attempt.

Success, failure, leaving and recovery

  • Success condition: there is no minimum score. Completing ten shots ends the attempt; the score determines the ticket payout.
  • All misses: a score of zero produces zero tickets, then the build resets the attempt normally.
  • Running out of arrows: if the attempt is still active and you have no bronze arrows in inventory or equipped, the judge offers 10 more for 100 coins. This is recovery stock, not an extra entry fee.
  • No bow equipped: the target refuses to fire. Equip a bow and try again; the attempt is not cancelled.
  • Too close to the target: move back behind the haystack/shooting line and retry.
  • Leaving / logging out: build 254 stores targetcount, targetscore and targethit as permanent player variables and has no zone-exit reset. An unfinished attempt therefore persists in the implementation rather than being forfeited merely by leaving the range.
  • Death: the shooting activity itself deals no damage and defines no safe-death override. Any unrelated death follows ordinary build-254 death/item-loss rules.
  • Multiplayer: score and attempt state are player-specific. The physical targets are shared world objects, but one player firing does not consume or lock a target for another player.

Archery tickets

The build item is named Archery ticket. It is members-only and stackable. The Competition Judge's final dialogue calls the payout “Archery Coupons”, but the actual inventory item, Ticket Merchant interface and purchase messages all use Archery ticket(s); the guide follows the item name rather than the dialogue inconsistency.

Tickets are the only persistent competition currency. There is no coin payout, random prize roll, reward cooldown or one-time unlock. You can repeat the 200-coin competition and save tickets across rounds until you can afford a reward.

Ticket Merchant reward exchange

The Ticket Merchant runs a fixed ticket exchange in the western part of the Ranging Guild. It is not a stock-based shop: the custom interface has no stock quantities, depletion or restock timer. Every purchase simply checks your Archery-ticket total, removes the fixed cost and adds the fixed item quantity.

There are no XP-for-ticket options, no random/grab-bag rewards, no coin rewards, no stock limits and no reward-specific purchase checks beyond owning enough tickets and having enough inventory space. Equipment requirements apply when using or wearing the item, not when buying it.

Post-cutoff exclusions: do not substitute modern Ranging Guild prices or later interfaces for this table. In particular, later RuneScape reduced several ticket prices, and the Ticket Merchant's direct Trade option replaced the old Talk-to interaction after the target snapshot. Build 254 intentionally uses the older six-reward, 100/140/150/2,000/2,000/2,400-ticket table.

Other Ranging Guild content is separate

The guild contains ordinary bow, armour and throwing-weapon shops, a Leatherworker, guards and a central practice tower. Those are useful guild features but are not rewards from the shooting competition. The practice tower is also a separate combat activity: climbing its ladder spawns groups of Tower Archers, and the Tower Advisors recommend a tower according to Ranged level. It does not feed the target-competition ticket state audited on this page.

Build deviations and reconstruction notes

  • Accuracy formula: the target script explicitly says its accuracy data comes from later OSRS/wiki material. The ring values, ten-shot structure, fee and ticket conversion are well-attested period mechanics; the exact random roll should be treated as build reconstruction.
  • Persistent unfinished attempts: competition variables are permanent and there is no exit reset, so build 254 allows the state of a partially completed attempt to survive leaving or logging out.
  • Ticket/Coupon wording: the payout dialogue says “Archery Coupons”, while the actual object and exchange call the currency “Archery ticket”. This is a build text inconsistency, not a second currency.
  • Ticket Merchant interaction: the build source notes that the NPC's Talk-to option was replaced by Trade in mid-2005; build 254 keeps Talk-to and opens the exchange directly because the exact old dialogue is unknown.
  • Ordinary guild shops: those shared stock inventories do restock, but that behaviour must not be incorrectly projected onto the fixed ticket exchange.

Historical evidence and cutoff notes

  • Ranging Guild historical records: place the guild east of Hemenster, identify it as members-only and date the original RS2-beta form to 21 January 2004, well before the 7 September cutoff.
  • Competition Judge records and preserved guides: verify the 200-coin entry, ten supplied bronze arrows, ten shots, one ticket per ten points and the black/blue/red/yellow/bull's-eye score ladder.
  • Preserved early-style Ranging Guild guides: record 5/10/15/25/50 Ranged XP for the five successful target regions.
  • Ticket Merchant / old exchange tables: preserve the six target-era rewards and the 100, 140, 150, 2,000, 2,000 and 2,400 ticket prices implemented by build 254.
  • Barb bolttip history: barbed bolt tips were added on 3 March 2004, so the 30-tip reward is valid for the September cutoff.
  • Build-254 source: all scripts, configs and interfaces under Content/scripts/minigames/game_ranging/, plus the build's existing Ranged skill/equipment requirements used to interpret the reward equipment.
