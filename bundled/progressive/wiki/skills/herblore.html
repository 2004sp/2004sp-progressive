Herblore is the members-only skill used to identify herbs and combine them with vials of water and secondary ingredients to make potions. This guide targets RuneScape as it stood on 7 September 2004: it keeps the contemporary unidentified-herb system and period potion names, includes the July and September 2004 additions that had already arrived, and separates the reconstructed build-254 mechanics wherever they disagree with surviving period material.

Starting Herblore

Historical route: contemporary RuneScape 2 material presents Druidic Ritual as the way to begin Herblore. The quest awards 250 Herblore XP, taking a fresh level-1 skill to level 3, and Kaqemeex explains herbs, vials, secondary ingredients and the pestle and mortar.

2004scape build-254 difference: the generic herb-identifying and potion-brewing routines do not check Druidic Ritual completion. They check membership and the player's current Herblore level. The build source even notes this missing quest gate when explaining why sub-level-3 Herblore accounts can exist. Treat Druidic Ritual as the historical access route, not as a universal mechanical gate in build 254.

How Herblore works

  • Identify herbs: herbs normally arrive as unidentified herbs in the 2004 system. Use the inventory Identify action once your Herblore level meets the herb's threshold. The later "grimy" and "clean" terminology did not replace this system until years after this cutoff.
  • Make an unfinished potion: use an identified herb with a vial of water. Build 254 checks the potion's Herblore level here and does not award XP for this first mixing step.
  • Add the secondary ingredient: use the required secondary ingredient on the unfinished potion. This produces the finished three-dose potion and awards the recipe's Herblore XP.
  • Grind ingredients when needed: a pestle and mortar turns Unicorn horns into Unicorn horn dust and Blue dragon scales into Dragon scale dust in build 254. Period guides describe the same preparation for antipoison and dragon-related recipes.
  • Decant partial potions: build 254 lets two partial potions of the same type be combined. The result is filled up to four doses, with any excess left as a remainder potion. A four-dose potion itself cannot be used as an input to the build's decant routine.

Period training approach

The April-2004 guide describes Herblore as a gradual, supply-limited skill rather than an activity with a fixed modern efficiency route. Its beginner advice is to complete the introductory quest, collect unidentified herbs from monsters such as druids, identify every herb you can, and turn early Guam leaves into Attack potions before moving to Marrentill and antipoison at level 5.

2004scape implementation notes

  • No universal Druidic Ritual gate: build 254's generic identify and brew routines check membership and Herblore level but not the Druidic Ritual quest state. This is the most important correction to the old local page.
  • Guam can expose the low-level edge case: the Guam item has no explicit identification-level parameter at ref 254 even though the level-up popup advertises it at level 3. That lines up with the build source's explicit comment about sub-level-3 Herblore accounts.
  • The popup is incomplete: it omits Toadflax at 30, Snapdragon at 59, three of the five Jungle Potion herbs and quest recipes such as level-25 Blamish oil.
  • Weapon-poison XP differs materially: the 24 April 2004 guide records 137.5 XP; build ref 254 awards 127.5 XP. A newer local Content checkout contains 137.5, but that later correction is not backported into the build-254 audit.
  • Other retained conflicts: build 254 uses Tarromin identification at 11 rather than the April guide's 10, Torstol identification at 75 rather than 77, Anti-firebreath at 69 rather than 68, and 106.3 XP for Super antipoison rather than the April guide's 105.
  • Decanting is implemented: compatible partial doses can be combined up to four doses, with excess preserved in the second vial. Four-dose inputs are rejected by the build routine.
  • Terminology quirk: the build level-up popup says "Zamorak Brews" at 78 while the target-period item is still named Zamorak potion.

Cutoff and uncertainty notes

Do not let later Herblore content leak backward into build 254. The active Content repository contains recipes from later 2004 quests, so this audit uses the project's exact 254 ref for implementation values.

  • 27 July 2004 is included: Toadflax, Snapdragon, Agility potion and Super restore are already inside the cutoff.
  • 1 September 2004 is included: Energy and Super energy potions are inside the cutoff; the same update expanded Super restore so it also restores non-combat stats.
  • 7 September 2004 is included: Magic potion and Potato cactus arrive on the cutoff date and are therefore part of this guide.
  • Post-cutoff quest recipes are excluded from the core guide: later September/October additions visible in newer source revisions — including recipes tied to quests released after 7 September — are not treated as target-period Herblore content.
  • Period/build disagreements stay visible: the audit does not assume every reconstruction value is a correction to surviving 2004 documentation, nor that every April value remained unchanged through September.
