Prayer is trained principally by burying bones and spent by activating temporary combat or restoration effects. At the 7 September 2004 cutoff the standard prayer book contains 15 prayers, all available to free-to-play players: it begins with Thick Skin at level 1 and ends with Protect from Melee at level 43. Later prayers must not be projected backward into this guide.

How Prayer works

Basic loop: bury bones to gain Prayer XP, activate prayers when their level requirement is met, and recharge spent Prayer points at an altar. Active prayers drain current Prayer points but do not reduce the player's base Prayer level.

  • Burying bones gives fixed XP: build 254 removes one bone and awards the item's configured bone_exp. There is no success roll and no Prayer level requirement to bury the ordinary bone types listed below.
  • Prayer points are a consumable pool: the maximum ordinary pool follows the player's Prayer level. Activating one or more prayers gradually drains current points; when the pool reaches zero the build deactivates the prayer effects.
  • Ordinary altars recharge, not train: the generic build-254 altar interaction restores current Prayer to the base Prayer level. It does not consume bones or award Prayer XP.
  • Stat prayers use three tiers: Defence, Strength and Attack each have +5%, +10% and +15% prayers. Only one prayer from the same stat family can remain active at once.
  • Rapid Restore and Rapid Heal affect regeneration: build 254 halves the corresponding regeneration timer from 100 to 50 while the prayer is active. Rapid Restore applies to restoring lowered stats other than Hitpoints; Rapid Heal applies to natural Hitpoints regeneration.
  • Protect Item keeps one additional item on death: build 254 normally protects three high-value items when unskulled and none when skulled; Protect Item adds one more protected item in either case.
  • The three overhead protection prayers conflict: Protect from Magic, Protect from Missiles and Protect from Melee cannot be active together. In build 254, the ordinary NPC combat handlers reduce matching protected hits to zero; PvP handlers reduce the matching maximum hit by 40%. Special encounter scripts can override or modify that ordinary behaviour.
  • Prayer bonus slows drain: equipped Prayer bonus increases build 254's drain-resistance value from a base 60 by two points per Prayer-bonus point. It changes how long the same Prayer-point pool lasts; it does not raise the Prayer level requirement of any prayer.

Training at the cutoff

Prayer training in the September-2004 snapshot is much simpler than modern guides suggest: bury bones, collect higher-XP bones when practical, and use one-time quest rewards. This guide deliberately does not import later altar-multiplication methods or modern XP/hour assumptions.

No invented XP/hour: September-2004 Prayer rates depend on how the bones are obtained, banking, monster competition, travel and player combat strength. The page records verified XP per bone and quest rewards rather than presenting modern market-based or click-optimised hourly rates as period fact.

Monastery / Prayer Guild

The Monastery west of Edgeville is the cutoff skill's special level-gated area. Build 254 models admission as joining Abbot Langley's order rather than as a generic door check.

  • Requirement: 31 base Prayer. The current script uses stat_base(prayer), so temporary Prayer boosts do not satisfy the admission check in build 254.
  • Membership: no members-map restriction is attached to the ladder/join sequence; the level-31 unlock is therefore treated as free-to-play.
  • Admission is persistent: joining sets %prayer_guild = 1, after which the player may climb to the upper floor without repeating the level dialogue.
  • Special altar: the upstairs monks' altar calls the recharge routine with base Prayer +2, temporarily restoring the current pool two points above the normal base maximum. Ordinary altars elsewhere call the routine at the base Prayer value.

2004scape implementation notes

Core implementation: scripts/skill_prayer/configs/prayers.dbrow defines the 15 prayers and level requirements; prayers.enum supplies drain effects; individual prayer scripts handle conflicts; prayer.rs2 handles drain/deactivation; bury_bone.rs2 and bones.obj handle training; and the combat/death scripts apply the actual prayer effects.

  • 15/15 cutoff book: the database runs from Thick Skin through Protect from Melee with no level-46/49/52 entries. This matches the corrected historical cutoff rather than the old page's 15/18 framing.
  • Old page omitted Thick Skin: the previous local level table began at level 4 because it effectively mirrored level-up popup milestones. The rewrite restores the level-1 starting prayer.
  • Old Priest in Peril note removed: the previous page incorrectly treated Retribution, Redemption and Smite as September-2004 Priest in Peril prayers missing from the build. Protect from Melee was still the highest-level prayer at the cutoff, so that note was anachronistic.
  • Protection implementation: ordinary NPC melee/ranged handlers set matching protected damage to zero, while PvP melee/ranged handlers scale the matching maximum hit to 60% (a 40% reduction). Encounter-specific scripts can contain their own protection logic, so the generic rule should not be assumed for every scripted boss or hazard.
  • Prayer bonus implementation: equip.rs2 sets drain resistance to 60 + 2 × prayerbonus. The guide's base drain timings are therefore build-derived rather than copied from a modern prayer calculator.
  • Bat-bone discrepancy: build 254 awards 4.5 Prayer XP for bat bones. Later preserved RS2/Old School lineage records 5.3, so the public table exposes the disagreement instead of silently rewriting either source.
  • Current checkout contains later bone content: Tai Bwo Wannai Trio bone rows are present in the source tree but belong after the 7 September cutoff and are excluded from the historical training progression.

Cutoff exclusions

Do not use a modern Prayer guide as the September-2004 prayer book. Several familiar training systems, prayers and rewards were introduced later.

  • Retribution, Redemption, Smite and later prayer additions: they are outside this page's 15-prayer cutoff set. The historical progression here ends at level 43 Protect from Melee.
  • Ectofuntus: introduced with the Port Phasmatys/Ghosts Ahoy content on 15 February 2005, so bone grinding, slime and four-times Prayer XP do not belong in a 7 September 2004 training route.
  • Player-owned-house altars: Construction and its chapel altar training are later content and are not projected backward into the cutoff.
  • Prayer skillcape: skillcapes were introduced in 2006; level 99 at this cutoff is simply the skill maximum, not a cape unlock.
