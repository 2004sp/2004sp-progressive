Fishing catches raw fish and other objects from fishing spots. Each method needs the correct tool and, where applicable, bait or feathers. Successful catches award Fishing XP and can then feed directly into Cooking. At the 7 September 2004 cutoff the progression runs from Shrimps at level 1 to Manta rays from the members-only Fishing Trawler at level 81.

How Fishing works

Basic loop: use the appropriate option on a fishing spot while carrying its tool. Build 254 checks the player's current Fishing level, verifies the tool and any bait, rolls for success, then adds the catch and Fishing XP. Fishing bait and feathers are consumed only when a catch succeeds.

  • One spot can hold several catches: the available catch pool expands with level. Small-net sea spots start with Shrimps and add Anchovies at 15; bait sea spots start with Sardines and add Herring at 10; fly-fishing starts with Trout and adds Salmon at 30; harpooning starts with Tuna and adds Swordfish at 50.
  • Tools are method-specific: a small net, fishing rod, fly-fishing rod, lobster pot, harpoon, big net and oily fishing rod are not interchangeable. Rod fishing also needs the correct consumable bait.
  • Current level matters in build 254: ordinary catch checks, Fishing Guild entry and Fishing Trawler reward checks read the current Fishing stat. This means temporary boosts can matter where the historical activity also allowed them.
  • Fishing and Cooking are separate checks: catching a fish does not imply the player can cook it. Cooking level, fire/range access and burn chance are handled by the Cooking skill.
  • Members methods add non-fish catches: big-net fishing can produce Mackerel, Cod, Bass, oysters, caskets, seaweed and low-value junk rather than a single predictable fish.

No invented XP/hour: catch XP is known, but period-correct hourly rates depend on success chance, travel, banking, bait supply, fishing-spot behaviour and player interaction. This guide therefore avoids presenting modern XP/hour figures as September-2004 fact.

Fishing Guild

The members-only Fishing Guild is north of West Ardougne. The normal entrance requirement is 68 Fishing. A July 2004 guide explicitly records that a Fishing potion raises Fishing by 3 and permits entry from base level 65 while boosted; build 254 likewise checks the current Fishing stat rather than an unboosted base value.

  • Facilities: fishing platforms, a bank, Fishing shop and range are all recorded by the July-2004 guide.
  • Catch variety: the guild supports several sea methods, including members big-net fishing as well as ordinary high-level catches.
  • Boost warning: reaching the door with a boosted 68 is enough in build 254. If the boost drops before trying to enter, the door check fails again.

Fishing Trawler

The Fishing Trawler is a members activity at Port Khazard, between Yanille and Ardougne. Period evidence and build 254 both use 15 Fishing as the minimum level to receive fish from the trip. The activity is cooperative: the crew must keep Murphy's boat afloat and its net working long enough to return with a catch.

2004scape implementation notes

Core implementation: scripts/skill_fishing/scripts/fishing.rs2 handles equipment and ordinary catch rolls; the individual fishing-spot scripts set level gates and catch pools; fishing.struct supplies ordinary fish XP/success values; levelup_unlocks_fishing.rs2 and skill_guide.dbrow provide the in-game unlock displays.

  • Post-cutoff Karambwan data: the current checkout contains Karambwanji at 5 and Karambwan at 65 plus Tai Bwo Wannai Trio state checks. That quest released 14 September 2004, so neither catch belongs in the 7 September historical unlock table.
  • Trawler level-guide mismatch: skill_guide.dbrow lists Sea turtle at 78 and Manta ray at 80 and even contains a TODO questioning the Sea-turtle value. levelup_unlocks_fishing.rs2, trawler_win.rs2 and the July-2004 RS2 guide all support 79 and 81, so the player-facing table uses 79/81.
  • Trawler XP mismatch inside the build: generic fishing.struct entries contain 57 XP for Sea turtle and 69 for Manta ray, but the actual Trawler reward script directly awards 105 and 115 XP. Because the Trawler script is the path that creates those rewards, this guide records 105/115 as build behaviour.
  • Lava-eel conflict: the 14 July 2004 RS2 guide records 30 Fishing XP for Lava eel, while build 254's fishing_struct_lava_eel gives 60. The discrepancy remains unresolved and is shown openly in the XP table.
  • Big-net seaweed discrepancy: the July-2004 guide records 1 Fishing XP for Seaweed. Build 254 calls stat_advance(fishing, 1); under the project's tenths-of-XP convention that is only 0.1 XP.
  • Later-source casket loot: the build's current casket-opening drop rates explicitly cite modern Old School RuneScape sources. Those rates are not used here as evidence for September-2004 casket contents.
  • Lava equipment loss: build 254 destroys a normal net, big net, harpoon, ordinary fishing rod or fly-fishing rod if it is deliberately used on the Lava-eel spot. This is a source-backed implementation behaviour; use the oily fishing rod instead.

Cutoff exclusions

Later Fishing content can exist in the current checkout without belonging to the target snapshot. Release chronology takes priority over mere source-tree presence.

  • Karambwanji and Karambwan: tied to Tai Bwo Wannai Trio, released 14 September 2004, after the cutoff.
  • Cave eels and later Lumbridge Swamp Cave catches: later quest/cave content is not part of the 7 September progression.
  • Barbarian Fishing, monkfish and other later training systems: these were introduced well after the target date and are not substituted for period training routes.
