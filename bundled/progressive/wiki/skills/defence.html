Defence is a free-to-play combat skill that makes hostile attacks less likely to land and gates many armour pieces. It does not directly subtract damage from a successful hit: higher Defence and stronger defensive equipment instead improve the roll used to avoid being hit. At the 7 September 2004 cutoff, the main milestones run from steel at level 5 through rune at 40 and the period Dragon armour pieces at 60, with several body-slot items also requiring Ranged levels or quest completion.

Mechanics

Direct melee training: choose a defensive melee style. A normal defensive hit awards 4 Defence XP per point of damage and applies an invisible +3 Defence-level modifier while that style is selected. A controlled style instead shares combat XP between Attack, Strength and Defence, awarding roughly 1.33 XP in each skill per point of damage and applying an invisible +1 modifier to all three melee skills.

  • Avoiding hits: Defence level combines with the relevant equipment defence bonus when an opponent attacks. A higher defensive roll makes a zero-damage miss more likely; it does not reduce the size of a hit that has already succeeded.
  • Different attack types: armour carries separate defensive bonuses against stab, slash, crush, Ranged and Magic attacks. Melee and Ranged defence use Defence directly; player Magic defence is unusual because Magic level also contributes strongly alongside Defence and the equipment's Magic-defence bonus.
  • Melee styles: defensive styles train Defence directly; controlled styles divide XP between Attack, Strength and Defence.
  • Ranged longrange: the longrange style splits damage XP between Ranged and Defence, awarding 2 Ranged XP and 2 Defence XP per point of damage plus normal Hitpoints XP.
  • Hitpoints XP: damaging combat that trains Defence also awards Hitpoints experience separately.
  • Temporary boosts: Defence-boosting potions and prayers can improve effective Defence in combat. This guide does not treat temporary boosts as equipment unlocks; armour rows show the normal base-level requirements used for equipping items.

Training methods

For repeatable training, Defence is primarily a combat-style choice. Use enemies you can hit consistently and sustain safely; slower kills against excessively defensive targets usually mean slower Defence XP.

Quest and progression links

  • Dragon Slayer is the most important free-to-play Defence quest: it gives 18,650 Defence XP and is an explicit equip gate for the Rune platebody and Green dragonhide body.
  • Holy Grail provides a large one-time 15,300 Defence XP reward.
  • Heroes' Quest adds 3,075 Defence XP among its broad combat and skill rewards.
  • Nature Spirit awards 2,000 Defence XP as part of its completion reward.
  • Observatory Quest can randomly award 875 Defence XP when Virgo is selected.
  • Legends' Quest can award selectable Defence XP and is the quest gate for the level-60 Dragon square shield.

2004scape implementation notes

The repository's already-audited quest guides establish the build-254 checks used here: Dragon Slayer gives 18,650 Defence XP and gates the Rune platebody and Green dragonhide body; Heroes' Quest gives 3,075 Defence XP; Nature Spirit gives 2,000 Defence XP; Observatory Quest can give 875 Defence XP for Virgo; and Legends' Quest permits Defence as a 7,650-XP choice while separately gating the Dragon square shield at 60 Defence.

The old Defence page only repeated the metal level-up messages and two quest gates. This audit adds the missing Hardleather and Studded body requirements, all four period dragonhide-body Defence gates, actual training mechanics, verified quest XP, and the release-day Dragon chainbody. It also separates historical availability from build-254 equip behaviour instead of assuming a level-up message proves every secondary condition.
