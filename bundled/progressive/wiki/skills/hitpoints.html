Hitpoints is the free-to-play combat skill that represents a player's health. Current Hitpoints fall when damage is taken and can be restored by food, other healing effects, or natural regeneration; reaching zero causes death. Unlike the other ordinary skills, a normal new RuneScape 2 character begins at level 10 Hitpoints rather than level 1. Raising the base level increases the normal maximum health and also contributes to the player's combat level.

Mechanics

Combat XP: for ordinary combat in build 254, every point of credited damage gives 1.33 Hitpoints XP in addition to the experience assigned by the chosen melee, Ranged or Magic combat style. A hit of 10 damage therefore gives 13.3 Hitpoints XP before any special NPC combat-XP multiplier is applied.

  • Base versus current Hitpoints: the base skill level is the player's normal maximum health. Damage lowers the current value without lowering the base level; healing raises the current value again.
  • Death: build 254's damage routine queues player death when current Hitpoints reaches 0.
  • Melee: accurate, aggressive, defensive and controlled melee styles all award the same normal Hitpoints XP from damage. The style only changes how the other combat XP is distributed between Attack, Strength and Defence.
  • Ranged: damaging bow, crossbow and thrown-weapon attacks award normal Hitpoints XP alongside Ranged XP. Longrange still gives Hitpoints XP even though its combat-skill XP is split between Ranged and Defence.
  • Magic: damaging combat spells award Hitpoints XP from damage alongside their Magic experience. Spell-casting XP that is awarded independently of damage is not converted into extra Hitpoints XP.
  • Healing is not training: eating food or receiving another healing effect restores current Hitpoints but does not itself award Hitpoints experience.
  • Natural regeneration: build 254 gives Hitpoints its own regeneration timer. Each health-regeneration trigger heals 1 Hitpoint; the normal login timer is set to 100 cycles, while the Rapid Heal prayer changes that timer to 50 cycles while active.
  • Combat level: build 254 explicitly includes base Hitpoints together with Defence and half of Prayer in the base portion of the combat-level calculation. Raising Hitpoints can therefore raise combat level even though it unlocks no equipment.

Historical edge case: RuneScape Classic used a special starting Hits-XP value that did not line up with RuneScape 2's level-10 XP threshold. Some transferred Classic accounts could therefore appear below the normal level-10 RuneScape 2 starting state. This guide describes the ordinary new-character progression used for the September-2004 skill.

Training methods

Hitpoints is normally trained as a by-product of combat rather than by selecting a Hitpoints-specific attack style. For ordinary enemies, total credited damage is what matters: whichever combat method deals that damage also supplies the Hitpoints XP.

Healing and survival

Hitpoints training and Hitpoints restoration are separate systems. Combat raises the base skill through experience, while food and other healing effects restore the current value toward its normal maximum. This distinction matters when planning dangerous training: a higher Hitpoints level gives a larger health pool, but remaining alive still depends on armour, food, prayer, enemy damage, poison and the safety of the area.

2004scape implementation notes

  • 1.33 XP per damage is explicit: build ref 254's general combat-XP procedure converts credited damage into 1.33 Hitpoints XP after applying the NPC combat-XP multiplier.
  • Hitpoints is independent of combat style: the general routine awards Hitpoints XP after the style-specific Attack, Strength, Defence, Ranged or Magic allocation.
  • No level-up unlock entries: levelup_unlocks_hitpoints exists but contains no level values at ref 254. The previous local page was correct on this narrow point, but it mistook that empty popup list for a complete skill guide.
  • Combat-level contribution: combat_level.rs2 includes base Hitpoints in the defensive/base component of the player's combat level.
  • Health regeneration is separate: stat.rs2 excludes Hitpoints from ordinary stat restoration and instead heals it with a dedicated health_regen timer. The login script starts that timer at 100 cycles, and Rapid Heal switches it to 50.
  • Death at zero: damage.rs2 queues player_death when current Hitpoints reaches zero.
  • Cannon bypass: the build-254 multicannon script directly advances Ranged by 2 XP per credited point of damage. It does not award Hitpoints XP.

Cutoff and terminology notes

Use the 2004 name: the skill is Hitpoints in this September-2004 guide. Jagex did not rename the skill to Constitution and multiply displayed health values by ten until 3 March 2010. "Constitution" and "life points" are therefore post-cutoff terminology and are not used for target-period mechanics here.

  • Original skill: the health skill dates to RuneScape's 4 January 2001 launch; the earliest Classic name was Hits, but the target RuneScape 2 skill is Hitpoints.
  • Normal RuneScape 2 start: a new account begins at level 10 with 1,154 Hitpoints XP rather than level 1.
  • No later health systems: Constitution-era ten-times health scaling, later foods designed to overheal, later skillcapes, later minigames and other post-cutoff Hitpoints mechanics are excluded.
  • No invented XP/hour: Hitpoints XP/hour depends on total damage output, target XP multipliers, supplies and combat method. The guide therefore records the verified per-damage rate instead of importing modern training rates.
