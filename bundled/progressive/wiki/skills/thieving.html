Thieving is a members-only skill used to pickpocket NPCs, steal from market stalls, open or loot certain locked and trapped objects, and bypass Thieving checks during quests. It entered RuneScape on 30 April 2002 with pickpocketing, lock-picking, trap-disarming and market-stall theft already part of the skill. This guide stops at the 7 September 2004 snapshot, so later additions are excluded even when later source trees contain their level-up text.

Mechanics

  • Pickpocketing: click an eligible NPC and attempt to steal its configured loot. The target has a Thieving requirement and success chance. A failed attempt can make the NPC react, stun the player briefly and deal damage.
  • Market stalls: a stall has its own level requirement, XP award, loot and respawn delay. In the reconstructed server implementation, nearby owners or guards can catch the attempt instead of letting the theft complete.
  • Chests and traps: some chests require a Thieving level and may need a lockpick or trap-disarming interaction. Failed trap handling can deal damage; particular scripted traps can also move the player elsewhere.
  • Locked doors and quest traps: Thieving is also checked directly by quest and location scripts. These checks are not all generic training methods and may award no Thieving XP.
  • Membership: Thieving itself is members-only at the target date. Every ordinary skill unlock below therefore requires membership even when the surrounding area has other access rules.

Cutoff warning: fish stalls and Fremennik pickpocketing are not September-2004 content. They arrive with The Fremennik Trials on 2 November 2004, so the level-42 fish-stall and level-45 Fremennik rows from the old stub have been removed.

Training approach

There is no single mandatory route. Pickpocketing is portable but carries stun/damage failures, while stall training depends on stall respawns and whether an owner or guard can see the theft. Chests can be useful for particular locations or loot but are less universal as a level-by-level route.

2004scape implementation notes

  • Unlock-script boundary: the old page was built from active level-up entries but mixed the target-date ladder with later content. The September cutoff removes fish stalls and Fremenniks even if a later/current progressive source tree exposes those level-up entries.
  • Pickpocket configuration: the reconstructed early-2004 server snapshot gives each NPC its own level, XP, chance parameters, loot, stun behaviour and failure damage rather than using one generic reward table.
  • Stall configuration: the same source snapshot defines baker, tea, silk, fur, silver, spice and gem stalls with their own levels, XP, loot and respawn ticks. Stall scripts check configured owners/guards before granting the theft.
  • Gem-stall quirk: the reconstructed configuration explicitly uses 16 XP for gem stalls and comments that this differs from the later 160-XP value.
  • Chest/trap behaviour: scripted trapped chests are members-gated and can damage the player on failure; some trap scripts can teleport the player. Chest requirements therefore need to be read per object rather than treated as a single universal rule.
  • Quest XP is implementation-sensitive: Hazeel Cult, Tribal Totem and Fight Arena have documented differences between surviving 2004 guide values and the audited build-254 quest scripts. This page uses the build values while flagging the disagreement.

Cutoff exclusions

  • Fish stalls: excluded. They are introduced with The Fremennik Trials on 2 November 2004, after the target snapshot.
  • Fremennik pickpocketing: excluded for the same 2 November 2004 boundary.
  • Blackjacking: excluded. It belongs to later desert quest/content progression and is not part of the 7 September 2004 skill set.
  • Pyramid Plunder: excluded. The minigame is post-cutoff content.
  • Farming-era Master Farmer progression and later Thieving additions: excluded unless independently present by the target date.

Source notes

  • Historical release: the preserved 30 April 2002 Thieving update introduces the members skill with pickpocketing, lock-picking, trap-disarming and market-stall theft, and introduces Tribal Totem with a 21-Thieving requirement.
  • Period manual: the preserved 2003 official manual describes Thieving as members-only and documents the same core method families, including pickpocketing, locks, traps and stalls.
  • Build-254 cutoff: this wiki targets 7 September 2004 / build 254. Post-cutoff unlock text is filtered against historical release dates rather than accepted merely because it exists in a later source tree.
  • Reconstructed server mechanics: the archived 2004Scape/Server snapshot dated 18 May 2004 supplies the detailed pickpocket, stall and trapped-chest mechanics used above. Because that archive predates build 254, it is treated as implementation evidence, not silently relabelled as the September ref.
  • Local quest cross-checks: the already-audited Watchtower, Tribal Totem, The Dig Site, Troll Stronghold, Temple of Ikov, Legends' Quest, Biohazard, Hazeel Cult, Fight Arena and The Tourist Trap pages provide the quest requirements and reward values summarized above.
  • November boundary: preserved release records for The Fremennik Trials place the quest, Fremennik pickpocketing and fish stalls on 2 November 2004, which is why both old-stub rows are excluded.
